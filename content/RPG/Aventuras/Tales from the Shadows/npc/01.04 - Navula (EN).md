# Navula Tide-Sage

## Snapshot
- **Role**: Swamp witch and planar balance keeper; essential mentor for shadow lore.
- **Motivation**: Preserve equilibrium between realms and pass on hard-won knowledge before death.
- **Leverage**: Values secrets, rare reagents, and respect; opposes anyone endangering coastlines or desecrating graves.
- **Stat Baseline**: `Archmage` (MM p.342) reflavored with hag abilities (`bestow curse`, `geas`, lair actions in grotto).
- **Deep Dive**: Refer to [[npc/apend.01 - Integrated NPCs Complete Guide#Navula (Witch + Oma Rattenfanger Fusion)|Integrated NPCs]].

- **Act I – Cryptic Informant**: Introduces shadow incursions and warns about the lighthouse cult ([[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)#Scene 4: The Abandoned Lighthouse|Act I]]).
- **Act II – Lore Broker**: Provides insight on Faylene’s hex and Sogmonnath’s legend in exchange for the Agent’s secrets ([[02.00 - Act II - Political Intrigue (Lv5-7)#Adventure 9: Shadow Court|Act II]]).

## Scene Hooks
- **Secret-for-Secret Bargain**: Each question answered requires PC to divulge equivalent truth; failure imposes disadvantage on future dealings.
- **Grotto Trial**: Tests visitors with shadow illusions; success grants boon (`shadow ward`: resist necrotic damage once).
- **Last Lesson**: Before final battle, Navula gifts ritual component or prophecy if PC promises to honor tides (inspires finale choices).

## Mechanical Notes
- Lair actions: summon grasping kelp (restrain), flood the grotto (difficult terrain), call spectral fish (grant advantage vs. fiends).
- Assign `Favor` score (0-3); each fulfilled request increases it, unlocking deeper lore or unique potions.
- Rewards include `Shadow-Stilled Talisman` (advantage on saves vs. shadow possession) and rare reagents for plague cure.
