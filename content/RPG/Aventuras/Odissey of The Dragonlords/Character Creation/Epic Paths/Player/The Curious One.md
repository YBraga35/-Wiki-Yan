**The Curious One**  
Your ancestors have long held the history of Thylea in great reverence, recording its stories for posterity. From a young age, your family has drilled into you the importance of the past, and has made you commit much of Thylea’s history to memory. However, you see a flaw in their views: they place import only on the past and disregard the present. You know that a great change is coming, and that history is being made, even as your famous family focuses on the days of yore. You feel you have a duty to bear witness to the coming events that will shape Thylea; you will be the one to weave the stories that will be told for generations, even if you must leave your home behind to do it.

**For players who love:** Virgil, Hunter S. Thompson  
**Restrictions:** You must be a native to Thylea  
**Adventure Hook:** The Oracle believes she knows the path to the next era of Thylea

**Heroic Tasks**

* Witness a battle for the ages  
* Learn what became of the Dragonlords, their dragons, and the Lost Titans  
* Chronicle at least five secrets and myths about Thylea  
* Find items capable of aiding you in your chronicling. Choose one lesser and one greater item from the following list:  
    
    
  * (lesser) *Cape of the Mountebank*  
  * (lesser) *Robe of Eyes*  
  * (lesser) *Cloak of Protection*

  * (greater) *Tome of Clear Thought*  
  * (greater) *Tome of Understanding*  
  * (greater) *Tome of Leadership and Influence*

**Divine Blessing**  
*Blessing of the Storyteller.* You may reroll any one attack roll, ability check, or saving throw. You must use the second roll. The blessing cannot be used again until after a long rest. Additionally, You can speak, read, write, and understand all languages.
