### 📜 **IMPERIAL ASSET RETURN AND RECONVERSION DECLARATION**

**Issued by order of High Inquisitor Edum of the Bones, custodian of the Crimson Will, on Solar Day 08 of Frog, in the year 1479 of the Era of Light.**

**To the attention of: Quizar Vaelen Roend, Valen Braxigar, and the reanimated entity self-identified as _Ashvatta Kalpa_, also referred to as "the Awakened Good" or "the Reanimated Artifact".**

**CONSIDERING THAT:**

1. The piece acquired by inquisitorial authority and titled "Statue of Hero Caelen" has proven to be a sentient being, endowed with speech, mobility, magic, and claims regarding its own historical origin;

2. The said statue was funded by public resource amounting to **120,000 gold pieces**, constituting misappropriation of imperial property for unregistered purposes;

3. Its nature and behavior pose a risk to the symbolic stability of the Crimson faith and the Red Tower;
    

---

**THE FOLLOWING CONTRACT IS ESTABLISHED, with the aim of avoiding summary extermination or compulsory petrification:**

---

### CLAUSE I — Parties and Responsibilities

**1. Quizar Vaelen Roend**, a free citizen and licensed arcanist, is appointed **Spokesperson of the Endeavor**, assuming diplomatic, logistical, and arcane-legal responsibility over mission conduct.

**2. Valen Braxigar**, an unclaimed veteran of the Svaldir conflict, is named **Guardian and Defender of the Reanimated Artifact**, with authority to intervene physically in case of mission risk, population endangerment, or threats to Ashvatta itself.

**3. _Ashvatta Kalpa_**, previously inert and now manifested as an autonomous being, **acknowledges its status as imperial property under conditional liberty**, and commits to:

- Not invoke or manifest extraplanar powers without authorization or vital necessity;
    
- Actively participate in the search for **Temporal Fragments**, as indicated by the Crimson Manuscript;
    
- Submit to periodic inspections by designated arcanists;
    
- Not self-proclaim as savior, redeemer, or reincarnated deity, under risk of **reimposition into petrified state.**
    

---

### CLAUSE II — Mission Objectives

The aforementioned triad will embark on an investigative, exploratory, and compensatory journey aiming to:

- Locate three **Temporal Fragments**, whose magical properties will compensate for the logistical error committed;
    
- Eventually validate Ashvatta's existence as a legitimate historical entity or reclassify it as a "doctrinal anomaly";
    
- Partially or fully restore the invested value in the form of artifacts, discoveries, or pacts beneficial to the Crimson Empire.
    

---

### CLAUSE III — Custody and Subordination

Ashvatta will remain **under shared guardianship of Quizar and Valen**, both held responsible for its moral, magical, and physical containment.

Misconduct by Ashvatta will result in **collective punishment**, including reclassification of companions as accomplice traitors, **punishable by public sacrifice or exile to the Desert of the End**.

---

### CLAUSE IV — Supervision and Reporting

- Quizar must monthly report on Ashvatta's position, actions, and behavior;
    
- Valen must attest to the group's physical integrity and risks faced;
    
- Ashvatta will be heard through glyph-recorded testimonies, with the right to reply only if formally requested.
    

---

### CLAUSE V — Alternatives to the Present Contract

The individuals mentioned may **decline this contract** and, in such case, choose one of the options below:

**(A)** Compulsory conversion to the Crimson faith, with lobotomy and allocation to a spiritual reeducation zone;

**(B)** Transfiguration into stone for an indefinite period, to be exhibited in the Hall of Historical Errors;

**(C)** Ritual combat to the death with three inquisitorial champions, without magical armament;

**(D)** Arcane imprisonment in a timeless globe, with biannual visits from Edum of the Bones for preaching and guilt monitoring;

**(E)** Immediate acceptance of the present contract and initiation of the mission before the next full moon.

_A choice from the above options is mandatory. Failure to respond within 12 hours will be interpreted as acceptance of option (B)._

---

**Signed and sealed:**

**Edum of the Bones**  
_High Inquisitor and Supervisor of the Red Tower_
