# Gaius’s House in Mytros

## The Noble District

> The Noble District is the city’s mercantile‑aristocratic heart. At its center stands the Grand Agora — statues of the Five around the edge, and at the center a taller new statue of Sydon, a declaration of the Order’s rise.

## The Residence

> Gaius’ house rises in classical lines with martial flourishes: a Doric portico veined in blue like Sydon’s sea; a formal garden of geometric cypresses; low limestone walls with iron gates wrought in storm motifs. Discreet centurions stand watch.

Inside

> Within: a mosaic atrium depicting Sydon’s battle against the “Usurpers”; a receiving room of maps and tapestries; a private study of reports and arms; and at the heart, a skylit chamber where the **Scales of Persephone** rest upon a marble form, their glow shifting with the sun.

The Scales’ Signs

> The armor pulses brighter when Elion nears, repels Gaius with violent discharges, casts small arcs toward its true bearer — and may flash with echoes of past deeds.
