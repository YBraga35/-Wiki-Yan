# Part 1: After the Mossy Temple

With `Xaricus` defeated, the Mossy Temple is eerily silent except for the echo of the recent battle. Signs of Demetria’s presence are everywhere, yet she and the bulk of her forces have already withdrawn.  The party may have recovered the Horn of Balmytria or discovered that she took it with her.  Whatever the case, they now possess clues to her next moves.

## Clues the Players May Find

- **Loreus and Corinna**  
  If rescued, they can recount how Demetria used the horn to corrupt other fae and humans, forging an army of maddened satyrs and maenads.

- **Herkus the Druid**  
  If her madness is healed, Herkus can warn the party that Demetria’s next target is the Ancient Forest—specifically its *Heart of the Forest*, a site of immense druidic power.  He fears that she intends to corrupt it.

- **Fae Visitors (Dryads & Satyrs)**  
  If the players encounter the fledgling fae group that arrived “just in time,” they can confirm Demetria’s ambitions: she is moving to confront her ancestor, the mighty dryad Prosymeina, guardian of the Heart of the Forest.

# Part 2: The Heart of the Ancient Forest

The next phase of Demetria’s plan is to seize an ancient bastion of power, located many miles east of the Mossy Temple.

## The Trail of Madness

> **Narrative**  
> “Following the traces left by Demetria’s army, you delve deeper into the Ancient Forest. The trail is unmistakable—a scar of depraved foliage and violence. Broken branches, remnants of fire‑pits, and the sour scent of spilled wine mark the path. As you progress, the forest itself seems to wither. The trees whisper with hostile voices and shadows move unnaturally. You encounter small groups of satyrs and maenads, their eyes glowing with feverish madness, attacking without thought, driven solely by fanatical devotion to their new queen.”

The journey should be punctuated by escalating encounters with Demetria’s new rivals, illustrating the rapid growth of her influence.

## The Unexpected Rescue

When the party is on the brink of being overwhelmed by a greater host of maenads and satyrs, they are saved.

> **Narrative**  
> “From the forest shadows, silent arrows hiss and dispatch your assailants. Two agile figures, draped in leaf‑tattered cloaks and leather, appear. They are dryad hunters, their eyes glowing with ancient tree‑wisdom. ‘Quick, over here! Prosymeina sent us. You can’t stand against the fallen dryad alone.’”

The hunters guide the party through secret paths to a quiet, hidden glade where they will meet Prosymeina.

## The Dilemma of Prosymeina

Prosymeina is an immensely powerful dryad, an enchanting ancestor whose presence radiates Thylea’s very strength and calm.

> **Narrative**  
> “At the heart of the glade, beside a crystalline spring, stands a dryad whose beauty surpasses any mortal’s. Her skin resembles silver birch bark, and her hair flows like willow leaves. She introduces herself as Prosymeina, guardian of the Heart of the Forest.  
> ‘I know why you’re here,’ she says, her voice rustling with the wind. ‘My descendant, Demetria, has gone mad with grief and poisoned by the promises of a dark goddess, and has seized the Heart of the Forest for herself. She has profaned my home with her depraved rites.’  
> She sighs, and a profound sorrow hangs in the air. ‘I could crush her with a thought, but I’m bound. Long ago, all the dryads of this forest swore an oath to never harm one another or our sacred trees. Demetria used that oath against me. Her fae servants are protected by my own word. I cannot act directly.’”

### The Request for Help

Prosymeina explains that the only way she can act is if the oath is broken. She does not know how, but believes that the party, as outsiders not bound by forest law, might find a solution.

# Part 3: Next Steps – Breaking the Oath

The party’s goal is now to find a way to nullify Prosymeina’s oath.  This becomes a distinct quest.  Here are a few ways they might achieve it:

- **The Fates’ Trial**  
  The party may have to journey to the Fates’ Isle and barter with them.  The Fates could offer a method to *cut* the oath’s thread in exchange for a great sacrifice or the completion of a perilous task.

- **Thylea’s Ritual**  
  The party could be sent to the Golden Heart to commune directly with the mother‑goddess.  Thylea may provide a solution, but will demand proof of valor—perhaps defending one of her sacred beasts from a looming threat.

- **The Logic of the Oath**  
  An astute character might discover a loophole in the oath.  Perhaps the oath forbids *harming*, but not *imprisoning* or *exiling*.  Or perhaps the oath only applies within the bounds of the Ancient Forest, allowing Demetria to be lured outside.

## The Confrontation at the Heart of the Forest

Once the oath is broken, Prosymeina and her druids, aided by the party, can finally assault the Heart of the Forest to banish Demetria.

> **Narrative**  
> “The battle for the Heart of the Forest is a chaotic clash of primal fury.  Druids turn into beasts, dryads fire light‑laden arrows, and forest elementals rise to fight.  In the center, Demetria, astride a corrupted treant, fights with the rage of a shattered soul.  She fights not to win but to destroy, laughing amid the chaos as the power of the Horn of Balmytria flows through her, driving the earth around her mad.”

### The True Plan of Demetria (The Twist)

Nobody knows that defeat is exactly what Demetria desires.

> **Narrative**  
> “When the final blow lands, Demetria does not scream in pain but in triumph.  Her physical body dissolves into vines and thorns, yet her spirit—a dark green‑shaded shade—shoots toward Prosymeina.  ‘You gave me what I wanted, ancestor,’ her voice echoes in everyone's minds. ‘Death! And now, your power is mine!’”  
> Demetria used the profane revelry around the Heart of the Forest to bind her soul to its tree, just as Prosymeina’s is.  Upon “death,” her spirit invades Prosymeina’s mind the next time she sleeps, usurping her and granting Demetria control over the ancient dryad’s vast power.

This concludes “Phase 2” of Demetria’s plan, preparing her to become an even greater threat, now inhabiting one of the forest’s most powerful beings and ready to march upon Estoria.
