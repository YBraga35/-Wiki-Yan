# Encounter Matrix — Two-Player Adaptation (Lv 1‑20)

This guide calibrates **Tales from the Shadows** for a duo (PC + DMPC). It assumes:

- Two adventurers act every round (player Eldritch Knight + halfling Divine Soul DMPC).
- The DMPC stays support-focused (buffs, control, healing). If you allow full striker output, increase XP thresholds by an additional 25%.
- 2024 ruleset encounter XP thresholds (unchanged from 2014 DMG). Apply multipliers for multiple foes as usual, then compare adjusted totals against the targets below.

### How to Use
1. Calculate XP total for enemies in the encounter.
2. Apply multi-creature multiplier from DMG/Tasha’s (2 foes ×1.5, 3‑6 foes ×2, 7‑10 foes ×2.5, 11‑14 foes ×3, 15+ foes ×4).
3. Compare adjusted XP to the thresholds above.

> **Small Party Safety Valve**  
> If the adjusted XP sits extremely close to a Deadly threshold, introduce fail-safes (e.g., morale, environmental cover, optional objectives) because two-character tables have limited recovery.

## Challenge Banding Suggestions

- **Boss Fights**: Use a single creature CR ≈ party level +1, give it legendary resistances or “elite” actions for pacing. Avoid raw CR 6+ threats before level 5 unless you stage escape/banish options (see [[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)|Act I dossier]] lighthouse finale).
- **Duo-Friendly Waves**: Two combatants per wave keeps action economy manageable. If you exceed four enemies in play, implement minion rules (1 hit = defeat) or scripted disengagement triggers.
- **Environmental Pressure**: Layer hazards and skill challenges (plague exposure, courtroom objections, naval crossfire) to supplement combat stakes without overwhelming the action economy.

## Quick Checklists

- **Before Session**: confirm level, select encounters whose adjusted XP ≤ Hard threshold, prep one emergency escape valve.
- **During Play**: track condition economy (two characters suffer if both restrained/held); stagger enemy control effects.
- **After Session**: log encounter results + clock shifts directly into [[00.00 - Master Matrix (EN)|Master Matrix]] under “Maintenance Notes.”
