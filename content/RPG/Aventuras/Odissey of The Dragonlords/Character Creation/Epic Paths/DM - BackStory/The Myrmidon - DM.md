# The Myrmidon

While the Dragonlords were the champions of the First War, wars are not won by individuals alone. To support the Dragonlords, legions of hoplites were recruited. The first of these legions, the Myrmidons, sported the symbol of an ant upon their banner as they faced the armies of the Titans, symbolizing unity and the ability to stand together against otherwise insurmountable obstacles. No one is certain what became of the original Myrmidons, but you know one thing: your ancestor stood amongst them.

You have lived a simple life as a soldier of Mytros, but you feel it is your duty and destiny to reclaim your family’s lost honour and complete their task of vanquishing the armies of the Titans by standing alongside the Dragonlords.

**For players who love:** The 9th Legion, 300 (film)  
**Restrictions:** You must be from Thylea  
**Adventure Hook:** The Oracle knows of your ancestry and can give you information that would allow you to restore the army of the Dragonlords

## Heroic Tasks

* Recover the lost banner of the Myrmidons
* Recover the Legionnaire Commander’s legendary spear
* Be conferred the title of Master of Myrmidons by a true Dragonlord

## Divine Blessing
*Blessing of Elysium.* As an action you can call forth the spirits of the ancient Myrmidons, summoning 1d4-1 (minimum 1) Legionnaires (use **Gladiator** stats). Once used, you cannot summon the spirits again until 1d4 days have passed.

# Integrating the Myrmidon

## Meeting the Oracle
The Oracle knows of the character’s Myrmidon ancestry and knows that they are going to do great things, and are capable of uniting Thylea against the Titans. She informs them that Queen **Vallus** might know more about the Myrmidons and that the leader of the Dragonflight of old, **Xander Huorath**, slumbers at the **Necropolis of Telamok** and that they should seek him out.

> "I see that the blood of the Myrmidons runs through your veins. You, like your ancestors before you, shall unite Thylea against the Titans that threaten to destroy her. You must rally the people, recover the ancient symbols of your order, and be anointed their general by the new Dragonlords. Once done, you shall stand at the forefront of the army in the great battle that must be."

## Meeting Volkan
**Volkan** forged the equipment of the first Myrmidons. He mentions that it is possible that he could forge that equipment again if the right materials are available. For 300 gp, he can forge a *+1 Vicious Weapon* using the Mithral Forge.

## Meeting Xander
**Xander** informs the Myrmidon that they are a descendent of **Aristomache**, the Legionnaire Commander. She was a dear friend of Xander’s and he intended to make her a Dragonlord, but she perished in battle before he had the opportunity. Out of respect for Aristomache, he was buried with her *Banner of the Myrmidons* and the *Immovable Rod* it was once hung upon. Both items are located in a corner of his true tomb.

The Banner is damaged and tattered and currently cannot be used, but the *Immovable Rod* remains in good condition. Xander does not know how to repair the Banner but grants both items to the Myrmidon. He also is unaware of what happened to her famous Spear, but presumes it would be kept with other relics of the Dragonlords.

After delivering Commander Aristomache’s relics to the Myrmidon, Xander confers the title of Master of Myrmidons on to them, and asks that they stand as one of the new Dragonlords, the honour he wished to grant their ancestor.

## Meeting Estor
If the Myrmidon allows themselves to be possessed by **Estor**, he will reveal the location of the *Spear of the Myrmidon*. **Kyrah** advises against this, believing that someone else could help, and that Estor shouldn’t be trusted.

## The Wisdom of Vallus
**Vallus** speaks to the Myrmidon before they set sail on the *Ultros* and lets them know that the **Lotus Witch** might have some information on the original Myrmidons and that the Loom of the **Fates** could be used to repair the Banner, though she warns of their treacherous behaviour.

## Consulting the Lotus Witch
The **Lotus Witch** knows of the Myrmidons. She informs the Myrmidon that the *Spear of the Myrmidons* can be found in the lost treasure of the Dragonlords, as well as the whereabouts of the treasure, in the ‘mirror island’ of the Nether Sea.

## Repairing the Banner
The **Fates** offer to repair the Banner of the Myrmidon Legion, if a task is completed for them. This could be one of the three recommended in the book, or alternatively, the Fates may ask the party to slay a group of ichthys that the Fates are responsible for ‘creating.’ The ichthys are on the shore of their island, plotting to slay the coven. In exchange for their deaths, the Fates will use the loom to mend the Banner.

If the Fates are slain, have **Versi** (or the Doomed One, or potentially even the Myrmidon themselves) be capable of using the loom themselves to repair the Banner.

## Finding the Spear
The *Spear of the Myrmidon* is found among the treasure of the Dragonlords in Mirror Prison in the Nether Sea. This can be learned from the **Lotus Witch**, the **Fates**, the sphinxes of the **Island of Time**, or possibly through divination such as the spell *Legend Lore*.

## The Battle of Mytros
At the Battle of Mytros, add an additional epic event - ‘Rallying the Defenders’ - where the Myrmidon can rally the defenders of Mytros, its centurions and soldiers, to regroup and make a stand against the Titans’ armies. This could occur either around the palace, near the Colossus of Pythor, or near the colosseum.

Under the Myrmidon’s lead, the forces begin pushing back against the armies of **Sydon**, the culmination of which could include leading different **captains** and **soldiers** against one of the Twin Titans’ children, **Goloron** (**Young Empyrean**).

## Divine Boon
If the Myrmidon is ever killed, the spectral image of their ancestor comes into view and stands with a shield and spear over the body of the Myrmidon. At the start of the Myrmidon’s next turn, they come under the effects of the *Resurrection* spell. This can only happen once.

# Banner of the Myrmidon Legion
*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)*

Crafted from a thick, red fabric, this banner measures 5 feet high and 3 feet wide. The ant symbol of the Myrmidons is displayed on the fabric with round, metal plates sewn into it. It can be attached to a 10-foot pole to serve as a standard. Furling or unfurling the banner requires an action. The banner has the following properties.

***Mark of Courage.*** As a bonus action, you can touch the unfurled banner and cause it to emanate courage. You and your allies are immune to the frightened condition while within 20 feet of it. This benefit lasts for 10 minutes or until the banner is furled. Once you use this property, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

***Sentinel Standard.*** You can see invisible creatures while they are within 20 feet of the unfurled banner and within your line of sight.

***Standard's Shield.*** As a bonus action, you can touch the unfurled banner and invoke this power. Any ranged attack roll that targets you or an ally of yours has disadvantage if the target is within 20 feet of the unfurled banner. This benefit lasts for 1 minute or until the banner is furled. Once you use this property, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

# Spear of the Myrmidon
*Weapon (Spear), Legendary (Requires Attunement)*

You gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. This weapon has 9 charges and regains 1d6+3 expended charges each day at dusk. While holding the spear, you can expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it (save DC 16): *command* (1 charge), *mirror image* (2 charges), *spirit guardians* (3 charges), or *spirit shroud* (3 charges).

***Spectral Warrior.*** Once on your turn when you hit a creature with an attack using the spear, you can choose to summon a spectral warrior from the weapon. Until the start of your next turn, that creature is haunted by the warrior. That creature has disadvantage on any weapon attack it makes against you until the start of your next turn. This property has no effect against undead creatures.

***Haunting Phalanx.*** You can use an action while holding the spear to expend 3 charges and point it forward commandingly. 5 spectral, spear-wielding warriors appear before you and march in a 25 by 50-foot line in a direction you choose. Any creature within that line's area must make a DC 16 Intelligence saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes 6d10 force damage and is under the effects of the weapon's *spectral warriors* property. On a success, the creature takes half damage and isn't affected by the spectral warriors. Once this feature of the spear has been used, it can't be used again until you finish a short or long rest.
