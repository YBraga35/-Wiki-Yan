**The Seeking One**

You hail from a distant land, where your people worshiped foreign gods and spoke languages unknown to the people of Thylea. You sailed in search of the fabled Thylea as part of a research expedition, but your ship and most of your crew were destroyed in a storm. Found by fishermen and brought to the city of Mytros, you discovered that your arrival had been prophesied by the Oracle. You feel you have a duty to finish your expedition's task and record as much as possible about this mythical land. Perhaps meeting this so-called Oracle would help?

**For players who love:** Charles Darwin, Phileas Fogg, Ferdinand Magellan 

**Restrictions:** Must be an explorer from distant lands.

**Adventure Hook:** The Oracle believes she knows the path to the next era of Thylea and that you have been sent from the outside world by the gods in order to fulfil the prophecy.

 **Heroic Tasks**

* Find what happened to your fellow crew members. (The circumstances are up to the DM however my suggestion is that you have some crew as prisoners in the Forbidden Library, etc.)  
* Learn what became of the Dragonlords and their dragons.  
* Find items capable of aiding you in your chronicling. Choose one item from the following list:  
  * *Cape of the Mountebank*  
  * *Robe of Eyes*  
  * *Cloak of Protection*   
* Chronicle at least five secrets and myths about Thylea  
* Find the fabled treasures of the Dragonlords. Choose one item from the following list that will be located in the treasure trove:  
  * *Gem of Seeing*  
  * *Necklace of Prayer Beads*  
  * *Glamoured Studded Leather*  
  * *Rod of Rulership*

**Divine Blessing**

*Blessing of the Seeker.* You may reroll any one attack roll, ability check, or saving throw. You must use the second roll. The blessing cannot be used again until after a long rest. Additionally, you can speak, read, write, and understand all languages.
