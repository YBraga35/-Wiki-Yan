# Toren “Tor” Underleaf

## Snapshot
- **Role**: Halfling Divine Soul sorcerer, DMPC support caster, and childhood friend of both Dagmara and the Agent.
- **Motivation**: Protect the Agent, balance Maal’s rigid doctrine with compassion, and keep trade routes viable for his merchant family.
- **Leverage**: Emotional history with the Agent, connections to merchant guilds, reliable battlefield support.
- **Stat Baseline**: Sorcerer 3 (Divine Soul) focused on buffs and emergency healing (`bless`, `shield`, `healing word`, `sanctuary`). Spell list tuned per [[00.12 - Encounter Matrix (EN)|Encounter Matrix]] for duo balance.

## Arc by Act
- **Act I – Faithful Companion**: Reunites at the docks, smoothing introductions and offering tactical support during early investigations ([[00.03 - Session One Briefing (EN)|Session Briefing]]).
- **Act II – Moral Counterweight**: Challenges hardline decisions, reminding the Agent of Maal’s compassion; liaises with merchants during political intrigue ([[02.00 - Act II - Political Intrigue (Lv5-7)|Act II]]).
- **Act III – Logistics Officer**: Coordinates supply lines for the blockade run and ritual components, stepping back from front-line combat.
- **Act IV – Witness of Character**: Testifies to the Agent’s integrity during tribunal; may channel Maal’s blessings in climactic scene.

## Relationship Web
- **Dagmara Stoneheart**: Childhood friend; Tor still grieves her loss and honors her investigative methods ([[npc/01.07 - Dagmara Stoneheart (EN)|Dagmara dossier]]).
- **Bonita Sandberg**: Assists healing efforts; they trade medical knowledge when workload overwhelms her ([[npc/01.05 - Bonita Sandberg (EN)|Bonita dossier]]).
- **Octant Faylene**: Respects her devotion, becomes key in re-centering her faith post-hex ([[npc/01.03 - Octant Faylene (EN)|Faylene dossier]]).
- **Merchant Guilds**: Maintains contacts granting access to ships, supplies, and rumors for [[03.10 - Naval Briefing (EN)|blockade prep]].

## Scene Hooks
- **Shared Memory**: Use flashback vignettes highlighting Tor and the Agent’s past to reinforce stakes; provide Inspiration when resolved thoughtfully.
- **Divine Surge**: In moments of crisis, Tor can spend sorcery points to twin a clutch spell on the Agent (limited to once per major scene).
- **Moral Compass**: If the Agent contemplates ruthless action, Tor voices dissent; reward alignment with his counsel via advantage on next social roll.

## Mechanical Notes
- Keep Tor’s offensive output modest—prioritize support spells and reaction-based protection.
- Track `Trust Beat` moments: when Agent validates Tor’s perspective, grant Tor an extra sorcery point usable that session.
- Equipment: signet of Maal (focus), merchant writ granting audience with guild reps.
