# Hilbre Isle — Sefton’s Shadow Court

## Overview
Hilbre Isle is a jagged rock fortress half-shrouded in perpetual twilight. Sefton Bane transformed the old lighthouse keep into a palace of obsidian glass, mirror corridors, and ritual arenas where shadow-fey courtiers practice politics and cruelty in equal measure.

Features prominently in [[02.00 - Act II - Political Intrigue (Lv5-7)#Adventure 9: Shadow Court|Shadow Court adventure]] and [[03.10 - Naval Briefing (EN)|Night of the Blockade]].

## Cinematic Beats
- **Approach by Fog (Act II)**: Describe a sea of mist pierced by phosphorescent lanterns. As the PCs near Hilbre, the water reflects starless sky; eerie viol music drifts across waves, inviting them closer.
- **Masquerade of Masks (Act II)**: During Sefton’s gala, shadow courtiers wear living masks that whisper temptations. Crystalline chandeliers cast prismatic light that warps reflections, foreshadowing mirror-based hazards.
- **Storm-Split Sky (Act III)**: When the blockade culminates, lightning forks around the island, revealing temporarily the true form of Sefton’s palace—thorny and alien. Great for cinematic battle descriptions.
- **Breaking the Pact (Act IV)**: Should Sefton be brought to justice or seek redemption, depict the mirrors cracking and twilight receding, letting natural starlight return.

## Story Hooks by Act
- **Act II**:
  - **Diplomatic Infiltration**: PCs attend a banquet to expose shadow contracts; success yields blockade intel.
  - **Prisoner Rescue**: Rotarist captives held in mirror cells; freeing them grants testimony for tribunal.
- **Act III**:
  - **Sabotage Gun Emplacements** before the blockade run; stealth mission across sea caves below the palace.
  - **Confront the Pale Maiden** assassin—defeating her weakens Sefton’s elite guard.

## Mechanical & Atmosphere Notes
- Magical lighting counts as dim light; creatures gain advantage on Stealth if wearing reflective garb provided at the court.
- Mirror-maze mechanics: each round inside, PC makes DC 15 Wisdom save or suffers displacement (teleport 10 ft to random space) and takes psychic damage from contradictory reflections.
- Fey contracts: social encounter using modified downtime rules—PC can gain boons but on failure suffers binding conditions (e.g., no lying, vulnerability to psychic).
