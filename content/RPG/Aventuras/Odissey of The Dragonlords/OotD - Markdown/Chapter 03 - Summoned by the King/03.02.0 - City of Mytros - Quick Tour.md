# City of Mytros — Quick Tour (Player Read‑Aloud)

Short descriptions to introduce major districts. Use as scene setters while moving through the city.

## Great Palace (Royal Hill)
> The Great Palace crowns a broad stair. Bronze law tablets line the entrance, and banners snap in the sea wind. Inside, cool marble halls swallow sound, and bronze‑armored guards stand in gleaming ranks.

## Theater of the Gods
> A hillside amphitheater of white marble, the stage polished like a mirror. When festivals come, fireworks and illusions paint the night.

## The Academy
> Colonnades and green lawns link a dozen marble buildings. Students debate under cypress shade; bells mark the hours of lectures and experiments.
> The Great Library smells of vellum and ink; the Hall of Heroes is lined with statues; the Orrery’s bronze spheres tick and hum; the Hall of Debate crackles with ideas.
