## 📋 How to Complete a Quick Reference

### Step 1: Read Source Material
Open `00.02 - Handling the Epic Paths [EN].md` and read the full section for your chosen path.

### Step 2: Copy Template
Use `Quick Reference - Template Epic Path [EN].md` as your starting structure.

### Step 3: Fill in Details
Refer to completed examples:
- `Quick Reference - The Demigod [EN].md` - Shows family drama, item quests
- `Quick Reference - The Vanished One [EN].md` - Shows memory mechanics, toxic romance

### Step 4: Key Sections to Complete

1. **AT-A-GLANCE**: One-sentence summaries
2. **MILESTONE TABLE**: When triggers happen (level/session)
3. **KEY NPCs**: 3-5 most important, with roleplay tips
4. **AT-TABLE CHECKLIST**: What to prep, what to watch for
5. **FAILURE STATES**: "If players skip X, do Y"
6. **DIVINE BLESSING**: Exact resurrection mechanic
7. **REWARDS TRACKER**: Items by level
8. **DRAMATIC MOMENTS**: Boxed text for 2-3 key scenes

### Step 5: Test It
- Can you run a session using ONLY this document?
- Is it 2-4 pages max?
- Does it have quick-scan tables?

### Step 6: Publish
Change `draft: true` to `draft: false` when done.
