## Handout 2: Children's Rhyme (Granny Willa's Song)

```
THE PUMPKIN HEAD'S PROMISE
(Do NOT sing after dark)

When wicker men walk under crooked moon's light,
And pumpkin heads glow with unholy sight,
Bring harvest flame blessed by morning's first dew,
For only sacred fire can see you through.

The Horned King hungers for autumn's first yield,
Blood and grain sown in cursed field,
Three circles turning, three voices must cry,
Or sleepers wake never, and harvesters die.

Iron and fire, iron and fire,
These guard against the King's desire,
But when the harvest moon grows thin,
That's when the wicker men begin.

- Traditional, Author Unknown
- Recorded by Granny Willa
```

## Physical Props (Optional)

**Easy to Prepare:**
- Real autumn leaves for atmosphere
- Battery tea lights for "pumpkin lights"
- Twine/string for wicker representations
- Actual pumpkin seeds in small bags
- Iron washers as "iron tokens"

**Sound Effects:**
- Church bells (YouTube/Spotify)
- Autumn wind sounds
- Crackling fire
- Whispers/chanting
- Crow calls

**Scent (if appropriate):**
- Apple candle for festival
- Smoky scent for burning
- Earthy/musty for grove

## Quick Handout Rules

1. **Physical Games:** Give Handouts 1, 2, 5 immediately
2. **Digital Games:** Share screen or send images
3. **Map Revelation:** Only after finding in-game
4. **Symbol Recognition:** Show when discovered
5. **Notes/Letters:** Read aloud or display
6. **Aftermath:** Send after session as memento

**If printing:**

- Prepare image files in order
- Have text ready to copy/paste
