# 1. Yelena Is Approached by the Messenger of the Lady of Coins

After Sydon’s storm ceases over Mytros, Yelena receives an unexpected message. The messenger is a skilled spy, disguised as an ordinary citizen, but every movement is calculated, every glance observant.

Approach:

> The messenger nears Yelena at a moment of relative privacy, perhaps as she watches the city recover from the storm. He speaks softly, with reverence:
>
> “Your Highness, I bring a message from the Lady of Coins. She has watched your deeds and recognizes in you the true heir to Themis’ throne. She offers Sacred Hospitality — Guest Friendship — a meeting under the ancient customs of the Great Mothers.”

He leaves a token: a silver coin bearing a serpent — proof of the offer and the oath.

# 2. Arrival at the Colossus of Pythor

The ascent is tense and symbolic. Yelena climbs the internal stairs of the colossal statue, each step echoing in the hollow until reaching the head.

Ascent texture:
- The interior blends grandeur and decay.
- Ancient chains and mechanisms line the walls.
- Steps echo like heartbeats.
- As she ascends, small apertures offer vast views of Mytros.

Arrival in the Head:

> At the spherical chamber at the top, a surprisingly luxurious setting awaits. Two round windows look south to the sea. Heavy silk curtains drape the walls. At center, a silk hammock holds a figure wearing a platinum helm.

# 3. The Scene with Moxena

Recognition:

> Moxena removes her helm, revealing her face. Though the years and her transformation into a medusa, something familiar remains in her eyes — the same compassion shown when she rescued the child Yelena.
>
> “My little princess… how you’ve grown. Thirteen years have passed since I set you on that boat, trusting Fate to guard you.”

Confession:

> “I was there that terrible night. I saw your mother, the true Queen Thesilea, murdered by my sisters. When I was ordered to cleanse the royal chambers and erase every trace of the old dynasty, I found you — a defenseless child hiding in a wardrobe. My conscience would not let me obey.”
>
> “For years I bore this weight. I tried to redeem myself fighting my sisters, but failed and fled. Now I serve as the Lady of Coins, gathering resources and information, waiting for the day the true heir would return.”

# 4. Words for the Exiled

Oath of Alliance:

> “Yelena, daughter of Thesilea, I offer more than words — I offer a sacred oath. Before the Great Mothers and before the Implacable Furies, I swear to dedicate my life and resources to your cause. I will help you reclaim the throne that is yours by right.”
>
> “In exchange, I ask only that you swear not to seek vengeance against me for the crimes I committed. My part in that terrible night is a stain I will carry forever, but my remorse is genuine.”

On the Future:

> “The road back to power will be perilous. The impostors who rule Themis are powerful and backed by Lutheria. But I know their weaknesses, their secrets. I can guide you through the safe paths of Amazon politics.”
>
> “When you are crowned queen, I will stand at your side as counselor, if you permit it. Together we can correct past wrongs and restore the honor of the Amazons of Themis.”

Final Warning:

> “Remember, my queen, power does not come only from the royal blood in your veins. You must prove your worth through deeds, not just words. The Amazons must fight beside the peoples of Thylea if we are to defeat the Twin Titans.”

This sets a bond of guilt, redemption, and loyalty — an engaging dynamic for the Exiled path’s development.
