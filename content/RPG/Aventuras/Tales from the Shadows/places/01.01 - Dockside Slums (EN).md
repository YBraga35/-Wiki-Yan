# Dockside Slums — Mudskipper Wards

## Overview
The Mudskipper Wards sprawl across warped piers, leaning tenements, and makeshift plague hospitals hugging Yllmourne’s harbor. Salt spray mixes with acrid quicklime as wardens burn infected debris. This district is the campaign’s first contact point with the Mudskipper plague and home to many of the Agent’s earliest allies and clues.

Key beats anchor in [[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)|Act I]], especially the Night Messengers, Plague Hill vigils, and Kael’s trial fallout.

## Cinematic Beats
- **Dawn Watch (Act I introduction)**: Fog rolls off the harbor, revealing silhouettes of fishermen hauling nets while coughing into bloodstained cloths. Describe gulls circling listless, wary of the smell. Use when the Agent first steps into the wards.
- **Plague Surge (Act I midpoints)**: Alarm bells ring; wardens drag bodies onto lime-flecked carts. Mudskippers flop out of storm drains, slick with brine. Perfect for heightening urgency before medical or investigative scenes.
- **Night Messengers (Act I hauntings)**: Lantern light guttering in alleyways; spectral children leave wet footprints leading toward the abandoned lighthouse. Employ echoing whispers and reflections in puddles to foreshadow the supernatural mystery.
- **Underworld Assembly (Act II)**: Makeshift marketplaces reopen under One-Eyed Cy’s protection. Contrast the grim plague imagery with vibrant commerce returning—Cy’s influence stabilizes the docks when PCs earn his trust.
- **Liberation Dawn (Act III aftermath)**: Once the cure spreads, describe dawn sunlight catching on scrubbed docks, incense replacing lime, and families returning to market stalls—visual payoff for player victories.

## Mechanical & Atmosphere Notes
- Treat narrow alleys as difficult terrain during chases; make sleet-covered planks require DC 13 Acrobatics to avoid slipping.
- Plague exposure checks: DC 12 Con save when spending extended time among untreated wards; success reflects proper precautions (masks, lime washes).
- Rumor table suggestion: roll d6 for quick dockside leads (e.g., glimpsed ghost, missing shipment, Rotarist zealots preaching mercy-turned-vengeance).
