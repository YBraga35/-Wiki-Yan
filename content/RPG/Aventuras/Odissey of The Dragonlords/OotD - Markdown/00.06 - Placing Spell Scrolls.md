### **Chapter 1: Heroes of Prophecy**

* A spell scroll could be included on one of the corpses in the boar’s cave

### **Chapter 2: The Great Labours**

* Estoria

* The Mithral Forge \- A spell scroll could be found for spells such as *Heat Metal*  
* The Mossy Temple \- the tome found in Demetria’s quarters could include any number of vile spells, especially those dedicated to necromancy and dreams, as it was penned by Lutheria  
* The Necropolis at Telamok \- Damon may have any number of spell scrolls on his person, his own spellbook could be found somewhere in the Necropolis (likely in Area: X9), and spell scrolls or spellbooks could be made treasures to be found in some of the various tombs 

### **Chapter 3: Summoned by the King**

* The Academy at Mytros presents a great opportunity for finding any number of spellbooks, either those belonging to the academy’s staff, or in the academy libraries  
* The shop *Zorin’s Concoctions* could sell spell scrolls  
* Moxena could have spell scrolls of *Nondetection* in the Colossus of Pythor, used to protect herself from Scrying  
* The party could have the opportunity to obtain Chondrus’ spellbook, if they can steal it from him  
* The Collector’s spellbook can be obtained in a lockbox at the Great Menagerie

### **Chapter 4: Voyage of the Ultros**

* Spell scrolls could be included as gifts from the Academy during the Embarkation

### **Chapter 5: The Cerulean Gulf**

* The Fates could have a number of divination or necromancy based spell scrolls in their lair, or other scrolls or spellbooks taken from their victims  
* The Forbidden Library on the Island of Yonder presents a great opportunity for presenting large numbers of spellbooks and spell scrolls  
  * Chondrus’ spellbook could be obtained, should he be defeated in battle or should an accord be struck between he and the party  
  * Enigma’s spellbook can be found in area Y15

### **Chapter 6: The Forgotten Sea**

* Helios may have scrolls or spellbooks amongst his horde  
* Hexia may have scrolls or spellbooks amongst her horde

**Chapter 7: The Nether Sea**

* Spell scrolls for containing creatures, such as the spells *Imprisonment, Resilient Sphere, Hold Person,* and the *Dominate* spells could be found in the possession of the Ultroloth in command of the Prison Cube  
* Lutheria’s barge could contain many forbidden tomes of dark magic
