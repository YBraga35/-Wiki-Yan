# Opening Scene - Quick Guide (20 min Active Play)

> **🎯 Purpose**: Hook players IMMEDIATELY with action, then flashback to setup. This is the REVISED version incorporating DM Framework critiques.

**Original Version**: `00.10 - Opening Scene [EN].md` (30+ min of passive narration)
**This Version**: ACTIVE cold open → flashback → choices with consequences

---

## ⚡ STRUCTURE OVERVIEW

| Section | Time | Player Agency | Stakes |
|---------|------|---------------|--------|
| **Cold Open** | 3 min | HIGH (combat decisions) | Life/death of Javon & children |
| **Flashback Setup** | 5 min | MEDIUM (listen & decide) | Understanding context |
| **Kyrah's Mystery** | 7 min | HIGH (investigation, suspicion) | Trust vs. suspicion |
| **Sacrifice Choice** | 5 min | VERY HIGH (moral decision) | Divine favor, faction standing |

**Total Runtime**: 20 minutes of engaged play (vs. 30-40 min passive original)

## 🎬 COLD OPEN: BOAR ATTACK (3 MIN)

### Setup
Players arrive at the tavern MID-CRISIS. The corrupted boar is ALREADY attacking.

### Read-Aloud (Boxed Text)
> The trail to Estoria has been long, but you can see the town's lights ahead. As you crest the final hill, screams pierce the evening air. You see the Sour Vintage tavern—your destination—but something is WRONG.
>
> A massive boar, easily twice the size of a warhorse, is ramming the tavern's front door. Its tusks glow with sickly green light. Children are screaming inside. An old man—Javon, the innkeeper—stands in the doorway with a pitchfork, trying to hold the beast back.
>
> "RUN, CHILDREN! GET TO THE CELLAR!" he shouts, then turns to face the boar. The beast charges.
>
> **What do you do?**

### Mechanics

**Corrupted Boar (Reduced Stats for Fast Combat)**:
- AC 13, HP 30
- Attack: +5 to hit, 2d6+3 piercing damage
- Special: On critical hit, target makes DC 12 Con save or poisoned (1 minute)
- **Behavior**: Focuses on breaking into tavern (children inside)

**Javon** (Commoner):
- AC 10, HP 8
- Will die in 2 rounds if players don't intervene
- If saved: "Thank the gods you were here. That poet said you'd come..."

**Success**: Boar defeated, Javon lives, children safe
**Partial**: Javon dies protecting children (heroic sacrifice)
**Failure**: Children harmed (campaign starts with tragedy)

### DM Notes
- **Tension**: Children crying inside creates urgency
- **Choice**: Save Javon? Save children? Both?
- **Foreshadowing**: Boar's green glow = Sydon's corruption (mention this)
- **Epic Path Moment**: Let each PC shine (one saves Javon, one fights boar, one protects children)

## 🌅 FLASHBACK: THE TITAN SCENE (5 MIN)

### Transition
After combat, as players catch breath:
> As the corrupted boar dies, its green glow fades. In that moment, your vision BLURS. You see something—a memory? A vision? No... a TRUTH.

### Boxed Text (Muse Invocation - SHORTENED)
> *(Read in epic, bardic voice)*
>
> Hear now, mortals, of the world of Thylea—a land where gods walk, Titans rage, and heroes are forged in prophecy's fire.
>
> Five centuries ago, the Dragonlords came. They made peace with the Titans—Sydon of the Seas and Lutheria of Dreams. For 500 years, that peace held. But now, the **Oath of Peace** ends.
>
> The Titans awaken. And they hunger for revenge.

### Vision: Praxys Tower
> Your vision shifts. You see a tower of impossible height, carved from living stone in the middle of a storm-tossed sea. Two figures stand at its peak.
>
> **Sydon**, Lord of Storms—twelve feet of divine fury, eyes like hurricane winds, voice like thunder:
> "The oath ENDS, sister. No more sacrifices. No more appeasement. We will TAKE what is ours."
>
> **Lutheria**, Lady of Dreams—beautiful and terrible, crowned with black roses, voice like silk over knives:
> "And the mortals? The little heroes the Oracle gathers?"
>
> Sydon laughs. "Let them come. Let them TRY. They will learn what it means to defy gods."
>
> The vision ends. You're back at the tavern. The boar's corpse still smokes.

### Player Response
"What was that?" Let them react. Then Javon (or survivors) say: "The poet said you'd see visions. Come inside. She's been waiting for you."

## 🎭 KYRAH'S MYSTERY (7 MIN)

### Setup: NOT an Info-Dump

Kyrah does NOT immediately reveal everything. She's NERVOUS, EVASIVE, and clearly hiding something.

### Kyrah's Entrance
> Inside the tavern, you see a young woman in her early twenties, beautiful with dark hair and haunted eyes. She's been pacing. When she sees you, relief and FEAR cross her face.
>
> "You're here. Thank the gods—well, thank HER. The Oracle. She sent me to find you."
>
> She pauses, looking at the boar's corpse outside.
>
> "It's already started, hasn't it?"

### Roleplay Tips
- Kyrah is **evasive** about WHO she is ("Just a poet. The Oracle sent me.")
- She **avoids eye contact** when talking about herself
- She **knows things she shouldn't** (players' names, Epic Path details)
- If pressed: "I can't explain now. We need to get to the Oracle. Please."

### Investigation Phase (Let Players Choose)
Players can:
1. **Question Kyrah** (Insight DC 12: She's hiding something BIG)
2. **Examine the Boar** (Nature DC 13: Divine corruption, Sydon's mark)
3. **Talk to Locals** ("That poet's been asking about you for days. She knew you'd come.")
4. **Rest or Prepare** (Kyrah urges speed: "The Oracle is in danger.")

### DM Secrets (Don't Reveal Yet)
- Kyrah is secretly the Goddess of Music (Kyrah/Eurythmia)
- She's been watching them for weeks
- The boar attack was Sydon's warning ("Kill the heroes before they reach the Oracle")

## 🔥 THE SACRIFICE (5 MIN)

### Journey to Rock of Estor
> Kyrah leads you to the Rock of Estor—a massive stone outcropping where Estoria makes its sacrifices to the gods. A pyre is already built. A white bull stands tethered nearby, adorned with flowers.
>
> "The Oracle said you must make a choice before you meet her," Kyrah says. "A sacrifice. To which god do you offer this bull? Your choice will shape your destiny."

### The Choice (WITH MECHANICAL CONSEQUENCES)

Players choose which god receives the sacrifice:

#### Option 1: Sydon (Lord of Storms)
**Immediate Effect**:
- Sky darkens, thunder rolls
- Bull is consumed by lightning
- Players feel weight of storm god's gaze

**Mechanical Benefit**: **Storm's Chosen**
- 1/day: Reroll any save vs. lightning/thunder damage
- Order of Sydon faction: Starts FRIENDLY

**Consequence**:
- Five Faithful faction: Starts HOSTILE
- Kyrah disapproves (subtle frown)
- Chapter 3: Sydon remembers this ("You honored me once. Pity you turned against me.")

#### Option 2: Thylea (Mother Goddess)
**Immediate Effect**:
- Ground blooms with flowers
- Bull peacefully falls asleep, then fades into earth
- Gentle warmth surrounds party

**Mechanical Benefit**: **Thylea's Embrace**
- Advantage on Survival checks in natural environments
- Centaurs, Satyrs, Druids: Start FRIENDLY
- Can speak with animals 1/day

**Consequence**:
- Order of Sydon: Neutral (not hostile, but not friendly)
- Estoria hunters offer guidance in Chapter 2

#### Option 3: Mytros (Goddess of Dawn)
**Immediate Effect**:
- Dawn breaks early (even if night)
- Bull's body becomes pure light, ascends
- Divine radiance blesses party

**Mechanical Benefit**: **Dawn's Favor**
- City gates of Mytros/Estoria always open to party
- +2 to Persuasion checks with city officials
- 1/day: Cast *Bless* (self only)

**Consequence**:
- Mytros faction: Starts FRIENDLY
- King Acastus takes notice (political attention)

#### Option 4: Pythor (God of Battle)
**Immediate Effect**:
- Bull fights bravely against divine fire, then falls
- Bronze light marks each hero's weapon
- Smell of forge smoke

**Mechanical Benefit**: **Battle's Blessing**
- +1 to hit with weapon attacks (1st attack each combat)
- Pythor's personal attention (important for Demigod path)

**Consequence**:
- Estoria: Starts FRIENDLY (Pythor is their king)
- Volkan (Pythor's father) is pleased

#### Option 5: Vallus (Goddess of Wisdom)
**Immediate Effect**:
- Time seems to slow
- Bull sees visions, dies peacefully
- Each hero receives brief prophetic flash

**Mechanical Benefit**: **Vallus's Insight**
- 1/day: Gain advantage on one Intelligence/Wisdom check
- Scholars/mages treat party as respected peers
- Bonus: One free *Augury* casting (during campaign)

**Consequence**:
- Academy of Mytros: Starts FRIENDLY
- Vallus watches party with interest

#### Option 6: Volkan (God of the Forge)
**Immediate Effect**:
- Bull's body becomes molten metal, forms ingot
- Each hero feels forge-heat in their bones
- Weapons glow briefly

**Mechanical Benefit**: **Forgeborn**
- One weapon/armor gains +1 (non-magical enhancement)
- Dwarves: Start FRIENDLY
- Discount at forges (10% off)

**Consequence**:
- Mithral Mines (Chapter 2): Warmer welcome

### Kyrah's Poem (OPTIONAL - Only if players engage)
If players ask Kyrah to sing, she performs a brief song about heroes and prophecy. This is a PREVIEW of her divine nature.

> *(Sing or read musically)*
> "When the Oath breaks and the Titans wake,
> When the seas rise and the mountains quake,
> Heroes shall come with fire in their eyes,
> To stand where the Dragonlords fell and rise."

**Insight DC 15**: There's POWER in her voice. This isn't just a song—it's a blessing.

## ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. **DON'T read the entire Muse invocation** (it's 3+ minutes of passive listening)
   - Use shortened version above

2. **DON'T let Kyrah info-dump** ("I'm the goddess of music and here's everything...")
   - Keep her mysterious until Chapter 2 reveal

3. **DON'T make sacrifice meaningless** ("You sacrifice the bull. Okay, moving on.")
   - Use the mechanical benefits table
   - Describe divine response vividly
   - Track faction changes

4. **DON'T skip the cold open** (starting with "You arrive at a tavern...")
   - Start with ACTION: boar attack in progress

5. **DON'T force players into one god**
   - Present all 6 options
   - Let them debate (5 min max)

## 🎯 SUCCESS METRICS

**This opening is successful if**:
- [ ] Players made meaningful choices (saved Javon? which god?)
- [ ] Combat felt urgent (children in danger)
- [ ] Kyrah feels mysterious (not trusted yet, but intriguing)
- [ ] Players are asking questions ("What was that vision?" "Who is Kyrah REALLY?")
- [ ] Sacrifice felt important (mechanical benefit noted)

**Red flags to watch**:
- Players seem bored (you talked too much)
- Players don't care about Kyrah (you revealed too much)
- Sacrifice felt random (you didn't explain consequences)
