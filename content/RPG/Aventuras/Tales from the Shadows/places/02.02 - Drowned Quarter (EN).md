# Drowned Quarter — Sunken Ward

## Overview
The Drowned Quarter is a partially submerged neighborhood where broken rooftops jut from brackish canals. Once a thriving artisan district, it now houses spectral sailors, Choir agents, and desperate refugees. It becomes central during investigations into the *Abyssal Choir* and later as staging ground for naval operations.

Core references: [[02.00 - Act II - Political Intrigue (Lv5-7)#Adventure 11: The Choir in Shadows|Choir in Shadows]], [[03.00 - Act III - Rise of the Hero (Lv8-12)#Night of the Blockade|Naval arc]].

## Cinematic Beats
- **Whispering Flood (Act II)**: Moonlight glints on waterlogged streets; each ripple whispers names of drowned sailors. Use low humming rumble to dramatize ghostly attempts to warn PCs.
- **Shadow Regatta (Act II climax)**: Choir cultists row silent skiffs between half-collapsed buildings, torchlight smeared across slick walls. Incorporate sudden shadow tentacles whipping from water.
- **Refugee Encampment (Act III)**: After partial liberation, makeshift pontoons connect rooftops; Rotarist healers and Cy’s smugglers deliver supplies, casting hope amidst ruin.
- **Echoes of Justice (Act IV)**: Freed spirits form spectral jury rows, nodding approval when truth about the Choir surfaces in tribune scenes.

## Story Hooks by Act
- **Act II**:
  - Retrieve spectral testimonies to implicate Sefton (vital evidence for [[03.10 - Naval Briefing (EN)|legal route]]).
  - Investigate underwater vaults—combine Athletics (swim) and Arcana to decode summoning beacons.
- **Act III**:
  - Train blockade crews using canal skirmishes; success grants advantage on future naval maneuvers.
  - Coordinate evacuation of survivors to earn Thranengi goodwill.

## Mechanical & Atmosphere Notes
- Use partial cover from crumbled walls; moving between rooftops requires Acrobatics DC 13 or athletics with ropes.
- Water hazards: falling into canals forces Con saves vs. hypothermia; also risk of being grabbed by undead arms (use `Swarm of Quippers` reskinned).
- Skill challenge idea: “Spectral Convocation” where PCs must interpret ghost gestures (Insight/Religion) while resisting frightened condition.
