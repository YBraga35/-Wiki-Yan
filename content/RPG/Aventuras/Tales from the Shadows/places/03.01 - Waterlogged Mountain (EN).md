# Waterlogged Mountain — Sogmonnath’s Gate

## Overview
Waterlogged Mountain is a colossal sea stack riddled with mining tunnels, flooded caverns, and ancient sealing chambers imprisoning the entity Sogmonnath. Temporal fractures distort reality here, making it the primary setting for the campaign’s existential arc in Acts III and IV.

Reference [[03.00 - Act III - Rise of the Hero (Lv8-12)#Discovering Sogmonnath|Discovering Sogmonnath]], [[03.00 - Act III - Rise of the Hero (Lv8-12)#Adventure 12: The Time-Twister's Daughter|Time-Twister’s Daughter]], and [[04.00 - Act IV - Cosmology and Faith (Lv13-20)|Act IV]].

## Cinematic Beats
- **Temporal Tremor (Act III entrance)**: As PCs descend, describe mineshafts flickering between eras—a pickaxe swings by itself, leaving fresh grooves in ancient wood, while ghost miners argue about wages in reversed speech.
- **Frozen Storm (Act III core)**: In deeper caverns, raindrops hang motionless mid-air before reversing and rising back into cracks. Use to highlight the unnatural nature of Sogmonnath’s confinement.
- **Seal Chamber Revelation (Act III climax)**: A vast circular vault lined with sigil pillars; translucent barrier holds back an amorphous mass blending rock, water, and void. When PCs touch the dais, visions of the first sealing erupt, projecting around them.
- **Planar Convergence (Act IV)**: Time fractures widen—different versions of the PCs flicker in and out, offering warnings or temptations. As tribunal verdict nears, the mountain pulses in sync with Maal’s judgment, awaiting final resolution.

## Story Hooks by Act
- **Act III**:
  - Recover Temporal Anchor for Erythria (Time-Twister’s Daughter) to stabilize fractures.
  - Stop cult saboteurs planting explosives to breach the seal—tie to [[npc/01.02 - Sefton Bane (EN)|Sefton]]’s agents.
- **Act IV**:
  - Conduct the final sealing ritual involving Donsax, Maal’s mandate, and voluntary sacrifice (per [[04.00 - Act IV - Cosmology and Faith (Lv13-20)#Finale Structure|Act IV finale]]).
  - Alternate path: negotiate with Sogmonnath’s consciousness to reform the seal under new terms—roleplay-heavy encounter referencing [[00.20 - Maal Doctrine (EN)|Maal doctrine]].

## Mechanical & Atmosphere Notes
- Implement `Temporal Instability` mechanic: every hour, roll d6. On 1-2, time slows (initiative disadvantage); 3-4, time speeds (extra legendary actions for foes); 5-6, reality flickers (PC duplicates grant advantage).
- Gravity anomalies: certain tunnels shift vertical orientation—use Acrobatics/Strength checks to avoid falling into subterranean lakes.
- Environmental hazards: `Entropy Oozes` and `Temporal Echo` creatures (draw from custom stat blocks in Act III dossier).
