## Race Course
There are 8 stages throughout the city of Mytros. The racers go through one stage per round. This is somewhat abstract. All riders are considered to be in a clump and fairly close to each other throughout the race.
## Stage 1: Mt. Volkan
The race begins at the Colosseum of the Great Games, within the crater of Mt. Volkan. A rickety causeway track leads down the mountainside to the Temple District. Racers can make opposed strength checks to try to knock each other off their chariot. The loser of the opposed strength roll must make a DC 10 Dexterity save or fall off the track.
One of Acastus’ dragon-mounted centurions catches falling racers and returns them to their chariot. In this case, racers can gain only half of their minimum speed in this stage.
## Stage 2: Temple of the Five
Acolytes of the Temple of the Five line the streets of the Temple District, ready to cast spells to aid riders. If a rider succeeds on an additional DC 12 skill check, they can pull close enough to gain the benefits of a random cleric spell (d4):
: add a d4 to an ability check: add a d4 to a saving roll: +2 bonus to AC for the rest of the race: add a d4 to attack rolls and saving throws for the rest of the race
## Stage 3: Temple of Sydon
Racers round the Temple of Sydon, where water jets spray intermittent streams across the road that can knock them off their chariots. In addition to their normal skill checks, each racer must make a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw. On a success, the racer stays in their chariot. On a failure, the racer falls off and can gain only half of their minimum speed.

## Stage 5: Temple of Lutheria
Beautiful followers of Lutheria are standing on the rooftops above the streets, preparing to dump wine on racers passing by. Racers must make a DC 13 Dexterity save. On a failure, they are temporarily blinded at the end of the round and they can gain only half their normal speed.

## Stage 8: The Colossus of Pythor
The end of the race is at the Colossus of Pythor. To get there, racers must successfully jump their chariots off the cliff of the Noble District and land on the streets of the Harbor District below. This requires a DC 13 chariot skill check. On a failure, the racer and their chariot crash and do not win. On a success, the racer makes it to the finish line.
Of the finishers, total points determine placement of the winners!

## Post-Race Thoughts
Players had a ton of fun! Whole race took a little over 3 hours, with 1 round of driving checks and 1 round of combat per stage. There was a lot more hopping from chariot to chariot than I expected.
Before the race, generate dual rolls for all the NPCs, so you can switch between advantage/disadvantage checks quickly
Scale up chariot speeds to be slightly more accurate, better reflect distances for determining attack & spell ranges? I just set the range for attacks at 50 points, and melee attacks could be done against targets at the same point total as you.
Player suggestions - Guarantee blue shell to the last place racer - Lower the concentration check DC for casting spells with somatic component - Could be fun to have 2 people per chariot, one driver and one saboteur - Could be fun to add ramming mechanics, knocking drivers out of their chariots
