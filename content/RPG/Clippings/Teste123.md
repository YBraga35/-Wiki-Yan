Store Name and Description  
Name: Thornwhistle’s Aegis & Arms  
Description: Nestled at the corner of Ironhaven’s bustling market square, Thornwhistle’s Aegis & Arms is a stout stone shop front crowned by an ironbound oak door. Its windows glow with forge-fire light, and weapons of every sort—blades, bows, polearms—hang alongside finely crafted shields and helms. Patrons hear the rhythmic ring of hammer on anvil as armor is shaped to fit, and rumors of masterwork weapons draw adventurers from all across the region.  

Store Owner and Employees  
• Barundin Thornwhistle (Owner): A grizzled dwarven blacksmith and retired mercenary. Broad-shouldered and bearded, he lost an eye to a hill giant’s club and now pours that same relentless drive into perfecting each blade and cuirass.  
• Marisella “Miri” Quickshot (Apprentice): A lean tiefling artificer with bright amber eyes and nimble fingers. She experiments with minor enchantments under Barundin’s supervision, hoping one day to craft legendary arms.  
• Gunnar Ironhand (Journeyman Smith): A taciturn half-orc with an unmatched talent for tempering steel. His heavy strikes yield weapons known for both balance and bone-crushing weight. He rarely speaks, but winks at newcomers when they find just the right weapon.

Rumor (Overheard Conversation)  
Two cloaked figures huddle just outside the shop’s forge door late in the evening:  
“You have it?” whispers the first, finger tapping the pommel of a heavy sack.  
The second glances inside at the glow of the anvil. “Aye. Barundin doesn’t know it’s missing yet. That Flame Tongue blade—he sold it to the Crimson Concord last night.”  
The first’s eyes gleam. “Then we strike at dawn. With that sword, we can topple House Vianne and claim the northern passes for ourselves.”  
They melt into the shadows, leaving only the echo of scheming ambitions—and the faint scent of burning iron—on the street.
