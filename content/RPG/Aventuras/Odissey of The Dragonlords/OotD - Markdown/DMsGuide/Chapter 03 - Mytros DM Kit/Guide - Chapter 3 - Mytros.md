# Guide – Chapter 3 – Mytros

Objective: support the DM when the heroes return to Mytros after recovering the Ultros, kicking off the city arcs: Great Games, Debates, Academy, Colossus, Sewers, and palace intrigue — tying everything to the party’s Epic Paths.

## Mytros Overview
- Metropolis of ~50,000; two hills (Temple Hill and Civic‑Royal); Stygian Row in between; harbor with a bronze colossus; Mount Volkan behind the city.
- Political tension: Acastus’ popularity vs. waning faith in the Five; Order of Sydon rising; Vallus stabilizes the state.
- Heroes’ return: fame, Adoring Fans, invitations to Games, Debates, and audiences.

## Factions and Forces
- King Acastus: legitimacy via modern “Dragonlords” and a silver dragon; craves public victories.
- Queen Vallus: wisdom/stability; measures character with “clean” quests (Sewers, Nest of the Forgotten).
- Order of Sydon (Gaius): zealots; antagonize the Five; push for exposure during the Games.
- Chondrus (Lutheria): intrigue, vice, palace vault heist; influence in Stygian Row/Theater.
- The Academy: Machaon, Chalcodon, Kerodotos, Atreus; Dean Akimedes; Planemaster Orena (Orrery and navigation).
- Lady of Coins (Moxena): parallel power at the Colossus; offers Sacred Hospitality; key thread for the Exiled path.
- Delegations: Amazonians, Aresians, Scions of Sydon; diplomacy and rivalries on display.

## Locations and Hooks
- Great Palace: audiences; Acastus vs. Vallus; public announcements.
- Temple/Theater: civic‑religious events; targets for Chondrus.
- Colossus of Pythor: Lady of Coins’ domain; access via sewers.
- Academy (Library, Hall of Heroes, Orrery, Hall of Debate): competitions, prestige, books (languages/tools/lore).
- Stygian Row: fame, crime, cults.
- Sewers: decaying infrastructure, monsters, covert routes.

## Great Games (structure)
- Suggested daily loop: morning Preliminaries (no magic); afternoon Arena/Individual events; day’s end Grand Spectacle/Relay; nighttime social events (Celestial Celebrations).
- Quick rules: anti‑magic bracers in prelims; “Mystic Coaches” and crowd can grant advantage (DC 14) during spectacles.
- Medals/boons: drachma by placement; unique prizes (e.g., Battle of 100 Hands → temporary extra limbs/near‑haste)
- Social sequence: faction entrances; proposals (Horus/Amazonians; Acastus/Antikythera); confrontations (Scions); dramatic closing.
- References: `03.01 Great Games`, `03.02 Party Planning`, `03.08 Proximo's Guide`.

## Debates and the Academy
- Qualification via Persuasion/Performance/History/Insight; semifinals among PCs; finals with thorny topics (temples taxation, free education, moral obligation of magic).
- Science Fairs and prizes; reading for languages/tools/lore in the Library.
- References: `03.04 Great Debates`, `03.05 Academy`, `03.06 Books`.

## Essential Sidequests
- Sewers of Mytros: Ghoul King (ghast + ghouls — “graverobber’s curse”); elemental blockade (water weird + mephits); Nest of the Forgotten (Ismene Neurdagon). Outcomes: flow restored, alliance in the Nest, route to the Colossus; amulet vs. detection.
- Colossus of Pythor: Sacred Hospitality with Moxena; alliance/break; espionage network; access via sewers; political/moral impact.
- Palace and Street Intrigue: palace vault heist (Chondrus), cult of Helios (Zephyrus), chimera in the gulf, managing Adoring Fans.
- References: `03.07 Sewers`, `03.08 Proximo`, base chapter `Original/03 - Summoned by the King.md`.

## Suggested Timeline (2–3 weeks)
- Week 1: audience, street fame; sewers (elementals/ghouls); first visit to the Colossus; Celestials at night.
- Week 2: Games loop (prelims/arena/individuals/spectacle) + social events; Debates begin.
- Week 3: Games and Debates finals; Colossus resolution; tie up threads (Helios, chimera, vault); embark with the Ultros and new allies.

## Tying the Party to the City
- Social capital (honorary titles), residence/quarters (Academy/district), public commitments, dependents (Nest, students), allies (Orena navigator).

## Epic Paths Integration (summary)
- Demigod: public trials at the Games; Volkan/Forge bridge; “A Night to Remember” at chapter end to free Pythor.
- Exiled: Colossus/Moxena; Amazonians at the Games; Horus/contracts; reputation toward Themis.
- Vanished One: Versi as arbiter; Debates/Academy for social legitimacy; Telamok is outside the city.
- Cursed/Lutheria: Sewers/ghouls; Theater/Wine Festival; Chondrus.
- Gifted/Academic: Akimedes, Orena, Fairs and Library (languages/tools).
- Haunted: Scions’ cold interest (Goloron), Stygian Row and theater.
- Details: see `Epic Paths - Integration.md`.

## Anora/Pythor Notes (campaign canon)
- Anora: sacrifice completed in Ch. 2. In Ch. 3, romance (companions) and social appearances only.
- Freeing Pythor from the Throne: only at the end of Ch. 3 (external reference: “A Night to Remember”), enabling him to sail with the party on the Ultros.
