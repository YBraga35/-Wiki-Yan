# The Vanished One - Quick Reference for Table Use

> **🎯 Purpose**: Ancient Dragonlord returned from 500 years ago, lost memories, destined to rebuild the order.

---

| Element | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Divine Patron** | Versi the Oracle (possessive lover) |
| **Central Conflict** | Rebuild the Dragonlords / Navigate Versi's obsessive love / Face descendant Gaius |
| **End Goal** | Reclaim legendary armor & crown, find dragon egg, restore order, remember past |

| **Theme** | Memory, legacy, time's passage, toxic love, second chances |

## 🎭 KEY NPCs - QUICK ROLEPLAY

### Versi the Oracle (Obsessive Lover)
- **Relationship**: Oracle who fell in love at first sight; possessive, jealous, manipulative
- **Personality**: Regal, prophetic, loving but toxic, will kill romantic rivals
- **Voice**: Ethereal, musical, becomes cold/sharp when jealous
- **Wants from PC**: Return after saving world; eternal romance; NO OTHER LOVERS
- **First Meeting**: Oracle Temple (Session 1) - demands oath before revealing armor location
- **Critical Scene**: Jealous murder attempt if PC sleeps with someone else
- **Danger Level**: HIGH - she's immortal, powerful, and obsessed
- **Stat Location**: Oracle Versi stat block (Monster stats)

### Gaius (Descendant, Antagonist)
- **Relationship**: Your descendant (5-10 generations removed), Order of Sydon commander
- **Personality**: Zealot, conflicted, believes killing ancestor atones for Dragonlord sins
- **Voice**: Cultured, Estorian accent, hides inner turmoil
- **Appearance**: Vaguely resembles PC (adjust as needed)
- **Wants from PC**: Death (to atone); stolen armor won't attune to him
- **First Meeting**: Estoria (early) - doesn't reveal relationship yet
- **Critical Scene**: Yonder Island confrontation - "Only by killing my ancestor can I atone"
- **Stat Location**: Gaius stat block (Island of Yonder)

### Xander Huorath (Undead Dragonlord)
- **Relationship**: Fellow Dragonlord from 500 years ago, now undead lich
- **Personality**: Bitter, trapped, still loyal to old order
- **Voice**: Raspy, ancient, tinged with sorrow
- **Wants from PC**: Free him from undeath (maybe)
- **First Meeting**: Necropolis of Telamok (Session 8-10)
- **Critical Scene**: Hands over crown; reveals armor stolen by "blood relative"
- **Memory Trigger**: Recognizes PC immediately; emotional reunion

### Estor Arkelander (Ghost, Deceptive)
- **Relationship**: Fellow Dragonlord, now malevolent ghost, wants to possess PC
- **Personality**: Charming, deceptive, darkly charismatic, corrupt
- **Voice**: Smooth, persuasive, hides malice
- **Wants from PC**: Possession of body to return to life
- **First Meeting**: Ultros ghost ship (Session 12-15)
- **Critical Scene**: Offers "help recovering memories" (it's a trap for possession)
- **Player Advantage**: PC has advantage on Insight checks vs. Estor (gut feeling)

### Archdruid Sunarah (Ally)
- **Relationship**: Remembers PC from 500 years ago; inspired by PC to rebel
- **Personality**: Wise, grateful, protective of dragon eggs
- **First Meeting**: Chelone's Brow or Egg Nursery
- **Critical Scene**: "You inspired me to fight. I owe you everything." - hands over dragon egg freely

## 🎲 AT-TABLE CHECKLIST

### Before Session (First Epic Path Beat):
- [ ] Print Versi's oath (player must swear it)
- [ ] Decide: Does PC remember anything, or total amnesia?
- [ ] Prepare Crown of Dragonlords item card
- [ ] Design PC's tomb in Telamok (personality-based)
- [ ] Have Gaius's description ready (resembles PC)

### During Necropolis Arc (Sessions 8-10):
- [ ] Tomb description is emotional, personal
- [ ] Xander recognizes PC immediately ("You've returned!")
- [ ] Crown delivery is ceremonial
- [ ] Ask player: "What memory surfaces as you see this?"
- [ ] Allow 15 min for memory RP

### During Ultros Arc (Sessions 12-15):
- [ ] Estor is subtly wrong (PC senses it)
- [ ] Versi appears dramatically at departure (vision of dragon egg)
- [ ] If PC romanced someone: Versi confronts them NEXT DAY

### During Yonder Confrontation (Sessions 16-20):
- [ ] Gaius has legendary armor displayed
- [ ] "I am your blood. Only by ending you can I atone."
- [ ] Armor rejects Gaius but calls to PC
- [ ] Choice: Kill Gaius? Redeem him? Spare him?

### Romance Danger Zone:
- [ ] If PC consummates romance: Versi knows immediately (divination)
- [ ] Next morning: Versi appears, tries to kill rival
- [ ] Versi: "You swore an oath. You belong to ME."
- [ ] This can fracture party if PC doesn't handle carefully

## ⚠️ RED FLAGS - When Players Are Ignoring the Path

**Signs the player isn't engaging**:
- [ ] PC hasn't asked about their past in 5+ sessions
- [ ] PC avoided Necropolis entirely (skipped Great Labor)
- [ ] Player seems uninterested in memories/flashbacks
- [ ] PC is pursuing Versi romance without seeing red flags

**Intervention Options**:
1. **Soft Reminder**: NPC recognizes PC: "By the gods, you look just like the statue in Mytros!"
2. **Medium Push**: Versi sends dream: "Your armor calls to you from the island of traitors"
3. **Hard Redirect**: Gaius ambushes party, taunts PC about "bloodline's shame"
4. **Memory Trigger**: Spontaneous flashback during combat (Wisdom save or be stunned by memory)
5. **Out-of-game**: "Your character is from 500 years ago - want to explore that mystery, or change paths?"

## 🔄 FAILURE STATES & ALTERNATIVES

### Scenario: PC refuses Versi's oath
**Standard Path**: Oath sworn, Versi reveals tomb location
**Alternative A**: Versi withholds information; PC must find armor another way (rumors in Estoria)
**Alternative B**: Versi is heartbroken, becomes antagonist instead of ally
**Alternative C**: Kyrah intervenes: "Just swear it, deal with it later" (sets up tragic conflict)

### Scenario: PC kills Gaius
**Standard Path**: Confrontation, choice to spare or kill
**If Killed**: Armor transfers to PC, but haunted by guilt/Gaius's ghost
**Alternative**: Gaius is redeemed, becomes ally, helps PC recover memories

### Scenario: PC wants to avoid Versi's romance
**Standard Path**: Toxic romance subplot
**Alternative A**: PC rejects her early; she becomes bitter enemy
**Alternative B**: PC explains they need time; Versi gives space (delay, not removal)
**Alternative C**: Versi accepts PC is aromantic/not interested; shifts to mentor role (requires high Persuasion)

### Scenario: PC dies before Divine Gift used
**Standard Path**: Versi appears, kisses PC, resurrection
**Dramatic Moment**: "I will not lose you. Not again. Not ever."
**Cost**: Versi's obsession intensifies (disadvantage on future saves vs. her charms)

### Scenario: Players skip Necropolis Great Labor
**Standard Path**: PC finds crown and learns about Gaius
**Alternative A**: Xander's ghost appears in dreams, begs PC to visit tomb
**Alternative B**: Crown is elsewhere (Castle or Sleeping Dragon Isle)
**Alternative C**: Gaius has BOTH crown and armor on Yonder

## ⚡ DIVINE BLESSING & RESURRECTION

**How it Works**:
If the Vanished One is killed, Versi senses it immediately (divine bond from oath). Within 1 hour, she appears in a shimmer of moonlight, kneels beside PC, and speaks:

> "No. The Fates cannot be this cruel. I have waited 500 years for you. You will NOT leave me again."

Versi leans down and kisses PC. Divine light flows from her lips into PC's body. PC is resurrected as per _Raise Dead_ spell (no material components).

**Costs**:
- **To PC**: Disadvantage on saves vs. Versi's charm/enchantment magic (she has a stronger hold)
- **To Versi**: None (she would sacrifice anything for PC)

**Can Only Happen**: Once per campaign

**Dramatic Presentation**:
- Versi appears regardless of distance (teleportation)
- She is weeping (first time anyone has seen her cry)
- Other NPCs witness this and realize: "The Oracle is in love"
- Versi: "You belong to me. Promise you'll never leave me again."
- If PC refuses: She becomes cold, withdrawn, potentially hostile

**DM Note**: This deepens Versi's obsession. She may start interfering more in PC's life (divination, sending dreams, appearing uninvited).

## 📖 QUICK LORE REFERENCE

**What the PC Knows** (varies by DM):
- **Total Amnesia**: Nothing before waking up recently
- **Partial**: Fragments, dreams, sense of "I've been here before"
- **Aware**: Knows they're from 500 years ago but missing details

**What the PC Discovers**:
- **Session 1**: You're one of the legendary Dragonlords (Versi confirms)
- **Session 5**: Your tomb exists in Telamok (you died 500 years ago)
- **Session 8**: You had descendants (Gaius)
- **Session 10**: Gaius blames Dragonlords for allying with Titans
- **Session 12**: Estor was corrupted even back then
- **Session 15**: Many NPCs remember/honor you (Sunarah, memorials)
- **Session 20**: You played a role in the gygan massacre at Yonder
- **Endgame**: Why you were "vanished" vs. died like other Dragonlords

**What the PC Never Learns** (DM Only):
- Versi manipulated events to bring PC back (not natural reincarnation)
- Gaius has tried to kill himself multiple times (shame at ancestry)
- Estor murdered several Dragonlords before his own death
- If PC completes romance with Versi: she will trap them in Oracle Temple forever

## 🎬 DRAMATIC MOMENTS - SETUP

### Opening Prophecy & Oath (Session 1)
**Scene**: Oracle's private chamber
**Boxed Text**:
> "My heart, the Fates are truly cruel to separate us so quickly after you came to my rescue. Before I tell you of your great destiny, I ask that you swear an Oath, here and now, that once you have fulfilled your fate and prevented the Ruin of Thylea... you will return to me. And may our hearts sing together forever."

*[Let player respond]*

> "I have always had a 'weakness' for mortals... but I never knew what it was to love, not truly, until I looked upon you, [Name]. Now I will speak of your destiny: you are fated to see your order reborn—I see you, once again wearing your legendary armor and crown. You must retrieve them. Look for them in the Necropolis of Telamok. Old friends await you there."

**Player Hook**: "You're a legendary hero from 500 years ago. But you don't remember. Why?"

### Your Tomb (Session ~10)
**Scene**: Necropolis of Telamok, discovery of own tomb
**Setup**: Design tomb to reflect PC's personality
- Warrior: Mosaics of fighting gygans
- Healer: Tending to wounded soldiers
- Mage: Casting spell that saved city

**Xander's Greeting**: "By all the gods... you've returned. I... I never thought I'd see you again."

**Memory Trigger**: Ask player: "What memory surfaces? Who were you in this moment on the wall?"

**Crown Delivery**: Xander hands it over with shaking hands (undead can't cry, but voice breaks)

### Gaius Confrontation (Session ~20)
**Scene**: Gaius's chamber, Island of Yonder
**Setup**: Armor displayed prominently; Gaius is praying before it
**Gaius**: "I knew you'd come. The armor called to you, didn't it? It rejects me. Because I'm not worthy. But neither are you."

**Revelation**: "I am your blood, Dragonlord. Your descendant. And I will atone for your sins by ending your line."

**Stakes**: Armor, family legacy, Gaius's life, party's trust
**Possible Endings**:
- Kill Gaius: Armor transfers; haunted by descendant's ghost
- Redeem Gaius: Difficult persuasion; he joins party
- Spare but don't redeem: Gaius flees, swears revenge
- Gaius kills himself: Armor transfers; PC feels profound guilt

### Versi's Jealousy (If Triggered)
**Scene**: Morning after PC consummates other romance
**Setup**: Versi appears in doorway, face cold, eyes blazing
**Versi**: "You swore an oath. You belong to ME. This mortal has poisoned you against me."

**Combat**: Versi attacks romantic rival (not PC initially)
**Choice**:
- Protect rival: Versi sees this as ultimate betrayal
- Let Versi win: Rival dies; PC has failed to protect them
- Persuade Versi: DC 20+ Persuasion; she's nearly mad with jealousy

**Aftermath**: Party now knows Oracle is dangerous
