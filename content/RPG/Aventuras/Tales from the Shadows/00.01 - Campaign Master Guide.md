# Campaign Master Guide: Tales from the Shadows in Yllmourne

## Campaign Premise

**The Elevator Pitch**: An Agent of Maal arrives in the plague-ridden port city of Yllmourne to investigate the murder of Dagmara, a fellow servant of the god of life, death, and law. What begins as a simple homicide investigation unravels into a sprawling conspiracy involving planar corruption, naval political intrigue, religious persecution, and extraplanar forces manipulating Yllmourne's fate. Over 20 levels of play, the heroes will expose hidden cults, navigate factional warfare, defend sacred knowledge, and ultimately confront the architects of Yllmourne's suffering.

This campaign adapts the *Tales from the Shadows* adventure anthology (levels 1-8) to the richly detailed Yllmourne setting, extending the narrative through original content to level 20. Players experience a journey from street-level investigation to high-stakes planar confrontation, all while grappling with questions of justice, mercy, and the price of truth.

## Four-Act Structure Overview

The campaign follows Yllmourne's established four-act structure, with *Tales from the Shadows* adventures providing the investigative backbone for Acts I-II:

### **Act I: Horror on the Docks (Levels 1-4)**
*"The Investigation Begins"*

The Agent of Maal arrives to find Yllmourne gripped by plague (the Mudskipper), mysterious ship sinkings, and Dagmara's brutal murder. Adventures 1-6 from *Tales from the Shadows* are adapted to introduce the city's factions, establish the plague's supernatural origins, and uncover the first threads of conspiracy. The act climaxes with a public trial that prevents a scapegoat's lynching, earning the party both allies and enemies.

**Key Themes**: Body horror, urban decay, establishing trust, uncovering surface truths

### **Act II: Political Intrigue (Levels 5-7)**
*"Powers Behind the Throne"*

With Dagmara's murder solved but deeper questions remaining, the heroes navigate the treacherous politics between Cy (legitimate Governor) and Sefton (criminal Admiral). Adventures 7-9 are adapted to reveal religious conspiracies, protect threatened knowledge repositories, and expose corrupt officials. The act culminates in discovering that extraplanar forces—agents of Naran and Thellos—orchestrate Yllmourne's suffering.

**Key Themes**: Moral complexity, faction manipulation, information warfare, religious persecution

### **Act III: Naval Campaigns (Levels 8-12)**
*"Taking the Fight to the Sea"*

Having exposed the conspiracy on land, the heroes must dismantle the planar agents' maritime operations. Original adventures focus on ship-to-ship combat, infiltrating smuggling rings, liberating cursed islands, and building alliances with naval factions. The remaining *Tales from the Shadows* adventures (10-14) are woven into this nautical narrative, adapted to feature corrupted vessels, haunted lighthouses, and underwater threats.

**Key Themes**: Exploration, tactical warfare, building power, confronting corruption at its source

### **Act IV: Planar Reckoning (Levels 13-20)**
*"The Final Voyage"*

The conspiracy's architects—powerful servants of Naran (illusions/deception) and Thellos (madness/entropy)—must be confronted in their planar strongholds. Original high-level content features planar travel, epic set-piece battles, and moral reckonings about Yllmourne's future. Will the heroes restore Maal's order, forge new alliances between gods, or reshape the city entirely?

**Key Themes**: Cosmic stakes, testing convictions, legacy, apotheosis

## Core Themes

### **Investigation Over Combat**
While combat is present, this campaign prioritizes investigation, evidence-gathering, and connecting dots. Many encounters can be resolved through clever questioning, infiltration, or negotiation. The best sessions end with "aha!" moments, not just body counts.

### **Justice vs. Expediency**
As an Agent of Maal, the protagonist must balance perfect justice (following every law, uncovering every truth) with practical mercy (knowing when exposure causes more harm than good). Key NPCs will test these convictions.

### **Religious Conflict Without Zealotry**
Yllmourne features competing faiths (Maal, Onotas, Tempus, plus shadow deities). The campaign explores how religion can inspire, corrupt, or manipulate without portraying any faith as wholly evil. Prelate Faylene is misguided, not monstrous; the Onotanian Royal Guard are zealous, not irredeemable.

### **Maritime Intrigue**
The sea is both highway and haunted realm. Ship sinkings, smuggling routes, cursed islands, and naval battles create a distinct flavor. Players should feel Yllmourne's identity as a port city constantly.

### **The Price of Truth**
Some investigations are better left incomplete. Some truths destroy more than they illuminate. The campaign asks: when should secrets stay buried?

## Adaptation Philosophy

### Why *Tales from the Shadows* Fits Yllmourne

The original *Tales from the Shadows* adventures are set in the Shadow Realm—a dimension of mist, corruption, and liminal horror. Yllmourne's setting provides natural parallels:

1. **Planar Corruption**: The Shadow Realm's influence becomes manifestations of Naran (illusions hiding truth) and Thellos (madness corrupting order)
2. **Isolated Communities**: Shadow Realm's scattered settlements map perfectly to Yllmourne's barrier islands, cursed docks, and quarantined districts
3. **Body Horror**: The Mudskipper plague provides visceral equivalents to Shadow Realm transformations
4. **Investigation Focus**: Both settings reward careful observation, NPC interaction, and connecting environmental clues

### Adaptation Principles

**1. Preserve Core Mysteries**: Each *TftS* adventure's central mystery (who/what/why) remains intact, but locations, NPCs, and stakes are reskinned to Yllmourne.

**2. Integrate Faction Clocks**: Every adapted adventure affects at least one of Yllmourne's five faction clocks:
- **The Plague** (supernatural disease progression)
- **Cy's Legitimacy** (Governor's public support)
- **Sefton's Dominance** (Admiral's criminal empire)
- **Library Preservation** (knowledge vs. zealotry)
- **Planar Incursion** (extraplanar agents' success)

**3. Contextual NPC Decisions**: Don't automatically replace *TftS* NPCs with Yllmourne equivalents. For each adventure, assess:
- **Keep Original**: If the NPC has unique traits that enhance the adventure
- **Substitute**: If an Yllmourne NPC serves the same narrative function better
- **Fuse**: Combine characteristics (e.g., a *TftS* villain gains an Yllmourne NPC's backstory)
- **Add**: Introduce new NPCs that bridge both sources

**4. Maintain Investigative Structure**: Even when combat-heavy, each adventure includes interview opportunities, evidence gathering, and moments to theorize.

## Milestone Leveling

This campaign uses milestone leveling tied to narrative beats, not XP. Players level when they achieve significant story progress:

| Level | Milestone |
|-------|-----------|
| **2** | Complete first investigation (Dagmara's murder scene processed) |
| **3** | Survive first major supernatural threat (plague-touched encounter) |
| **4** | Prevent the lynching/complete Act I trial |
| **5** | Expose first major conspirator (mid-Act II) |
| **6** | Successfully infiltrate enemy faction headquarters |
| **7** | Complete Act II climax (Faylene's hex exposed, scapegoat absolved) |
| **8** | Win first major naval engagement |
| **9** | Liberate cursed island or lift major planar effect |
| **10** | Forge critical alliance or neutralize major enemy faction |
| **11** | Discover planar agents' true identities |
| **12** | Complete Act III climax (maritime operations dismantled) |
| **13** | First successful planar journey |
| **14** | Defeat first planar lieutenant (servants of Naran/Thellos) |
| **15** | Secure major planar artifact or knowledge |
| **16** | Survive major planar catastrophe or betrayal |
| **17** | Complete major ritual or achieve epic boon |
| **18** | Defeat second planar lieutenant |
| **19** | Reach final planar stronghold |
| **20** | Final confrontation with conspiracy's architects |

**DM Note**: Feel free to adjust pacing. Some groups may take 30 sessions to reach level 5; others may do it in 10. The key is that leveling feels *earned* and *celebrated*.

## DM Preparation Tips

### What to Read First

**Essential Reading Order**:
1. This Master Guide (you're reading it now)
2. Yllmourne Campaign Guide - Acts I-II (understand factions, clocks, setting)
3. Yllmourne Complete Content - NPCs (familiarize with Cy, Sefton, Faylene, Navula, Callista)
4. Opening Hooks document (prepare Session 0/1)
5. Act I guide (prepare first 3 adventures)

**Reference Materials**:
- Keep Yllmourne NPC roster handy during sessions for quick stat blocks
- Bookmark faction clock rules—update them after every session
- Have ship combat rules ready by level 6

### Session Prep Structure

**For Each Session**:
1. **Review Last Session** (10 min): What clues did players discover? What NPCs did they befriend/antagonize?
2. **Predict Player Actions** (10 min): Where will they likely go? Who will they investigate?
3. **Prepare 3 Scenes** (20 min):
   - One scene you're confident they'll trigger
   - One backup scene if they go a different direction
   - One "wildcard" scene for unexpected choices
4. **NPC Voices** (5 min): Practice 2-3 key NPC voices/mannerisms
5. **Clue Budget** (5 min): Ensure players can discover at least 2-3 useful clues this session

**Between Adventures**:
- Update all five faction clocks
- Note which NPCs now trust/distrust the party
- Plant seeds for next adventure (rumours, job offers, mysterious messages)

### Running Investigations

**The Three Clue Rule**: For every critical revelation, provide at least three independent ways to discover it. If players must learn "Faylene is hexing Callista," ensure they can discover this through:
1. Interviewing Callista's servants
2. Examining alchemical purchases at the docks
3. Divination magic or tracking Faylene's movements

**Fail Forward**: If players fail an Investigation check, don't withhold information—give it to them anyway, but with a complication (they're spotted, they take longer, they owe someone a favour).

**Red Herrings**: Use sparingly. Players will naturally generate false theories; you don't need to add more confusion. Dead ends should still provide *some* useful information.

## Tone Guidelines

### Balancing Horror Without Overwhelming

This campaign features body horror (Mudskipper plague), cosmic dread (planar corruption), and moral darkness (political murders). However, it should not become a grimdark slog:

**Do**:
- Describe plague symptoms vividly but briefly; focus on characters' emotional responses
- Use shadows and suggestion more than explicit gore
- Provide moments of levity (dockside tavern banter, absurd bureaucratic obstacles)
- Celebrate victories—when players solve a mystery, let them feel smart and heroic

**Don't**:
- Dwell on suffering for shock value
- Make every NPC untrustworthy or cruel
- Remove player agency through "inevitable" doom
- Forget that Maal represents order and hope, not just death

### Session Zero Expectations

**Discuss with Players**:
- **Themes**: This campaign involves disease, murder investigations, and moral ambiguity. Check for sensitive topics.
- **Tone**: "Gothic mystery with political intrigue," not "survival horror" or "high fantasy epic"
- **Pacing**: Expect sessions with minimal combat but intense roleplay
- **Character Ties**: How does each PC connect to Maal's Agent or Yllmourne?

### Adjusting on the Fly

**If Players Want More Combat**: Emphasize nautical battles, add corrupted creature encounters, use more aggressive enemy factions.

**If Players Want More Investigation**: Add interview scenes, expand library research sequences, introduce forensic magic systems.

**If Players Want More Politics**: Increase Cy vs. Sefton tensions, add council meeting scenes, give players opportunities to swing public opinion.
