# The Madness of Herkus: Decoding Fragmented Prophecies

This guide structures and deepens the goblin‑druid Herkus’s insanity, turning a confused NPC into a source of mystery and forewarnings for the players.

## The Nature of Herkus’s Madness

Herkus’s madness is not random. It is the direct result of a magical trauma inflicted by Demetria. As a druid, his bond with the natural world was his tongue and his strength. Now that bond is broken and twisted. He still sees the truth of the world, but can only express it through the symbolism of the forest.

### Principles of Herkus’s Speech

1. **Natural Metaphors**  
   For Herkus, everything is an element of the woods.  
   - **People:** They are trees, stones, or animals. A strong person is a “tall tree” or “hard rock”; a treacherous person is a “snake in the grass.” The heroes may be “young trees” or “iron stones.”  
   - **Emotions & Intentions:** Natural phenomena. Anger is “fire” or “storm.” Sadness is “still water” or a “sick root.” Love or passion can be a “beautiful flower” or “sweet fruit.”  
   - **Threats:** Predators, plagues, or disasters. A monster is a “devil goat.” An evil plan is a “rotting root that feeds on the land.”

2. **Broken Connections**  
   His mind jumps between thoughts. He might start by talking about a “tree” (a person) and immediately link a “root” (the past or the soul of that person) to a “stone” (an obstacle or a place). Logic exists, but it is a logic of sensations and symbols, not of syntax.

3. **Literal & Simple Truths**  
   Occasionally, during a moment of clarity or panic, a simple literal truth slips out amid the confusion. Sentences such as “Beware of the hostess” are perfect examples.

4. **Focus on the Immediate Threat**  
   Most of his madness circles around what broke him: **Demetria**, her corruption, her henchmen (crazed satyrs), and her plan for the **Ancient Forest**.

## Creating New Speech for Herkus

Use this structure to improvise cryptic statements for Herkus, based on what the players are doing or where they’re heading.

**Formula**: `[Central Warning Element] + [Natural Metaphor] + [Primitive Feeling / Action]`

### Examples

- **Warning of a floor trap**  
  - *Element*: Trap.  
  - *Metaphor*: Biting ground, hungry root.  
  - *Speech*: “The ground bites! Hungry root waits with an open mouth. Do not step!”  

- **Indicating a secret door**  
  - *Element*: Hidden passage.  
  - *Metaphor*: Wind from nowhere, breathing stone.  
  - *Speech*: “Stone breathes. Cold wind… comes from nowhere. Ear to the wall.”  

- **Describing a NPC’s sorrow**  
  - *Element*: Grief.  
  - *Metaphor*: Willow that weeps sap, still water in heart.  
  - *Speech*: “Beautiful tree weeps. Much rain inside. Stagnant water… does not flow. Root suffocates.”  

- **Foretelling a villain’s arrival**  
  - *Element*: Villain approaching.  
  - *Metaphor*: Wolf with storm scent, shadow that devours light.  
  - *Speech*: “Shadow comes. Storm scent and teeth. Sun hides. Run!”

# Herkus's Riddles: The Temple, The Lady, and The Lost

### **About the Mossy Temple (Sobre o Templo Musgoso)**

- **Riddle 1:** "Stone weeps green tears... The old bones drink... and forget the sun."
    
    - **GM's Insight (PT-BR):** Refere-se ao musgo e à umidade constante do templo. As "lágrimas verdes" são o musgo, e os "ossos velhos" (as paredes do templo) estão tão saturados com a escuridão e a água corrompida que "esqueceram" a luz e a vida.
        
- **Riddle 2:** "The water sleeps. Black dreams... Do not wake the water. It is thirsty."
    
    - **GM's Insight (PT-BR):** Um aviso direto sobre a água estagnada e corrompida pela influência de Lutheria. "Sonhos negros" aponta para a origem do pesadelo, e a sede da água é uma metáfora para sua capacidade de corromper e consumir quem a toca ou bebe.
        
- **Riddle 3:** "Roots have fingers now. They hold screams... Listen to the walls."
    
    - **GM's Insight (PT-BR):** A natureza no templo foi torcida e agora é predatória. As raízes não são mais passivas; elas "agarram" e absorvem a dor das vítimas. O som está preso na própria estrutura do lugar.
        

### **About Demetria (Sobre Demetria)**

- **Riddle 1:** "The mother willow... her branches broke. Now she poisons the stream."
    
    - **GM's Insight (PT-BR):** Demetria era uma figura protetora e carinhosa ("mãe salgueiro"), mas a perda de sua família ("seus galhos quebraram") a transformou. Agora, em vez de nutrir, ela ativamente espalha sua dor e corrupção ("envenena o riacho").
        
- **Riddle 2:** "She wears a cloak of lost butterfly wings... So pretty... so quiet."
    
    - **GM's Insight (PT-BR):** Uma imagem da beleza trágica de Demetria. As "asas de borboleta" são as almas ou a inocência de suas vítimas, que ela coleciona em sua loucura. A beleza da imagem contrasta com o horror do que ela faz.
        
- **Riddle 3:** "Her heart is a hollow log. Only angry echoes live there now."
    
    - **GM's Insight (PT-BR):** Descreve o vazio emocional de Demetria. Seu coração não tem mais vida própria; é apenas um receptáculo para a raiva e a dor do passado, que "ecoam" em suas ações presentes.
        

### **About the Children inside (Sobre as Crianças lá Dentro)**

- **Riddle 1:** "Little saplings... planted in nightmare soil. They will grow thorns... or not grow at all."
    
    - **GM's Insight (PT-BR):** As crianças são "pequenas mudas" inocentes. O "solo de pesadelo" é o templo e a influência de Demetria. O enigma aponta para os dois destinos terríveis que as aguardam: serem transformadas em monstros ("crescer espinhos") ou simplesmente morrerem.
        
- **Riddle 2:** "Their laughter was caught in a spider's web... Sticky with tears."
    
    - **GM's Insight (PT-BR):** A alegria e a inocência das crianças ("risadas") foram roubadas e presas. A teia de aranha representa o plano inescapável de Demetria, e as "lágrimas" são a tristeza que permeia todo o processo.
        
- **Riddle 3:** "They are seeds. She wants to make them sing the root's song. A bad song. A mad song."
    
    - **GM's Insight (PT-BR):** As crianças são "sementes" com potencial. Demetria quer forçá-las a se conectar com a natureza corrompida ("a canção da raiz") que ela agora serve. A "canção ruim e louca" é a transformação em maenads, uma perversão da vida.
