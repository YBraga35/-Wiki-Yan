# The Lost One

You hail from a distant land, where your people worshipped foreign gods and spoke languages unknown to the people of Thylea. You sailed in search of the fabled treasures of the Dragonlords, but your ship was destroyed and most of your crew were killed in a calamitous storm. Washed ashore and brought to the city of Mytros, you discovered that your arrival was prophesied by the Oracle. 

You may not believe in any of the Oracle’s prophecies, but one thing is for certain: you are somehow stranded in Thylea - your fate is now here. If the Oracle believes that you are one of the chosen few, then you must do what you can to help.

**For players who love:** *The Chronicles of Narnia*, *The Wizard of Oz*  
**Restrictions:** Must be an explorer from distant lands.  
**Adventure Hook:** The Oracle believes you have been sent from the outside world by the gods in order to fulfill the prophecy.

## Heroic Tasks

- Find your surviving crew members.  
- Find a way to get back home.  
- Find the fabled treasures of the Dragonlords, rumoured to include tens of thousands of gold pieces, countless gemstones, and one of the following legendary items, which has caught your attention:

### Legendary Items

- *Ascendant Dragon Vessel*  
- *Ascendant Dragon’s Wrath Weapon* (any weapon)  
- *Ascendant Dragon-Touched Focus*  
- *Ascendant Scaled Ornament*  

## Divine Blessing

### Blessing of Success

When you would fail any one d20 check, you can instead choose to succeed (turning a miss into a hit or a failure into a success). Once used, this blessing cannot be used again until after you complete a long rest.
