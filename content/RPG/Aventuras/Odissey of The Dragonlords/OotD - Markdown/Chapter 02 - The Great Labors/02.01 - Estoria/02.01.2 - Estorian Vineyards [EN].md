# The Estorian Vineyards

The road from Estoria gives way to rolling, sun-kissed hills, where the Estorian Vineyards stretch out like a great, green sea under the Thylean sky. The afternoon light casts long, dancing shadows from meticulously kept rows of grapevines, their leaves like a thousand emerald shields glinting in the golden haze. The air is thick with the sweet, heady perfume of sun-ripened grapes and rich, dark earth.

There's a profound tranquility here—the gentle rustle of leaves in the warm breeze, the distant buzz of fat, lazy bees drifting from flower to flower. But beneath the serene beauty lies a palpable tension, a quiet worry that seems to cling to the very vines themselves. The symmetry and order of the vineyard feel like a desperate attempt to hold back a looming chaos.

# Meeting Thericles

Amidst the endless rows of green and purple, a lone figure moves with a practiced, almost mournful rhythm. This is **Thericles**. He’s a man in the prime of his life, with the sturdy build of a former scout and hands that are both strong and gentle as he tends to his vines. The sharp _snip-snip_ of his pruning shears is the only sound that cuts through the afternoon's calm.

As you draw closer, you see the deep lines of worry etched into his brow and the dark circles of sleepless nights beneath his kind eyes. His shoulders are slumped, not from the weight of his work, but from a far heavier burden. When he finally notices you, he straightens up, forcing a weary smile that doesn't quite reach his eyes. His gaze is searching, desperate, scanning you as if you might be the answer to an unspoken prayer.

"Travelers," he calls out, his voice steady but laced with a raw edge of panic. "Forgive me... I don't often receive visitors. Can I help you?" But it's clear from his expression that he is the one who desperately needs help.

## Quest: The Missing Daughter

Thericles will quickly abandon any pretense and pour out his story. His hope is a flickering candle in the wind, and he sees the spark of heroes in the party.

- **The Plea:** He takes a deep, shaky breath, his eyes pleading.
    
    > "Thank the gods you're here. Please, you look capable... you must help me. It's my daughter... my **Corinna**. She's been gone for a week. Vanished without a trace. I've searched day and night, walked the edge of the **Oldwood** until my feet bled, called her name until my throat was raw... but there's nothing. It's like the forest just... swallowed her whole. I'm begging you. Find my daughter. I fear the worst has happened."

- **The Truth (DM's Knowledge):** Corinna was lured into the Mossy Temple by a tribe of goatlings who serve the corrupted dryad, Demetria. She is being held captive, destined for a terrible ritual.

- **The Reward:** Thericles shakes his head when asked about payment, gesturing to the vineyard around him.

    > "I don't have much coin... this land is my life's work. But I have this."
    > 
    > He retrieves a long, slender object wrapped in oilcloth. As he unwraps it, a perfectly balanced javelin is revealed, its tip crackling with faint, contained energy.
    > 
    > "It's a relic from my time in the king's service. A javelin of lightning. It is all I have of value... but my daughter... she is all that matters. Bring her home safe, and it is yours. No questions asked."
