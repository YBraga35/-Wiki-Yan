# Bonita Sandberg

## Snapshot
- **Role**: Wharfmaster-healer, Dagmara’s confidante, and key witness anchoring the plague investigation.
- **Motivation**: Honor Dagmara’s memory by stopping the Mudskipper curse and safeguarding dockside families.
- **Leverage**: Oath to heal, fierce loyalty to the docks, desperate need for trustworthy allies.
- **Stat Baseline**: `Priest` (MM p.348) specialized for battlefield medicine (bonus action stabilize, maximized healing spells).
- **Deep Dive**: Reference [[npc/apend.01 - Integrated NPCs Complete Guide#Bonita Sandberg (Dagmara's Colleague)|Integrated NPCs]] for expanded hooks.

## Arc by Act
- **Act I – Source of Truth**: Delivers Dagmara’s journal and medical findings; central to [[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)#Scene 2: Plague Hill Investigation|plague investigation]].
- **Act II – Targeted Witness**: Survives assassination attempt orchestrated by Choir agents; protecting her cements public trust in the Agent ([[02.00 - Act II - Political Intrigue (Lv5-7)#Adventure 7: Attempts on Bonita|Act II]]).
- **Act III – Cure Architect**: Cooperates with Faylene and Navula to brew the cleansing ritual ([[03.00 - Act III - Rise of the Hero (Lv8-12)#The Ritual of Cleansing|Act III]]).
- **Act IV – Tribunal Advocate**: Testifies about plague origins, bolstering Maal’s case against Sefton and corrupt officials ([[04.00 - Act IV - Cosmology and Faith (Lv13-20)|Act IV]]).

## Relationship Web
- **Dagmara Stoneheart**: Best friend; stores guilt and grief that propel her action ([[npc/01.07 - Dagmara Stoneheart (EN)|Dagmara dossier]]).
- **One-Eyed Cy**: Provides informal protection and supplies to her clinic ([[npc/01.01 - One-Eyed Cy (EN)|Cy dossier]]).
- **Octant Faylene**: Initially trusts; later helps heal her post-hex ([[npc/01.03 - Octant Faylene (EN)|Faylene dossier]]).
- **Deputy Marshal Harlan Cross**: Works closely to keep witnesses safe ([[npc/01.08 - Deputy Marshal Harlan (EN)|Harlan dossier]]).
- **Agent of Maal**: Relies on the Agent to ensure Dagmara’s death sparks true justice.

## Scene Hooks
- **Harbor Clinic Crisis**: Use as emotional beat when patients overflow her infirmary; offers side quest to secure supplies.
- **Journal Debrief**: After each clue milestone, Bonita compares the Agent’s findings with Dagmara’s notes—grant advantage on related checks.
- **Public Support**: Her endorsement sways public opinion during [[01.20 - Tribunal Script (EN)|Kael’s trial]], granting bonus success if protected.

## Mechanical Notes
- Treat her medical facility as providing a single free use of `lesser restoration` per long rest (if intact).
- Assign `Trust` rating (0-3). Each broken promise reduces it, limiting access to her intel or clinics.
- Rewards: access to Dagmara’s coded notes, healing poultices, reliable dockside rumor network.
