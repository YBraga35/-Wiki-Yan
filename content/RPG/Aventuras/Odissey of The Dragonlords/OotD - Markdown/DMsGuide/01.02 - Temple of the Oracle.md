# The Temple of the Oracle

> _“Never trust the words of that witch, Versi. She once claimed she saw no great deeds in my future, yet look now—am I not the King of Mytros?”_
> 
> — King Acastus, on the Oracle of Thylea

The Temple of the Oracle is the legendary home of Versi, an ancient naiad with the gift of prophecy. Recently, she issued a dire warning: the Doom of Thylea is near, and only the heroes from her vision can save the land from destruction.

After slaying the corrupted boar, you are celebrated as the "Chosen of the Oracle." Now, guided by Kyrah, you have arrived at the Temple to claim your reward and discover your destiny.

> **Kyrah:** _"Though others have failed, you slew the boar that plagued the Midlands. Now, you have come to the Temple to learn more of your destiny from the water nymph, Versi. She is the daughter of Sydon, but has always been an ally to mortals. However, I have not heard from her in two weeks, and I fear something terrible has happened."_

## The Current Situation

The situation is more complex than it appears. Sydon, Heleka, and the Order of Sydon have intertwined, yet distinct, objectives.

- **Sydon:** Furious with his daughter's latest prophecy, Sydon sees it as an act of insubordination. He sent **Heleka** with simple orders: capture Versi, without harming her, and bring her to his tower in Praxys. He also sent the **Order** to "purify" the temple and ensure its attendants serve him from now on.
    
- **Heleka:** The sea witch is sadistic and is taking advantage of the situation. Though forbidden from harming Versi, she delights in torturing and killing the temple acolytes. Her plan is to break Versi's spirit with the suffering of her followers, causing the Oracle herself to beg to be taken away, just to stop the carnage.
    
- **Braeca and the Order:** Leading the soldiers, Braeca was ordered by Gaius to "investigate troubles at the temple" and kill the heroes on sight. However, he is bewildered by Heleka's cruelty and has lost control. Now, he holds the surviving attendants hostage "for their own good," hoping the heroes die in the witch's traps so he can emerge as the "savior" who restored order in Sydon's name.
    
- **Versi:** The Oracle foresaw that Sydon would send someone to capture her, but she also saw that she would not be harmed and that the heroes would arrive in time to receive the prophecy. Trusting her vision, she remained in the temple. The outcome, however, was more terrible than she expected.
    

## Key Locations

### O1. The Whispering Steam Gorge

The Temple is located in a fissure in the earth. As you descend, the air grows heavy and humid, and the scent of pine gives way to the strong, metallic odor of sulfur. The canyon walls weep mineral-rich water, creating rust-colored streaks on the stone. A sinister, perpetual mist clings to the ground, muffling sound and reducing visibility to a mere 60 feet. The only sounds are the hissing of steam vents and the echoing drip of water.

The path is treacherous, and geysers of boiling steam erupt from the ground. A DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) or Intelligence (Investigation) check reveals a pattern. A worn stone near the entrance has a nearly illegible inscription:

> _"The Titan's breath is a jealous fury. Heed the short warning, respect the long sigh, and flee its final roar."_

To pass safely, the group must time their movements, crossing sections of the path immediately after the violent eruption ("final roar"). Failure requires a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw to take half of 2d6 fire damage.

**Heleka's Servants:** Four **steam mephits** hide in the mist, summoned by Heleka to block the way. They will try to lure the heroes into the geysers.

### O2. The Temple Portico

Through the mist, the temple's facade emerges. Carved into the canyon wall, its scale is humbling. Above the entrance, a magnificent frieze depicts Sydon as an wrathful judge, with three eyes that seem to follow you.

On guard are four soldiers from the Order of Sydon. Their leader, **Braeca**, steps forward.

> **Braeca:** _"Halt, strangers. This place is being purified. The Oracle has fallen silent, a sign that the Gods have abandoned her to Sydon's fury. The 'Chosen' of a silent Oracle are chosen for nothing but ruin. Turn back, before you further offend the Lord of Storms."_

If you mention Kyrah, he will scoff: _"Kyrah, the poet? A teller of pretty lies. The age of mortals is ending."_ The four **soldiers** are nervous and can be intimidated, but will fight if cornered.

### O3. The Temple Chamber

The air inside is thick with the smell of incense, dried blood, and fear. The once-serene chamber has been desecrated. A massive bronze statue of Sydon dominates the space. At its feet, a reflection pool is murky. The bodies of several acolytes lie in pools of blood.

**Braeca** and four more soldiers have cornered the surviving attendants. An elderly priest, **Proteus**, his face a mask of grief, shouts:

> **Proteus:** _"You fools! This is blasphemy! You defile this place while a monster from the deep tortures our lady! Heleka—that is the witch's name! She delights in every scream, for she knows it torments the Oracle's spirit! Sydon's justice? This is Lutheria's cruelty! Can't you see you are mere pawns?"_

**The Pool's Riddle:** The rectangular pool at the statue's base has a faint magical glow. Near it, an inscription in an ancient dialect reads:

> _"I have no voice, but I show the truth. I have no eyes, but I reflect the world. I drink the light, but am forever dark. To see beyond, you must first offer a sacrifice not of blood, but of brilliance."_

**Solution:** Casting a spell that produces light (like _light_) on the pool will cause it to glow, showing a brief, horrific vision of Heleka torturing an acolyte in the grotto below.

**Treasure:** Braeca and his men carry 25 gp and 100 sp. Braeca also possesses a bronze symbol of Sydon.

### O4. Locked Stairway Door

The door leading to the cellar is locked. The key is in the Attendants' Chambers (O5). It can be broken down with a DC 20 Strength (Athletics) check.

### O5. The Attendants' Chambers

These seven sparse rooms are the attendants' dormitories. Heleka has enchanted four **flying swords** and left them hanging on the wall next to the key to the door (O4). The swords attack as soon as the key is touched.

**Treasure:** Proteus's room contains a small chest with six **potions of healing**.

### O6. Mimic Door

The door at the bottom of the stairs is a **Mimic** loyal to Heleka. It attacks anyone who touches it.

### O7. The Oracle's Grotto

Liquid blue light shimmers on the damp walls of this grotto. The room is filled with mist from hot springs. A **Rug of Smothering** is positioned to attack anyone who tries to advance.

### O8. The Oracle's Chamber

At the chamber's entrance, **Versi** is suspended over an underground river by tendrils of animated water. Further in, the hideous **Heleka** cackles as she ritually tortures an acolyte.

> **Heleka:** _"Hush now, little priest. Your screams are music to my ears and a dagger in your lady's heart. She sees every tear, feels every cut. Oh, she is powerful, the Oracle! Powerful enough to watch you die and do nothing!"_

Versi is being held by a **Water Weird**, which is entirely focused on imprisoning her and will not retaliate. To free her, the heroes must solve a riddle that Versi herself projects into their minds:

> _"She is a creature of jealous depths... born of spite and shadow. To unbind the river's grasp, you must answer its heart... What does the Stormlord fear most, yet create with every breaking wave?"_

**Answer:** Silence or Calm. Speaking the answer aloud causes the Water Weird to lose its cohesion, freeing Versi.

**Treasure:** Heleka carries a **pearl** (100 gp) and a note from Sydon. The Oracle's belongings include five **black pearls** (500 gp each), three **emeralds** (1000 gp each), and six fine **brooches** (750 gp each). Taking them invites a curse.

## The Prophecy and Departure

Once rescued, Versi, her voice weak but resonant, delivers her prophecy:

> _"I see... the fiery heart of a mountain, where the weapons of the gods sleep... I see... a horn, carved from the mother of dragons, that calls not to beasts, but to the echoes of fate... I see... a tomb where the first lord lies, yet his greatest treasures are not buried with him... Three labors... three threads in a tapestry of doom. Only when you have held the fire, drunk the echo, and claimed the lost legacy... will the path to the forgotten sea be known to you. But beware... for the path is sailed by a ghost, captained by a sin that can never be atoned for."_

### The Great Labors

Kyrah and Proteus help interpret the vision:

1. **Claim the Weapons of the Dragonlords:** In Telamok, the necropolis in the Mithral Mountains. The guardian, Damon, requires a special coin.
    
2. **Light the Fires of the Mithral Forge:** The ancient dwarven forge needs to be rekindled. Kyrah suggests seeking out the dwarves near Estoria.
    
3. **Drink from the Horn of Balmytria:** A legendary dragon horn that grants visions, kept in a reliquary in Estoria.
    

### Leaving the Temple

As you prepare to leave, Proteus approaches.

> **Proteus:** _"Heroes, you have saved our lady. About your task in Telamok... there is something you need."_

With trembling hands, he withdraws a single coin of polished metal, gleaming like bronze. On one side, a dragon; on the other, a mounted warrior.

> **Proteus:** _"A polished Dragonlord's coin. The only key that will open the doors of Telamok. Present it to Damon."_

After Proteus steps away, Kyrah offers one last piece of advice.

> **Kyrah:** _"Forget what Proteus said about not seeking fame. Fame is the path to immortality! But one hero can outshine the others. Therefore, I suggest you swear an **Oath of Fellowship**. A sacred vow that will bind you together. When one of you gains fame, you will all share in it."_

If the heroes agree, they must toast with wine and recite the oath:

> _"I am [name], and I swear by my life and my honor that I will keep the oath of fellowship with the warriors who stand beside me."_
