Notes for DMs (delete this before showing players): - The times for reading books were based on the players having ~100 days from getting to Mytros to the BoM, you may want to rebalance the times to fit your campaigns. - The Bestiaries give players in-character access to a handful of statblocks when they finish reading the book. - The lorebooks give PCs access to myths in the appendixes, the rest of them can be found in the Forbidden Library. - I use mostly Biochip’s crafting rules with my own homebrew supplement. I also categorized the recipes in Biochip’s supplement by toolset.
