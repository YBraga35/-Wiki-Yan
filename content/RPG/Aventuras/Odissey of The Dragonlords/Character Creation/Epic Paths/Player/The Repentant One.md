**The Repentant One**  
You were once a powerful and proud sphinx. Your mind was like a maze, and you were known for your keen intellect, cutting wit, and your… mercurial nature. You hoarded great treasure, grave secrets, and possessed immense magical prowess, rarely sharing either of the former with those who dared encroach on your sacred isolation, but readily using the latter to dispose of the interlopers, cursing or slaying them with great impunity as you pleased. 

For your actions, you were banished from the homeland of the sphinxes, and settled in mainland Thylea, where more and more mortals came before you, and all met grisly fates. Your actions and fate amused the Lady of Dreams, who hoped she had found a kindred spirit. She decided to test you as one of her practical jokes. In her mortal guise she vexed you beyond belief, but when you went to curse her, she revealed herself and in her fury at your lack of ‘a sense of humour’ she stripped away your powers and rendered you a mere mortal \- forced to walk the earth and bear witness to your crimes. 

Now, awash with terrible humility and regret, you must attempt to rebuild your strength and undo what your pride has wrought.

**For players who love:** Sissyphys, Circe, Prince Zuko  
**Restrictions:** You must be from Thylea  
**Adventure** **Hook:** The Oracle claims to know how you can find absolution

**Heroic** **Tasks**

* Obtain forgiveness  
* Find out if you can return to your true form  
* Reclaim your most prized possessions. Pick one lesser and one greater magic item from this list:

  * *(lesser) Helm of Comprehending Languages*  
  * *(lesser) Ring of Puzzler’s Wit*  
  * *(lesser) Ring of Mind Shielding*


  * *(greater) Crystal Ball of Mind Reading*  
  * *(greater) Rod of Absorption*  
  * *(greater) Rod of Alertness*

**Divine Blessing**   
*Blessing of the Sphinx:* You gain immunity to psychic damage, the charmed and frightened conditions, as well as any divination spell that you do not wish to be affected by.
