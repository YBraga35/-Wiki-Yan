## The Oath of Peace in Thylea

The Oath of Peace is the cornerstone of Thylea's recent history and the main catalyst for the campaign's events. Understanding its nuances, both what is public knowledge and the secrets it hides, is fundamental to running the adventure.

### Public Version (Common Knowledge in Thylea)

This is the version of the story that most mortals, and even many leaders, know.

- **What It Is:** A 500-year truce forged at the end of the First War between the Titans (Sydon and Lutheria) and the Five Gods, who emerged to protect mortals. The oath was an act to prevent the total destruction of Thylea, which was threatened by the fury of the Titans.
    
- **Known Terms:**
    
    - **500-Year Truce:** Sydon and Lutheria swore not to take revenge on the mortals of Thylea for a period of 500 years.
        
    - **Sacrifices and Honor:** In return, mortals must offer daily sacrifices to the Titans, and their temples must be preserved and honored.
        
    - **End of Open War:** The Oath prevented direct war between the pantheons. However, devotees of the gods can fight each other without breaking the Oath.
        
- **The End of the Oath:** The campaign begins months before the end of these 500 years, and the Oracle has prophesied the "Doom of Thylea" as a direct consequence.
    

### The Secret Truth (Expanded Community Lore)

This is the more complex and tragic version, which serves as the true engine of the plot. Most of this information is hidden and can be a major revelation for the players.

- **The Origin of the Five Gods:** The fundamental secret is that the Five Gods are actually the surviving dragons of the Dragonlords who ascended to divinity. Balmytria, the great silver dragon, became Mytros; her bronze dragon relatives became Volkan, Pythor, Vallus, and Kyrah.
    
- **The Game of the Gods:** The ascension was not a miracle, but a theft. The First War was a bloody stalemate. Balmytria challenged Sydon to a game, betting her life against a portion of the Titans' divine power. Lutheria secretly intervened to ensure Sydon's victory, but Balmytria's suicide over the game board, combined with Lutheria's divine energy, allowed the dragons to "steal" the divine sparks and become the Five Gods.
    
- **The Real and Secret Terms of the Oath:**
    
    - **Secrecy Clause:** The Oath of Peace is a magical contract that enforces secrecy about the true origin of the Five Gods. They are magically prevented from revealing that they are dragons. This explains their silence about the past.
        
    - **Return of Power:** At the end of the 500 years, the "Dragon-Gods" must return the divine portfolios they stole. This is the real reason for their desperation and why they sponsor heroes: they need to find successors before their power and their very existence as gods come to an end.
        

### Where to Find the Oath of Peace?

Players can find the text or information about it in a few key locations:

- **Temple of the Five (Mytros):** The original scroll was given by Damon to Vallus at the Temple of the Five. It is likely that the original text (or a faithful copy) is kept in the temple's vaults or in Vallus's personal library.
    
- **Tower of Praxys (Sydon's Domain):** A copy of the oath is kept in Sydon's treasure vault. Obtaining this copy would be a great feat and would provide the players with physical proof of the terms, including the secret clause.
    
- **Forbidden Library in "Somewhere":** Ancient texts, such as those by Chondrus, which allude to the truth behind the Oath, can be found here, although the location of this library is a mystery.
    
- **The Isle of Yonder:** In Chondrus's notes, found in the library of the Order of Sydon's fortress, players can discover the priest's suspicions about the true nature of the gods and the oath.
    

### Who Knows the Truth and Can Speak About It?

Different NPCs have varying levels of knowledge. The magical secrecy clause prevents the Dragon-Gods from simply telling the truth.

- **Complete Knowledge:**
    
    - **The Five Gods (now Dragons):** They know everything, but cannot speak openly about their draconic origin due to the oath. Kyrah, in desperation, may try to give clues or break the oath indirectly.
        
    - **Sydon and Lutheria:** As signatories, they know all the terms. They might use the secret as a form of blackmail or mockery.
        
    - **Damon:** He was not a dragon and is not bound by the same clause. He delivered the oath and knows the truth, but his reclusive and enigmatic nature makes him a difficult source of information. He is, perhaps, the most direct source if the players can gain his trust.
        
    - **Versi, the Oracle:** As the mediator of the Oath, she knows the truth but speaks in prophecies and riddles. She can guide the players to the right clues.
        
    - **The Furies:** As the cosmic enforcers of all oaths, they know the exact terms, but they are dangerous and impartial entities who only care about the letter of the law, not mortals.
        
- **Partial Knowledge or Suspicions:**
    
    - **Chondrus:** The high priest of Lutheria in Mytros strongly suspects the truth. His writings reveal his doubts about Balmytria's death and the rise of the Five. He is a biased but valuable source of information.
        
    - **Aesop:** As the consort of one of the original Dragonlords, he may have fragments of the true story or suspicions based on what his husband, Adonis, told him.
        

### The Text of the Oath (Expanded Version)

Below is the text of the Oath of Peace, as presented in the _DM's Guide to Odyssey of the Dragonlords_, which you can use as a handout for your players when they finally find it.

> [!title] OATH OF PEACE
> 
> BE IT KNOWN TO ALL, AND TO ALL WHOM IT MAY CONCERN, OR TO WHOM IT MAY IN ANY WISE APPERTAIN, THAT FOR MANY YEARS PAST, DISCORD AND WARFARE HAVE BEEN WAGED UPON THYLEA BETWEEN THE GODS AND THE TITANS.
> 
> LET THIS OATH OF PEACE BE SWORN BETWEEN THE ESTEEMED EMPYREAN TITANS AND THE NEW GODS.
> 
> BY THIS OATH, NO WAR SHALL BE WAGED BETWEEN THE GODS AND THE TITANS. THE GODS AND TITANS SHALL NOT CAUSE VIOLENCE OR WARFARE UPON THE OTHER PARTY, OR THOSE SWORN TO SERVE THEM, OR THOSE WHOM THEY PROTECT, EXCEPT TO DEFEND THEMSELVES OR THEIR FOLLOWERS. THE TITANS SHALL NOT TAKE REVENGE FOR THE WAR UPON THE MORTALS OF THYLEA.
> 
> BY THIS OATH, THE TITANS SHALL RETAIN THEIR RIGHT OF APPEASEMENT. THE GODS SHALL MAINTAIN AND HONOR TEMPLES TO THE ESTEEMED TITANS AND SHALL PERMIT SACRIFICES TO SYDON AND LUTHERIA AS HAS ALWAYS BEEN.
> 
> BY THIS OATH, THE FOLLOWING SHALL BE HELD IN SECRECY, AND THIS SECRECY SHALL BIND ALL WHO KNOW OF IT. THE DRACONIC ORIGIN OF THE NEW GODS AND THE SOURCE OF THEIR DIVINE PORTFOLIO SHALL REMAIN SECRET. AT THE OATH'S END, THE GODS SHALL RETURN THEIR STOLEN PORTFOLIOS TO SYDON AND LUTHERIA.
> 
> LET THIS OATH BE HELD IN BLOOD AND HONOR FOR 500 YEARS AND A DAY.
> 
> SO LET IT BE, AND SO IT IS SWORN UNDER THE GUIDANCE OF THE FURIES.
> 
> AS WITNESSED BENEATH THE GOLDEN HEART, WITH THE ORACLE AND A MORTAL SOUL.
> 
> SYDON, LORD OF STORMS
> 
> VALLUS, GODDESS OF WISDOM
> 
> PYTHOR, GOD OF BATTLE
> 
> LUTHERIA, LADY OF DREAMS
> 
> KYRAH, GODDESS OF MUSIC
> 
> VOLKAN, GOD OF FORGES
