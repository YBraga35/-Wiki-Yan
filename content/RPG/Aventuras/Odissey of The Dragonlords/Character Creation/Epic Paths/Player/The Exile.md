# The Exile

Your mother was the **Amazon Queen of Themis, Thesilea VI**. Thirteen years ago, a group of three sisters **murdered your mother and her main supporters** in a single night of bloodshed. They now **rule over the main island** and the **smaller tribes** scattered throughout the Cerulean Gulf. You **escaped in the chaos**, and have been **preparing for your return ever since**.

News from Themis is dire. Bands of **maenads roam the island**, accompanied by their **goat-headed children** and **leagues of cultists**, keeping the populace in check. **Bloody bacchanals** are held every night. The **Great Mothers** of the past are no longer revered — now all is done in the name of **Lutheria**.

You must **overthrow the usurpers**, **prove that your worthiness extends beyond your blood**, and **reclaim your crown**.

---

## Inspiration

**For players who love:** The Amazons, Wonder Woman  
**Restrictions:** You must be a **woman from Thylea**  
**Adventure Hook:** The Oracle claims to have seen how you can reclaim your throne.

---

## Heroic Tasks

- **Defeat the usurpers**
- **Prove yourself worthy to rule through a Trial of Strength**
- **Reclaim a relic of the Amazon Queens**, the **Oathkeeper Chakram**  
  _(a +2 Oathbow that functions as per a chakram)_

---

## Divine Blessing

### Blessing of the Great Mothers

When you are reduced to **0 hit points**, you instead **drop to 1 hit point** and gain a number of **temporary hit points equal to twice your level**.  
Once utilized, you **can’t use this feature again until you finish a long rest**.

---

## Other Species

The **Sisterhood of Equals** is made up of all the races from across Thylea.  
Your **species has little bearing** on your story, other than determining **what race your mother was**.
