## ⚔️ **Scene 1 – The Scream of Sableford**

**Setting:**  
A small riverside village surrounded by dense woodlands. Sableford is quiet, rural, and unprepared for danger.

**Narrative Introduction:**

> The evening air in Sableford is thick with smoke and firelight. Villagers chat quietly by the central firepit, when a sudden scream echoes from the northern path.  
> Shadows dance in the treeline. Dark shapes — goblins or worse — descend on the fields, brandishing blades and torches. Panic spreads like wildfire.

**Enter Ispin and Cudgel:**

> From the western road, the thunder of hooves breaks through the chaos. Two riders charge in: one, a burly man with a round green shield — **Ispin Greenshield**. The other, a grizzled dwarf in steel, roaring with laughter — **Cudgel Ironsmile**.
> 
> “Back, you cowards!” Cudgel yells, cleaving a goblin in one stroke.  
> “You alright, folks?” Ispin shouts, dismounting between villagers and monsters. “No one ruins supper in Sableford!”

**Player Role:**

- The players, as young commoners, **assist in defending the village**: guiding children to safety, helping a wounded elder, reinforcing barricades, or confronting a stray goblin with improvised tools.
    
- Ispin fights off most of the invaders with Cudgel, but always glances back to ensure the villagers — especially the players — are safe.
    

**Closure:**

> As the last goblin flees, Ispin wipes blood from his blade and laughs, breath heavy.  
> “Now… tell me someone saved the ale.”  
> That night, beside a bonfire, the players share a meal and laughter with their saviors. Ispin raises his mug:  
> “To friends not made on the battlefield, but saved there.”

## 🛡️ **Scene 2 – The Chapel at Vallen**

**Setting:**  
A mountaintop chapel and outpost, cold and windswept. Villagers here are hiding from something.

**Narrative Introduction:**

> The sky above Vallen is bruised with clouds. A monstrous howl echoes from the pines — a predator that shouldn’t exist.  
> Villagers whisper of a great black wolf, with burning eyes and jagged teeth. Its attacks have left homes broken and livestock slaughtered.

**Arrival of Ispin and Beklin:**

> A small horse climbs the trail, its rider far too large for it. Ispin dismounts, his green shield shining faintly.  
> Beside him, a woman in polished silver armor surveys the chapel with sharp eyes — **Beklin Uth Viharin**.  
> “This is no ordinary beast,” she says.  
> Ispin nods. “Then it deserves an extraordinary welcome.”

**Player Role:**

- The players, still villagers or youths, take part in **healing the injured**, **tending frightened children**, or **helping scout the area**.
    
- Ispin sits among them at night, tells a soft story by firelight, and **reassures them not with power, but kindness**.
    

**Key Dialogue:**

> “I’ve faced worse things than monsters,” Ispin says to a trembling child. “The worst is fear… and we beat it, every time we hold someone’s hand.”

**Closure:**

- The wolf is defeated (offscreen, or through narration), and the players witness the quiet aftermath: Ispin sewing a gash on Beklin’s arm with shaking hands.
    

> “You stitched this better when we were drunk in Kalaman,” she mutters.  
> “I was more focused,” he grins.

## 🍻 **Scene 3 – The Last Song in Bramblebrook**

**Setting:**  
A cozy tavern in Bramblebrook, "The Oakshadow Inn." A warm fire crackles, food is plentiful, and the danger is behind them.

**Narrative Introduction:**

> Days have passed since the last threat. Tonight, Bramblebrook celebrates. There is laughter, music, and clinking mugs. Ispin has insisted: _every table has a drink and every heart has a story._

**Scene Description:**

- The players are now treated not as children, but equals — Ispin and his companions **toast to their bravery**, whether they fought or just stood firm.
    
- Beklin and Cudgel (if present) sing off-key. Ispin tells another impossible story of a sea monster, complete with wild gestures and spilled ale.
    

**Player Role:**

- Each player gets a moment: a toast, a story, a private conversation with Ispin. He may give them a small keepsake, a phrase, or just a laugh they’ll remember for years.
    

**Ispin’s Speech:**

> He stands on a table, mug held high:  
> “I don’t fight for glory. I fight for _this_ — music, smiles, good drink, and people I’d give my life for.  
> You don’t need a sword to be brave. Sometimes, bravery is just staying kind in a world that forgets how.”

**Closure:**

> The night fades into starlight. Ispin sits beside the players one last time, quieter now:  
> “One day, you’ll all do great things. Just promise me you’ll tell your own stories. Loudly.”  
> He grins. “And if I’m not around… drink one for me, alright?”
