# Naval Briefing — Breaking Sefton’s Blockade

English-language prep kit for Act III’s “Night of the Blockade” sequence. Coordinates with material in [[03.00 - Act III - Rise of the Hero (Lv8-12)#Original Content: Night of the Blockade|Act III Guide]].

## Situation Overview
- **Date**: Three days after exposing the *Abyssal Choir*’s conspiracy (end of Act II).
- **Enemy Forces**: Commander Dreshka’s flotilla (10 ships, half undermanned), sea serpent asset, covert Rotarist saboteurs.
- **Allied Options**: Merchant coalition, Thranengi raiders, Onotanie legal counsel.
- **Objective**: Lift blockade by force, law, or alliance—choose approach that fits current faction clocks and player preference.

## Strategic Approaches

### 1. Naval Defiance (Combat-Focused)
- **Prep Checklist**:
  - Secure 4–6 hulls (letters of marque already granted in Act II).
  - Spend 500 gp on crew drills for +2 to naval checks.
  - Harvest alchemical fire from Rotarist caches (DC 15 Investigation during downtime).
- **Skill Challenge Flow**:
  - Navigation: DC 18 Survival (Tor Underleaf can assist).
  - Tactics: DC 16 Intelligence (Battlefield planning).
  - Morale: DC 14 Charisma (speech from ship’s quarterdeck).
- **Set-Piece Events**:
  - **Sea Serpent Strike**: Use CR 8 plesiosaurus variant; emerges on round 3.
  - **Storm Front**: Apply disadvantage to ranged attacks unless Weather Sense check succeeds.
  - **Boarding Action**: Opportunity for heroics; limit to 4 enemies on deck to respect duo action economy.

### 2. Legal Audacity (Social/Investigation)
- **Evidence Packet**:
  - Ghost testimonies from [[02.00 - Act II - Political Intrigue (Lv5-7)#Adventure 11: The Choir in Shadows|Adventure 11]].
  - Financial ledgers proving Sefton profiteering.
  - Witnesses: Minqena, Thranengi envoy, surviving merchant captain.
- **Courtroom Sequence** (international tribunal or Onotanie court):
  - Opening Statement (DC 18 Persuasion).
  - Evidence Presentation (DC 16 Investigation).
  - Cross-Examination (Opposed Insight vs. Deception).
  - Legal Precedent (DC 17 History; referencing Maal’s maritime law codex).
  - Final Verdict swing via DC 20 Persuasion or use of divine omen (Channel Divinity expenditure for advantage).
- **Outcome Paths**:
  - Full victory: Blockade declared illegal, reparations ordered.
  - Partial: Blockade reduced, PC gains political leverage for later acts.
  - Failure: Case dismissed, but public opinion shifts (treat as narrative success for Act IV setup).

### 3. Thranengi Alliance (Hybrid Guerrilla)
- **Negotiation Targets**: War matriarch Shasa Wavebreaker, shaman Rinnatide, scout flotilla captains.
- **Prerequisites**: Aid rendered during Act I/II reduces Persuasion DC from 20 to 16.
- **Operations Timeline**:
  - **Week 1**: Sabotage Sefton supply sloops (Stealth DC 17 at night).
  - **Week 2**: Coordinated hit-and-run raids on blockade perimeter (use skill challenge or quick combats).
  - **Final Strike**: Assault Commander Dreshka’s flagship; adapt stat block (CR 9 veteran with command spells).
- **Moral Check**: Ensure Thranengi tactics stay within Maal’s law (Insight DC 18). Failure may trigger future political fallout.

## Player-Facing Handout Summary
Provide the player with a distilled choice matrix:

| Approach | Strengths | Risks | Key NPCs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Naval Defiance | Clear martial victory, boosts citizen morale | Casualties, resource cost | Captain Thorne (loyalist), Tor Underleaf (tactician) |
| Legal Audacity | Preserves lives, gains political capital | Requires airtight evidence, risk of corruption | Magistrate Selis (judge), Bonita (testimony) |
| Thranengi Alliance | Builds cross-cultural alliance, flexible engagements | Must manage reprisals, cultural misunderstandings | Shasa Wavebreaker, Navula (mediator) |

Encourage the player to select path during downtime narrative; adjust prep scenes accordingly.

## Mechanical Integrations
- Reference duo encounter limits in [[00.12 - Encounter Matrix (EN)|Encounter Matrix]] when staging shipboard fights.
- Apply faction clock impacts from [[apend.01 - Special Mechanics Guide#Faction Clocks|Special Mechanics]] after resolution (e.g., Sefton clock -2 on victory).
- If Maal intervenes (miracle, omen), log divine favor in [[00.20 - Maal Doctrine (EN)|Maal Doctrine Notes]].

## Debrief Checklist
After the scenario, record:
1. Chosen approach and immediate outcome.
2. Ships lost/saved or legal precedent established.
3. Changes to Cy’s legitimacy, Sefton’s dominance, Planar Incursion clocks.
4. Narrative hooks seeded for Act IV reprisals.
