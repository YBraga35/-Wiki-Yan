# Summoned by the King

Use these read‑alouds and prompts to play the opening scenes as the party enters Mytros under Sydon’s storm and faces the court.

## Arrival Under the Storm

> Traveling the Heartlands, the sky ahead darkens unnaturally. Black clouds coil over your destination like a bruise upon the heavens. The wind howls with the scent of ozone and salt. Rain begins — first a fine mist, then a torrent that turns the road to mud.

## The City Gates

> Nearing the gates, the storm’s fury is undeniable. The marble‑and‑bronze jewel is scourged by wind and lightning; banners hang in tatters. The streets are empty; prayers echo behind shuttered doors.
>
> Centurions usher you inside. A thunderclap shakes the city. In the storm’s eye, a monstrous, three‑eyed visage forms — Sydon. His gaze sweeps the city, then fixes upon you. With a snarl that vibrates your bones, he hurls a bolt of lightning.

Action: roll initiative; the lowest is targeted by a lightning bolt (save applies). Continue once the shock subsides.

## The Great Palace

> The Great Palace crowns a broad stone stair. Within, cool marble colonnades lead to the Royal Hall. Servants move in a hush; bronze‑armored guards stand rigid.

## The Throne Hall

> Rain hammers the high roof. At the far end, upon a golden throne, sits a powerfully built man in fine regalia — King Acastus. On the steps below, four figures await: a timelessly beautiful queen; an imposing commander in cerulean; a sinister tiefling in black; and a finely dressed young woman.

Prompts for Play
- Acastus’ opening: “My advisors cannot agree. Therefore, you — the Oracle’s Chosen — must decide our course. Mytros’ fate rests in your hands.”
- Vallus’ presence is steady and piercing; she studies each hero with measured calm.
- Commander Gaius’ disdain is thinly veiled — he mutters “Usurpers” and watches for weakness.
- Chondrus smiles as if the storm were a private joke.

From here, proceed to the city arcs (Great Games, Debates, Academy, Colossus, Sewers) as the party chooses.
