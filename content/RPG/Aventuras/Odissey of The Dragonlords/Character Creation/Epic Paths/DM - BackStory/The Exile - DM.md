# Integrating the Exile

## The Betrayal

Thirteen years ago a group of three Amazonian sisters were tempted by Lutheria with great power should they act on the ambitions of the eldest sister and usurp the throne. In a single night of bloodshed the Queen and her main supporters were murdered as the sisters stole their lives and identities. For this deed the three sisters were transformed into medusae, as both a ‘reward’ from Lutheria and a curse for their treachery and regicide.

The Exile was aided in escaping by the youngest sister, Moxena. When the eldest sister ordered Moxena to clear out the deposed queen’s living quarters, she found the soon-to-be Exile as a defenceless child. Rather than killing her as ordered, she snuck the child on a boat and pushed it out to sea, trusting in Fate to protect her.

Years later, either out of regret for the betrayal, or in hopes of seizing greater power for herself (or perhaps some mixture of the two sentiments). Moxena attempted to overthrow her two sisters and claim the throne. This ended poorly, and led to Moxena’s flight from Themis, and her eventual rise as the Lady of Coins in Mytros, after being rescued by Hexia.

## Meeting the Oracle

Versi will tell the Exile that she has a dangerous path ahead of her, but she need not walk it alone. The woman who once saved the Exile’s life waits for her in Mytros, “in the head of god.” Beyond that, Versi will tell the Exile that she must prove her worth extends beyond her blood, and that the Amazons must fight alongside the settlers if the Twin Titans are to be defeated.

> "Your Highness. Thank you for coming before me. I can see the path to your throne is a treacherous one indeed, but you needn't walk it alone. In the head of god, there is a woman. She could offer you either aid, or be the first villain to fall to your duly deserved revenge. Beyond her, I see that you must right the wrong the Amazons committed long ago. Only if they stand by the people of Thylea against the Titans can the Twins be felled. To retake your throne, you must not only exact brutal vengeance, but prove your worth lies beyond your blood."

## The Queen’s Warriors

The Exile may wish to speak with the Amazons that serve Queen Vallus. They are confused by the Exile’s survival, having been told long ago that the Princess was executed for cowardice and bringing shame to the royal house. If told the Exile’s side of the story they are easily convinced. They will not leave Vallus’ service, however, but can tell the Exile some of what they know is going on in Themis, and that they were exiled for supposedly bringing dishonour to the Amazons, something they dispute severely. In truth, they were exiled for their loyalty to the true queen, and the imposters didn’t want them uncovering the truth.

## The Lady of Coins

Moxena will send word to the Exile after the party handles the storm Sydon sent to assault the city. The messenger (spy) that Moxena sends on her behalf makes it clear that this offer is one of Guest Friendship. The messenger only refers to his patron only as the Lady of Coins, letting Moxena reveal her identity when she and the Exile meet.

Over the course of a lavish meal in the head of the Colossus of Pythor, Moxena will explain to the Exile the events described under **The Betrayal**, and that not only has she come to greatly regret betraying the Exile’s mother, but she is also willing to pledge herself to the Exile and her effort to reclaim the throne.

After revealing her side of the story, Moxena will offer an alliance by way of a mutual oath ‘sworn in the name of the Great Mothers and before the Merciless Furies.’ The Exile must swear that she will never take vengeance upon Moxena and in return Moxena will aid her in retaking her throne.

If the Exile rejects the alliance she may leave in peace, as per the rules of Guest Friendship. Following that, hostilities will commence, and Moxena will send the forces of the Cult of the Snake against her.

If the Exile accepts the alliance and swears the oath, Moxena will dedicate herself and her resources to the Exile’s cause. She hopes to ingratiate herself and become an advisor to the Exile when she is crowned Queen.

Moxena is depicted as being a far more 'good' character in this Epic Path than in the module. Should you wish to keep her true to her depiction in the adventure, have her lie to and manipulate the Exile in hopes of taking power for herself.

## Arrival in Themis

The Amazons of Themis believe that Thesilea VI executed her own daughter after being deemed unfit for the throne. Her return confuses them, especially if the Exile (and Moxena, if present) attempt to explain what truly happened thirteen years ago. Many Amazons are displeased with the shift towards Lutheria’s worship and will readily support the Exile if she has proven herself in the Trial, otherwise they may need convincing.

The Amazon Hunting Party encounter will still take the party to the Sacred Temple/Amazon Prison to see the false Thesilea, while the Amazon War Party encounter has instead been sent by the false Queen to assassinate the Exile.

If Moxena is present, she will relay she knows of some loyalists on the island that could help create a distraction, allowing the party to covertly approach the Sacred Temple/Amazon Prison. In addition, she relays its layout to the Exile best her memory serves.

## The Role of Darien

If the Exile Epic Path is chosen, Darien is not an heir to the throne or the child of Thesilea VI, but instead the daughter of one of the Queen’s closest allies. Darien and the Exile were childhood friends, confidants, and constant training partners. It is possible the two were going to become Lifemates one day, be it out of sororal or romantic love.

Darien survived that night in the same way described in the module, but instead of Thesilea forcing the three sisters to swear an oath to spare Darien’s life, it was one of Thesilea’s advisors, who surrendered her life in exchange for her daughter.

Darien desires vengeance for her mother and to be reunited with the Exile above all else. She will support the Exile’s bid to become Queen of the Amazons, and will only agree to become Queen herself if the Exile either dies, or if it is because the two rule the Amazons together as Lifemates.

## The False Queen

The imposter Thesilea VI speaks in a voice unnervingly similar to her mother, but with a serpentine accentuation to it. She curses Moxena’s name for letting the Exile escape all those years ago.

For a more challenging encounter, use the stats of Hythonia from *Mythic Odysseys of Theros* (though you may wish to remove her Shed Skin ability, depending on your party’s level).

## The Trial of Strength

In order to become the true Queen of Themis and of the Amazon tribes of the Cerulean Gulf, the Exile must defeat the roc that dwells in the Forgotten Sea and sacrifice it in the name of the Sisterhood of Equals. When running this encounter you should ignore the existence of the second roc, unless the party defeats the first one too easily. This event can be done before or after Themis.

## Crowning a Queen

Once both the Trial of Strength has been completed and the False Queen has been slain, the Exile can be crowned Amazonian Queen. If the Trial is completed first, the event should occur in Themis at the Sacred Temple. If confronting the False Queen was completed first, then either have an Amazonian raid ship follow behind to perform the coronation aboard their vessel or the Ultros before returning to Themis (this saves the party time in needing to double back to Themis), otherwise have the party return to Themis to complete the coronation.

The coronation ceremony is meant to be performed by an Amazon that the Exile trusts. This could be any of the loyalists, or Moxena or Darien, but is typically not performed by the would-be Queen’s potential Lifemate. The speech is simple:

> “Let the Great Mothers of the past find you worthy, Keeper of the Code of the Amazons, the Queen of Themis!”

If the Exile has someone they wish to join them on the throne, they may confirm them as their Lifemate and Queen-Consort of Themis. If this is done, the Exile and their Lifemate immediately gain the benefits of the Wedding effect from the *Ceremony* spell (this functions the same regardless as to whether or not the relationship is platonic or romantic).

## The Battle of Mytros

Should the Exile have been crowned Queen of Themis, Amazon raid ships enter the harbour behind Sydon’s forces. After a moment of terror for the locals and temporary celebration of the gygans, the Amazons let out their battle cries and attack the Titan’s forces from behind. This reduces the casualty count by 1/3 at the end of the battle, unless the city is completely destroyed, in which case the Amazons lose most of their warriors in a failed assault.


## Divine Boon

If the Exile dies, the ghost of her mother, the true Thesilea VI, immediately appears above her, righteous and proud. She grabs hold of her daughter and lets out an Amazonian battlecry that rouses the Exile from her final slumber. The Exile comes under the effects of the True Resurrection spell. This can only happen once.
