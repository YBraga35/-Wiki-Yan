The ancient forest presses in, its canopy a dense, suffocating blanket that greedily swallows the light. The air grows heavy and still, the typical clamor of birds and beasts replaced by an unnerving silence. Every snapped twig underfoot sounds like a crack of thunder in the oppressive quiet. As you follow the haphazard trail of spilled wine, the path vanishes into a wall of thorny underbrush. Pushing through, you emerge into a dimly-lit clearing, a cathedral of shadow held up by the gnarled pillars of ancient oaks.

Suddenly, the silence is shattered. A violent crashing from the brush ahead, and a young woman bursts into the clearing. Her breath comes in ragged sobs, her face a mask of terror streaked with dirt and tears. She stumbles to a halt, her eyes wide with disbelief as she sees you. "You... are you real?" she gasps. "Please, you have to help me!"

Before you can answer, more crashing sounds echo from the trees she fled. Six hideous figures emerge from the gloom, stepping into the dappled darkness. They are monstrous amalgams of man and beast, with the shaggy legs and cloven hooves of goats, but the torsos and jeering faces of cruel men. Their eyes gleam with a euphoric, malicious madness, and the cloying, sweet scent of strange fungi and spilled wine hangs about them like a shroud. This is no mere ambush; it is the violent, bleeding edge of a bacchanal, a hedonistic ritual where pleasure and pain are woven into a terrifying tapestry.

One of the goat-men lets out a harsh, bleating laugh that scrapes the air. "Give us the sweetling," it rasps, its voice a hateful gravel. "We've had our dinner, but she'll make a fine dessert!"

They don't wait for a reply. They begin to fan out, their movements jerky and unnatural, taunting you with vile insults that are both absurd and chillingly personal.

"Get lost, you pathetic starvelings!" one shrieks.

"Your face inspires me... to vomit!" cackles another, pointing a grimy finger.

A third one stamps a hoof, chanting in a manic rhythm, "Bald legs! Bald legs! Bald legs!"

Their cruel laughter fills the clearing as they raise their crude weapons. The fight is upon you, a desperate struggle against crazed revelers on the doorstep of a corrupt and ancient temple.
