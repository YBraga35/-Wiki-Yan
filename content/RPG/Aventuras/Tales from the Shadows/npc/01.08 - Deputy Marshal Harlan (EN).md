# Deputy Marshal Harlan Cross

## Snapshot
- **Role**: Yllmourne’s incorruptible law officer; bridges official authority with Maal’s investigative mandate.
- **Motivation**: Uphold the law, protect citizens, and prove that due process can prevail despite political pressure.
- **Leverage**: Commitment to justice, loyalty to Maal’s doctrine, and access to city guard resources.
- **Stat Baseline**: `Veteran` (MM p.350) with proficiency expertise in Investigation and Insight; carries arrest authority once per session (imposes restrained on cooperative targets).
- **Deep Dive**: Detailed profile in [[npc/apend.01 - Integrated NPCs Complete Guide#Deputy Marshal Harlan Cross|Integrated NPCs]].

## Arc by Act
- **Act I – Investigative Partner**: Deputizes the Agent and shares case files, ensuring legitimacy during dockside inquiries ([[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)#Act Overview|Act I]]).
- **Act II – Political Pressure**: Faces orders to close the case; sides with Agent during [[01.20 - Tribunal Script (EN)|Kael’s trial]] and later clandestine operations ([[02.00 - Act II - Political Intrigue (Lv5-7)|Act II]]).
- **Act III – Defense Coordinator**: Helps organize guard units to assist in curing rituals and naval operations.
- **Act IV – Tribunal Counsel**: Provides legal testimony and procedural expertise during Maal’s final reckoning ([[04.00 - Act IV - Cosmology and Faith (Lv13-20)|Act IV]]).

## Relationship Web
- **Agent of Maal**: Sees the Agent as proof Maal hasn’t abandoned Yllmourne.
- **Mayor Myles Lockton**: Increasingly fraught; Harlan resists orders to stand down.
- **Bonita Sandberg**: Protects her as key witness and values her medical insight ([[npc/01.05 - Bonita Sandberg (EN)|Bonita dossier]]).
- **One-Eyed Cy**: Maintains uneasy truce; respects Cy’s code while condemning crime ([[npc/01.01 - One-Eyed Cy (EN)|Cy dossier]]).
- **Octant Faylene**: Shares faith but questions her judgment once hex exposed ([[npc/01.03 - Octant Faylene (EN)|Faylene dossier]]).

## Scene Hooks
- **Evidence Intake**: Requires proper chain-of-custody; success grants advantage on future legal checks.
- **Line in the Sand**: Issues ultimatum to corrupt officials; inspires the Agent to rally civic allies (roleplay milestone increasing Cy’s Legitimacy clock).
- **Marshal’s Stand**: Offers guard detachment for [[03.10 - Naval Briefing (EN)|blockade run]] if PC proves plan is lawful.

## Mechanical Notes
- `Marshal’s Commission`: Once per session, allows the Agent to call in 1d4 guards as reinforcements (arrive in 1d4 rounds).
- `Tenacious Scribe`: Whenever evidence is formally logged with Harlan, grant +2 to subsequent Persuasion checks referencing that evidence.
