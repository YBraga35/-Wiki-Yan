# Act II: Political Intrigue (Levels 5-7)

## Act Overview

**Theme**: *"Powers Behind the Throne"*

Having exposed the surface conspiracy in Act I, the party now navigates the treacherous political landscape of Yllmourne. Prelate Faylene, humiliated but unbroken, escalates her plans. The Onotanian Royal Guard threatens the Library with destruction. Governor Cy and Admiral Sefton's cold war heats up, forcing the party to choose sides—or forge their own path. Most critically, the party begins to understand that mortal conspirators are merely *pawns* in a planar game orchestrated by servants of Naran (god of illusions/deception) and Thellos (god of madness/entropy).

**Narrative Arc**:
- **Act II Opening**: Faylene retreats but accelerates her hex; Royal Guard surrounds the Library; ships continue to sink
- **Rising Action**: Party infiltrates enemy strongholds, uncovers forbidden knowledge, exposes corrupt officials
- **Climax**: Faylene's mass hex is revealed and disrupted; the scapegoat from Act I is absolved; extraplanar agents are identified
- **Resolution**: The planar conspiracy is exposed, setting up Act III's naval campaigns against the *Abyssal Choir*

**Faction Clock Focus**:
1. **The Plague** (6-clock): Stabilized from Act I but can worsen if hexes spread
2. **Cy's Legitimacy** (4-clock): Vulnerable to Faylene's manipulations and Sefton's undermining
3. **Sefton's Dominance** (6-clock): Grows if party allies with criminals or if Cy is weakened
4. **Library Preservation** (4-clock): Critical—Royal Guard plans imminent assault
5. **Planar Incursion** (8-clock): Advances significantly; party finally becomes aware of this clock

**Level Progression**:
- Start at **Level 4** (post-Act I trial)
- Reach **Level 5** after completing Adventure 7
- Reach **Level 6** after completing Adventure 8
- Reach **Level 7** after Act II Climax

## Adventure 7: The Lost Treasure of Paletree Rise

### Original TftS Summary
Treasure hunters seek a legendary hoard hidden in ancient ruins. Heroes must navigate traps, puzzles, and rival expeditions to claim it first.

### Yllmourne Adaptation: Secrets in the Library's Depths

**Hook**: The Onotanian Royal Guard, led by **Commander Vareth**, demands access to the Library's restricted archives, claiming they contain "heretical texts that threaten the faith." Elara Moonshadow begs the party to discover what the Royal Guard *really* seeks before they burn the entire collection.

**Level Range**: 4-5

### Location Mapping

| TftS Location | Yllmourne Equivalent |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Paletree Rise (ancient ruins) | Library's Sub-Basements (forgotten archives) |
| Treasure vault | Forbidden Knowledge Vault (restricted section) |
| Rival expedition camp | Royal Guard encampment outside Library |
| Trapped passages | Magical wards and mechanical traps protecting secrets |

### NPC Integration

**Keep Original**:
- **Rival Treasure Hunter** (TftS): In Yllmourne, this becomes **Cassian Vex** (chaotic neutral human rogue), a freelance thief hired by Faylene to steal specific texts before the Royal Guard arrives.

**Add**:
- **Commander Vareth** (lawful evil human paladin of Onotas, Level 6): Zealous leader of the Royal Guard, genuinely believes he's protecting the faith but is manipulated by Faylene.
- **Elara Moonshadow** (neutral good half-elf wizard, Level 5): Head Librarian, desperate to preserve knowledge.

**Substitute**:
- **The Scholar Guide** (TftS) → **Brother Aldric** (neutral good human cleric of Maal, Level 3): A fellow Agent of Maal stationed at the Library, knowledgeable about the archives.

### Key Scenes

**Scene 1: The Siege Begins**
- **Situation**: Royal Guard (20 soldiers + Vareth) surround the Library; Cy refuses to intervene ("matters of faith are beyond my jurisdiction")
- **Challenge**: Party has 48 hours before Vareth orders the assault
- **Social Encounter**:
  - **Meet Vareth**: He's a true believer, not a monster; DC 16 Persuasion to convince him to delay (requires religious arguments or proof the Library isn't heretical)
  - **Rally Defenders**: Elara, Brother Aldric, and 8 scholar-NPCs will defend if needed (narratively messy—scholars aren't warriors)

**Scene 2: Descending into the Archives**
- **Location**: Sub-basement levels, accessible via hidden staircase
- **Atmosphere**: Musty, labyrinthine, lit by magical everburning torches
- **Encounter**: **Cassian Vex** is already inside, picking locks
  - **Negotiation** (DC 14 Persuasion): Cassian reveals Faylene hired him to steal texts about "planar bindings" and "divine suppression rituals"
  - **Combat** (if hostile): **Cassian Vex** (use **Assassin** stat block, CR 8—too strong for direct combat at Level 4; party should negotiate or flee)
  - **Alliance**: Cassian can be bribed (200 gp) or convinced Faylene will betray him (DC 15 Insight reveals he suspects this)

**Scene 3: The Trapped Corridor**
- **Challenge**: Ancient magical wards protect the Forbidden Vault
- **Traps**:
  1. **Glyph of Warding** (explosive runes): DC 15 Investigation to spot, DC 16 Arcana to disable, 5d8 thunder damage on failure
  2. **Arcane Lock** on vault door: DC 17 Arcana to dispel or *knock* spell bypasses
  3. **Guardian Construct**: **1 Animated Armor** (CR 1) and **2 Flying Swords** (CR 1/4 each) activate if traps are triggered
- **Clues**: Brother Aldric knows a passphrase in Old Lhodosian (grants advantage on Arcana checks)

**Scene 4: The Forbidden Vault**
- **Discovery**: Shelves of ancient tomes, scrolls, and relics
- **What Faylene Seeks**:
  - **"The Codex of Bound Divinity"**: A text describing how to suppress a god's influence in a region (Faylene plans to weaken Maal's presence in Yllmourne)
  - **"Rituals of the Choir Eternal"**: Instructions for summoning water elementals and binding them to idols (the *Abyssal Choir*'s methods)
  - **A Sealed Letter**: Written 50 years ago by Yllmourne's founder, admitting the city was built on Thessan's sacred site to "break the old gods' power"

**Scene 5: The Choice**
- **Dilemma**: What to do with the knowledge?
- **Options**:
  1. **Destroy the Codex**: Prevents Faylene from using it but loses valuable knowledge (Library Preservation -1 tick)
  2. **Hide the Codex**: Move it to a safer location (requires trust in an NPC like Brother Aldric or Navula)
  3. **Publicize the Founder's Letter**: Expose Yllmourne's corrupt origins (political chaos; Cy's Legitimacy -1 tick but Library Preservation +2 ticks as public supports knowledge)
  4. **Give Codex to Vareth**: He burns it, earns his trust, but loses knowledge forever
- **Cassian's Betrayal**: If not allied, Cassian attempts to steal the Codex during this scene (opposed Sleight of Hand vs. Perception)

### Investigation Hooks

**Primary Mystery**: What does the Royal Guard really want?
- **Answer**: Faylene manipulated Vareth into believing the Library harbors anti-Onotas texts; she wants the Codex for her own plans

**Secondary Mystery**: Why does Faylene want to suppress Maal?
- **Answer**: Maal's law-focused faith opposes her chaos-driven hex magic; weakening Maal makes her rituals more effective

**Tertiary Hook**: What is the *Abyssal Choir*?
- **Answer**: A cult serving Naran and Thellos, using water elementals to sink ships and spread plague

### Faction Clock Impact

- **Library Preservation**: +2 ticks if party successfully defends knowledge and negotiates with Vareth
- **Cy's Legitimacy**: -1 tick if Founder's Letter is publicized (reveals city's corrupt origins); +1 tick if party avoids bloodshed
- **Planar Incursion**: -1 tick if party secures the ritual texts (denies enemies resources); +1 tick if Faylene acquires the Codex

### Rewards

**Treasure**:
- 3 **spell scrolls** (levels 2-3, DM's choice based on party composition)
- **Tome of Understanding** (Wisdom +2, requires attunement and 48 hours of study over 6 days)
- Founder's Letter (political leverage)
- 300 gp worth of rare books (can be sold or donated)

**Allies**:
- Brother Aldric joins as a recurring NPC ally
- Cassian Vex (if allied) provides underworld contacts

**Experience**: Milestone to **Level 5**

## Adventure 8: The Horn of Revels

### Original TftS Summary
A cursed musical instrument compels listeners to dance to exhaustion. Heroes must infiltrate a decadent party to destroy it.

### Yllmourne Adaptation: Infiltrating Seafarers' Rest

**Hook**: Admiral Sefton hosts a "neutral ground" gala at **Seafarers' Rest** (his fortified tavern/headquarters). All major factions are invited. The party receives mysterious invitations suggesting that Sefton possesses evidence about the *Abyssal Choir*—but it's a trap. Faylene's agents plan to use a cursed artifact (the **Horn of Thellos**) to drive attendees into murderous frenzy, framing Sefton and igniting factional war.

**Level Range**: 5-6

### Location Mapping

| TftS Location | Yllmourne Equivalent |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Noble's masquerade ball | Seafarers' Rest gala (pirates, merchants, nobles mingling) |
| Ballroom | Main tavern hall (multi-level, balconies, stages) |
| Cursed musician's chamber | Hidden ritual room in the cellar |
| Escape route (secret passage) | Smuggler's tunnels beneath the tavern |

### NPC Integration

**Keep Original**:
- **The Cursed Musician** (TftS): In Yllmourne, this becomes **Thalia the Siren** (neutral evil tiefling bard, Level 6), a Thellos cultist who wields the Horn.

**Add**:
- **Admiral Sefton** (chaotic neutral human fighter, Level 7): Charming, dangerous, genuinely wants peace (for business reasons); respects strength.
- **Faylene's Agent**: **Sister Morwen** (lawful evil human cleric, Level 5), disguised as a servant, coordinates the attack.

**Fuse**:
- **The Rival Spy** (TftS) + **Cy's Informant**: **Lysandra Cole** (neutral good human rogue, Level 4), one of Cy's spies, also infiltrating; can be ally or complication.

### Key Scenes

**Scene 1: The Invitation**
- **Delivery**: A street urchin delivers sealed invitations to each PC
- **Content**: "Your presence is requested at Seafarers' Rest. Come armed with wit, not steel. Answers await those brave enough to seek them. — A Friend"
- **Clues**:
  - **DC 13 Investigation**: The paper is expensive, the seal is Sefton's (but the handwriting isn't his)
  - **DC 15 Insight**: This feels like a setup

**Scene 2: Entering the Gala**
- **Location**: Seafarers' Rest, lavishly decorated but heavily guarded
- **Atmosphere**: Pirates, nobles, merchants, and criminals mingling; tense but festive
- **Challenge**: Weapons are forbidden (checked at the door); party must smuggle in gear or rely on improvisation
  - **DC 14 Sleight of Hand**: Hide a dagger or wand
  - **DC 16 Deception**: Convince guards your holy symbol is "just jewelry"
  - **Backup Plan**: Lysandra (if befriended) has weapons stashed in the cellar

**Scene 3: Social Maneuvering**
- **NPCs Present**:
  - **Sefton**: Holding court, surprisingly affable; knows the party by reputation
  - **Callista Whitesail**: Attending for business; grateful to see the party
  - **Faylene**: Absent (too suspicious to attend personally)
  - **Commander Vareth**: Present begrudgingly (Cy ordered him to "keep the peace")
- **Objectives**:
  1. **Gather Intel**: Interview Sefton about the *Abyssal Choir* (he knows they're hurting his smuggling but not who they are)
  2. **Spot Threats**: DC 14 Perception to notice Thalia tuning a strange horn backstage; DC 16 Insight to realize Sister Morwen is nervous
  3. **Build Alliances**: Convince Sefton to trust the party (DC 15 Persuasion; easier if they've antagonized Cy)

**Scene 4: The Horn Sounds**
- **Trigger**: Thalia takes the stage, plays the **Horn of Thellos**
- **Effect**: All creatures within 60 feet must make **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw** or become charmed for 1 minute, forced to dance/attack nearest creature (DM's choice based on Thellos's chaotic nature)
- **Chaos**: 30+ NPCs go berserk; Sefton's guards try to restore order; Vareth assumes this is Sefton's doing
- **Challenge**: Party must stop Thalia while avoiding collateral damage
  - **Combat**: **Thalia the Siren** (use **Glamour Bard** stat block, CR 5) + **2 Cult Fanatics** (CR 2 each)
  - **Environmental Hazards**: Rampaging NPCs, fires from overturned lanterns, stampedes

**Scene 5: The Cellar Ritual**
- **Discovery**: If the party investigates during the chaos, they find Sister Morwen in the cellar, maintaining a ritual circle that amplifies the Horn's power
- **Encounter**: **Sister Morwen** (use **Evil Cleric** stat block, CR 5) + **1 Water Elemental** (CR 5, summoned as backup)
- **Objective**: Break the ritual circle (DC 15 Arcana or Athletics to destroy the focal runes) to weaken the Horn
- **Evidence**: Morwen carries letters from Faylene detailing the plan: "Frame Sefton, ignite war, seize power in the chaos."

### Investigation Hooks

**Primary Mystery**: Who sent the invitations?
- **Answer**: Faylene's agents, luring the party into the trap to eliminate them or discredit them

**Secondary Mystery**: Why attack Sefton's stronghold?
- **Answer**: Faylene wants to frame Sefton as a Thellos cultist, giving Cy justification to move against him (but Cy's forces are also weakened in the ensuing chaos)

**Tertiary Hook**: What is Sefton's connection to the *Abyssal Choir*?
- **Answer**: None—he's a red herring. The real *Abyssal Choir* agents are among Faylene's Rotarists.

### Faction Clock Impact

- **Sefton's Dominance**: +2 ticks if party saves him and exposes Faylene's plot (he becomes a grateful, if dangerous, ally)
- **Cy's Legitimacy**: +1 tick if party prevents factional war; -1 tick if violence escalates
- **Library Preservation**: +1 tick if Morwen's letters are recovered (evidence of Faylene's conspiracy helps Brother Aldric's defense)
- **Planar Incursion**: +1 tick (Thellos's influence grows from the chaos) OR -1 tick if party stops the ritual quickly

### Rewards

**Treasure**:
- **Horn of Thellos** (cursed artifact): Can cast *confusion* once per day, but wielder must make DC 15 Wisdom save or suffer short-term madness
- Morwen's letters (evidence against Faylene)
- Sefton's gratitude: Access to smuggling routes, 500 gp, and 1 **uncommon magic item** of party's choice from his "collection"
- 400 gp looted from cultists

**Allies**:
- Sefton becomes a reliable (if morally grey) ally
- Lysandra Cole (if befriended) becomes Cy's liaison to the party

**Experience**: Milestone to **Level 6**

## Adventure 9: The Sodality of Silent Rites

### Original TftS Summary
A secret society performs dark rituals to gain immortality. Heroes must infiltrate their ranks and expose their corruption before a major ritual is completed.

### Yllmourne Adaptation: The Rotarist Conspiracy

**Hook**: After Adventures 7 and 8, the party has connected the dots: the **Rotarists** (Faylene's secret cult within the Onotanian faith) are the mortal agents of the planar conspiracy. The final piece falls into place when Brother Aldric discovers references to **"The Sodality of Silent Rites"**—an ancient cult to Naran and Thellos that supposedly died out centuries ago. It's been resurrected by Faylene, and she plans a **mass hex ritual** to simultaneously curse all of Cy's key supporters (including the party).

**Level Range**: 6-7

### Location Mapping

| TftS Location | Yllmourne Equivalent |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Secret society headquarters | Temple of Onotas (hidden sub-level) |
| Ritual chamber | Faylene's sanctum beneath the temple |
| Initiation catacombs | Forgotten crypts of Yllmourne's founders |
| Final ritual site | The Drowned Altar (submerged shrine to Naran/Thellos in the harbour) |

### NPC Integration

**Keep Original**:
- **The Cult Leader** (TftS): **Prelate Faylene** herself, revealed as the mastermind.

**Add**:
- **The Planar Agents**: For the first time, the party encounters **Naran's Whisper** (CR 9, custom stat block: invisible stalker with illusion magic) and **Thellos's Herald** (CR 8, custom: insane priest with psychic abilities).

**Substitute**:
- **The Undercover Investigator** (TftS) → **Dagmara's Spirit**: Her ghost returns one last time, having gathered intelligence from the afterlife, to guide the party.

### Key Scenes

**Scene 1: The Discovery**
- **Trigger**: Brother Aldric finds a hidden journal in the Library's archives
- **Content**: The Sodality of Silent Rites' history:
  - Founded 300 years ago to worship Naran and Thellos
  - Suppressed by Onotas's rise
  - Faylene secretly revived it 10 years ago after being "touched by visions" (actually contacted by planar agents)
- **Clues**: The journal includes a map to the Temple's hidden sub-level and the ritual's date: **three nights from now, at high tide**

**Scene 2: Infiltrating the Temple**
- **Location**: Temple of Onotas, Yllmourne's grandest religious structure
- **Challenge**: The sub-level entrance is beneath Faylene's private chambers
- **Options**:
  1. **Stealth Infiltration**: DC 16 Stealth (group check) to sneak in at night
  2. **Social Deception**: Pose as new Rotarist recruits (DC 17 Deception, requires knowing passwords from previous adventures)
  3. **Brute Force**: Fight through 12 **Acolytes** (CR 1/4) and 3 **Priests** (CR 2)—loud and attracts Commander Vareth's Royal Guard

**Scene 3: The Catacombs**
- **Location**: Crypts beneath the Temple, filled with sarcophagi of Yllmourne's founders
- **Atmosphere**: Oppressive, psychic whispers (DC 13 Wisdom save or suffer disadvantage on Wisdom checks)
- **Encounter**: **3 Specters** (CR 1 each) of founders who made pacts with Naran/Thellos
- **Clues**:
  - Inscriptions reveal Yllmourne was founded explicitly to suppress Thessan and the "old gods," empowering Onotas
  - Faylene discovered this and believes she's "restoring balance" (delusional justification)

**Scene 4: Faylene's Sanctum**
- **Location**: Hidden chamber, walls inscribed with prayers to Naran and Thellos
- **Discovery**: Faylene's ritual notes, dozens of mirrors (scrying foci), and a list of 20 hex targets (including Cy, Callista, Brother Aldric, and each PC)
- **Encounter**: **Faylene** (use **High Priest** stat block, CR 5, with additional illusion and enchantment spells) + **Naran's Whisper** (invisible, casts *major image* and *phantasmal killer*)
- **Roleplay**: Faylene is unrepentant, believing chaos will "free Yllmourne from tyranny"
- **Challenge**: Faylene attempts to flee via *dimension door* to the Drowned Altar if reduced below 30 HP

**Scene 5: The Drowned Altar**
- **Location**: A submerged shrine in the harbour, accessible only at low tide or by swimming
- **Setup**: Faylene and 8 **Rotarist Cultists** (use **Cultist**, CR 1/8) perform the mass hex
- **The Ritual**:
  - If not stopped, 20 mirrors shatter simultaneously across Yllmourne, cursing all targets
  - Cursed victims (including PCs) must make DC 16 Wisdom saves or suffer *bestow curse* effects
- **Encounter**:
  - **Faylene** (wounded from Scene 4 if applicable)
  - **Thellos's Herald** (use **Priest of Madness** homebrew: CR 8, casts *confusion*, *phantasmal killer*, *crown of madness*)
  - **2 Water Elementals** (CR 5 each) guarding the altar
- **Environmental Hazards**: Rising tide (water level rises 5 feet every 2 rounds; PCs must make DC 12 Athletics checks or be swept away)

### Investigation Hooks

**Primary Mystery**: What is Faylene's endgame?
- **Answer**: Mass hex to incapacitate Cy's faction, allowing her and the Rotarists to seize control of Yllmourne's faith and politics

**Secondary Mystery**: Who are Naran's Whisper and Thellos's Herald?
- **Answer**: Extraplanar servants sent to manipulate mortals; they've been posing as "visions from the gods" to guide Faylene

**Tertiary Hook**: Why target the party specifically?
- **Answer**: The party has disrupted too many plans; they're now priority threats to the planar conspiracy

### Faction Clock Impact

- **Cy's Legitimacy**: +3 ticks if ritual is stopped (prevents mass hex, proves party's loyalty)
- **Library Preservation**: +2 ticks if Faylene is exposed (removes pressure from Royal Guard)
- **Planar Incursion**: -3 ticks if both planar agents are banished/killed (major blow to conspiracy); +2 ticks if ritual succeeds
- **The Plague**: -1 tick if ritual is stopped (Faylene's hex amplified plague symptoms)

### Rewards

**Treasure**:
- Faylene's **+1 Mace** (inscribed with Onotas's symbols, now corrupted)
- **Ring of Mind Shielding** (Faylene wore this to avoid scrying)
- Ritual notes (proof of conspiracy, can be used politically)
- 600 gp worth of ritual components (silver, rare incense, gemstones)

**Major Loot**:
- **Mirror of Naran** (very rare wondrous item): Allows *scrying* once per day, but user must make DC 13 Wisdom save or be observed by Naran's servants

**Allies**:
- Dagmara's ghost is finally laid to rest, granting a permanent **blessing of protection** to the Agent of Maal (+1 AC)

**Experience**: Milestone to **Level 7** after Act II Climax

## Act II Climax: Absolution and Revelation

### Setup
With Faylene's ritual disrupted and the Rotarist conspiracy exposed, two major story threads resolve:

1. **Faylene's Fate**: Captured or killed, she must face justice
2. **The Scapegoat's Absolution**: Kael (from Act I) is formally cleared of all charges

### The Absolution Scene

**Location**: Governor's Palace, public ceremony

**Attendees**: Cy, Commander Vareth, Callista, Sefton (surprisingly), Elara, Brother Aldric, hundreds of citizens

**Structure**:
1. **Cy's Speech**: Thanks the party, admits his government failed to see the conspiracy
2. **Evidence Presentation**: Ritual notes, Morwen's letters, witness testimony (Cassian, if alive) prove Faylene's guilt
3. **Kael's Pardon**: Publicly exonerated; Cy offers him compensation and a government position (Kael tearfully accepts)
4. **Party's Reward**: Cy grants them **Letters of Marque** (authority to act as privateers against the *Abyssal Choir*), funds for a ship, and noble titles (if desired)

### Faylene's Fate (Player Choice)

**Option 1: Execution**
- Faylene is publicly executed for treason and heresy
- **Effect**: Cy's Legitimacy +1, but Onotanian faith is divided (some see her as a martyr)

**Option 2: Imprisonment**
- Faylene is locked in Yllmourne's dungeons
- **Effect**: She becomes a recurring villain (escapes in Act III or IV)

**Option 3: Exile**
- Faylene is banished from Yllmourne
- **Effect**: She plots revenge from afar, potentially allying with remaining planar agents

**Option 4: Redemption**
- Party convinces Cy that Faylene was manipulated by planar forces (DC 18 Persuasion)
- Faylene is placed under Brother Aldric's supervision for "spiritual rehabilitation"
- **Effect**: Controversial but merciful; some NPCs respect this, others see it as weakness

### The Revelation

As the ceremony concludes, **Brother Aldric** pulls the party aside with urgent news:

*"I've been studying the texts we recovered. The *Abyssal Choir* isn't just a cult—it's a **ship**. A vessel crewed by Naran and Thellos's servants, sailing between planes. It's been docking in hidden coves around Yllmourne, spreading the plague, sinking ships, and coordinating cults. And according to Pip's drawings from Act I... it's preparing for a major operation. Something called the **Tidal Reckoning**."*

**Cliffhanger**: A sailor bursts in, shouting that three ships sank last night—all merchant vessels carrying food for Yllmourne. The city has **two weeks of food left**. The *Abyssal Choir* is trying to starve Yllmourne into submission.

## Transition to Act III

With the land-based conspiracy exposed, the fight shifts to the sea. Act III will feature:
- **Naval Campaigns**: Hunting the *Abyssal Choir* across Yllmourne's waters
- **Island Adventures**: Remaining *Tales from the Shadows* adventures adapted as cursed islands and haunted lighthouses
- **Building Alliances**: Recruiting Sefton's smugglers, Cy's navy, and independent sailors
- **Dismantling Operations**: Destroying cursed idol caches, freeing enslaved water elementals, liberating corrupted islands

The party now has resources, allies, and authority—but the true enemy has revealed itself, and the stakes escalate to existential.

## DM Guidance for Act II

### Balancing Political Factions

**The Core Tension**: Cy vs. Sefton vs. Faylene

- **Cy**: Legitimate but weak; needs the party's help but distrusts bold action
- **Sefton**: Illegitimate but effective; respects strength and pragmatism
- **Faylene**: Ideological extremist; believes she's a hero

**Player Agency**: Allow players to support, oppose, or manipulate all three. There's no "correct" choice—each alliance has consequences.

### Running Social Intrigue

**Tips**:
1. **Use Timers**: "You have 48 hours before the Royal Guard attacks" creates urgency
2. **Offer Imperfect Information**: Let players make decisions without knowing everything
3. **Consequences Matter**: If players publicly humiliate Vareth, he becomes an enemy
4. **Respect Player Creativity**: If they devise a brilliant plan you didn't anticipate, reward it

### Handling the Planar Agents

**Naran's Whisper** and **Thellos's Herald** are the first "boss-tier" enemies. They should feel *alien*:
- They speak in riddles or madness
- They don't fear death (returning to their planar masters is acceptable)
- They're manipulative, not directly confrontational (prefer proxies)

**Combat Tactics**:
- **Naran's Whisper**: Uses invisibility, illusions, and hit-and-run tactics; never fights fair
- **Thellos's Herald**: Revels in chaos; uses *confusion* to turn PCs against each other

### Adapting to Player Choices

**If Players Ally with Sefton**:
- Act III becomes "pirates vs. cult" maritime warfare
- Cy grows distant; Royal Guard may become antagonists

**If Players Ally with Cy**:
- Act III becomes "official navy vs. cult" campaigns
- Sefton feels betrayed; may withhold useful information

**If Players Stay Neutral**:
- Both factions offer competing quests; players must balance reputations
- Hardest path but most flexible

### Session Structure for Act II

Each adventure (7-9) should take 2-4 sessions:
- **Session 1**: Investigation, planning, social encounters
- **Session 2**: Infiltration, mid-adventure combat, revelations
- **Session 3** (if needed): Climax, boss fight, aftermath
- **Session 4** (if needed): Downtime, faction management, setup for next adventure

---

## Key Takeaways for Act II

**What Players Should Feel**:
- **Empowered**: They have resources, allies, and authority now
- **Paranoid**: Every NPC could be compromised by Naran or Thellos
- **Morally Complex**: There are no easy answers—justice vs. mercy, truth vs. peace
- **Driven**: The conspiracy is bigger than they thought; they *must* stop it

**What the Party Should Know by Act II's End**:
1. The *Abyssal Choir* is a literal ship crewed by planar cultists
2. Naran and Thellos are the true antagonists, not Faylene
3. The plague, ship sinkings, and hexes are all coordinated attacks
4. The Tidal Reckoning (Act III's climax) threatens to destroy Yllmourne entirely
5. They need a ship, a crew, and naval allies to fight the coming war

---

**Next**: Act III (Levels 8-12) will be developed by the DM using remaining *Tales from the Shadows* adventures adapted to maritime settings and original naval campaign content.
