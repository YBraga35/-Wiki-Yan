## The Letter

_My dear child,_

_I write these words across the span of centuries, knowing that you will one day stand in this place, bearing burdens no mortal should have to carry. I write to the haunted. To the one who walks with ghosts._

_You know what it means to be pursued by the past. To carry the weight of those who came before. To hear voices in the silence—voices of accusation, of regret, of unfinished business. You understand, perhaps better than any of your companions, what it means to inherit a legacy of blood and ash._

_I, too, was haunted._

_When I first came to Thylea, I believed we would build something beautiful. I believed that power—used wisely—could be a force for good. I believed that the settlers we protected deserved safety, prosperity, a future free from the terrors they had fled._

_But in seeking to build that future, we destroyed countless others._

_I watched as the Dragonlords—my beloved companions, my oathsworn—transformed from protectors into conquerors. I watched as Xander's noble heart grew heavy with doubt. I watched as Estor's ambition curdled into cruelty. I watched as Rizon's strategic brilliance became ruthless efficiency. And I watched as the peoples of Thylea—peoples with their own dreams, their own families, their own sacred places—were driven from their lands or slaughtered where they stood._

_I tried to stop it. I placed myself between blade and victim. I shielded the innocent with my own body. I argued, pleaded, roared my dissent. But I was only one voice against many, and the tide of conquest could not be turned by good intentions alone._

_The ghosts of those I failed to save have haunted me across lifetimes._

_When I made my final sacrifice—when I poured my essence into the creation of the Oath of Peace—I did so knowing that it would not erase what had been done. The dead would not return. The stolen lands would not magically heal. The trauma inflicted upon this world would echo for centuries._

_But I hoped—desperately, fiercely—that my sacrifice might give future generations the chance to make different choices. To be better than we were. To heal what we had broken._

_And now you stand here, haunted one. You carry ghosts of your own. Perhaps they whisper to you of failure. Of guilt. Of sins that cannot be undone. Perhaps they tell you that you are cursed, that you are damned, that no redemption is possible._

_Listen to me: they are wrong._

_The past cannot be changed. The dead cannot be brought back. The harm we have done—the harm you have witnessed or suffered or perhaps even inflicted—cannot be erased. These are truths, and we must bear their weight._

_But the future is not yet written._

_You have been given a gift that many never receive: the chance to make amends. Not by undoing what was done—that is impossible—but by choosing differently going forward. By protecting instead of conquering. By healing instead of harming. By standing as a shield for those who cannot defend themselves._

_The ghosts you carry do not have to be chains. They can be teachers. They can remind you, in every moment of temptation, what path you must not take. They can show you, through their pain, what truly matters._

_You are haunted because you remember. Because you carry the weight of what should not be forgotten. This is not a curse—it is a responsibility._

_The armaments you will claim from Xander's tomb are tools of immense power. They were forged in an age of conquest and stained with innocent blood. But tools have no inherent morality. What matters is how they are used._

_I give you this charge: wield these weapons not as instruments of domination, but as instruments of protection. Use the power you inherit not to impose your will upon others, but to defend those who have no power of their own. And when the ghosts whisper to you—as they surely will—let them remind you why this matters._

_You will face a choice in the days to come. A choice between justice and vengeance. Between healing and harm. Between the path of the old Dragonlords and something new._

_I cannot tell you which choice to make. That is yours alone to bear._

_But I can tell you this: you are stronger than you know. The very fact that you are haunted—that you feel the weight of the past—means you have not lost your capacity for empathy, for compassion, for growth. Those who feel nothing are the ones who repeat history's worst atrocities._

_You feel everything. And that is your power._

_So carry your ghosts, haunted one. Let them walk beside you. Let them whisper their warnings. Let them show you the path you must not take. And then choose—consciously, deliberately, with full knowledge of what came before—to be better._

_The world does not need more conquerors. It needs guardians. Healers. Protectors. It needs people who remember the cost of power and wield it with care._

_Be that person. Be the Dragonlord we should have been._

_And know that across the span of centuries, across the boundary between mortal and divine, I believe in you. I believe you can carry this burden without being crushed by it. I believe you can face your ghosts and not be consumed by them._

_I believe you can heal what we broke._

_Walk forward with courage, haunted one. The shadows you carry are proof that you still have light within you. And light, however small, can pierce even the deepest darkness._

_With hope eternal,_

**Balmytria**  
_The Silver Dragon_  
_Guardian of the Lost_

## P.S.

_Look to the north, when the moon rises full over the Mithral Mountains. There you will see a constellation in the shape of a dragon—my constellation, though few remember it now. When you feel most alone, most burdened by your ghosts, look to that constellation and remember: you are not alone. The dead walk with you not to drag you down, but to remind you why you must keep moving forward._

_And if ever you need counsel, seek out those who also remember. Damon knows what it means to lose everything and still choose to protect. Kyrah understands the weight of secrets and the cost of hidden truths. Even Xander, in his final moments, chose redemption over pride._

_You are part of a long line of haunted souls who chose to become something more than their pain. Walk that path with honor._
