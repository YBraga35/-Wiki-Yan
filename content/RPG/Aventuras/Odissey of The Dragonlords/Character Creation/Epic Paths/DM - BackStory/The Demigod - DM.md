# Integrating the Demigod

The Demigod is a child of **Pythor**, the God of Battle. Pythor has spent centuries obsessing over his failures. He believes that the Demigod is his path to **redemption**. Pythor wants the Demigod to perform the tasks that he himself failed to accomplish—and to take his place as the **God of Battle**.

---

## Meeting the Oracle

**Versi** expects great things from the Demigod. She encourages them to travel to **Estoria** and meet their father, Pythor.

> "You are a beauteous thing, you, but a flower cannot bloom if its stem wilts. So too must you, to see your destiny through, go to your divine parent, Pythor, now King of Estoria. He wallows in his wine and his grief, long having lost the luster of his glory days. You shall forge the weapon he could never wield, defeat his ancient foe, and save his true love. In doing so, you shall walk a path that would allow you to outshine your father in heroism, and perhaps even join him in godhood."

---

## Meeting Pythor

When the heroes first meet **Pythor** in **Estoria**, he recognizes the Demigod as one of his children. He laments that the green dragon **Hexia** took the Demigod’s mother from him. Hexia now dwells somewhere in the **Forgotten Sea**.

---

## Pythor's Hammer

When Pythor learns that the Demigod wants to finish building his greatest weapon, he will offer up his magical hammer—**Pythor’s Hammer**. He explains that the hammer is essential to forge the weapon.

Before handing it over, however, Pythor makes the Demigod **swear an oath** to save his daughter **Anora** (the Demigod’s half-sister) from being sacrificed to **Sydon**.

Afterward, he suggests the Demigod speak with **Volkan**, his father, who also lives in Estoria.

---

## Meeting Volkan

Volkan greets yet another grandchild kindly, though with less patience than he may have shown the first. Still, he has a "good feeling" and tries to be warm.

He explains that the Demigod will only be able to forge Pythor’s unfinished weapon using the **Mithral Forge**. He instructs them to **relight the forge** and **gather the components** necessary to complete the weapon.

---

## Forging the Weapon

Once the fires of the **Mithral Forge** are relit, Volkan can forge the weapon if given the appropriate materials.

When the weapon is complete, Volkan sends a **keledone** to deliver it to the Demigod.

---

## Pythor's Greatest Foe

The Demigod eventually discovers that their **mother** is a **prisoner in Hexia’s lair** on the **Island of the Dragon**. By defeating Hexia, the Demigod will:

- Defeat Pythor’s greatest foe  
- Rescue their mother

### The Island of the Dragon

On the island, **Hexia** uses the beasts to **spy on the party**. Through **illusory magic**, she makes it seem like a dragon is stalking them.

She may also **shapeshift into a humanoid**, appearing as an **elven woman** with copper skin and forest green eyes. Claiming to be a marooned soul tormented by the monster of the island, she tries to learn more about the party—especially the Demigod and Pythor.

If her disguise is somehow seen through (magic won't reveal her shapeshifting, but may detect Hexia herself), she will **summon beasts to attack** before **retreating to her lair**.

#### Optional: Hexia's Monologue

At the lair, consider using *OotDL: Hexia's Monologue* to give her a dramatic moment, **berating Pythor** as she confronts the end of her life.

---

## Reunited with Mother

The Demigod’s mother knows a little about the **Forgotten Sea** and can offer **vague descriptions of each island**.

She asks the Demigod to **succeed where Pythor failed** and **save Thylea from the Titans**.

She is incredibly proud of her child. Though she felt **some sympathy for Hexia** during their years together, she is immensely glad to be **reunited with her true love**, if Pythor is present.


## Divine Boon

If the Demigod is ever killed, Pythor will appear within 1 hour and surrender a portion of his divine spark to raise them back to life. This can only happen once, and Pythor suffers one level of permanent exhaustion as a result.
