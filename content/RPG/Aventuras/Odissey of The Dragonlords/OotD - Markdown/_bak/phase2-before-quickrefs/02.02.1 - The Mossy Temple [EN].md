# Deepening Woodhike Village

This detailed guide expands the first Great Task, turning the quest for Balmytria’s Horn into a complex, grim narrative arc focused on the tragic figure of Demetria. The adventure explores themes of grief, corruption, and the consequences of the original Dragonlords, setting a tone of moral ambiguity for the campaign.

## Part I: Adventure Hooks

The mission does not start with a decree but with the desperation thatps into Estaria.

### Hook 1: The Desperate Appeal (Estaria)

In a tavern or market, the heroes are approached by **Thericles** (see *Estaria, E13*)—a respected vintner. His voice blends anguish and hope.

> **Thericles speaks:**  
> “You… you are the Oracle’s Chosen, aren’t you? Stories of your deeds already travel the city streets. My name is Thericles. My daughter, Corinna, and her friends… they vanished. They were last seen near the Ancient Forest, lured by promises of an eternal festival. I have… a family relic to offer… only return it safely.”

The reward is a **Javelin of Lightning**. This personal encounter anchors the mission in a resonant emotional goal: it’s not merely an artifact but a rescue of innocent youths.

### Hook 2: Whispers in the Forest (Estaria)

While investigating the disappearance, the heroes might notice a nervous, hooded satyr trying to avoid eye contact. He is **Loreus**. If approached, he whispers urgently.

> **Loreus whispers:**  
> “Stay away from the forest! A… a dark fugue has taken the old temple. The music… wine… it’s not what it seems. The Lady of that place… she offers not celebration, but forgetting. The children… I tried… but I am only a…”

If pressed, Loreus panics and runs into the forest, fearing Demetria’s wrath. He leaves behind a thin trail of thick, sticky wine—the same that the heroes will find later.

### Hook 3: The Horn’s Theft (Estaria)

At the **Dragon Sanctuary (E7)**, the heroes find the priest **Aesop** in panic.

> **Aesop speaks:**  
> “The Horn of Balmytria… it was stolen! Thieves breached the sanctuary two nights ago. All that remained were… these hoofprint marks and stains of a strange, dark wine. The trail leads into the Ancient Forest. The Oracle said you should drink from the Horn. Now I fear it has fallen into profane hands.”

Investigating the broken relic reveals satyr footprints and the scent of corrupt magic, pointing directly to the forest’s heart.

## Part II: Journey to the Shadow

Traveling from Estaria to the Mossy Temple is a descent into a domain corrupted by Demetria’s sorrow. The journey should last at least two days, allowing tension‑building encounters.

### Day 1: Wine‑Trail and Madness

The trail left by the thieves is easy to follow. Dark wine stains mark the trees and floor. The forest itself feels sick: the air is damp and heavy, birds are silent, and the trees bleed a dark sap.

**Encounter: Mad Satyrs**

The heroes find the remnants of a recent raucous gathering. Broken wine bottles, smoldering campfires, and the smell of excess and despair hang in the air. The site becomes a trap.

- **Creatures:** 1d4+2 satyrs (eyes glowing red) and 1d4 horned goats emerge from shadows, attacking with reckless abandon. They do not speak, only emit screams of fury and ecstasy.
- **Tactics:** They fight to the death, utterly consumed by the corrupted wine from the Horn. A Medicine check (DC 13) on a fallen body reveals that the wine altered their physiology, making them stronger yet unstable.

### Day 2: Silent Warning

Deeper in the woods, the group encounters a strangely twisted tree that seems to writhe in agony. A nature‑ or magic‑sensitive character (druid, ranger, or someone blessed by Thylea) may sense a trapped consciousness within it.

**Encounter: The Suffering Tree (Herkus)**

The tree is, in fact, **Herkus**, a gnome druid transfigured by Demetria so he cannot alert others. He cannot speak but can use gestures or mental images to convey a warning.

- **Communication:** He does not warn of the children’s abduction—something the heroes already know. Instead, he projects an image of a colossal tree pulsing with light, surrounded by shadows and vicious beasts. He shows Demetria, not in the temple, but in this holy place, performing a dark ritual.
- **Revelation:** Herkus reveals Demetria’s true goal: to corrupt the **Heart of the Ancient Forest**, the region’s most sacred shrine. This escalates the stakes. It is no longer merely a rescue but a prevention of a catastrophe that will poison the entire Ancient Forest.

## Part III: The Mossy Temple (Detailed Locations)

Upon arrival, the heroes find the temple in grotesque silence. Demetria and most of her forces have departed. The air is thick with the smell of spilled wine and blood.

### T1 – Vertical Stones

Seven large vertical stone pillars, covered in moss, mark the clearing’s entrance. They radiate an aura of ancient power.

- **Druidic Blessing:** A druid or ranger resting here for a short time receives the benefits of a long rest (once every 24 hours).
- **Herkus the Squirrel:** If the players have not interacted with the tree, they find Herkus here in squirrel form. He is frantic, tossing nuts and squeaking, trying to attract attention. If captured or wounded, he turns back into his insane gnome self but can be an odd companion.

### T2 – Temple Entrance

Colossal marble pillars draped in vines form the façade. Gnarled, animated roots, controlled by Demetria, guard the entrance.

- **Animated Roots:** (AC 10, 10 HP). Roots attack any non‑fey creature approaching. On a hit (+3), the target is *Grabbed* and *Restricted* (DC 10 to escape). Roots attempt to drag victims toward the stagnant water in T5.

### T3 – Ruined Ante‑Chamber

A wide rectangular hall filled with damp leaves and earthy scents.

- **Broken Shield:** On the floor lies a bronze shield with the Dragonlord symbol. An inscription shows *Estor Arkelander subjugating six‑armed gygants*. If Kyrah is present, she comments bitterly.

> **Kyrah speaks:** “Estor… ever so proud of his ‘order’ forced upon us. He didn’t see the creatures of this land, only obstacles to his glory.”

### T4 – Sydon’s Chamber

A colossal statue of Sydon dominates the room. He holds a polished adamantine mirror that reflects a beam of light to a small hole in the floor.

- **Statue & Mirror:** Both are highly resistant (AC 18/23, 100/30 HP). Attacking them makes a huge noise that alerts the entire complex.
- **Secret Passage:** The hole is a passage to the **Ancient Forest (T8)**. A Small creature can crawl through it.
- **Gygant Hymn:** Characters examining the statue’s base may find an inscription in an ancient tongue. With an Intelligence (History) check (DC 14) or the *comprehend languages* spell, they can decipher the hymn to Sydon.

### T5 – Ancient Baths

A massive pool of stagnant, dark water, with roots and vines climbing the walls.

- **Hazards:** Two **Gray Slimes** hide in the water. The **Animated Roots** will actively try to pull the heroes into the pool to drown them and hand them over to the slimes.

### T6 – Descending Staircase

A staircase to lower levels guarded by four six‑armed gygant statues.

- **Stone Guardians:** Two statues are **Gargoyles** (no flight speed) that animate and attack when an intruder descends.
- **Offering to Sydon:** If a character places an offering (coin, rations) into one of the bronze bowls the statues hold and offers a brief prayer to Sydon, the guardians remain inert.

## Part IV: The Corrupted Heart of the Temple

The lower levels hold the most visible corruption from Demetria.

### T9 – Mushroom Cavern & Lost Youths

A cavern full of luminescent fungi (Madon) and eight adolescents in drugged euphoria.

- **Hostages:** These are Corinna’s friends. They are under the influence of the mushroom and Demetria. They see her as a benevolent goddess.
  
  > **Lysandra speaks:** “Demetria showed us true beauty… freedom. Soon we will be initiated, free from the world’s chains.”

- **Rescue:** They cannot be reasoned with. They must be forcibly removed and kept away from Demetria’s influence for 24 hrs (DC 10) for persuasion to work.
- **Guardians:** Two **horned goats** lurk in shadows, preventing anyone’s escape.

### T10 – Flower Henchment & Unexpected Allies

A small flower‑covered cave. Here the heroes find Fey who oppose Demetria’s methods.

- **Fey Diplomats:** Two **satyrs** and a **dryad** dwell here. They disapprove of mortals’ abduction and fear Estaria’s retaliation. They won’t help directly but can offer information.
  
  > **Dryad speaks:** “Demetria is blinded by sorrow. The loss of her sister to a Dragonlord’s axe poisoned her soul. She seeks vengeance, not justice. Beware—her sorrow is a bottomless pit.”

- **Loreus, the Passionate Satyr:** If the heroes didn’t meet him earlier, they meet him here. He is genuinely concerned for the children and the darkness consuming Demetria. He is drawn to the heroes’ courage and kindness.
  
  > **Loreus speaks:** “I knew her before… before the shadow in her heart became everything she is. What she’s doing in the kitchen… it’s not a banquet. It’s a ritual. Please, stop her.”

- **Potential Companion:** Loreus might offer to join the group. He is a valuable forest guide and knows the temple’s secrets.

### T14 – Culinary Horrors

The smell of roasted meat fills the air. Two **horned goats** serve as cooks. An Investigation check (DC 15) reveals the horrifying “banquet”: the carved bodies of the missing youths (humans) are being prepared for consumption.

### T15 – Banquet Hall & Final Confrontation

Instead of Demetria, the heroes find **Xaricus**, an obese, gluttonous satyr left behind. He sits on an improvised throne, sipping the **Horn of Balmytria**. The surviving children are hidden in a nearby cupboard, terrified; Loreus hid them there in a desperate attempt to save them.

- **Xaricus, the Ruler of Gluttony:** He is a stationary HP pool. He hardly moves but summons lieutenants (1d4 horned goats) and uses fire and confusion spells.

  > **Xaricus mutters between burps:** “Fools! The Mistress no longer needs this place. While you play in the ruins, she marches to claim her true power! The Heart of the Ancient Forest will be the cradle of her vengeance, and Estaria… ah, Estaria will burn!”

- **Horn of Balmytria:** The fight offers a chance to reclaim it.
- **Rescue:** Finding the children alive grants a moment of relief and significant Mercy.

### T16 – Demetria’s Chambers

The rooms are empty yet filled with decadent luxury.

- **Treasure:** A locked chest (DC 15 to open) contains jewellery stolen from the adolescents (worth 500 gp).
- **Lutheria’s Tome:** The most important item is a leather‑bound tome written by Lutheria herself. It details the ritual to turn humans into **Maenads** and men into **Mad Satyrs**, along with necromancy and dream spells. This is a powerful, dangerous artifact—direct hook to future conflicts with Lutheria’s followers.

## Part V: Consequences & Next Steps

### The Horn’s Dream

Drinking the Horn of Balmytria induces an 8‑hour sleep with a vivid dream: the heroes see themselves aboard the **Ultros**, a ghostly ship manned by skeletons, clutching a strange bronze device (the **Antikythera**). This dream is the main hook for the next campaign arc: finding the ghost ship and its cursed captain, Estor Arkelander.

### Demetria’s Tragedy

Demetria is not a simple villain. Her fury stems from the loss of her sister dryad, whose sacred tree was felled by **Estor Arkelander** to craft the Ultros’ mast. Her plan to destroy Estaria is a direct revenge against Estor’s legacy. Her methods mirror the violence Dragonlords inflicted upon native Thylea. She has become the monster they created.

> **Kyrah’s reflection:** “See what Estor’s ‘heroism’ sowed. He seeded hatred in this forest, and now all of us reap the bitter harvest. Will you be any different?”

### Epic Path Hooks

- **The Cursed:** Lutheria’s tome may hold clues on the nature of curses or rituals to break them.
- **The Haunted:** Demetria’s story, a soul tormented by loss, may resonate deeply with the Haunted, perhaps offering insight on dealing with one’s own ghosts.
- **The Vanished:** Mention of Estor Arkelander and the Ultros provides a direct hook for the Vanished’s quest for their past.
