# Act III: Rise of the Hero (Levels 8-12)

## Overview

Act III marks the transformation from street-level investigator to recognized champion. The PC transitions from solving individual crimes to addressing systemic crises threatening Yllmourne's survival. This act features three parallel objectives that converge into a heroic climax:

1. **Medical Crisis**: Find and implement a cure for the mudskipper plague
2. **Political Crisis**: Break the stranglehold between Cyrullon's exploitation and Sefton's blockade
3. **Existential Crisis**: Discover and contain the threat of Sogmonnath beneath the Waterlogged Mountain

The act culminates in the PC earning the title **Talesman of Maal** (level 9 milestone) and discovering that Yllmourne's troubles are orchestrated by extraplanar forces, setting the stage for cosmic confrontation in Act IV.

**Key Themes**: Heroic recognition, political reform, cosmic conspiracy revelation, medical triumph, naval intrigue

**Level Progression**:
- Start: Level 8
- Talesman Recognition: Level 9 (mid-act)
- End: Level 12

## Adapted Adventures

### Adventure 10: The House of Reciprocities

**Level**: 8
**Location**: Yllmourne Archives and Memory Vault (beneath Rotarist Church or ancient civic building)
**Original Concept**: Bearfolk shaman's stolen memories
**Yllmourne Adaptation**: Minqena the librarian or another trusted NPC loses critical memories to a parasitic entity

#### Setup

Minqena (or alternative NPC: dock union organizer, Rotarist priest, or Navula the merchant) approaches the PC in distress. They cannot remember crucial information—perhaps the location of medical texts needed for plague research, testimony against Cyrullon, or warnings about Sogmonnath they investigated years ago.

Investigation reveals their memories weren't simply forgotten but **stolen** by a psychic parasite dwelling in the House of Reciprocities, a metaphysical location tied to Yllmourne's collective memory. This extradimensional pocket exists where the city's forgotten knowledge accumulates.

#### Investigation Phases

**Phase 1: Identifying the Theft** (DC 16 Investigation or Insight)
- Minqena exhibits specific gaps: can describe walking into the archives but not what they found
- Other scholars report similar "blind spots" in their research
- Arcane examination (DC 18 Arcana) reveals psychic scarring consistent with memory extraction

**Phase 2: Locating the House**
- Ancient church records or Thranengi smuggler rumors mention the House of Reciprocities
- Requires ritual to access: collecting three "forgotten things" (abandoned ship's nameplate, unclaimed letter, disowned family heirloom)
- Ritual conducted at threshold location (old courthouse, decommissioned lighthouse, first pier of Yllmourne)

**Phase 3: The House Interior**
- Extradimensional mansion resembling Yllmourne's architecture from different eras
- Rooms shift between maritime guild halls, noble estates, and common homes
- NPC's memories manifested as objects: a book they read, a conversation as echoing voices, a crucial realization as glowing sigil

#### The Memory Thief

**Nothic of Forgotten Lore** (variant nothic with 120 HP, INT 18, psychic resistance)
- Feeds on knowledge rather than flesh
- Can extract memories as action (DC 17 WIS save or lose proficiency in one skill until memory restored)
- Lair actions: rooms rearrange, illusory duplicates of the PC appear speaking forgotten secrets
- Weakness: vulnerable to radiant damage (represents illuminating truth)

**Negotiation Option**: Creature is lonely, hoarding memories for companionship. Persuasion DC 20 or offering it alternative purpose (caretaker of Yllmourne Archives) can resolve peacefully.

#### Resolution

- Recovered memories reveal critical information for one of the parallel objectives:
  - **Plague Cure**: Location of pre-imperial medical texts describing similar outbreaks
  - **Political Reform**: Evidence of Cyrullon's tax fraud or Sefton's false-flag operations
  - **Sogmonnath**: Ancient warnings about seismic activity patterns predicting awakenings

**Reward**: Memory Crystal (rare wondrous item): Once per long rest, perfectly recall any event witnessed in last 30 days.

### Adventure 11: Among the Shades and Shadows

**Level**: 9
**Location**: The Drowned Quarter (submerged district) and spectral ship graveyard
**Original Concept**: Stygian shade seeking identity
**Yllmourne Adaptation**: Ghosts of sailors murdered in false-flag sinking reveal Thranengi/Sefton conspiracy

#### Setup

Ships continue sinking despite increased patrols. The PC discovers these aren't accidents or Thranengi raids—they're **orchestrated murders** designed to justify Sefton's blockade while generating salvage for corrupt officials.

Spectral sailors begin manifesting in the Drowned Quarter (partially flooded slum). Unlike typical undead, these shades retain intelligence and desperation to reveal their killers.

#### Investigation Structure

**Phase 1: Haunting Pattern**
- Shades appear at locations corresponding to where their ships sank
- Each ghost can communicate one fragmented message before fading (recharge at dawn)
- Messages seem contradictory: "Sefton guards attacked us" vs. "Thranengi wore Sefton colors" vs. "Our own captain betrayed us"

**Phase 2: The Drowned Quarter Descent**
- PC must dive to sunken ships using water-breathing magic or equipment
- Underwater hazards: swarms of **spectral mudskippers** (treat as shadow swarms), collapsing structures, currents
- Each ship's captain's quarters contains logbook revealing piece of conspiracy

**Phase 3: The Shade Council**
- Seven primary ghosts (one per major sinking) convene at midnight during new moon
- They manifest fully for one hour, creating **shadow court** in flooded warehouse
- PC serves as advocate, helping shades piece together their fragmented memories

#### The Conspiracy Revealed

Cross-referencing ghostly testimonies with logbooks unveils:
1. **Thranengi mercenaries** hired by intermediaries (traced to shell companies)
2. **Sefton officers** providing patrol schedules and blind spots
3. **Cyrullon dock inspectors** falsifying cargo manifests to inflate insurance claims
4. **Mastermind**: Kaelen Vross (introduced in Act II) coordinating from shadows, but receiving orders from **extraplanar contact** (first hint of Naran/Thellos involvement)

**Combat Encounter**: Kaelen's Cleanup Crew
- Kaelen Vross (CR 8 assassin with shadow magic)
- 2x Corrupted Sefton Guards (CR 5 veterans with necrotic weapons)
- 1x Bound Wraith (controlled by Kaelen, CR 5)
- Environment: Flooded warehouse with partial cover, water hazards (DC 15 Athletics to move through difficult terrain)

#### Resolution Options

**Confrontation Path**: Defeat Kaelen in combat; he reveals extraplanar masters before dying/escaping
**Legal Path**: Present evidence to honest Sefton commander (Captain Thorne, if still alive) or Onotanie magistrate
**Spectral Path**: Empower shades to drag Kaelen into afterlife (requires DC 18 Religion check to perform binding ritual)

**Consequences**:
- Sefton's blockade justification collapses (if evidence made public)
- Cyrullon's insurance scheme exposed (if logbooks delivered to authorities)
- First concrete proof that surface politics are manipulated by planar forces

**Reward**: Shroud of the Drowned (very rare cloak): Grants advantage on Stealth in dim light/darkness, once per long rest become incorporeal for 1 minute.

### Adventure 12: The Time-Twister's Daughter

**Level**: 10
**Location**: Waterlogged Mountain temporal anomaly zones
**Original Concept**: Temporal vault in Oshragora
**Yllmourne Adaptation**: Time fractures caused by Sogmonnath's stirring, featuring lost expeditions from different eras

#### Setup

Earthquakes intensify around the Waterlogged Mountain. Miners report "ghost crews" working veins already exhausted, tools appearing and vanishing, and workers aging rapidly or reverting to children.

Investigation reveals temporal fractures forming concentric rings around Sogmonnath's prison. These aren't natural—they're sympathetic reactions to the entity's awakening consciousness distorting local time.

#### Temporal Zones

**Outer Ring (1-2 years displacement)**
- Miners encounter their future/past selves (causes confusion, potential paradoxes)
- Tools left behind appear decades rusted or brand-new
- DC 14 Investigation to navigate without temporal disorientation (disadvantage on attacks/checks for 1 hour on failure)

**Middle Ring (10-50 years displacement)**
- PC encounters **lost expedition from 30 years ago**, still believing it's their present
- This group includes younger version of current Yllmourne NPC (perhaps Minqena's mentor, Navula's predecessor, or Cy's parent)
- They possess warnings about Sogmonnath written in journal—but in their timeline, Yllmourne already fell

**Inner Ring (Centuries displacement)**
- Pre-imperial Yllmourne visible as ghostly overlay
- Ancient Lhodan priests attempting original sealing of Sogmonnath
- Observing (not interfering) reveals ritual components: Donsax (Red Menza's saber), divine mandate of Law, willing sacrifice

#### The Time-Twister's Daughter

In the deepest fracture, the PC encounters **Erythria**, a chronomancer's daughter trapped in temporal loop. She's not native to Yllmourne but was imprisoned here by Sogmonnath's influence when investigating the entity decades ago.

**Erythria's Deal**:
- She can stabilize the fractures temporarily (buying weeks before Sogmonnath fully awakens)
- Price: PC must retrieve her father's **Temporal Anchor** from her home timeline (requires brief planar jaunt)
- Alternative: Convince her to sacrifice her existence to permanently seal this fracture (DC 22 Persuasion, grants +2 bonus to final sealing ritual in Act IV)

**Combat/Challenge**: Paradox Elementals
- 3x Temporal Anomalies (use air elemental stats but attacks cause aging: DC 16 CON save or gain 1 exhaustion)
- Battlefield shifts between past/present/future states every round (changes terrain, cover, hazards)

#### Resolution

**Stabilization Outcome**: Fractures sealed for now, but seismic activity continues
**Knowledge Gained**:
- Sogmonnath's nature: ancient entity of entropy and stagnation, antithesis to Law
- Sealing requirements: Donsax, divine authority, specific ritual timing (during conjunction)
- Ominous warning: "Those who desire chaos already work to free it"—connecting to Naran/Thellos conspiracy

**Reward**: Fragment of Erythria's Hourglass (rare wondrous item): Once per long rest, reroll initiative and take new result, or impose disadvantage on enemy's saving throw by showing them their failure.

## Original Content: Sacred Medical Ritual

**Level**: 9-10
**Objective**: Discover and implement the mudskipper plague cure
**Structure**: Three-part quest requiring coastal exploration, monster combat, and ritual performance

### Part 1: The Healer's Research

Minqena (post-memory restoration) or Rotarist Church healer identifies three components needed for ancient curative ritual:

1. **Deepmist Kelp**: Grows only in cave systems accessible during lowest monthly tide
2. **Pristine Mudskipper Ichor**: From mudskippers *not* infected by plague (nearly extinct locally)
3. **Blessing of Flowing Waters**: Divine invocation requiring sacred site

### Part 2: Coastal Expedition

**The Kelp Caves** (Skill Challenge + Combat)
- Accessible 4-hour window during spring tide
- Navigating caves: DC 15 Survival to find kelp groves, DC 18 Athletics to avoid riptides
- Guardians: **Giant Mudskipper Alpha** (CR 6 behir variant with amphibious trait) protecting territory
- Environmental hazards: rising water (chase/escape element), bioluminescent jellyfish swarms (poison damage)

**Pristine Mudskipper Hunt**
- Rumors place uninfected population on isolated atoll claimed by Thranengi
- Options: negotiate with Thranengi tribe (DC 18 Persuasion, offering trade goods or military aid), stealth mission (series of DC 16-20 Stealth checks), or ecological restoration (purify local habitat first with lesser restoration cast at 5th level or higher)

**Sacred Site Pilgrimage**
- Pre-imperial shrine to water deity (compatible with Maal's Law domain)
- Requires purification ritual: defeating **corrupted water elemental** (CR 7) tainted by Sogmonnath's influence
- Blessing granted through PC's connection to Maal (automatic if good standing, DC 16 Religion check otherwise)

### Part 3: The Ritual of Cleansing

Performing the cure requires:
- **Location**: Yllmourne's central cistern or public fountain
- **Duration**: 8-hour ritual (vulnerable to interruption)
- **Complication**: Kaelen Vross (if still active) or Cyrullon saboteurs attempt to stop it (waves of CR 2-4 enemies every hour)
- **Skill Checks**: DC 18 Medicine to properly prepare components, DC 16 Religion to maintain divine focus, DC 14 Arcana to stabilize alchemical reactions

**Success**: Plague cure distributed through water system, infections cease within 1 week, death rate plummets

**Fame**: PC recognized as city savior, gains **Advantage on Charisma checks** with Yllmourne citizens for remainder of campaign

## Original Content: Night of the Blockade

**Level**: 11
**Objective**: Break Sefton's maritime stranglehold through bold action
**Structure**: Multi-path climax with naval, diplomatic, and covert options

### The Situation

With plague conspiracy exposed (from Adventure 11), Sefton's blockade loses political cover but Commander Dreshka refuses to lift it without "assurances" (i.e., control over Yllmourne). Onotanie remains neutral, unwilling to risk war with Sefton.

The PC must choose approach:

### Path 1: Naval Defiance

Organize coalition of merchant ships, Thranengi allies, and commandeered vessels to **force the blockade**.

**Preparation** (1 week):
- Recruit ships: DC 18 Persuasion with merchants (offering profit share), DC 16 with Thranengi (honor-based appeal)
- Acquire naval combat supplies: cannons, alchemical fire, reinforced hulls
- Train crew: Investing 500gp and passing DC 15 group Athletics check grants +2 to naval combat rolls

**The Blockade Run** (Naval Combat)
- Use DMG naval combat rules or simplified skill challenge
- PC's fleet (6 ships) vs. Sefton blockade (10 ships, but half are undermanned)
- Goal: Break through to open sea and return, proving blockade ineffective
- Key rolls:
  - Navigation: DC 18 Survival (PC or trusted NPC)
  - Combat tactics: DC 16 Intelligence (tactical advice)
  - Morale: DC 14 Charisma (inspiring speech during battle)

**Complications**:
- Sefton **sea serpent** asset (CR 8 plesiosaurus variant with breath weapon)
- Storm arrives mid-battle (DC 15 Weather Sense to anticipate and use to advantage)
- Potential friendly casualties create moral weight

### Path 2: Legal Audacity

Use evidence from Adventure 11 to prosecute Sefton in Onotanie courts or international maritime tribunal.

**Preparation**:
- Compile evidence: Ghostly testimonies (DC 16 Persuasion to convince court they're admissible), logbooks, financial records
- Recruit legal advocate: Onotanie lawyer costs 1,000gp but provides +3 to Persuasion checks
- Character witnesses: Minqena, reformed Cyrullon official, Thranengi elder

**The Trial** (Roleplay/Skill Challenge)
- Opening statement: DC 18 Persuasion (disadvantage if PC lacks formal legal training)
- Evidence presentation: DC 16 Investigation (demonstrating chain of conspiracy)
- Cross-examination: Opposed Insight vs. Deception against Sefton's lawyer (NPC has +7)
- Closing argument: DC 20 Persuasion (advantage if PC made emotional connection to victims)

**Outcome**:
- Success: Onotanie rules blockade illegal, Sefton forced to withdraw
- Partial success: Blockade reduced but not eliminated, Sefton pays reparations
- Failure: Case dismissed, but evidence leaked to public creates political pressure

### Path 3: Thranengi Alliance

Coordinate with Thranengi tribes to systematically hunt Sefton vessels, making blockade unsustainable.

**Preparation**:
- Negotiate alliance: DC 20 Persuasion (DC 16 if PC previously aided Thranengi)
- Coordinate tactics: DC 17 Intelligence (guerrilla naval warfare planning)
- Ensure restraint: DC 18 Insight (preventing Thranengi from excessive violence that'd justify Sefton retaliation)

**The Hunt** (Series of combat encounters over 2 weeks)
- Hit-and-run attacks on isolated Sefton ships
- Sabotage operations: destroying supply depots on islands
- Psychological warfare: ghost ship illusions, spreading rumors of sea monsters

**Key Encounter**: **Captain Dreshka's Flagship**
- Dreshka (CR 9 veteran with spellcasting: command, hold person, dispel magic)
- Elite marine squad (4x CR 5 veterans)
- Ship-mounted ballista (treat as +10 attack, 4d10 piercing)

**Climax**: PC must choose—capture Dreshka for leverage, sink flagship as statement, or negotiate conditional surrender

### Resolution

Regardless of path, breaking the blockade has consequences:

**Success Outcomes**:
- Yllmourne gains de facto independence, celebrated as hero
- Trade resumes, economy recovers
- PC earns favor with Onotanie (future ally) or Thranengi (cultural honor)

**Complications**:
- Sefton retreats but plans revenge (plot hook for Act IV)
- Cyrullon, now primary exploiter, increases pressure
- Extraplanar manipulators shift tactics (Naran/Thellos agents go underground)

**Talesman Recognition**: If PC achieved this *and* cured plague, spontaneous public celebration leads to formal recognition (see below).

## Original Content: Discovering Sogmonnath

**Level**: 11-12
**Objective**: Investigate Waterlogged Mountain, confirm ancient threat, identify connection to planar conspiracy
**Structure**: Dungeon descent with investigation, combat, and cosmic horror revelation

### The Hook

Following Adventure 12's temporal anomalies, earthquakes worsen. Tunnel collapses kill dozens. Survivors report:
- Voices speaking in dead languages
- Gravity reversals in deepest shafts
- Workers entering trance and digging toward "the Sleeper"

Rotarist Church or independent scholars confirm: something beneath Waterlogged Mountain is **awakening**.

### The Descent

**Level 1: Active Mines** (Investigation)
- Interview traumatized miners (DC 14 Medicine to treat shock before questioning)
- Examine collapse patterns (DC 17 Investigation): deliberate, creating spiral path downward
- Cultist presence: 6x CR 2 cultists (former miners) attempt to stop PC, chanting "The Stillness rises"

**Level 2: Abandoned Imperial Works**
- Ancient Lhodan mining operation from centuries ago, preserved in stable pocket
- Frescoes depict entity: **massive amphibious creature with properties of both matter and void**
- Research notes (Draconic): "Sealed by Law Bearers, Red Menza's blade the key, must never rust, must never wake"
- Trap: Pressure plate releases poisonous gas (DC 16 Perception to notice, DC 18 Thieves' Tools to disable, 6d6 poison damage on failure)

**Level 3: The Sealing Chamber**
- Massive circular room with seven pillars inscribed with divine law
- Central dais holds empty **sword stand** (Donsax should rest here but was claimed by Red Menza decades ago)
- Without Donsax, seals weaken visibly: cracks spreading, runes dimming

**The Vision**: Touching dais triggers psychic impression
- PC experiences memory of original sealing: ancient priests of Law binding Sogmonnath
- Entity's nature revealed: **Incarnation of Stagnation**, antithesis to progress and change
- If freed, would trap Yllmourne (potentially all of Lhodos) in eternal, decaying "moment"

### The Prison Wall

Descending further (DC 15 Athletics, rope and pitons required), PC reaches:

**Sogmonnath's Prison**: Translucent barrier of solidified Law separating mortal realm from entity's domain
- Through barrier: writhing mass of flesh/stone/water in impossible geometry
- Cannot cross barrier yet, but **tendrils of influence** can

**Combat**: Manifestations of Stagnation
- 2x **Entropy Oozes** (black pudding stats but radiant vulnerability, attacks impose slow effect)
- 1x **Temporal Echo of Sogmonnath** (CR 9 aboleth variant, psychic attacks cause time dilation: victim loses next turn on failed DC 17 WIS save)

### The Conspiracy Connection

Investigating chamber reveals:
- **Recent excavation tools** (within last month)
- **Planar residue** (DC 18 Arcana): signature matches infernal or shadowfell magic
- **Written orders** in encoded script (DC 19 Investigation to decode): "Prepare foundation. Master requires avenue of awakening. Donsax will be delivered when alignment occurs."

PC realizes: Naran/Thellos agents have been **actively trying to free Sogmonnath** as part of larger planar conspiracy.

### Immediate Action

PC must stabilize seals temporarily:
- Cast *glyph of warding* or similar at each pillar (consumes spell slots/components)
- Channel divine energy if Maal cleric/paladin (expend 3 uses of Channel Divinity)
- Ritual requiring DC 20 Religion check and 1,000gp in material components

**Success**: Buys 1-3 months before catastrophic failure
**Failure**: Buys 2-4 weeks; earthquakes worsen immediately

### Return to Surface

PC emerges with critical intelligence:
1. Yllmourne sits atop cosmic threat
2. Donsax (Red Menza's saber) is KEY to permanent sealing
3. Planar conspirators want Sogmonnath freed
4. City has weeks, not years, to act

This discovery **forces Act IV**: PC must escalate to divine/planar authorities, as mortal solutions are insufficient.

## Level 9 Milestone: Talesman of Maal

Upon completing two of three major achievements (plague cure, blockade broken, Sogmonnath discovered), PC is formally recognized.

### The Ceremony

**Location**: Largest public square or Rotarist Church
**Attendees**: Hundreds of citizens, Navula, Minqena, reformed Cyrullon officials, Thranengi representatives

**Navula's Speech** (or senior Church official):
> "In times of shadow, Law seeks a voice. In times of plague, Mercy seeks hands to heal. In times of tyranny, Justice seeks a champion. [PC name] has been all three. We name you **Talesman of Maal**, one who speaks Law into being through action."

### Mechanical Benefits

**Talesman of Maal** (supernatural boon):
- **Authority of Law**: Once per long rest, cast *zone of truth* or *calm emotions* without components
- **Voice of the People**: Advantage on Persuasion checks when advocating for common folk or arguing legal/ethical principles
- **Maal's Regard**: If PC dies in service of Law or Justice, Maal intervenes (DM discretion: automatic *revivify* effect or divine quest to return)

### Social Benefits

- **Fame**: Known throughout Yllmourne and spreading to Onotanie/Sefton
- **Resources**: Citizens donate; PC gains 5,000gp in gifts/funds for "the cause"
- **Allies**: Can call upon militia (20x CR 1/2 guards), merchant coalition, or Thranengi war party once per act

## Political Arc Resolution

Act III concludes with Yllmourne's political landscape transformed:

### Cyrullon's Fate

**Defeated**: If evidence from Adventures 10-11 used, Cy flees or is arrested. Power vacuum creates chaos—PC must help establish interim governance.

**Reformed**: If PC offered Cy redemption arc, he becomes reluctant ally, using exploitation networks for legitimate trade. Uneasy partnership.

**Escaped**: Cy goes underground, resurfaces in Act IV as infernal court witness or corrupted agent.

### Sefton's Position

**Weakened**: Blockade broken, Dreshka disgraced. Sefton withdraws but plots long-term revenge.

**Negotiated**: Compromise reached—Sefton gets trade concessions but recognizes Yllmourne autonomy.

**Hostile**: If PC used extreme violence, Sefton declares PC outlaw, complicating planar travel in Act IV.

### The Union Rises

Dock workers, emboldened by PC's advocacy, form **Yllmourne Free Workers' Coalition**. This becomes proto-democratic institution:

- Elects representatives from each district
- Negotiates with merchants/nobles as unified voice
- In Act IV, serves as governing body when PC travels to planar courts

### Improvised Election

PC can facilitate Yllmourne's first mayoral election (optional):

**Candidates**:
- Navula (merchant perspective, stability)
- Reformed Cyrullon lieutenant (pragmatic, knows system)
- Rotarist Church candidate (theocratic leanings)
- PC (if interested in governance)

**Outcome**: Depends on PC's campaign support (series of Persuasion checks, public debates, reputation). Winner shapes city's direction in epilogue.

## Act III Climax: Unmasking the Architect

**Level**: 12
**Setup**: With surface-level threats addressed (plague cured, blockade broken, Sogmonnath identified), the true mastermind reveals themselves.

### The Confrontation

**Location**: Kaelen Vross's secret sanctum (if he survived earlier encounters) or Waterlogged Mountain sealing chamber

**The Revelation**:
A planar agent—**Advocate Naran** (infernal lawyer) or **Shadow of Thellos** (avatar of darkness god)—appears, congratulating PC on "preparing the stage."

**Their Monologue**:
> "Did you think plague and politics were random? Sogmonnath's stirring coincidence? Yllmourne is *plaintiff and evidence* in a trial that will reshape divine law. Your heroism makes the case... compelling."

**They explain**:
- Yllmourne chosen as test case for infernal precedent: "Cities built on injustice belong to Shadow"
- PC's reforms are *problem*—they prove Law can triumph, weakening infernal claim
- Sogmonnath's release is Plan B: if legal case fails, destroy evidence (city itself)
- PC must choose: accept offer to "join prosecution" (betray Maal for power), or fight

### Combat: The Advocate's Departure

The agent doesn't fight directly (CR too high for level 12), but leaves enforcers:

- **2x Chain Devils** (CR 8 each, infernal contract enforcers)
- **1x Shadow Demon** (CR 4, Thellos's spy)
- **Environmental hazard**: Room collapsing/planar instability (10d6 force damage per round if not in safe zones, DC 16 Dexterity)

**Objective**: Survive and force agent's retreat, not kill them (they're Act IV's final boss)

### The Offer

Before vanishing, agent presents **contract**:
- Sign, and PC gains epic-tier power NOW (boon of high magic, legendary item)
- Price: Testify *against* Yllmourne in divine court (Act IV becomes prosecution witness role)
- Refusal: "See you at trial, Talesman. Bring your evidence. We'll bring ours."

**If PC signs**: Campaign takes dark turn—Act IV becomes redemption arc or villain protagonist path

## Transition to Act IV

Act III ends with PC understanding:

1. **Yllmourne is pawn** in cosmic legal battle over divine law
2. **Earthly victories mean nothing** if infernal court claims jurisdiction
3. **Sogmonnath's fate** tied to trial outcome—freed if city "belongs" to Shadow
4. **PC must go to the source**: appeal directly to Maal's High Tribunal

**Navula's Final Counsel**:
> "You've saved Yllmourne from itself. Now you must save it from the heavens. Seek the Celestial Courts. Make our case to the divine. Prove that Law, not Shadow, defines our fate."

**Cliffhanger**: PC receives **Summons to Tribunal** (glowing scroll appearing via celestial courier):
> "The matter of *Yllmourne v. The Infernal Compact* will be heard in the Court of Eternal Law. You are called to testify. Failure to appear constitutes forfeiture."

**Next Session**: Act IV begins with planar travel preparation.

## DM Tips for Act III

### Pacing the Parallel Objectives

Don't force linear progression. Let PC choose which crisis to tackle first:
- **Plague urgency**: Citizens dying daily creates time pressure
- **Blockade pressure**: Economic collapse is slower but steady
- **Sogmonnath mystery**: Earthquakes escalate gradually

Use **clocks** (Blades in the Dark style): Track "weeks until plague becomes fatal," "blockade chokehold tightens," "Sogmonnath awakens." PC's choices advance/delay different clocks.

### Making Politics Personal

Tie each faction to NPC relationships:
- Cyrullon's fall affects his family (sympathetic niece who helped PC earlier)
- Sefton blockade starves sailor PC befriended in Act I
- Union organizer is dockworker whose child PC saved from plague

### Foreshadowing Act IV

Drop hints throughout:
- Kaelen's notes mention "Masters beyond the Veil"
- Temporal fractures show alternate timeline where infernal court won
- Ghostly sailors whisper: "They promised us justice in the courts beyond death—lies"

### Rewarding Heroism

Act III should *feel* triumphant despite cosmic stakes:
- Public celebrations, monuments in PC's honor
- Citizens naming children after PC
- Former enemies (reformed Cy, pardoned cultist) express gratitude
- Yllmourne transforms visibly: cleaner, safer, hopeful

This makes Act IV's stakes (defending this hard-won peace) emotionally resonant.

### Balancing Combat and Investigation

Ratio target: 40% combat, 40% investigation/social, 20% exploration

If PC prefers one pillar:
- Combat-focused: Add more cult/saboteur encounters
- Investigation-focused: Expand conspiracy web, more clues to decode
- Social-focused: Emphasize union organizing, election campaign mechanics

### The Sogmonnath Revelation

This is campaign's horror moment. Emphasize:
- **Alien nature**: Descriptions defy geometry, language inadequate
- **Existential dread**: Entity doesn't hate mortals—it's indifferent, which is worse
- **Cosmic scale**: Yllmourne's problems are *microscopic* compared to this threat

Use Sanity rules (optional): First encounter with Sogmonnath manifestation requires DC 15 Wisdom save or gain indefinite madness.

## Conclusion

Act III transforms the PC from street investigator to recognized champion, but reveals that heroism alone is insufficient. Mortal reforms can't protect against cosmic legal battles. The stage is set for Act IV's planar journey, divine litigation, and final confrontation with the conspiracy's architects.

**Key Emotional Beats**:
- Pride (Talesman recognition)
- Horror (Sogmonnath's nature)
- Determination (facing planar conspiracy)
- Hope (Yllmourne transformed by PC's efforts)

**Setup for Act IV**:
- Donsax must be retrieved (from Red Menza or successor)
- Divine court journey requires planar travel
- Infernal prosecutors already preparing their case
- Yllmourne's fate—and divine law itself—hangs in balance
