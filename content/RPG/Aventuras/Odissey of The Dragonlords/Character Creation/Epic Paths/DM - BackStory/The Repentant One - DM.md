# The Repentant One

You were once a powerful and proud sphinx. Your mind was like a maze, and you were known for your keen intellect, cutting wit, and your… mercurial nature. You hoarded great treasure, grave secrets, and possessed immense magical prowess, rarely sharing either of the former with those who dared encroach on your sacred isolation, but readily using the latter to dispose of the interlopers, cursing or slaying them with great impunity as you pleased.

For your actions, you were banished from the homeland of the sphinxes, and settled in mainland Thylea, where more and more mortals came before you, and all met grisly fates. Your actions and fate amused the Lady of Dreams, who hoped she had found a kindred spirit. She decided to test you as one of her practical jokes. In her mortal guise she vexed you beyond belief, but when you went to curse her, she revealed herself and in her fury at your lack of ‘a sense of humour’ she stripped away your powers and rendered you a mere mortal - forced to walk the earth and bear witness to your crimes.

Now, awash with terrible humility and regret, you must attempt to rebuild your strength and undo what your pride has wrought.

**For players who love:** Circe, Prince Zuko  
**Restrictions:** You must be a from Thylea  
**Adventure Hook:** The Oracle claims to know how you can find absolution

## Heroic Tasks

* Obtain forgiveness
* Find out if you can return to your true form
* Reclaim your most prized possessions. Pick one lesser and one greater magic item from this list:

    * *(lesser) Helm of Comprehending Languages*
    * *(lesser) Ring of Puzzler’s Wit*
    * *(lesser) Ring of Mind Shielding*
    * *(greater) Crystal Ball of Mind Reading*
    * *(greater) Rod of Alertness*
    * *(greater) Wand of Polymorph*

## Divine Blessing
*Blessing of the Sphinx:* You gain immunity to psychic damage, the charmed and frightened conditions, as well as any divination spell that you do not wish to be affected by.

# Integrating the Repentant One

## Meeting the Oracle
**Versi** will tell the Repentant One that there is another of their kind that has been banished from the Island of Time, the sphinx homeland, for also abusing their station and powers, and that they too have wantonly used their powers to curse people, including the entirety of Scorpion Island. Versi claims that while it may not be possible to undo all the damage that the Repentant One did as a sphinx, undoing this curse could help them find absolution.

> “The Merciless Furies alone know the harm you have wrought on mortals seeking their own destinies, o’ Repentant One. Exiled, transfigured, and brought low, you have still found your way here, on a path towards absolution. This desire for forgiveness marks you as different from another of your kind, also banished from your homeland. Beneath the stars of the mighty scorpion, she has worked a wicked curse. Undoing it will lead you down the path towards healing your wounded soul, if not undoing all the grief and strife you have rendered unto Thylea’s children.”

## Victims of the Sphinx
After the first or second of the Great Labours, rumours of the Repentant One’s presence in **Estoria** lead many to make their way to the city. These people are former victims of the Repentant One, and are furious with them, and confused that their tormentor is meant to be one of the so-called ‘Heroes of Prophecy.’

Ask the Repentant One’s player how they typically cursed people when they were a sphinx. Examples could include being transformed into creatures such as talking animals, or animal headed beings, made horrendously ugly, or otherwise transfigured.

A *Remove Curse* spell, or other effect could alleviate some of their symptoms, but the true purpose of this encounter is for the Repentant One to try to appease their victims, who seek oaths sworn before the merciless Furies that the Repentant One will do better in the future, and will help people instead of harming them for their own amusement.

If the other PCs stand up for the Repentant One, especially if the Oath of Fellowship has been sworn, the victims of the sphinx will seem impressed that anyone is willing to side with the Repentant One, and may be more lenient with them.

## Chondrus the Arcane Master
While in **Mytros**, the Repentant One is approached by **Chondrus** (likely after the storm is dealt with). He claims to have a gift from his mistress, who is an admirer of ‘their work.’ If the party does not yet know what the Repentant One has done, he shares all the grisly details before giving them their lesser artifact.

## Lutheria’s Dream
At some point, **Lutheria** will torment the Repentant One with horrible nightmares of their victims' fates, from being slain to transfigured or worse. She presents them as having been amusing to her and wonders if the Repentant One wouldn’t be happier returning to the way things used to be rather than trying to do good and ‘right’ by their victims.

## The Wisdom of Vallus
**Vallus** believes that the Lord of Time, the androsphinx who rules the **Island of Time**, has reclaimed the Repentant One’s greater artifact, and that they must find absolution before he would return it to them. Beyond this, she has heard that the Repentant One seeks to travel to **Scorpion Island**, and relays to them which constellation it is under if they do not already know that information.

## Scorpion Island
On **Scorpion Island**, the Repentant One can speak to **Nessa** (or **Pholon**, if he hasn’t been met yet), who will relay to them the nature of the curse on the island. They desire new riders and could bond with the Repentant One. They are in awe of the idea of the curse being broken. They offer to guide the party to the **Tower of the Lotus Witch**.

## Meeting the Lotus Witch
The **Lotus Witch** welcomes her ‘cursed and wretched cousin’ to the Tower, and asks what they think of her ‘work’ with the centaurs and the children in the tower. She explains why she cursed them all, feeling justified at the ancient centaurs actions and the attempts of ‘foolish mortals’ to stop her since.

If asked, the **Lotus Witch** relays that the curse on the Repentant One has wiped away what they once were and she does not believe it possible for them to reclaim their true form as a sphinx, but she does believe that their former powers are within reach.

Convincing the **Lotus Witch** that she is wrong will be a difficult process and should require some keen roleplaying and possibly some difficult checks. Only if she sees the folly of her actions will she break the curse, otherwise she will have to be slain to end its effects.

## The Roar
After breaking the curse on **Scorpion Island**, a great and terrible roar echoes across the seas of Thylea. The Repentant One knows that this is a summons from the Lord of the **Island of Time** and that they now have the chance to return after their exile. They also know they will be tested and should only go to the **Island of Time** once they feel ready to confront their Lord. You may wish to have this information come to the Repentant One as something akin to their original mind sharing knowledge with them or them remembering an obscure fact.

## The Island of Time
If the party attempts to approach the **Island of Time** before dealing with the curse on **Scorpion Island**, they find themselves unable to find the island. **Kyrah** or another NPC (or means of divination) recommend handling other business before approaching.

Otherwise, the party arrives at the Repentant One’s homeland. Scenes of familiarity come to the Repentant One, who may guide the party through the island. They intrinsically understand the system of the roars being a timer.

The **Androsphinx** presents the following simple riddle to the Repentant One:

> “It may be given - not taken or bought. What the sinner desires the saint may not. Of what do I speak?”

The answer is *repentance, absolution,* or *forgiveness.* Answers such as *mercy* or *understanding* are accepted but the **Androsphinx** frowns slightly at them.

Optionally, if you desire a boss battle, the **Androsphinx** then states they shall be tried by combat to see if they are worthy of returning to the sphinx’s fold.

Regardless, if all is done properly, the **Androsphinx** smiles and at the resulting feast, presents the Repentant One with their greater artifact. He sadly states that it is likely that their true form is lost to them, or it is at least beyond his power to restore, but they are welcome to rejoin their people on the island whenever they please. The joyous roars of other sphinxes echo this sentiment.

At this point, the Repentant One should obtain their Divine Blessing, granting them a measure of their sphinx powers back.

## Confronting Lutheria
**Lutheria** bemoans that the Repentant One has become such a dreadful bore. Perhaps some jokes will make them more at ease? No? What if she offers to restore them to their true form? If the Repentant One agrees to swear a permanent Oath of Service to her, they become an NPC sphinx under the DMs control, and join **Lutheria** against the party (if the Repentant One has sworn an Oath of Fellowship, this breaks it). Otherwise, **Lutheria** is offended and seems almost sympathetic towards the Repentant One, lamenting that ‘these heroes’ have made such a goodie-goodie out of someone she once felt kinship to.

## Divine Boon
If the Repentant One dies, their past self emerges from the weave of time, appearing as a great and powerful sphinx. They cast the *True Resurrection* spell on their present day self, restoring them to life, before returning to their own time. This can only happen once.
