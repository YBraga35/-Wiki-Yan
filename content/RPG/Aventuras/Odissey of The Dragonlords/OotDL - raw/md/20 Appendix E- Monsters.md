# Appendix E: Monsters

This appendix details monsters and nonplayer characters that appear in this book. Most of the monsters in Thylea are inspired by Greek mythology. Many of the most common fantasy monsters (orcs, goblins, etc.) are not present in the Forgotten Land.

When adding monsters to the world of Thylea, you should restrict your choices to this list:
