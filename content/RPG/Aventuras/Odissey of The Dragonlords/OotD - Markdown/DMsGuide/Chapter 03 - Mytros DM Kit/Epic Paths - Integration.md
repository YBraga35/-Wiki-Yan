# Epic Paths – Integration in Mytros (Ch. 3)

How each Path can shine in Mytros, with triggers, anchor NPCs, scenes, and outcomes.

## Demigod (Pythor)
- Triggers: performance in the Games (public trials); invitations from Vallus; attention from Pythor/Volkan.
- Scenes: Arena/Spectacles victories; Science Fair (Volkan judge) with usable inventions; talk with Vallus about “public example.”
- Outcomes: massive recognition; bridge to Forge and crafting; NOTE: “A Night to Remember” only at the end of Ch. 3 to free Pythor from the Throne so he can sail with the Ultros.

## Exiled (Themis)
- Triggers: Horus’ approach; Lady of Coins at the Colossus; Amazonians’ arrival at the Games.
- Scenes: Sacred Hospitality; confession/alliance with Moxena; dilemmas with contracts; honor on the field (Amazonians).
- Outcomes: power base in Mytros (resources/espionage); reputation for the return to Themis.

## Vanished One
- Triggers: Versi as arbiter of the Games; Academy invitations; attention from court.
- Scenes: Debate finals; public display of virtues; street scenes (fans/adulators) and private reflection.
- Outcomes: social legitimacy; reinforced ties with Versi for future chapters (Telamok outside Mytros).

## Cursed / Lutheria‑linked
- Triggers: Sewers (Ghoul King); Theater/Wine Festival; Chondrus’ approaches.
- Scenes: moral choices (Nest of the Forgotten); temptations at Celestials; palace vault investigation.
- Outcomes: public redemption or vice; marked enemies in the underworld.

## Gifted / Academic
- Triggers: Akimedes (dean), Orena (Orrery), invitations to Science Fairs and lectures.
- Scenes: creative projects; reading (languages/tools/lore); workshops/talks.
- Outcomes: prizes (tome of learning), expedition invitations, Orena as navigator hire for the Ultros.

## Haunted
- Triggers: cold interest from Scions (Goloron); Stygian Row whispers; theater.
- Scenes: omens/apparitions in social events; honor duels in arena.
- Outcomes: public catharsis; memorable duels; fame as a “dark hero.”

## General Tips
- Guarantee 1–2 scenes per week directly touching each Path.
- Use crowds/murals to reinforce public consequences.
- Tie rewards (boons, tomes, contacts) to deeds that advance the Path.
