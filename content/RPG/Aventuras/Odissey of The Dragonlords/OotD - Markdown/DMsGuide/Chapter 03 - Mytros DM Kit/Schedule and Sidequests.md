# Schedule and Sidequests – Ch. 3 Mytros

Simple daily structure to run Games + socials, and the key sidequests with objectives, triggers, and outcomes.

## Daily grid (7 days of Games as a base)
- Morning: Preliminaries (no magic; bracers). Storytelling (Tales of Victory) for gold/thematic inspiration.
- Midday/Afternoon: Arena (Grand Melee/Chariots/King of the Mountain/Beasts) and/or Individuals (archery, equestrian, gauntlet, sisyphus ascension, beast breaking).
- Day’s end: Grand Spectacle/Relay (CrashGem or Great Relay). Mystic Coach and crowd can grant advantage (DC 14) with narration.
- Night: Celestial Celebrations (Kylix Krown, Dramatis Personae, Riddle Roundtable, Bladder Ball). Intrigue and betting.

Suggestion: interleave 1 pure urban day (Sewers/Colossus/Intrigue) every 2 Games days.

## Sidequests

### Sewers of Mytros
- Objectives: remove/negotiate with the Ghoul King; clear the elemental blockade (water weird + mephits); discover and aid the Nest of the Forgotten; expose Ismene (if applicable).
- Triggers: Vallus’ request; rumors of disappearances in Stygian Row; patrol clues; smell/floods in the low city.
- Allies/Enemies: Nest (potential allies), ghouls and ghast, elementals/mephits, Neurdagon (double‑edged politics).
- Outcomes: restored flow; covert access to the Colossus; amulet vs. detection; social reputation (compassion/justice) and possible boon from Vallus.
- Reference: `03.07 - Sewers of Mytros.md`.

### Colossus of Pythor (Lady of Coins)
- Objectives: contact under Sacred Hospitality; weigh alliance/break; obtain intel/resources; avoid public exposure.
- Triggers: Horus’ invitation; sewers route; delegations whisper about “the Lady” above the city.
- Allies/Enemies: Moxena, Horus; Order of Sydon/guards (if exposed); blackmail victims.
- Outcomes: useful alliance (intel, smuggling, protection) at political/moral cost; strong enmity if betrayed; hooks for the Exiled path.
- Reference: `Original/03 - Summoned by the King.md` (Colossus), your Moxena scenes.

### Palace and Street Intrigue
- Objectives: investigate the palace vault heist (Chondrus); monitor Helios cult (Zephyrus); contain chimera in the gulf; manage Adoring Fans.
- Triggers: Party Planning topics; Celestials rumors; stands and merchants chatter.
- Allies/Enemies: Versi (arbiter), guards, merchants; Chondrus and cells; Scions staging incidents.
- Outcomes: political capital, popular support, reduced friction during Games/Debates.
- References: `03.02 - Great Games Using Party Planning.md` (Topics), `00.00 - Complete OotDL.md` (lore).

### Debates and Academy
- Objectives: qualify; win semifinals/finals; secure prizes (tome, favors); present projects in a Science Fair.
- Triggers: invitations from Machaon/Akimedes; PC curiosity; public challenges by Atreus/Chalcodon.
- Allies/Enemies: masters, students; rivals and skeptics.
- Outcomes: academic prestige, prizes, invitations (Orrery/expeditions). Orena may negotiate service with the Ultros.
- References: `03.04`, `03.05`, `03.06`.

## City State (checklists)
- PCs’ Fame: low/medium/high (drag fans? hinder stealth?)
- Popular mood: pro‑Vallus / pro‑Acastus / pro‑Sydon (modulates crowds)
- Standing with Vallus: allied/neutral/cold (official boons)
- Standing with Acastus: allied/neutral/rival (opportunities vs. traps)
- Order of Sydon: contained/influential/aggressive (event tension)
- Lady of Coins: allied/cordial/hostile (access to Colossus/resources)

## Sailing with the Ultros
- Ideal conditions: Games/Debates finals concluded; minimum resolution in Sewers/Colossus; allies recruited (Orena as optional navigator).
- Pythor note: freed from the Throne only at the end of Ch. 3 (see external ref “A Night to Remember”).
