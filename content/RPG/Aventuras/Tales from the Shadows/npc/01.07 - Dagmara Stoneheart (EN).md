# Dagmara Stoneheart

## Snapshot
- **Role**: Fallen Agent of Maal whose murder ignites the campaign; persists as guiding presence through journals and visions.
- **Motivation (Posthumous)**: Ensure her investigation reaches conclusion, protect Yllmourne’s citizens, and uphold Maal’s mandate.
- **Leverage**: Journals, spectral visitations, and allies who still honor her memory.
- **Stat Representation**: When appearing as spirit, treat as `Specter` with custom ability allowing once-per-session guidance (grant reroll on Wisdom save).
- **Key Reference**: Evidence summary in [[01.10 - Dagmara Journal Summary (EN)|Dagmara’s Journal]]; extended biography in [[npc/apend.01 - Integrated NPCs Complete Guide#Dagmara Stoneheart|Integrated NPCs]].

## Narrative Function
- **Act I**: Source of primary mystery; journal entries and ghostly messenger direct the Agent toward lighthouse cult ([[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)#Scene 1: The First Haunting|Act I]]).
- **Act II**: Her legacy persuades Cy and Bonita to trust the Agent; final missives implicate the Rotarist hex.
- **Act III**: Inspiration for plague cure team; spectral guidance can deliver cryptic warnings during [[03.00 - Act III - Rise of the Hero (Lv8-12)#The Ritual of Cleansing|Ritual of Cleansing]].
- **Act IV**: Posthumous testimony via divine magic helps the tribunal reconstruct the conspiracy.

## Relationship Web
- **One-Eyed Cy**: Former lover; his grief is a lever for cooperation ([[npc/01.01 - One-Eyed Cy (EN)|Cy dossier]]).
- **Bonita Sandberg**: Best friend and keeper of her research ([[npc/01.05 - Bonita Sandberg (EN)|Bonita dossier]]).
- **Octant Faylene**: Prayed together; Faylene’s guilt intensifies once hex revealed ([[npc/01.03 - Octant Faylene (EN)|Faylene dossier]]).
- **Toren Underleaf**: Childhood friend; his loyalty to the Agent is cemented by shared past with Dagmara ([[npc/01.09 - Toren Underleaf (EN)|Toren dossier]]).
- **Agent of Maal**: Shares training lineage; Dagmara’s reputation boosts authority during investigations.

## Scene Hooks
- **Graveyard Vigil**: Ghost appears when Agent shows mercy, granting Bless-like boon for next encounter.
- **Journal Cross-Check**: Use as narrative device to confirm or contradict player theories.
- **Tribunal Vision**: Maal permits single question to Dagmara’s spirit—prepare answer referencing stored notes.

## Mechanical Notes
- Blessing: Once per long rest, Agent can invoke Dagmara’s memory to reroll a failed Investigation check tied to her case.
- Spirit Interaction: Speaking with dead requires honest intent; lying causes her to withdraw and imposes disadvantage on next social scene.
