This section describes how to integrate each of the epic paths into the story. Each epic path includes a secret divine blessing that can be used to bring the hero back to life. Before running any of the epic paths, you should read the Epic Paths section in the Creating Heroes appendix.

## Sharing Epic Paths

The epic paths are designed to give each player their own unique motivations and moments to shine. They work best if they are not shared.

However, if your players wish to share an epic path, the best way to do this is to create characters who are siblings or otherwise closely related. They will effectively play as partners who share the same story. In this case, each should receive their own rewards, and you may want to differentiate them by adjusting the items for their chosen classes.

# The Demigod - Kaýrion (Son of Pythor)

The Demigod is the child of Pythor, the God of Battle. Pythor has spent centuries obsessed with his failures. He believes the Demigod is his path to redemption. Pythor wants the Demigod to perform the tasks that he himself failed to accomplish—and to take his place as the God of Battle.

### Meeting the Oracle

Versi expects great things from the Demigod. She encourages them to travel to Estoria and meet their father, Pythor.

> "You are a beauteous thing, but a flower cannot bloom if its stem wilts. So too must you, to see your destiny through, go to your divine parent, Pythor, now King of Estoria. He wallows in his wine and his grief, long having lost the luster of his glory days. You shall forge the weapon he could never wield, defeat his ancient foe, and save his true love. In doing so, you shall walk a path that would allow you to outshine your father in heroism, and perhaps even join him in godhood."

### Meeting Pythor

When the heroes first meet Pythor in Estoria, he recognizes the Demigod as one of his children. He laments that the green dragon Hexia took the Demigod's mother from him. Hexia dwells somewhere in the Forgotten Sea.

### Pythor's Hammer

When Pythor learns that the Demigod wants to finish building his greatest weapon, he will offer up his magical hammer (Pythor's hammer). Pythor will explain that the hammer will be needed to forge the weapon. But first, he makes the Demigod swear an oath to save his daughter Anora (the Demigod's half-sister) from being sacrificed to Sydon.

After giving the Demigod his hammer, Pythor suggests that the Demigod talk with Volkan, his father, who also dwells in Estoria.

### Meeting Volkan

Volkan tells the Demigod that they will only be able to forge Pythor's unfinished weapon using the Mithral Forge. He tells the Demigod to relight the Mithral Forge and find the components required to complete Pythor's unfinished weapon.

### Forging the Weapon

Once the fires of the Mithral Forge are relit, Volkan can forge the unfinished weapon when given the appropriate materials. When the weapon is complete, Volkan sends a keledone to deliver it to the Demigod.

### Pythor's Greatest Foe

The Demigod eventually discovers that their mother is a prisoner in Hexia's lair on the Island of the Dragon. By defeating Hexia, the Demigod will defeat Pythor's greatest foe and rescue their mother in one fell swoop.

The Demigod's mother has some knowledge of the Forgotten Sea and can give a vague description of each island. She asks the Demigod to succeed where her husband failed and save Thylea from the Titans.

## Divine Blessing

If the Demigod is killed, Pythor will appear within 1 hour and surrender a portion of his divine spark to raise them back to life. This can only happen once, and Pythor suffers one level of permanent exhaustion as a result.

# The Vanished One - Elion

The Oracle, Versi, has lived for millennia and has never loved anyone—until she laid eyes on the Vanished One. She will do anything to ensure the Vanished One lives, but her jealousy knows no bounds.

### Meeting the Oracle

Versi asks the Vanished One to swear an oath that they will return to her once the prophecy is fulfilled and the world is saved from disaster. Only after this oath will she reveal that the Vanished One's famous armor, along with the crown of the Dragonlords, is located in Xander's Tomb in the Necropolis of Telamok. She refuses to reveal this information before the oath.

> "My heart, the Fates are truly cruel to separate us so quickly after you came to my rescue. Before I tell you of your great destiny, I ask that you swear an Oath, here and now, that once you have fulfilled your fate and prevented the Ruin of Thylea... you will return to me. And may our hearts sing together forever."
> 
> (let the player respond)
> 
> "I have always had a 'weakness' for mortals... but I never knew what it was to love, not truly, until I looked upon you, (Vanished One's name). Now I will speak of your destiny: you are fated to see your order reborn—I see you, once again wearing your legendary armor and crown. You must retrieve them. Look for them in the Necropolis of Telamok. Old friends await you there."

### Versi's Jealousy

Versi uses her divination magic to watch over the Vanished One. She becomes extremely jealous if anyone shows romantic interest in them. If the Vanished One consummates another romance, Versi confronts them the next day. She will try to kill the person they were intimate with.

### Gaius in Estoria

Gaius is a descendant of the Vanished One. Alter his appearance as needed so that it vaguely resembles the Vanished One, but the passage of centuries means he may not look that similar. Gaius knows the Vanished One is his ancestor but will not reveal this. He remains interested in seeing the heroes sacrificed and will readily suggest that the Vanished One replace Anora, though he will gladly accept all the heroes to get rid of them at once.

### Your Tomb

In the Necropolis of Telamok, one of the first tombs discovered belongs to the Vanished One. It is splendid in decoration (designed to reflect the character's personality), but the artifacts they seek are not present.

### Conversation with Xander

The undead Xander in the Necropolis of Telamok recognizes the Vanished One and hands over the crown of the Dragonlords. However, their legendary armor was stolen by a man who "shares your blood."

This man is Gaius, Commander of the Order of Sydon. He keeps the Vanished One's legendary relic in his room on the Island of Yonder. The armor rejects his attempts at attunement, but he persists in trying.

### Conversation with Estor

Estor claims the Vanished One showed true bravery during the war, though he believes they are now "fat with the affections of that water nymph." He offers 'redemption' and help in recovering the Vanished One's memories if they accept his proposal of possession.

### Versi's Vision

When the heroes depart on the _Ultros_, Versi appears and tells the Vanished One where to find one of the dragon eggs scattered across Thylea.

- (If Bronze/Fire Dragon) She had a vision of an egg under the constellation of the Prisoner, corresponding to the bronze dragon egg on Fire Island (area V15).
    
- (If Silver Dragon) An iceberg floats in the Forgotten Sea.
    
- (Other/Alternative) She says the "mysteries of the Dragonlords" are hidden beneath the Mist in the Cerulean Gulf (at the Castle), or that the egg is under the stars of the Turtle, "guarded by an old friend" (Archdruid Sunarah keeps it in stasis in the Egg Nursery).
    

### Memorials

Memorials to the Vanished One and the other Dragonlords exist on the Isle of the Sleeping Dragon and at Chelone's Brow. In these places, the Vanished One is treated with great reverence, and both the Elders of the Sleeping Dragons and Archdruid Sunarah readily hand over an egg entrusted to them. Sunarah remembers the Vanished One from the time of the war against the Titans, saying they inspired her younger self to rebel against the gygans of the Typhoon Palace.

### Confrontation with Gaius

Gaius reveals himself to be a descendant of the Vanished One and claims that only by killing his ancestor can he atone for the sins committed by the Dragonlords against the Twin Titans. As blood of his blood, Gaius was able to retrieve the artifacts from the tomb in Telamok.

### The Forgotten Dragon

If the heroes find Hezzebal, the dragon guarding the Mirror Prison in the Nether Sea, the dragon blames the Vanished One for its long imprisonment.

## Divine Gift

If the Vanished One dies, Versi will appear within 1 hour and kiss them, restoring them to life. This can only happen once.

## Memories

### Your Tomb in Telamok

Design the new tomb in Telamok according to the Vanished One's personality (a warrior might have mosaics fighting a gygan or blemmys, while a healer might be tending to the wounded, etc.).

Relate a memory from these scenes or ask the player to describe a moment that reveals what their hero was like during the war.

Include details that show their glory and pride, but also that they left a loved one or child behind—or another hint of descendance, whether as a philanderer or as someone with a family. If the player does not want their character to have children, the tomb can show a beloved younger sibling who continued the bloodline.

### Mithral Mines

Here, the Vanished One recalls Volkan and the dwarves forging weapons for them, perhaps even their legendary relic.

### Mytros

The Vanished One remembers the city, much smaller and crowded with desperate survivors during the war. Now, it is resplendent and bustling. A sense of pride fills them, along with the feeling of having missed the city's growth beyond what it was. They may remember Telamok, who helped found the city.

### The Ghost Ship Ultros

The Vanished One remembers the place and Estor's dark nature. They don't recall him completely, and Estor takes advantage of this, trying to convince them they were friends. The Vanished One may have a gut feeling that something is wrong and has an advantage on checks to perceive Estor's intentions—to possess their body.

### Island of Yonder

The Vanished One remembers the massacre of the gygans here, which stained the seas red and bloodied the fields. Ask the player what the Vanished One's reaction to the massacre was, and what role they played—fighting on the island but retreating where Estor did not, or if they learned of it too late while fighting elsewhere.

# The Doomed One - Basil

When the Doomed One was born, Lutheria demanded they be sacrificed along with many other children as part of an annual wine festival. Of all the parents of these children, only the Doomed One's refused. Consequently, Lutheria slaughtered the Doomed One's parents and directed her entire army of servants to torment the child endlessly as a practical joke.

### Lutheria's Joke

As long as Lutheria lives, her servants will focus their attacks on the Doomed One. This includes goatlings, jackalweres, lamias, maenads, and all hostile undead. It should be obvious to the Doomed One that these creatures are singling them out.

### Meeting the Oracle

The Oracle's corrupted sister, Demetria, knows more about the fate that has been laid upon the Doomed One. Versi explains that Demetria can be found in the Mossy Temple.

> "You whose birth was cursed, whose joyous day of coming to the world was spoiled, I can see the torment that has been laid at your feet, but my sight is obscured from seeing the perpetrator of your misfortunes. Speak with my sister, Demetria, where she weeps and rages in the Oldwood. She has a connection to that which is fated to be your ultimate doom."

### Meeting Demetria

Demetria recognizes the Doomed One. She reveals that the Doomed One is destined to die by the workings of Lutheria. She says that only the Fates can say more. This may also be revealed in the dream when the heroes drink from the Horn of Balmytria.

### Consulting the Fates

The Fates (pg. 140) reveal the full prophecy that accompanied the Doomed One's birth. They were to suffer all their life and die three times to satisfy the Queen of Death. The Fates also reveal the full story of the Doomed One's birth parents—and the cruelty of Lutheria's practical joke.

### The Fates' Loom

The Fates possess a magical loom that can be used to forge an item capable of protecting against Lutheria. This requires the Doomed One to either kill the Fates or bargain with them.

### Confronting Lutheria

When the Doomed One confronts Lutheria on her throne in the Nether Sea, the Titan will take no actions for one round as she is consumed by uncontrollable laughter. She fondly recalls all of the pointless suffering that she has inflicted upon the Doomed One.

## Divine Blessing

If the Doomed One is killed, they will immediately be affected by the _true resurrection_ spell. This can happen only twice. No matter how the Doomed One is raised from the dead, they will remain dead forever after dying for the third time. This restriction ends once Lutheria is dead.

# The Haunted One - Zephyr

No one believes the Haunted One's family existed—not even the gods. Only the Fates know the truth. The Haunted One was prophesied to “break the power of death.” When Lutheria learned of this, she hunted them down and slaughtered their entire family.

But Mytros, the Goddess of Dawn, intervened. Before Lutheria could deliver the killing blow to the Haunted One, Mytros used divine magic and erased the Haunted One's entire existence from memory. Lutheria forgot her purpose and returned to her throne.

Only the Fates remember the prophecy or the existence of the Haunted One and their family. Mytros saw that the prophecy could only be fulfilled if all traces of the Haunted One's history were forgotten. The souls of their family remain trapped in Lutheria's scythe.

### Meeting the Oracle

Versi tells the Haunted One that her corrupted sister, Demetria, possesses one of their family's lost artifacts. Demetria can be found in the Mossy Temple in the Oldwood.

### Defeating Demetria

Demetria possesses one of the Haunted One's minor family artifacts. It was stolen and brought to the temple by one of her goatling servants.

### Attuning to the Artifact

When the Haunted One attunes to the minor artifact, they hear whispers from the voices of their family. The voice of the person they loved most in life (mother, father, spouse, child, etc.) will be the loudest. This voice tells them that the other family artifact is in the hands of the Amazon Queen.

> For Zephyr → He would have a wife [Kalista], and possibly a child.

### The Amazon Queen

The larger family artifact is indeed in the possession of the Amazon Queen on the Island of Themis. When the Haunted One attunes to the larger artifact, the whispers of their family become louder. The beloved relative will tell the Haunted One that the Fates know the truth.

### Consulting the Fates

The Fates reveal the Haunted One's entire history, including the prophecy and the fact that Mytros erased their family from all memory. The Fates say that only by breaking Lutheria's crystal scythe can the Haunted One free their family's souls.

### Confronting Lutheria

If the Haunted One confronts Lutheria directly on her throne in Hypnos, Lutheria will vaguely remember the prophecy and realize she made a mistake. She will direct all her efforts to killing the Haunted One before anyone else.

### Destroying the Scythe

Destroying Lutheria's crystal scythe releases the souls of the Haunted One's family so they can pass into the afterlife. The memory and knowledge of their existence also return to the world.

## Divine Gift

Once attuned to a family artifact, the Haunted One can escape death once. If the Haunted One is killed, the spirits of their loved ones will possess the hero so they can continue their battle as if they had just taken a short rest. This can only happen once.

# The Gifted One

The Gifted One is the grandchild of Sydon, who fell in love with a mortal sorceress decades ago. Eventually, Lutheria convinced her brother that such a romance was corrupting him. Sydon murdered the sorceress, unaware that she had already given birth to a daughter; the Gifted One is the child of this daughter.

### Meeting the Oracle

Versi reveals the Gifted One's conflicting ancestry, explaining that Sydon murdered their sorceress grandmother. She gives the Gifted One a locket that once belonged to her.

### The Whispering Locket

A week after receiving the locket, the spirit of the Gifted One's grandmother will speak through it. She will whisper to the Gifted One that they must resurrect the Dragonlords of old. But first, they must take revenge on Sydon.

### The Wisdom of Vallus

Before the heroes set sail on the _Ultros_, Vallus offers her counsel to the Gifted One. She believes the Gifted One will restore glory to the order of the Dragonlords. But first, they must recover the fortress of the Dragonlords. It can be found in the possession of a dragon in the Garden of Helios.

### Dragon Eggs

The Gifted One can find a dragon egg in various places in the world of Thylea.

### Fortress of the Dragonlords

The instant fortress can be found in the hoard of the gold dragon that rules the Garden of Helios. When summoned, it manifests as a tower with draconic architectural motifs.

### The Silver Dragon

When the Gifted One finds Nephele, the silver dragon in Praxys, their grandmother speaks to them through the locket. She reveals that the silver dragon is soulless. If the Gifted One breaks the locket, her soul can enter the dragon.

In this case, the Gifted One gains an adult silver dragon as an ally. However, the dragon is as arrogant and mercurial as the sorceress was in life. She will only help in the battle against Sydon.

### Confronting Sydon

When the Gifted One confronts Sydon, he is disappointed that one of his descendants could be so small and weak.

## Divine Gift

If the Gifted One is killed, their body will disintegrate. Vallus will then use a _true resurrection_ spell to bring them back to life. She then uses her magic to return the Gifted One to their companions. This can only happen once.

# The Lost One

The Lost One is a hero who came from the outside world as a survivor of a shipwreck or as an explorer seeking fame and fortune. Unlike the other epic paths, this one works well if more than one player decides to create a character with this path. If more than one player creates a character with this path, their rewards should be added to the lost treasure of the Dragonlords.

### Meeting the Oracle

Versi believes that the Lost One will find a way back home, but first they must find the lost treasure of the Dragonlords.

> "I sense distant shores upon you. Your very venture here to these lost lands is an odyssey in and of itself. But your journey has only just begun. Seek you a way to return home or to carve out a place for yourself here in forgotten Thylea? I cannot say. What I do know, fabulous wealth was brought to this land by the last great arrivals, the Dragonlords of yore. You must recover their lost treasure - with those boons in hand, you will find a way to sail back through the Forgotten Sea, or to make yourself a legend here in these lands."

### Meeting Pythor

When Pythor meets the Lost One, the god will offer to tell what he knows of the lost treasure of the Dragonlords, but only if his daughter is rescued before being sacrificed to Sydon. When his daughter is safe, Pythor reveals that Estor Arkelander was the last in possession of the treasure. Estor is now the undead captain of the ghost ship, the _Ultros_.

### Estor's Bargain

Once the heroes have gained control of the _Ultros_, Estor Arkelander will reveal to the Lost One that he buried the treasure of the Dragonlords in the Nether Sea. The treasure is sealed in a massive prison meant to contain a creature used by the gods as a weapon at the dawn of time. Estor will guide the heroes there, but only if the Lost One swears an oath to allow him first pick of the treasure hoard—there is a single item that he wants above all else.

### The Wisdom of Vallus

When Vallus gives the Antikythera device to the heroes, she explains that it can be modified. A skilled gygan smith can rework it so that it can reveal a path through the Forgotten Sea to the world beyond. This work can be done by one of several gygan smiths—Steros, Bront, or Garzoon.

### The Dragonlords' Treasure

The lost treasure of the Dragonlords is sealed in the broken 'mirror' prison that was used to imprison the Kraken in the Nether Sea. The heroes must defeat the corrupted dragon that guards the treasure. Afterwards, if the Lost One swore the oath to Estor, the ghost takes the _xiphos of slaughter_ from the treasure hoard.

## Divine Gift

Once the _Ultros_ has been claimed, if the Lost One is killed, then Estor will appear and possess the body of the Lost One, bringing them back to full health and powers as if they had just completed a full rest. The player can play as the possessed hero and try to extricate themselves from the situation where they were defeated. This can only happen once.

# The Dragonslayer

The dragon responsible for the tragedy that destroyed the Dragonslayer's childhood home is the gold dragon known as Helios.

### Meeting the Oracle

Versi tells the Dragonslayer that they are destined to become the bane of evil dragons. They must seek the Forge-Guardian in the Mithral Mines to learn more about the artifacts they need.

### The Mithral Forge

The Forge-Guardian will give the Dragonslayer their minor magic item. The Forge-Guardian says that the Dragonslayer must obtain a weapon powerful enough to fight a dragon. The gygans of Yonder supposedly forged such weapons in the First War.

### Consulting the Fates

The Fates reveal that the Dragonslayer must seek the wisdom of the Sun God under the constellation of the Chariot. He will help the Dragonslayer find the vengeance they seek.

### The Forbidden Library

The Dragonslayer's greater magic item can be found in the possession of Chondrus on the Island of Yonder. He will offer it willingly if the players agree to his bargain.

### Confronting Helios

If the Dragonslayer reaches the Garden of Helios, the Sun God will focus all his attention on them. During dinner with Helios, the Sun God will reveal that he was expecting the Dragonslayer's arrival for a long time. The Fates told him that he would be killed by someone from the Dragonslayer's family/city/tribe, so he rained fire upon their home long ago. As soon as Helios assumes his dragon form, he will try to devour the Dragonslayer.

### Confronting Talieus

In the Great Arena of Sydon's Tower, Talieus and his audience will focus their ridicule on the Dragonslayer, and the dragons will prioritize attacking the Dragonslayer.

## Divine Gift

If the Dragonslayer is killed, they will be visited by Morta (the night hag) from the Island of the Fates. She will resurrect the Dragonslayer and reveal that their fate is not to die here. This can only happen once.

# The Cursed One

### The Ancestral Curse

An ancient ancestor of the Cursed One offended the Twin Titans, and as punishment, the Titans joined forces to weave a curse that would eternally plague the ancestor's bloodline. To do this, they drew upon the unholy powers of the Maw of Typhon, sealing the curse in the ancestor's blood, who was imprisoned within the Maw itself.

The only way to end this torment is to delve into the Maw of Typhon and destroy the ancestor, thus ending the affliction of the Cursed One, their family, and their entire bloodline.

### Nature of the Curse

Work with the Cursed One's player to decide the specific nature of the curse. It should be related to the character's abilities—whether through race, class, subclass, or other traits.

> Example: A druid might carry the curse of one day getting lost in animal form and going mad, but in the meantime, can control their transformations to some degree.

> For Basil: When he uses a heritage spell, he has a chance of adverse effects. We will use the decreasing die system, i.e., d20 (rolling a 1) becomes a d12, then (rolling a 1) becomes a d10, ... down to d4. It resets with a long rest.

### Meeting the Oracle

Versi tells the Cursed One that they must speak with Damon, the ancient lich who guards the Necropolis of Telamok, to better understand the nature of the curse.

> "You, Cursed One, whose veins run black with the foulest of hexes, whose punishment is disproportionate to the crimes committed ages ago. I see that you must travel to the City of the Dead. There, you must speak with the silent guardian that watches over all of Telamok. He shall reveal the truth of your curse, for he was there when it was placed upon your blood."

### Meeting Damon

Damon knew the Cursed One's ancestor and was present when the curse was laid. Through images and visions, he reveals that the curse is unique in all of Thylea, created jointly by Sydon and Lutheria.

He shows the Cursed One a black tower under the stars of the Scorpion, with a lotus flower blooming at its top. This figure, the **Lotus Witch**, will know how to break the curse.

Damon then gives the Cursed One a long-lost family heirloom: a **Gem of Brightness**.

### Meeting the Lotus Witch

The Lotus Witch reveals that the magical origin of the curse lies on the island of Typhon. Only by going there and diving into its volcanic heart can the Cursed One achieve their desire. However, it will be necessary to possess a magic item powerful enough to withstand a fiery death.

### The Greater Artifact

The Lotus Witch will only give the Cursed One the necessary artifact if they are able to correctly answer a sufficient number of her riddles. Otherwise, the Cursed One must kill her.

### Typhon Island

Upon arriving at Typhon Island, the Cursed One can leap into the volcanic core. If they have the artifact granted by the Lotus Witch, they and their allies will emerge unharmed on the **ash plains of Hades**.

There, the Cursed One will find their ancestor—an ancient figure weathered by time—who will say that their death will end the curse.

When the Cursed One tries to kill them, their body freezes—the laughter of the Titans echoes in the air—and the ancestor transforms into a **Typhon**. After defeating it, it returns to its original form and can finally be killed.

### Ending the Curse

Once the ancestor is slain, all curses afflicting the members of the Cursed One's bloodline come to an end, and their **Gem of Brightness** is fully recharged. The Cursed One awakens again on the island of Typhon.

## Divine Gift

If the Cursed One dies, Damon will immediately appear and resurrect them with the _True Resurrection_ spell—a spell he can only cast a single time and only for the Cursed One, as part of an Oath sworn to their ancestors. If necessary, Damon will also aid in the battle that led to the Cursed One's death.

# The Bastard

King Acastus received a prophecy when he was young. The Oracle claimed she saw no great deeds in his future and that everything he would accomplish would be overshadowed by his offspring, who was one day destined to take his life. Since then, Acastus has lived in fear of death and obscurity. Afraid, he ensured he would never sire a child, keeping a close watch on his concubines and remaining distant from his wife.

The Bastard's mother was Acastus's favorite concubine. He gave her a family heirloom—a unique coin, which she wore as a pendant. One day, she happily told him she was pregnant, hoping he would legitimize the child. Instead, he plotted her execution. Vallus learned what was happening and could not allow it. She hid the Bastard's mother, saying she was exiled, as she knew the woman was too charmed by Acastus to believe the truth of his betrayal. Only one other person witnessed the exile: the king's mother, Idylla. Vallus forced her to swear she would never reveal what she had seen to Acastus.

After many years of searching for the Bastard and their mother, Acastus found them. He ordered one of his men to take his dragon to the Bastard's home and burn it down with both of them inside (any of the Bastard's neighbors were threatened, bribed, killed, or otherwise silenced). Fate protected the Bastard, but their mother was not so lucky.

### Meeting the Oracle

Versi reveals that while the exact identity of the Bastard's father is obscured from her sight, she sees greatness throughout their lineage. She recognizes the coin as a burnished Dragonlord coin, and judges, by its age, that it was one of the first to be minted. From this point on, the coin gains the abilities of a common burnished coin. Versi believes it may have special properties beyond the usual, though it is currently "dormant." She encourages the Bastard to show the coin to Damon, in the Necropolis of Telamok, and states:

> "A member of your blood sleeps restlessly in the necropolis."

> "You carry a cruel name that burns deep in your noble soul, Bastard. I admit that the identity of your father and your mother's killer is obscured to my sight, but I can see greatness spreading through your family tree. The symbol you carry, a gift to your mother, is a burnished Dragonlord coin, and I see it was one of the first minted. Take it to the City of the Dead and present it to the silent guardian of that place. He will show you the way to the tomb where a member of your blood sleeps restlessly."

### Meeting Damon

Damon will not take the Bastard's special coin. Instead, he will direct them to the tomb of Telamok Arkelander, revealing that one of the Bastard's ancestors resides there.

### Tomb of Telamok

Upon entering the area, the sarcophagus containing the immortal corpse of Telamok begins to stir. When opened, he reaches out to the Bastard, gesturing for the coin. Upon his touch, the coin gains the properties of a **Stone of Good Luck**. He spits dust and claims the Bastard is not "his," but rather part of his "foolish brother's" lineage. Then, he will attack, focusing on the Bastard.

### House Arkelander

The Bastard or other party members may try to learn more about the Arkelander family after confronting Telamok. Any native Thylean will know that Estoria was named after Estor Arkelander. A History check or a conversation with Pythor or Aesop reveals that the only surviving member of the Arkelander line is King Acastus of Mytros.

## Summoned by the King

After dealing with the storm, Acastus dismisses the group—except for the Bastard, with whom he wishes to speak privately. You can run the "Summoned by the Queen" event separately, as Vallus prefers to speak with the group without the Bastard present (see below for more on Vallus's treatment of the Bastard).

If the Bastard is wearing the coin visibly, Acastus recognizes it immediately. Otherwise, he notices the resemblance between the Bastard and himself, or between the Bastard and their mother, and connects the dots. He will ask about the coin and say that he gave it to the "love of his life."

Acastus pretends to be overjoyed to be reunited with his child and feigns acceptance. He explains that Vallus exiled the Bastard's mother due to her immense jealousy, and that he searched for her for years without success. If the Bastard reveals the mother's death, Acastus feigns devastation and lies, saying he believes Vallus used her divine powers to rain fire on the house, knowing he was about to be reunited with his true love. He asks, however, that the Bastard keep this theory secret, as Vallus is adored by the city and, of course, is a goddess.

Before officially recognizing the Bastard as his child, Acastus asks them to deal with the matter of the **Ultros**, promising to then reward them with all the love and glory they deserve. Secretly, he hopes his ancestor will kill his offspring.

## Estor's Frustration

Upon meeting Estor, the ghost immediately recognizes the Bastard as being of his blood. He laments Acastus's cowardice for sending his own child instead of facing his fate. Nevertheless, he says the Bastard should "bring honor to the family" by taking command of the **Ultros**.

## Return to Acastus

Upon returning to Mytros, Acastus feigns delight at the Bastard's success. However, he is furious—especially if the Bastard has become the captain of the **Ultros**. He makes a show of presenting them to the city, taking them to the palace balcony and flying over Mytros mounted on Icarus. During his speech, he tries to attribute the Bastard's great deeds to the blood they share. Be careful when writing this scene: it should not seem too obvious that Acastus is just using the Bastard.

If the Bastard asks for his weapon, Acastus will say he will give it "when the time is right," as he still needs it to protect Mytros.

## The Wisdom of Vallus

Vallus feels shame for not being able to protect the Bastard's mother and, because of this, avoids interactions with them. During the Departure, however, Vallus quickly pulls them aside and whispers:

> "All will be revealed under the stars of the Warrior.
> 
> Someone who knows the truth waits for you there."

She is referring to the **Island of Exiles** and **Idylla Arkelander**.

## Meeting Idylla

Upon meeting the Bastard, Idylla immediately recognizes them as Acastus's child—by the resemblance to her son or to the Bastard's mother. She is delighted and approaches with affection, inviting them to her home on the island, where she reveals the whole truth:

- Acastus planned the murder of the Bastard's mother, something Idylla desperately tried to prevent.
    
- The mother's exile was a lie to protect them.
    
- Idylla swore an oath to Vallus not to reveal the truth and to protect the Bastard.
    
- Acastus spent years hunting the Bastard.
    
- When he discovered Idylla knew everything, he allowed her exile (though this was not the only reason).
    

Before leaving for exile, Idylla took luxury goods and smuggled something special: **a silver dragon egg**, from the same clutch as Icarus. She reveals where Acastus really got his dragons. She may use the egg to convince the Bastard to take her aboard the **Ultros**.

Idylla insists on accompanying the Bastard on the journey, offering her influence, contacts, and political skills. She constantly advises them, tries to convince them to take revenge on Acastus and to **take the throne**—all to return to power and become a royal advisor at the Bastard's side.

## Assassination Attempt

Acastus finds out about the Bastard's visit to the Island of Exiles—either through spies (using a Sending Stone) or a false Dragonlord following the **Ultros**. Knowing his charade has been exposed, he decides to act:

Acastus sends two or three centurion captains from Mytros (with maximum hit points) mounted on **young brass dragons** to assassinate the Bastard and the other heroes. The dragons may flee if their riders are killed. They can use their fire breath against the ship's **ballistae** instead of focusing directly on the heroes—to avoid an overly difficult combat.

## Confronting Acastus

Idylla advises **not to confront Acastus yet**. She says the focus should be on their destiny and the Titans—and that when the time is right, they will return to Mytros to take the throne and exact their revenge. If Idylla is not present, Kyrah, Versi, or another NPC can give the same advice.

## The Battle of Mytros

During the final battle, the moment of revenge arrives. In the Mad King event, Acastus will reveal everything he has done during his delusions. After the battle, he apologizes to the Bastard, showing some remorse.

He gives the Bastard his **legendary weapon** (replaces the Lifethief if it wasn't chosen earlier; if so, it returns with full charges) and, with his last breath, begs his child to save the city.

If Tarchon and other false Dragonlords survive the fight, they swear loyalty to the Bastard.

## Claiming the Legacy

If Vallus survives the Battle of Mytros, she may abdicate the throne in favor of the Bastard. As Acastus's heir—and possibly with Idylla's influence—the Bastard can ascend to the throne.

⚠️ This may conflict with the **Haunted One** epic path. The DM should use good judgment to decide who will rule Mytros, ensuring that the players and characters are in agreement with taking on such a responsibility.

# The Exile - Yelena

## The Betrayal

Thirteen years ago, a group of three Amazon sisters was tempted by Lutheria with great power if they acted on the eldest sister's ambitions and usurped the throne. In a single night of bloodshed, the Queen and her main supporters were murdered as the sisters stole their lives and identities. For this deed, the three sisters were transformed into medusae, as both a "reward" from Lutheria and a curse for their treachery and regicide.

The Exile was helped to escape by the youngest sister, Moxena. When the eldest sister ordered Moxena to clear out the deposed queen's chambers, she found the future Exile still a helpless child. Instead of killing her as ordered, Moxena placed the child in a boat and pushed her out to sea, trusting that Fate would protect her.

Years later, perhaps out of regret for the betrayal or in the hope of gaining more power for herself (or perhaps a mixture of both feelings), Moxena tried to overthrow her two sisters and reclaim the throne. This ended badly, leading to Moxena's flight from Themis and her rise as the Lady of Coins in Mytros, after being rescued by Hexia.

## Meeting the Oracle

Versi will tell the Exile that she has a dangerous path ahead, but that she need not walk it alone. The woman who once saved her life awaits her in Mytros, "in the head of the god." Furthermore, Versi will tell the Exile that she must prove her worth goes beyond her blood, and that the Amazons must fight alongside the settlers if the Twin Titans are to be defeated.

> "Your Highness. Thank you for coming to me. I see that the path to your throne is indeed treacherous, but you need not walk it alone. In the head of the god, there is a woman. She could offer help... or be the first villain to fall before your just revenge. Beyond her, I see that you must right the wrongs the Amazons committed long ago. Only if they fight alongside the people of Thylea against the Titans can the Twins be defeated. To retake your throne, you must not only seek brutal vengeance, but prove that your worth extends beyond your blood."

## The Queen's Warriors

The Exile may want to talk to the Amazons who serve Queen Vallus. They are confused by the Exile's survival, as they were told long ago that the princess was executed for cowardice and for bringing shame to the royal house. Upon hearing the Exile's side of the story, they are easily convinced. However, they will not abandon Vallus's service, but they can tell the Exile some of what they know about what is happening in Themis—and that they were exiled for supposedly dishonoring the Amazons, something they severely contest. In truth, they were exiled for their loyalty to the true queen, and the impostors did not want them to discover the truth.

## The Lady of Coins

Moxena will send a message to the Exile after the group deals with the storm sent by Sydon to attack the city. The messenger (a spy) that Moxena sends makes it clear that this is an offer of Sacred Hospitality (Guest Friendship). The messenger refers to his patron only as the Lady of Coins, letting Moxena reveal her identity when she meets the Exile.

During a lavish meal in the head of the Colossus of Pythor, Moxena will tell the Exile the events described in **The Betrayal** (see above), and that she not only deeply regrets betraying the Exile's mother but is also willing to swear loyalty to the Exile and her effort to retake the throne.

After revealing her side of the story, Moxena will offer an alliance through a mutual oath, "sworn in the name of the Great Mothers and before the Merciless Furies." The Exile must swear that she will never seek revenge against Moxena, and in return, Moxena will help her retake her throne.

If the Exile rejects the alliance, she may leave in peace, as per the rules of Sacred Hospitality. After that, hostilities will begin, and Moxena will send the forces of the Serpent Cult against her.

If the Exile accepts the alliance and takes the oath, Moxena will dedicate herself and her resources to the Exile's cause. She hopes to become the Exile's advisor when she is crowned queen.

> **Note to the DM**: Moxena is portrayed as a much more "good" character in this Epic Path than in the original module. If you wish to keep her true to her representation in the adventure, have her lie and manipulate the Exile with the intention of taking power for herself.

## Arrival in Themis

The Amazons of Themis believe that Thesilea VI executed her own daughter after deeming her unfit for the throne. Her return confuses them, especially if the Exile (and Moxena, if present) tries to explain what really happened thirteen years ago. Many Amazons are unhappy with the shift to the cult of Lutheria and will readily support the Exile if she has proven herself in the Trial. Otherwise, it may be necessary to convince them.

The encounter with the Amazon Hunting Party will still lead the group to the Sacred Temple/Amazon Prison to see the false Thesilea, while the encounter with the Amazon War Party was sent by the false Queen with the mission to assassinate the Exile.

If Moxena is present, she will reveal that she knows some loyalists on the island who can help create a distraction, allowing the group to discreetly approach the Sacred Temple/Amazon Prison. Additionally, she describes the layout of the place as best as her memory serves.

## The Role of Darien

If the Exile's Epic Path is chosen, Darien is not heir to the throne nor the daughter of Thesilea VI, but rather the daughter of one of the Queen's closest allies. Darien and the Exile were childhood friends, confidantes, and constant training partners. It is possible that the two were destined to become Life Companions one day, either through sisterly or romantic love.

Darien survived that night in the same way described in the module, but instead of Thesilea forcing the three sisters to swear to spare her life, it was one of Thesilea's advisors who gave her own life in exchange for her daughter's.

Darien desires revenge for her mother's death and, above all, to be reunited with the Exile. She will support the Exile's claim to the Amazon throne, and will only agree to become Queen if the Exile dies or if they are to rule together as Life Companions.

## The False Queen

The impostor Thesilea VI speaks with a voice frighteningly similar to her mother's, but with a serpentine accent. She curses Moxena's name for having allowed the Exile to escape all those years ago.

> **Optional more challenging encounter**: For a tougher combat, use the stats for Hythonia from _Mythic Odysseys of Theros_ (though you might want to remove the **Shed Skin** ability, depending on the group's level).

## The Trial of Strength

To become the true Queen of Themis and the Amazon tribes of the Cerulean Gulf, the Exile must defeat the roc that inhabits the Forgotten Sea and sacrifice it in the name of the Sisterhood of Equals. When running this encounter, ignore the existence of a second roc, unless the group defeats the first one too easily. This event can occur before or after the visit to Themis.

## The Coronation of a Queen

Once both the Trial of Strength has been completed and the False Queen has been defeated, the Exile can be crowned Queen of the Amazons. If the Trial is completed first, the coronation should take place in Themis, at the Sacred Temple. If the False Queen is confronted first, the ceremony can be held aboard an Amazon attack ship or on the _Ultros_ itself before returning to Themis (this saves travel time), or the group can return directly to Themis to complete the coronation.

The coronation ceremony should be conducted by an Amazon whom the Exile trusts. It could be one of the loyalists, Moxena, or Darien, but it is not normally done by the future Queen's potential Life Companion. The speech is simple:

> **"May the Great Mothers of the past judge you worthy, Keeper of the Amazon Code, the Queen of Themis!"**

If the Exile wishes to have someone by her side on the throne, she can confirm them as her Life Companion and Queen-Consort of Themis. If this is done, the Exile and her Companion immediately gain the benefits of the **Marriage** effect from the _Ceremony_ spell (this works equally in platonic or romantic relationships).

## The Battle of Mytros

If the Exile has been crowned Queen of Themis, Amazon attack ships enter the port of Mytros behind Sydon's forces. After a moment of terror for the residents and a brief celebration by the gygans, the Amazons let out their war cries and attack the Titan's forces from behind. This reduces the number of casualties by **1/3** at the end of the battle, unless the city is completely destroyed—in which case, the Amazons lose most of their warriors in a failed assault.

# The Masterpiece - Atlas

## The Masterpiece

The Masterpiece was created by Volkan during the First War, though it never saw combat during that conflict, as the Mithral Forge was deactivated during the war. Volkan kept the Masterpiece inactive in his forge in Estoria for several centuries, working on it little by little, trying to perfect it without access to his full range of tools in the Mithral Forge. This caused the process of perfecting his 'living weapon' to take several centuries longer than it would have otherwise. But now that the Oath of Peace has begun to weaken, Volkan has decided it is time to unleash his Masterpiece against the Twin Titans.

## Meeting the Oracle

Versi shows some compassion for the Masterpiece, which was created only as a war machine. She declares that until they find a soul willing to bind with them forever, they will never know anything of life beyond death and combat. The Oracle tells the Masterpiece that they must seek a soul that will agree to merge with them, thus making them "complete."

> "What a tragedy this is, not only to be chained to war and conflict by fate, but also to have been born to know nothing but battle.
> 
> Masterpiece, I see you fulfilling your glorious purpose, present in the battle to come, but I also see that you will remain incomplete, even after achieving such a purpose.
> 
> To avoid this empty future, you must seek a soul, and make it yours.
> 
> Before your mission can truly begin, you must return to the place where you were created, and speak with she who dwells in the flames of the forge—she can guide you forward."

## Reunion with Volkan

Upon returning to Volkan's Hall, the God of the Forge will show extreme zeal for his favorite invention—like an overprotective father. He worries if it has been "damaged" and if it is "functioning correctly" so far. If Kyrah is present, she will appear somewhat jealous of the affection Volkan shows for the Masterpiece.

## The Mithral Forge

Everything about the return to the Mithral Forge seems familiar to the Masterpiece. They are haunted by hazy memories of their creation and of seeing countless "siblings" who did not achieve the same level of consciousness as they did.

Upon entering area F20, the Masterpiece will find the exact spot where they were created. If made of marble, they will find the pedestal where they were sculpted, along with the hammer, chisel, and tools with which Volkan shaped them from the stone. If made of metal, they will find the mold used to cast them. This scene should evoke strange feelings in the Masterpiece. What is it like to return to the place where they were "born"? What does it mean, in fact, to be alive? Who are they really, beyond another weapon forged?

## Meeting the Forge-Guardian

The Forge-Guardian recognizes the Masterpiece as one of "Volkan's attempts to create a super-soldier." In exchange for help destroying her contract, she will agree to grant the Masterpiece their Minor Artifact. If the Guardian perishes, this item can be found at the Masterpiece's creation site (see above).

## The Wisdom of Vallus

During the Embarkation, Vallus informs the Masterpiece that the Fates may know a way to obtain a soul for the Masterpiece to call their own. However, she warns of their cruel hearts and wicked nature.

## Meeting the Fates

The Fates will inform the Masterpiece on how to obtain a soul, provided they perform a task for them. Alternatively, if at least two of the hags are killed, the third will yield and, in exchange for her life (and an Oath that they will not harm her), will reveal what she knows to the Masterpiece.

The Fates inform them that there are many ways to obtain a soul. Some examples include:

- Finding a sentient item willing to give its spark to the Masterpiece (the Fates know of one trapped in the belly of the hydra on Themis);
    
- Finding a "restless soul" willing to join with the Masterpiece;
    
- Obtaining Lutheria's scythe, in which the Goddess of Death has imprisoned countless souls she has reaped.
    

## Seeking a Soul

With the information from the Fates, the Masterpiece can now begin their search for a soul in earnest. Below are several options for obtaining a soul:

### Sentient Item: The Axe of Xander

The Axe of Xander communicates only through transmitted emotions. It will show a desire to help the Masterpiece, but only if they have already defeated several giants and demonstrate a desire to protect Thylea and its settlers, not just wage war. If the criteria are met, the Axe of Xander will consent to become part of the Masterpiece. It is up to the DM to decide if the weapon ceases to exist or continues to exist without consciousness.

### Sentient Item: Titansbane

Titansbane can communicate directly with the Masterpiece. It assures them that if they free Taleius, the Titan of the Forge, he will be able to forge a soul for the Masterpiece. To do this, they must use Titansbane to free Taleius from his bonds in Hypnos, the Throne of Dreams. After freeing him, Taleius returns Titansbane to the group, at which point the weapon will consent to become part of the Masterpiece. Again, it is up to the DM to decide if the weapon continues to exist or not.

### Estor Arkelander

After returning to the _Ultros_ from the Island of Fates, Estor will seek out the Masterpiece when they are alone. He will say that he has long seen them as a "worthy successor" to the Dragonlords, especially since they were created to destroy the Twin Titans. He will be even more direct if the Masterpiece is captain of the _Ultros_. If the Masterpiece agrees to become Estor's permanent host, his shadow will merge with their body. See Estor's Bargain (pg. 123) for more information on possession and its effects on the Masterpiece's personality.

### The Demon in the Bowl

Although the Fates do not recommend this option, Chondrus—if he is with the group—will suggest that if the Masterpiece is so desperate for a soul, they can contact an otherworldly entity he knows. If the group has already looked into the bowl in Chondrus's quarters on the Island of Yonder, they may have communicated with this entity: an arcanoloth who, in exchange for an Oath of Service, will obtain a soul for the Masterpiece by dubious means.

### The Scythe of Lutheria

If the group manages to destroy Lutheria's crystalline scythe, several shades will emerge. These lost souls have forgotten who they were, having been imprisoned by Lutheria for thousands of years. They are willing to become part of the Masterpiece, as they cannot move on without an identity. By joining with the Masterpiece, they assimilate its personality and integrate seamlessly into it.

### Pacting with Lutheria

If the group decides to ally with Lutheria, she will grant a soul to the Masterpiece. She claims it belonged to an ancient worshipper who defied her three times and, as punishment, had everything that defined them stripped away, leaving the soul "blank"—perfect for a new host.

### A Gift from Mytros

If the Masterpiece fails to obtain a soul by all other means described (or by others you create), consider having Mytros grant them a soul after the Battle of Mytros.

## Becoming Ensouled

Upon obtaining a soul, the Masterpiece feels truly alive. The sensation can be described as if they had lived their entire existence wearing gloves, and now, they have been removed, allowing them to finally experience the world as it is. Upon becoming ensouled, the Masterpiece receives the benefits of the _Bless_ spell for 24 hours (no concentration required), as a manifestation of their euphoria.

## Fulfilling Your Purpose

The moment this goal is considered fulfilled can vary. Suggestions include:

- Defeating Lutheria in Hypnos;
    
- Confronting Sydon in Praxys;
    
- Or entering the battlefield during the Battle of Mytros.

# The Demigod - Child of Vallus

You are the mortal child of Vallus, the goddess of Wisdom. From an early age you have demonstrated wisdom beyond your years. You have been kept in secret by your mother. She has kept you hidden for reasons she has not explained to you. While her love for you is great it is also stifling. Vallus has kept you sheltered in Mytros your whole life and out of the public eye. You long to go out and explore and see the world. You want to know who your father is and how you can make your mark on the world.

### Meeting the Oracle

Versi says that the Demigod's divine heritage is strong and burns brightly. While she cannot see who their father is she encourages the Demigod to ask their other family members.

> "You are a curious spirit Demigod. Your Mother meant to shelter you, for you to live a life of safety and obscurity. However, she is wise enough to know that even she cannot defy fate. I promise this, your life will be neither safe nor quiet, but you will see much of the world. I can see you taking to the field of battle, glowing brilliantly like the sun. I admit, the identity of your Father is obscured from my sight. Yet, I know that you must find him if you are to survive your journey. Seek out the counsel of your family, the Five. Perhaps they can help you further and reveal what I cannot."

### The Truth of Your Parentage

The Demigod is the child of Vallus and Helios. Many years ago Vallus, enraged with Acastus' continued infidelity, traveled out into the wilderness. She came upon one of Helios' forgotten shrines at the same time he happened to be checking on it. Vallus immediately realized he was a dragon and in a moment of passion the Demigod was conceived. Vallus knew that Helios and Acastus would both want the child so she kept them secret. She swore an oath to herself that she would never tell anyone what had happened that night. Vallus fears what will happen if either of these two men find out about the Demigod.

### Family Encounters

- **Meeting Pythor:** Pythor will show no immediate reaction to the Demigod. He will ask to speak to them alone at some point and reveal he knows they are his niece or nephew. He encourages them to speak to his father as he thinks Volkan is the only one Vallus told.
    
- **Meeting Volkan:** Volkan will inform the Demigod that he has sworn an oath not to reveal the name of their father. He is sorry to be unhelpful but will offer an item to them in consolation (one of their lesser magic items).
    
- **Meeting Vallus:** Vallus will play the initial meeting with the Demigod calm and collected. Immediately afterwards Vallus will try to get them alone and begin doting on them. If asked about their father she will say that maybe it is time for them to finally meet him. While she can't reveal their name she will tell them that they must collect the Antikythera device in order to find him. She will give the Demigod a Periapt of Wound Closure to keep them safe.
    

### Finding Your Father

At this point the Demigod should try speaking with either the Fates, Lutheria or the Gynosphinx. Any of them can reveal the island they must travel to in order to find their father, Helios.

### Meeting Helios

Helios will not realize the Demigod is his child until it is brought up to him. If this happens he will be overjoyed to have his spawn with him. He will encourage them to join him in the real dinner (eating the other party members). When Helios breaks Guest Friendship, if the Demigod attacks him he will be disappointed. For one round he will chastise his child.

Vallus should visit the Demigod once Helios is taken care of. She will tell her story of their conception and of the need to hide them from both Helios and Acastus.

### Embracing Your Heritage

As soon the Oath of Peace ends, the Demigod should feel a change inside themselves. Vallus will go to them and reveal the truth of the Five Gods to them directly. This should make it clear to the Demigod at this point that they are in fact a full-blooded dragon. The end of the Oath of Peace has allowed them to transform into their true form. The Demigod gains the ability to transform into a Young Gold Dragon once per long rest. This acts as polymorph and lasts for 1 hour.

## Divine Boon

If the Demigod is ever killed, Vallus will appear within 1 hour and surrender a portion of her divine spark to raise them back to life. This can only happen once, and Vallus suffers one level of permanent exhaustion as a result.

- **The Island of the Lost:** Here, the Psychopomp can reunite with their lost lover's fading shade. The lover explains that Lutheria is using souls to power a dark ritual and that her crystal scythe must be destroyed.
    
- **Banishing Estor:** The _Xiphos of Slaughter_ is found in the Mirror Prison. This weapon alone can destroy Estor's spirit.
    
- **Confronting Lutheria:** Lutheria will gleefully swing her scythe at the Psychopomp, claiming she wishes to reunite them with their lover.
    
- **Destroying the Scythe:** Upon defeating Lutheria, the party obtains her scythe. Destroying it frees the souls within.
    
- **Love Never Dies:** The freed souls give their remaining energy to restore the Psychopomp's lover to life via _True Resurrection_.
    

## Divine Boon

If the Psychopomp is killed, they immediately receive the benefits of a _True Resurrection_ spell. For the next minute, they have resistance to all damage except force and radiant and can move through objects. This can only happen once.

# The Repentant One

You were once a powerful and proud sphinx. For your actions, you were banished and stripped of your powers by Lutheria, rendered a mere mortal. Now, awash with terrible humility and regret, you must attempt to rebuild your strength and undo what your pride has wrought.

### Meeting the Oracle

Versi tells the Repentant One of another banished sphinx who has cursed Scorpion Island. Versi claims that undoing this curse could help them find absolution.

> "The Merciless Furies alone know the harm you have wrought... Exiled, transfigured, and brought low, you have still found your way here, on a path towards absolution. This desire for forgiveness marks you as different from another of your kind... Beneath the stars of the mighty scorpion, she has worked a wicked curse. Undoing it will lead you down the path towards healing your wounded soul."

### Path to Absolution

- **Victims of the Sphinx:** After the first Great Labours, former victims of the Repentant One arrive in Estoria, seeking oaths of penance.
    
- **Chondrus:** He approaches the Repentant One in Mytros, claiming to be from an admirer of 'their work' (Lutheria), and gives them their lesser artifact.
    
- **Scorpion Island:** The Repentant One can speak to the centaurs, who desire new riders and are in awe of the idea of the curse being broken. They guide the party to the Lotus Witch.
    
- **The Lotus Witch:** She welcomes her 'cursed and wretched cousin.' Convincing her she is wrong will be difficult; otherwise, she must be slain to end her curse on the island.
    
- **The Island of Time:** After breaking the curse, a roar summons the Repentant One to their homeland. The Androsphinx there will test them with a riddle: "It may be given - not taken or bought. What the sinner desires the saint may not. Of what do I speak?" (Answer: Forgiveness/Repentance/Absolution). Success grants their greater artifact and their Divine Blessing.
    
- **Confronting Lutheria:** Lutheria bemoans that the Repentant One has become a bore and offers to restore their true form in exchange for an Oath of Service.
    

## Divine Boon

If the Repentant One dies, their past self emerges from the weave of time as a powerful sphinx. They cast _True Resurrection_ on their present-day self before returning to their own time. This can only happen once.

# The Timeless One

You are the child of Versi the First, the Titan of Prophecy. Foreseeing the downfall of her siblings, she sent you to the Island of Time. You returned centuries later. This new Thylea is strange, but Sydon and Lutheria are your true enemies.

### Meeting the Oracle

The Oracle, Versi, foresees that the Timeless One must recover the Staff of Kentimane from the Mossy Temple and take it to the Island of the Golden Heart to commune with Thylea.

> "Cousin, strange cousin, you are an anomaly... This was fated to be, foreseen by my namesake your mother, Versi the First... I have seen you fulfill her prophecy, now given to me. For your hand to guide Thylea's new dawn, you must seek out the Hand of Kentimane that sleeps in the heart of the Oldwood, and take it to the Island of the Golden Heart. There, you shall commune with our grandmother, Thylea herself."

### The Quest for the Titans

- **The Mossy Temple:** The Staff of Kentimane is inside Demetria's sacred tree. It can be retrieved by cutting the tree (potentially killing Demetria) or convincing her to remove it.
    
- **Communing with Thylea:** Bringing the staff to the Golden Heart allows the Timeless One to commune with the Mother Goddess. She grants visions of Thylea's history and a golden fruit needed to brew an elixir to heal the Lost Titans.
    
- **Seeking Knowledge:** The Lotus Witch or the Fates can reveal the locations of the Lost Titans.
    
- **Freeing the Lost Titans:** Freeing each Titan requires specific actions and tools (like the Staff of Kentimane and Titansbane) and unlocks new powers for the staff. The reunion with your mother, Versi the First, on Titan Island is a key moment.
    
- **The Battle of Mytros:** The freed Lost Titans can aid the party, granting the effects of a short rest once. They will also help impede Kentimane's progress during the final battle.
    

## Divine Boon

If the Timeless One dies, they come under the effects of the _True Resurrection_ spell as they are engulfed by a pair of the Hands of Kentimane, re-emerging bathed in Thylea's golden light. This can only happen once.
