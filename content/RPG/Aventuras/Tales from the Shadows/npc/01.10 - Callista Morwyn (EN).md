# Callista Morwyn

## Snapshot
- **Role**: Cursed champion warrior seeking redemption; optional ally and moral touchstone.
- **Motivation**: Break the shadow curse corrupting her body or die honorably before it consumes her.
- **Leverage**: Honor-bound loyalty, desperation for a cure, willingness to sacrifice herself for civilians.
- **Stat Baseline**: `Champion` fighter (VGtM p.212) with shadow corruption template—gain Shadow Step and necrotic weapon infusions but suffer escalating psychic backlash after each combat.
- **Deep Dive**: Additional hooks in [[npc/apend.01 - Integrated NPCs Complete Guide#Callista Morwyn (Cursed Champion)|Integrated NPCs]].

## Arc by Act
- **Act I – Seeking Aid**: Arrives chasing rumors of Navula’s cures; becomes short-term muscle for lighthouse raid ([[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)#Scene 4: The Abandoned Lighthouse|Act I]]).
- **Act II – Deterioration**: Curse worsens during political arc; players confront dilemma of using her strength vs. risking her soul ([[02.00 - Act II - Political Intrigue (Lv5-7)#Adventure 8: The Hexed Court|Act II]]).
- **Act III – Crucible**: Cure tied to dismantling Sefton’s shadow magic; decision point—salvation, noble sacrifice, or tragic fall ([[03.00 - Act III - Rise of the Hero (Lv8-12)#Night of the Blockade|Act III]]).
- **Act IV – Possible Ally**: If cured, testifies and aids Maal’s tribunal; if fallen, her fate becomes cautionary tale.

## Relationship Web
- **Navula**: Potential healer demanding steep secret barter ([[npc/01.04 - Navula (EN)|Navula dossier]]).
- **Bonita**: Provides medical support, tracking curse progression ([[npc/01.05 - Bonita Sandberg (EN)|Bonita dossier]]).
- **Sefton Bane**: Source of her curse (shadow court); thirsts for revenge if she survives ([[npc/01.02 - Sefton Bane (EN)|Sefton dossier]]).
- **Agent of Maal**: Looks to the Agent for judgment—mercy, justice, or sanction.

## Scene Hooks
- **Corruption Clock**: Track on four-step clock; each heavy combat tick advances it. At final tick she risks losing control unless saved.
- **Mirror Duel**: Shadow self taunts her in a mirror arena; Agent can aid via Insight/Religion checks.
- **Hero’s Choice**: Offer scenario where she can save civilians at cost of progression; emphasizes moral weight.

## Mechanical Notes
- While allied, she absorbs damage meant for civilians (reaction), but each use advances corruption clock by one.
- Provide `Blood Sigil` token that PCs can study to learn more about Sefton’s hexwork (grants advantage on Arcana vs. Choir magic).
- If cured, convert template to `Oath of Redemption Paladin` re-flavored as champion of balance.
