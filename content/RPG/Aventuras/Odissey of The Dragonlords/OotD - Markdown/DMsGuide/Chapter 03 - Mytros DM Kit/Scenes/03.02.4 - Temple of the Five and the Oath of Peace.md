# The Temple of the Five and the Oath of Peace

## Arrival at the Temple of the Five

> You return to the Temple of the Five — modest when compared to the grand Temple of Sydon, yet exhibiting far greater artistry and elevated architecture. The five stone columns at the entrance seem different now, as if bearing an invisible weight. Intricate carvings still adorn nearly every surface, complete with gilding and inset gemstones, but something in the air has changed.
>
> The temple is quieter than before. Fewer acolytes move along the halls; the five high priests look troubled, gathered in discussion near the central altar. When you enter, they turn with expressions of relief mixed with apprehension.

## Finding Chondrus

An acolyte reports that **Chondrus**, tiefling envoy of Lutheria’s cult, was seen entering the temple not long ago — unusual, as he rarely visits this place sacred to the Five.

> You find him in a side chamber, studying something with intense interest. Chondrus wears his usual black robes, and that subtle smile of pure malice and delight appears as you arrive.
>
> “Ah, the Oracle’s Chosen. How convenient.” His smooth, seductive voice echoes in the chamber. “I presume you did not come here to admire the architecture of the ‘Five Usurpers,’ as my friend Gaius calls them.”

## The Oath of Peace — Physical Location

Following Chondrus (or led by the priests), you are brought to a special chamber at the temple’s heart:

> At the center, accessible via a descending stair, lies a sacred crypt. Walls of pure white marble; eternal green‑and‑silver flames cast an otherworldly light.
>
> Upon a black onyx pedestal rests an ancient bronze tablet covered in arcane runes that glow with their own light. But the light is pulsing irregularly — like a sick heart. Some runes are dark; others flicker weakly.
>
> Ethereal silver chains — visible as faint distortions in the air — connect the tablet to five points in the walls, where symbols of the Five Gods are engraved. These chains are cracked, black fissures running along their length.

## The State of the Oath

One of the high priests, voice grave:

> “The Oath of Peace… is failing. With every passing day, more of its power dissipates. The runes that sustain it are going dark, one by one. When the last rune fades…”

He does not finish. All know what follows: the Oath ends, and the Titans are free to exact vengeance upon Thylea.

## Chondrus Intervenes

> Chondrus approaches the pedestal, hands behind his back, studying the tablet with academic fascination.
>
> “Fascinating, isn’t it? Five hundred years of peace… held by a single spell. But all things end.” He turns, eyes gleaming. “And when it ends, well… my lady, the Lady of Dreams, will be waiting. As will her brother‑husband, the Lord of Storms.”
>
> He circles the pedestal. “But perhaps… just perhaps… there is a way to **renew** the Oath. Or at least to **revise** it. The Titans are not irrational. They can be… **persuaded** — under the right conditions.”

## Atmospherics
- Sound: a low, constant hum emanates from the tablet — a distant, dissonant note.
- Temperature: markedly colder than the rest of the temple.
- Visual: brief visions flicker around the tablet — scenes of the Oath’s signing 500 years ago.
- Divine Presence: magically sensitive characters feel the waning presence of the Five converging here.

## What Chondrus May Reveal
If pressed or if the heroes show genuine interest:
1. Titans’ whereabouts: the Oath does not physically imprison them — it prevents full power over Thylea.
2. Sydon: resides in his tower, **Praxys**, in the ocean.
3. Lutheria: dwells on **Yonder**, somewhere in the Forgotten Sea.
4. Nature of the Oath: a mutual agreement, not a prison — but terms have been violated on both sides.
5. What the Titans want: depends on whom you ask… and what you’re willing to offer.
