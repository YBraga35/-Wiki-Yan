# **Invocation to the Muse**

Sing, O Muse, of memory and fate!
Sing and weave for us the tale of the saviors,
Of the Heroes,
Of the Champions.
Sing of the Odyssey of the Dragonlords,
And tell us their story once more.

Sing of Finn, the Timeless One, who slept in the heart of ages while the world turned without him.
Whose mother’s foresight shielded him from a bitter doom, now a stranger in his own land, a living echo of a forgotten dawn.

Sing of Cygon, the Doomed, born under a star of sorrow.
Whose every step is shadowed by a prophecy of ruin, yet who spits in the eye of destiny and dares to forge his own path in defiance of the threads.

Sing of Adonis, the Demigod, with the fire of Pythor burning in his veins.
Whose strength could shatter mountains and whose pride could challenge the heavens, walking the path of heroes to claim a place among the stars.

Oh Muse of ages past and futures yet unwritten!
Sing of Thylea in its hour of need, in its time of crisis and its moment of salvation!
Sing, O Memory,
And let their odyssey begin anew in our time!

## **Opening Scene: _Chapter 1: Heroes of the Prophecy_**

_Somewhere in the heart of a churning, tumultuous sea, a spire of black stone pierces the heavens. At its zenith, a great star is held captive, casting a sinister, crimson glow over the turbulent waters below. Harpies shriek and circle the storm-lashed structure, their wretched cries drowned by the crashing waves and the deafening peals of thunder._

_On the throne of Praxys, the Tower of Storms, two massive figures stand over a perfect, living effigy of the continent of Thylea, painted upon the floor. The painting is breathtakingly real—clouds drift across its surface and ocean waves lap against its shores._

_Sydon stalks the room, his fury made manifest as he hurls lightning bolts at the painted lands, causing the storm outside Praxys to intensify with each strike. From the shadows, Lutheria glides forth, a being wreathed in darkness. She halts Sydon with a hand to his chest, then gestures toward the Heartlands of Thylea. Sydon nods, preparing to loose another bolt, but Lutheria simply shakes her head. She reaches down, her pale arm touching the map of Thylea, and where she touches, a great crescendo of screams and moans rises from the land._

_"No, husband. The mortals no longer heed your storms. Let us instead send a great beast to defile the lands they stole from us. I am certain they will appreciate the irony."_

# **Prologue**

_The future of Thylea is wreathed in shadow. Monsters stalk the roads, and a cancer of corruption threatens the great cities. Sydon demands daily sacrifices in every temple, while his sister-wife Lutheria steals away children for her secret rites. The Oath of Peace, once a mighty bulwark that protected the settlers from the Twin Titans, has begun to fray like an old rope. With its power waning, the Five Gods find their hands are tied. And now, the Oracle has prophesied the Doom of Thylea: the death of the gods, the destruction of the sacred tree, and the utter annihilation of the mortal races._

_You are names whispered across Thylea, mortals destined for great deeds. The citizens of the great city of Mytros already tell tales of your exploits, both real and imagined. Now, it seems, the time has come for you to rise and meet your fate._

_You have been summoned to the edge of the Heartlands by a poet in the Oracle's service, in a bid to avert the coming cataclysm. She has a test for you, to determine if you are the heroes of her vision._

_You are to meet this poet, a woman named Kyrah, at the Sour Vintage vineyard. The Sour Vintage is a rustic tavern at a crossroads, some twenty miles east of the city of Estoria. Its exterior is rough-hewn timber painted black, and the sign hanging by the door depicts a woman carrying a basket of grapes. Everything is cast in the warm, welcoming glow of the late afternoon sun. Inside, a comforting haze of pipe-smoke hangs in the air. You hear the clinking of tankards and the low murmur of the patrons, most of whom are local farmers._

_The tavernkeeper, a retired hunter named Aetala, has already greeted you and poured your drinks. All of you have arrived, though you have not yet been introduced. It seems your patron is late, and for now, you have a moment to yourselves._

In alphabetical order, ask the characters to describe themselves and what they are doing in the tavern.

Allow the characters to interact for a while, then proceed to _Kyrah Arrives_.

## **Kyrah Arrives**

**DM Info: Kyrah the Poet**

```
Kyrah knows all the rumors that have been circulating about the heroes and makes a point to acknowledge their unique talents.

Kyrah is, in fact, the goddess of music. She makes little effort to disguise her identity, but she carries herself like any other mortal woman. If anyone suggests she might be more than she seems, she feigns innocence and changes the subject. The locals recognize her as a poet from Mytros, but most assume she was simply blessed with the goddess's appearance.
```

_A dark-haired woman sweeps into the Sour Vintage and beckons you all to a corner table. She wears a simple white traveling dress with practical leather boots, and carries a large satchel that seems to be overflowing with dozens of scrolls and writing supplies._

> _"Greetings. I am Kyrah. Thank you for answering my summons. I will be direct. I am here as an agent of the Oracle. She believes that great destinies await you all, but great destinies are forged by great deeds."_

> _"The Twin Titans have corrupted the heart of an ancient boar and unleashed it upon the Heartlands. It has laid waste to all forms of civilization and slaughtered everything in its path. This boar was sent to punish the mortals of Thylea for their continued faith in the Five Gods. Many have tried to hunt the boar. All have failed. You, however, will not."_

**(Allow the group to respond)**

**(If asked about the boar, the Titans)** _"Despite what their followers among the settlers might think, Sydon and Lutheria have always hated the mortals of Thylea. For five hundred years, their hatred was chained by the Oath of Peace. But now the Oath's power has weakened... This ancient beast is one of many signs that they are planning for war again. You must hunt this beast and sacrifice it to one of the powers that be in Thylea. Great fame and riches are tied to the offering of such a grand sacrifice to the gods, as you well know. The Order of Sydon has demanded the boar be sacrificed to the Lord of Storms, but everyone in Thylea knows such offerings can shift the balance of power among the gods, so I would strongly advise not granting the honor of the kill to either of the Twins."_

**(If asked about the Five)** _"Once they may have acted directly, but the Oath of Peace binds their hands. I cannot speak for the Gods, of course, but I suppose they can only do what is possible, especially when their own priests abandon their temples. In these dark times, I suppose it is the gods who must have faith in mortals, rather than the other way around."_

**(Assistance)** _"I understand there are some hunters here tonight who have met the boar before and survived, if barely. You might ask them about their experiences with the beast. Or perhaps hear what the common folk whisper of the creature. Maybe see what Aetala can sell you—she's as well-stocked as any trading post in the area. Furthermore, I have hired two hunters of my own to help track the corrupted boar. They are to return here tomorrow, an hour before dawn, and guide us to the beast."_

**(If accused of being _the_ Kyrah, Goddess of Music)** _“Me? The Goddess of Music, Deception, and the swiftest, cleverest, most enchanting creature in all of Thylea? You flatter me. I’m afraid not _(she laughs)._ My parents named me in honor of the Muse, hoping I would grow up to be half as beautiful and talented as she… It has been a large shadow to live in, I'm afraid… But! Things are changing! Now that I am with all of you, I can finally begin to make my own mark on Thylea! I intend to chronicle your travels and deeds, and write a history to make the Mother Goddess herself laugh and cry!”_

**(A tale of two bards)** _“Oh! Do we have more than one here who plans to record the tales of the Oracle's Chosen? Hmm. I have an idea—and it's a good one, believe me, all of mine are. Mostly. Sometimes. I like them, at least. Ahem. So, how about we **_both_** work on our own epic poems about the group's deeds. When all is said and done, we shall have them both performed in the theatre at Mytros. Whoever gets the most applause wins!”_

**(Reward)** _“Ah! Of course! In preparation for this very question, I have procured these,” Kyrah pulls something from her overstuffed bag, withdrawing a quiver of gleaming, golden arrows. “One for each of you! You may use them… or perhaps keep them as mementos of what will undoubtedly be a grand start to your journeys together! Once the beast is dead and a sacrifice has been made, I can offer them to you with Versi’s blessing.”_

Spending the Night

The heroes can spend the rest of the evening drinking and talking with the locals. This is a good opportunity for the heroes to learn about each other, as well as hear about recent events and rumors. Kyrah is willing to answer any questions they might have about Thylea. The heroes can also purchase basic supplies from Aetala, the innkeeper.

The Innkeeper

The innkeeper is a retired hunter named Aetala (NG veteran). She has eyes that have seen a thousand dawns and a grip that could still crush a cyclops's hand. She knows the Heartlands better than most and has all the rumors from Estoria. She recognizes at least one of the heroes and urges them to hunt the boar. Aetala made her fortune hunting a similar beast decades ago.

Wounded Hunting Party

Huddled over their ale, these hunters are nursing wounds both seen and unseen from their own hunt for the boar. They set a trap for the beast by leaving twenty pounds of rotten fruit in the middle of a field and hiding in the surrounding fences. The boar caught them by surprise. It gored one of their friends with its tusks and dragged him into the forest. They tried to follow, but the sounds were too horrible, and so they came here to drink to the memory of their fallen friend.

Desperate Farmers

Most of the other patrons of the tavern are farmers who are too afraid to tend to their fields. Their families are hiding in farmhouse cellars or have fled to the safety of Estoria until the beast has been slain. If any of the farmers recognize the heroes, they grow angry with them: why aren't they out hunting the boar? What good are heroes, anyway?

---

Hunting the Boar

Kyrah and the two hunters are waiting outside the tavern an hour before dawn. The hunters are named Taneias and Javon (LG scouts). The morning is cold and shrouded in mist, the ground wet with dew. The hunters lead the party away from the tavern and into the rolling hills to the north. The party passes many farms and homesteads that have been destroyed by the corrupted boar.

_As you press north into the Heartlands, you pass the ravaged remains of countless farms and homesteads, all gored and trampled by the boar. The very rocks and trees along the path have been gouged by its tusks, leaving deep, ragged trenches in the earth. Carrion crows flock in the fields, picking at the grim remains of failed hunters and unfortunate settlers._

The party must make a DC 10 Wisdom (Survival) check to try and track the corrupted boar. A failure means the heroes spend a full day in a fruitless search. You may choose to run a random encounter on the plains during the hunt, regardless of whether they succeed on this check.

The Boar's Lair

Eventually, the party stumbles upon a cave in the hills where the boar has dragged its most recent kills. The boar is inside the cave, messily devouring some unfortunate explorers.

_From this vantage point, you can see for miles across the rolling hills to the west. A thick blanket of fog clings to the lowlands, and you can hear a soft chorus of birds greeting the morning sun. Eventually, you find the boar's trail: another scar of wanton destruction, splintered tree trunks, and spattered blood. The trail ends at the shadowed maw of a cave. Guttural grunts and a sickening, wet tearing echo from within._

Setting Traps

The party may choose to set a variety of traps for the boar. Each trap requires a successful Wisdom (Survival) check to set. Failing the check causes someone to stumble and make a loud noise, alerting the boar to the party's presence. Trap possibilities include:

- **Rope Snare.** This trap requires 50 feet of rope. It will entangle the boar’s legs, halving its movement speed and preventing it from using any charge abilities. Setting it requires a DC 12 Survival check.
    
- **Spike Pits.** This trap requires digging a 10-foot-deep hole and filling it with sharpened wooden stakes. If the boar fails to leap over the pit, it will take 3d6 piercing damage and become restrained. Digging the pit requires a DC 12 Survival check.
    
- **Spike Barrier.** This trap requires setting up a row of sharpened stakes, angled toward the cave entrance. If the boar charges into the barrier, it will take 2d6 piercing damage. Building the barrier requires a DC 12 Survival check.
    

### **Encounter: The Boar Attacks**

At this point, have the party make DC 16 Wisdom (Perception) checks. Anyone who fails the check will be surprised during the ensuing battle. One of the hunters is killed as soon as the boar appears:

_Suddenly, a bestial roar erupts from within the cave. A massive boar bursts from the entrance, goring one of the hunters with its tusks! Tossing the body aside like a doll, the creature rears up to its full height. It is an ancient boar with fierce, red eyes and bloody, yellowed tusks._

The great boar has several spears and arrows stuck in its back from failed attempts to kill it. The boar is bleeding from these wounds, which causes it to suffer one level of exhaustion at the end of each of its turns in combat. Feeling cornered, the boar will attempt to attack each hero at least once.

### Death of the Boar

When the boar is reduced to 0 hit points, it can be captured with a net and tied with ropes. Once captured, the boar fixes its gaze on the heroes and speaks its final words directly into their minds. Everyone hears its booming voice inside their heads:

_“Know this, mortals. Your sins cannot be scoured away. An ancient power slumbers in the heart of Thylea, and when it wakes, the age of Mytros will come to an end. As my broken body falls to ruin and decay, so too shall all mortal things fall to death and darkness. No prophecy will save you.”_

The boar’s cave is only 50 feet deep and is filled with stinking water, gore, and boar droppings. If the heroes decide to brave the filth of the cave and explore, there is a small amount of treasure to be found.

**Treasure:** The two mangled bodies inside the cave can be searched. Between them, they have 25 gp and 37 sp. One of them carried a _silvered spear_. Kyrah notes that it is custom to bury or burn the dead, and that each body should have two gold pieces placed over the eyes, payment for the Ferryman.

### Response to the Sacrifice

**Thylea:** A profound silence falls for a moment as the sacrificial flames begin to die down. The crackling of a burning branch gives way to a great chorus of birdsong from the trees, which were absent due to the boar's presence. Other forest creatures curiously emerge from their hiding spots and seem to gaze upon you with a knowing look before retreating back into the woods.

**Kentimane:** The ground begins to tremble violently. All of you, including Kyrah, are thrown to the ground. The earthquake continues as a pair of colossal arms, wrought of earth and stone, tear their way out of the soil. They clamp their massive hands around the burning remains of the boar and drag the charred body deep into the earth. As suddenly as it began, all is silent again.

**Sydon:** The grey morning turns black as pitch. Storm clouds form and burst with violent fury. Lightning incinerates the boar's remains, and the impact throws all of you back several feet. Suddenly, the storm dissipates. The sky clears. The sun hangs above, shining cheerfully down upon you. A pleasant breeze rises, and soft white clouds drift across a perfect blue sky.

**Lutheria:** The boar’s corpse decays in an instant. Maggots appear in droves and devour the charred remains of the beast until only its skeleton is left. The skeletal remains pull themselves upright, and for a moment, a chilling fear grips your hearts. Then, a great laugh begins. It is surprised and eternally amused. A cold shiver runs down your spine, and the bones crumble into a foul-smelling dust.

**Mytros:** The grey clouds part above, and a silver light descends from the heavens. The clouds dissipate, and the cool light of a silver dawn takes their place.

**Volkan:** The flames of the sacrifice intensify, burning the boar's body to ash. The smell of the forge fills the air, along with the scent of pipe tobacco.

**Kyrah:** (see below: “Kyrah Revealed”)

**Pythor:** You hear laughter on the wind, a booming and boisterous laugh, pleased with your efforts and triumph. It fills you with a faint sense of pride.

**Vallus:** An uncanny feeling of surprise washes over you. It is cautious and curious. Then, it is gone. The flames continue to consume the boar.

### No Sacrifice

You can hear Kyrah say, _“This is a bad idea…”_ as she begins to back away from the boar’s body. Suddenly, the ground begins to tremble. Tornadoes begin to rip across the landscape as the furious powers of Thylea express their rage. Your bodies begin to burn with an intense pain. Your armor sloughs from your body like wet clay, your effects fall from your hands, and you find yourselves crouched on all fours.

### Handout: Failure to Sacrifice

_For the next twenty-four hours, Kyrah watches over your new pig forms as you root about aimlessly and roll in the dirt and mud. She guides you back to the Sour Vintage and awaits the curse's end. It eventually lifts, and you find yourselves mortal once more—though now more aware than ever of the consequences, however light today, of disrespecting the Gods and Titans of Thylea._
