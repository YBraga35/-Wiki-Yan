This guide consolidates the provided texts to create a cohesive and impactful opening scene for the campaign, beginning with the invocation of the Muse, moving through the encounter at the Sour Wine Tavern, and culminating in the hunt and sacrifice of the Great Boar.

### **Part 1: The Call and the Fury of the Titans**

Begin the session with ambient lighting and, if possible, soft, epic background music. Read the following invocation, personalized for the heroes present, to establish the mythical tone of the journey.

#### **Invocation to the Muse**

> Sing, O Muse, of memory and of destiny!
> Sing and weave for us the tale of the saviors, of the Heroes, of the Champions.
> Sing of the Odyssey of the Dragonlords and tell us their story once more.
>
> Sing of the Demigod, in whose veins runs the fire of the gods, destined to walk the path of heroes and claim a place among the stars.
>
> Sing of the Vanished One, an echo of a forgotten age, who seeks a lost past to find the glory of their future.
>
> Sing of the Doomed One, born under a star of sorrow, who defies death three times and faces it as an equal.
>
> Sing of the Haunted One, whose family was erased from memory, and who now hunts the shadows to bring back the light that was stolen from them.
>
> Sing of the Gifted One, who carries in their blood the fury of the storm, an unwilling heir to the power of the Titans.
>
> Sing of the Cursed One, whose bestial blood was tamed and channeled for good, seeking redemption for the sins of their ancestors.
>
> Sing of the Dragonslayer, whose soul was forged in the ashes of vengeance, destined to face the greatest beasts of Thylea.
>
> Sing of the Lost One, a stranger in a strange land, who navigates unknown waters in search of the way back home.
>
> Sing of the Bastard, the denied heir, who seeks the truth of their birth to avenge their mother and claim their rightful place.
>
> Sing of the Curious One, the chronicler of fate, whose quest for knowledge will place them in the front row of the final battle for the soul of the world.
>
> Sing of the Exiled One, whose journey of vengeance will lead them to reclaim their crown and their people from the clutches of tyranny.
>
> Sing of the Masterpiece, forged for war, but destined to find a soul and write its own song.
>
> Sing of the Myrmidon, the heir of the forgotten legion, who will raise the banner of their ancestors to unite mortals once more.
>
> Sing of the Psychopomp, who hears the laments of the dead and will descend into the underworld to rescue their beloved from the clutches of the Queen of Death.
>
> Sing of the Repentant One, the fallen sphinx, who walks among mortals to atone for their sins and perhaps, just perhaps, find forgiveness.
>
> Sing of the Timeless One, the child of prophecy, who slept in the heart of time to awaken in the darkest hour and face the legacy of their mother.
>
> O Muse of ages past and futures yet unwritten!
> Sing of Thylea in her hour of need, in her time of crisis, and in her moment of salvation!
> Sing, O Memory, and may their odyssey begin anew in our time!

#### **Opening Scene: The Fury of the Titans**

> _Somewhere in the heart of a tumultuous sea, a black stone tower pierces the heavens. At its zenith, a great star is held captive, casting a sinister, crimson glow upon the turbulent waters below. Harpies scream and circle the storm-lashed structure, their miserable cries drowned out by the crashing waves and deafening thunder._
>
> _On the throne of Praxys, the Tower of Storms, two massive figures stand over a living, perfect effigy of the continent of Thylea, painted on the floor. The painting is of a stunning reality—clouds float over its surface and the ocean's waves crash against its shores._
>
> _Sydon paces the room, his fury manifest as he hurls lightning bolts upon the painted lands, causing the storm outside Praxys to intensify with each strike. From the shadows, Lutheria glides, a being cloaked in darkness. She stops Sydon with a hand on his chest, then gestures toward the Heartlands of Thylea. Sydon nods, preparing to unleash another bolt, but Lutheria simply shakes her head._
>
> _She leans down, her pale arm touching the map of Thylea, and where she touches, a great crescendo of screams and groans rises from the land._
>
> _"No, husband. The mortals no longer heed your storms. Let us instead send a great beast to defile the lands they stole from us. I am sure they will appreciate the irony."_

### **Part 2: The Sour Wine Tavern**

The future of Thylea looks bleak. The Oath of Peace, which once protected the settlers from the Twin Titans, has begun to weaken. Now, the Oracle has prophesied the death of the gods, the destruction of Mytros, and the annihilation of the mortal races.

#### **Tavern Description**

> _You have been summoned to the frontier of the Heartlands by a poetess in the service of the Oracle, in an attempt to avert the impending cataclysm. The meeting point is the Sour Wine tavern, a rustic establishment at a dusty crossroads about twenty miles east of the city of Estoria._
>
> _The exterior is rough-hewn wood painted black, and the sign hanging by the door depicts a woman carrying a basket of grapes. Everything is bathed in the warm, welcoming glow of the late afternoon sun. Inside, a comforting haze of pipe smoke hangs in the air, mixed with the smell of spilled wine and rabbit stew. You hear the clinking of mugs and the low murmur of patrons, mostly local farmers trying to drown their worries about the drought and rumors of war. Behind the bar, wiping a mug with a cloth that has seen better days, is the taverness. She is a broad-shouldered woman with graying hair tied in a thick braid. Her eyes have seen a thousand suns rise, and her handshake, as you discovered upon arriving, could still crush the hand of a cyclops. She is Aetala, a local legend in her own right. You have all arrived, though you have not yet been formally introduced. It seems your hostess is late, and for now, you have a moment to yourselves._

#### **Conversations at the Crossroads**

- **Aetala, the Retired Huntress:** Over the tavern's fireplace rests a stuffed chimera head. If asked about it, Aetala smiles. _"Ah, old Scylla. Not a real chimera, of course. She was what we called a 'Cliff Chimera,' a three-headed beast that terrorized the canyons of Estoria in my youth. Had the fury of a lion, the stubbornness of a goat, and the cunning of a snake. Lost good friends to her. Took me three days to hunt her down. The fame bought me this tavern. a fair trade, I'd say."_

- **Aetala's Interactions with the Epic Paths:** Aetala is a collector of stories. After serving drinks, she might approach a character, starting a conversation based on something she observes.

 - **(Demigod of Pythor):** _"You're no local farmer. That glint in your eye... reminds me of the stories about the children of the gods. They say Pythor, especially, left many unclaimed heroes all over Thylea."_

 - **(The Vanished One):** _"Looking for something, stranger? The bards sing of a Dragonlord who never died, just... vanished. A sad story, about a promise unkept."_

 - **(The Doomed One):** _"Relax, friend. I heard a tale about a child that Lutheria marked for spite. They say death follows her like a shadow. A terrible fate."_

 - **(The Haunted One):** _"You carry a weight. Reminds me of a story about a family that was cursed to be forgotten by all. How can one mourn for those whose names the wind has carried away?"_

 - **(The Gifted One):** _"Don't like storms? Sydon's power is terrible. They say he once loved a mortal with the fury of a hurricane, and that his blood still runs through these lands."_

 - **(The Cursed One):** _"That's quite a mark. There are tales of families who made pacts with beasts or titans, and carry the mark of their bargain for generations."_

 - **(The Dragonslayer):** _"I see you have no love for monsters. The stories about the fall of that city... dragonfire... it leaves a mark on a nation's soul."_

 - **(The Lost One):** _"You're not from around here. There are legends of a ghost ship, the Ultros, that sails to worlds beyond our own."_

 - **(The Bastard):** _"That's a relic. An old one. Reminds me of the stories about House Arkelander. They say the King fears a prophecy about his own blood."_

 - **(The Curious One):** _"A scholar? Be careful what you write. They say the Oracle saw a chronicler among the heroes who will decide our fate."_

 - **(The Exiled One):** _"You fight like an Amazon. The legends say the royal line was not extinguished. That an heiress escaped, carrying a thirst for vengeance."_

 - **(The Masterpiece):** _"You move with a grace... unnatural. The dwarves tell legends of Volkan trying to forge a perfect soldier."_

 - **(The Myrmidon):** _"A soldier. Reminds me of the stories of the Myrmidons, the legion that never retreated. They say their spirit still lives on."_

 - **(The Psychopomp):** _"You look like you've seen a ghost. The souls of the dead are restless. They say some mortals can hear them, a terrible gift."_

 - **(The Repentant One):** _"You have old eyes in a young face. I heard from a sailor that a sphinx was cursed to walk as a mortal, to learn humility."_

 - **(The Timeless One):** _"You have a calm that doesn't belong to this age. The druids believe the true prophecy belongs to an heir of the lost Titans, a child of time itself."_

- **The Desperate Hunters (Javon and Taneias):** In a corner, two young men tend to their wounds. If approached, Javon explains: _"We were from a village east of here. 'Were' because there's no village anymore. That beast... it tore through our homes as if they were made of straw. We're the only ones who escaped. A poetess found us, gave us food and a purpose. Vengeance."_

- **Desperate Ranchers:** Most of the other patrons are ranchers afraid to tend their fields. If they recognize the heroes, they might get angry: "What good are heroes, anyway?"

### **Part 3: The Arrival of Kyrah**

_A dark-haired woman enters the Sour Wine with a quiet purpose. She wears a simple white traveling dress and carries a large bag overflowing with scrolls. Her eyes, an unusual violet, scan the room and land on you. She smiles and beckons for you to join her._

_"Greetings. I am Kyrah. I thank you for responding. I will be direct. I am here as an agent of the Oracle. She believes that great destinies await you, but great destinies are forged by great deeds."_

#### **The Gaze of the Muse**

_Kyrah then turns to each of you, and her gaze seems to pierce beyond appearances._

- **(To the Vanished One):** "A knight from a bygone era, seeking a crown. The Oracle has a special interest in stories that refuse to end."

- **(To the Bastard):** "A dragon coin... a powerful symbol. Symbols of heritage can be both a blessing and a curse, especially when the legacy comes from House Arkelander."

- **(To the Timeless One):** "You have your mother's eyes. Eyes that have seen suns the rest of us only know in legends. Versi... the First Versi... would be proud."

- **(To the Masterpiece):** "Forged for a purpose. But every song, even one of war, can learn a new melody. The question is: who will be the composer?"

- **(To the others):** "Each of you is a stanza in an epic poem that has yet to be written. The Oracle has seen your threads on the loom of fate."

#### **The Mission**

_Kyrah leans forward, her voice lowering._

_"The Twin Titans have corrupted the heart of an ancient boar and unleashed it upon the Heartlands. It has destroyed everything in its path. This beast is not just a monster; it is a message, a test of faith. Many have tried to hunt it. All have failed. You, however, will not fail."_

- **If asked about the Titans:** _"Sydon and Lutheria have always hated mortals. For five hundred years, their hatred has been chained by the Oath of Peace. But now the Oath's power has weakened. You must hunt this beast and sacrifice it to one of the powers of Thylea. The Order of Sydon has demanded the sacrifice be for the Lord of Storms, but I would strongly advise against granting the honor to either of the Twins."_

- **If asked about the Five Gods:** _"The Oath of Peace ties their hands. In these dark times, I suppose it is the gods who must have faith in mortals, rather than the other way around."_

- **If accused of being the Goddess Kyrah:** _"(Laughs) Me? The Goddess of Music? My parents named me after the Muse. I intend to record your travels and write a story to make the Mother Goddess laugh and cry!"_

- **About the reward:** _"Ah! Of course!" Kyrah pulls from her bag a quiver of golden arrows. "One for each of you! Once the beast is dead and a sacrifice has been made, I can offer them to you with Versi's blessing."_

### **Part 4: The Hunt and the Sacrifice**

At dawn, Kyrah, along with Javon and Taneias, guides you into the hills. The morning is cold and shrouded in mist.

_As you advance to the north, the destruction becomes evident. Farms have been torn apart. The very rocks and trees have been scratched by the boar's tusks. Carrion crows gather in the fields, pecking at the grim remains of failed hunters._

#### **Challenge 1: Finding the Trail**

The group must try to track the boar. Ask for a **Wisdom (Survival)** check.

- **Success (DC 15+):** You find the main trail easily. The tracks are fresh, and you notice patches of dark blood and magical ichor. The beast is wounded and moving predictably. You will gain advantage on initiative.

- **Partial Success (DC 12-14):** After a few hours, you find the trail. It's a path of pure destruction, easy to follow.

- **Failure (Below DC 12):** The trail is chaotic. You lose the track but stumble upon the remains of an old Gygan patrol. One of them still holds a leather map that reveals the location of a hidden spring, allowing you to recover resources as if you had taken a short rest.

#### **Challenge 2: Setting an Ambush**

The group can choose to set traps for the boar. Each trap requires a successful **Wisdom (Survival) check DC 12**. Failing the check alerts the boar.

- **Rope Trap:** Requires 50 feet of rope. Reduces the boar's speed by half and prevents charge abilities.

- **Spiked Pit:** Requires digging a 10-foot hole. If the boar falls in, it takes 3d6 piercing damage and is restrained.

- **Stake Barrier:** Requires setting up a row of stakes. If the boar runs into it, it takes 2d6 piercing damage.

#### **The Lair and the Confrontation**

_The trail of destruction ends at the dark mouth of a cave. Guttural grunts echo from within. Suddenly, a bestial roar explodes from the cave. A massive boar erupts from the entrance, goring Javon with its tusks! Tossing the hunter's body aside like a doll, the creature rears up. It is an ancient boar with fierce red eyes and yellowed, bloody tusks. Several spears and arrows are embedded in its back._

The boar is bleeding from previous wounds, which causes it to suffer one level of exhaustion at the end of each of its turns in combat.

#### **Death of the Boar**

When the boar is reduced to 0 hit points, it can be captured. Once restrained, it speaks directly into the heroes' minds:

_“Know this, mortals. Your sins cannot be atoned for. An ancient power sleeps in the heart of Thylea, and when it awakens, the age of Mytros will come to an end. Just like my broken body, all mortal things will fall into death and darkness. No prophecy will save you.”_

**Treasure:** In the cave, the bodies of two explorers can be searched, containing 25 gp, 37 sp, and a _silvered spear_.

### **Part 5: The Sacrifice and the Consequences**

The heroes must now sacrifice the boar to a power of Thylea.

- **Thylea:** A great chorus of birds sings, and forest creatures emerge to watch with a knowing gaze before retreating.

- **Kentimane:** The ground shakes violently. A pair of giant arms emerges from the earth, grabs the boar's remains, and drags it into the depths.

- **Sydon:** Storm clouds form and a lightning bolt strikes the boar. The storm dissipates instantly, revealing a perfect blue sky.

- **Lutheria:** The boar's body rots instantly, devoured by maggots until only the skeleton remains. The bones rise, a cold laugh echoes, and then they turn to dust.

- **Mytros:** The clouds part and a silver light descends from the heavens, bathing the site.

- **Volkan:** The flames of the sacrifice intensify, and the smell of metallurgy and pipe smoke fills the air.

- **Kyrah (The Muse):** _“You're sacrificing this to... to m--her? The Muse? That's... odd. Right. Oh, you know what? Never mind.”_ Kyrah turns away. _“I know this might be a shock, so brace yourselves.”_ She turns back. _“It is I, Kyrah... Goddess of Music, and with my power, I swear to protect you, the Heroes of the Prophecy.”_

- **Pythor:** Booming, satisfied laughter echoes on the wind, filling the heroes with a sense of pride.

- **Vallus:** A supernatural feeling of surprise, caution, and curiosity fills the air for a moment, and then vanishes.

- **No Sacrifice:** Kyrah says, _“This is a bad idea...”_ and steps away. The ground trembles, tornadoes ravage the landscape. The heroes' bodies burn with intense pain, and they find themselves transformed into pigs. For 24 hours, Kyrah watches them root in the mud before the curse ends, leaving them more aware of the consequences of disrespecting the gods.
