# GM Guide: Chapter 3 – Summoned by the King

This English file adapts the PT “03.01 - Convocados pelo Rei.md.” It frames the tone, the court, and key NPC stances for the opening palace scenes.

## 1) Arrival in Mytros: A City Under the Titan’s Gaze

> As you travel the Heartlands, the sky ahead darkens unnaturally fast. Black, swirling clouds gather over your destination, a bruise on the firmament. The wind howls with the scent of ozone and brine; rain begins — a fine mist at first, then a torrent that turns the road into a stream of mud.
>
> Nearing the gates, the true scale of the Titan’s wrath becomes clear. The marble‑and‑bronze jewel is scourged by a colossal storm. Lightning tears the heavens; the wind is so strong the city’s great banners are shredded. Streets are deserted, citizens shuttered in prayer.
>
> As centurions guide you through the gate, a thunderclap shakes the city. In the storm’s eye, a terrifying visage forms — Sydon’s three‑eyed face. His gaze sweeps the city, then fixes on you. With a snarl that vibrates your bones, he hurls a crackling bolt your way.

Atmosphere: the weather is malevolent — Sydon’s presence. Faith in the Five is low; the Order’s power grows through fear.

## 2) The Great Palace: Center of Mortal Power
- Exterior: atop a long stair; civic iconography (royal seals and law tablets) rather than divine statuary.
- Interior: marble colonnades to the Royal Hall; servants quiet and tense; bronze‑armored guards at rigid attention.

## 3) The Throne Hall: A Snake’s Nest
Paint the room; seat Acastus; set four figures upon the steps below: a timelessly beautiful queen; an imposing commander in cerulean; a sinister tiefling in black; a finely dressed young woman.

### King Acastus
- Appearance: charisma and power; expansive gestures; confidence of one “born to rule.” A DC 15 Insight may glimpse desperation beneath.
- Secret Drives: he is terrified — Estor’s ghost haunts him; Sydon’s storm threatens his reign. He needs the heroes to fix the crisis — but also to absorb any blame.
- Example lines:
  - “My advisors cannot agree. Therefore you, the Oracle’s Chosen, must decide our course. Mytros’ fate rests in your hands.” (masterful offloading of responsibility)
  - On Estor: “The ghost of my noble ancestor? Gods… a tragedy. I knew the ship was cursed, but not that his spirit was so tormented. This only proves the urgency of your mission. Give him peace — and perhaps the Titans give us peace.”
