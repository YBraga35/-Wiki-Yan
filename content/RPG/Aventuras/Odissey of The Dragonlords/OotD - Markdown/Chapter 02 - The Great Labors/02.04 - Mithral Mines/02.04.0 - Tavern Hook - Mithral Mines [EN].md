"The Mithral Mines... even the name feels heavy on my tongue, like tasting ash. You ask what they were like? Before... all this?

(She pauses, her gaze distant, as if seeing a ghost of the past.)

They weren't always a place of shadows and echoes. I remember flying over the Sunstone Peaks when they shone like a crown on the world. The entrance to the mines... it was a work of art, not just a hole in the mountain. It breathed light. Inside, the walls didn't just hold metal; they held starlight trapped in stone. The air hummed, not with danger, but with purpose. With the ringing of hammers struck in creation, not in war. It was a place of beauty, hero. A beauty we thought would last forever."



**(A rhythmic, deep chant, meant to be sung to the beat of hammers on anvils.)**

Clang the stone and split the seam,
Chase the silver, starlit dream!
Down below the sunless sky,
Where the veins of mithral lie!

Chorus:

Heave! Ho! The mountain's heart!
Strike the steel and play thy part!
Heave! Ho! The forges burn!
For the silver we shall earn!

Pick and shovel, sweat and might,
Carve our kingdom from the night.
Brighter than the moon above,
Tis the metal that we love!

Chorus:

Heave! Ho! The mountain's heart!
Strike the steel and play thy part!
Heave! Ho! The forges burn!
For the silver we shall earn!

From the depths, a treasure drawn,
To greet the light of Mytros' dawn.
Armour, shield, and shining blade,
By the mountain's hand be made!
