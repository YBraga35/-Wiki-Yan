# Minqena Yorusi

## Snapshot
- **Role**: Half-elf archivist directing the Library of Lore; lynchpin for planar research.
- **Motivation**: Preserve knowledge at any cost and decode Sogmonnath’s imprisonment, even if her conclusions risk catastrophe.
- **Leverage**: Desires rare texts, logical arguments, and promises that protect her stacks; threatens anyone who imperils the library.
- **Stat Baseline**: `Archmage` (MM p.342) tailored toward rituals and non-lethal defenses (animated stacks, teleport runes).
- **Deep Dive**: See [[npc/apend.01 - Integrated NPCs Complete Guide#Minqena Yorusi (Librarian + Scholar Fusion)|Integrated NPCs]].

## Arc by Act
- **Act I – Research Partner**: Supplies plague records and hints at planar contamination ([[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)#Investigation Hooks|Act I hooks]]).
- **Act II – Arson Target**: Library faces Rotarist inquisition and arson attempts; protecting her grants crucial evidence ([[02.00 - Act II - Political Intrigue (Lv5-7)#The Library Inquisition|Act II]]; coordination with [[apend.01 - Special Mechanics Guide#Library Inquisition Clock|Faction clock]]).
- **Act III – Ritual Architect**: Advises on temporal fractures and Sogmonnath’s prison, sometimes pushing dangerous solutions ([[03.00 - Act III - Rise of the Hero (Lv8-12)#The Time-Twister's Daughter|Act III]]).
- **Act IV – Expert Witness**: Provides historical testimony validating Maal’s jurisprudence and condemning Sefton’s manipulations ([[04.00 - Act IV - Cosmology and Faith (Lv13-20)|Act IV]]).

## Relationship Web
- **Navula**: Mutual fascination and frustration; trades grimoires under strict terms ([[npc/01.04 - Navula (EN)|Navula dossier]]).
- **Bonita Sandberg**: Shares research on plague victims; Bonita tempers Minqena’s risk-taking ([[npc/01.05 - Bonita Sandberg (EN)|Bonita dossier]]).
- **Mayor Lockton**: Strained—budget cuts and political pressure heighten her distrust.
- **Sefton Bane**: Unwittingly supplies him with historical texts until his schemes exposed ([[npc/01.02 - Sefton Bane (EN)|Sefton dossier]]).
- **Agent of Maal**: Becomes trusted partner once PC demonstrates respect for scholarship.

## Scene Hooks
- **Restricted Stack Access**: Requires multi-step favor (retrieve lost tome, defend library) before she grants entry; failure shifts her attitude to Wary.
- **Knowledge for Consequence**: Offers intelligence about Choir at cost of raising Planar Incursion clock unless PC persuades her otherwise (DC 16).
- **Memory Fragment**: Sharing a treasured memory (or accepting memory extraction) unlocks insight into Sogmonnath’s seals (see [[03.00 - Act III - Rise of the Hero (Lv8-12)#Adventure 10: The House of Reciprocities|Act III]] logistics).

## Mechanical Notes
- Assign `Reference Pool`: each fulfilled request adds 1 die (d6) PC can spend for advantage on Arcana/History rolls in library scenes.
- When threatened, she directs animated shelving (treat as `Animated Armor`) and uses `Wall of Force` to shield texts.
- Rewards include curated research packets, ritual schematics, and letters of scholarly introduction.
