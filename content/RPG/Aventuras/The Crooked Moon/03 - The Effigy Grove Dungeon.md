# Act 3 - The Effigy Grove Dungeon (55-60 minutes)

## Grove Atmosphere
*The Effigy Grove reeks of rotting vegetation and burnt offerings. Wicker figures hang from every tree, their hollow eyes seeming to track your movement. The crooked moon's light filters through the canopy in unnatural patterns, creating shadows that move independently of their sources.*

## Dungeon Overview
Three connected chambers leading to the ritual site. Each tests different skills while maintaining folk horror atmosphere.

## Entry Paths (Player Choice)

### The Wicker Road (Main Path)
*Hanging effigies create a gauntlet. Eyes definitely following you now.*
- **Trap:** Strangling vines (DEX save DC 13 or restrained)
- **Guards:** 1 Cultist watching from trees
- **Benefit:** Direct route, can see ahead

### The Crow's Route (Canopy)
*Climbing through twisted branches 20 feet up.*
- **Challenge:** Athletics DC 13 to navigate
- **Hazard:** Disturb crow swarm (alert guards) if failed
- **Benefit:** Bypass Room 1, surprise advantage in Room 2

### The Underway (Tunnels)
*Cramped root tunnels, barely 4 feet high.*
- **Challenge:** CON save DC 12 or one level exhaustion
- **Encounter:** 2 Giant Centipedes (CR 1/4 each)
- **Benefit:** Arrive behind enemies in Room 3

## Room 1: Atrium of Phases

### Description
*A circular clearing 30 feet wide. Five stone disks set into the ground, each bearing a moon phase symbol. Wicker archways mark the cardinal directions. The northern arch glows with sickly green light.*

### Lunar Phase Puzzle

**The Mechanism:**
Stone disks must be activated in the correct order to open the path forward. Wrong order triggers defenses.

**Phases Present:**
- New Moon (black circle)
- Waxing Crescent (right curve lit)
- Full Moon (white circle)
- Waning Crescent (left curve lit)
- Half Moon (half lit)

**Solution:** New → Waxing Crescent → Full → Waning Crescent → New
(Represents the harvest cycle)

**Skill Checks:**
- **Arcana/Religion DC 13:** Understand the lunar cycle connection
- **Investigation DC 14:** Notice worn patterns showing frequent use
- **History DC 12:** Recall harvest festivals follow moon phases

**Failure Consequence:**
- Wicker shards explode: 2d6 necrotic damage (DEX save DC 13 for half)
- Can retry (puzzle doesn't lock out players)
- After 2 failures, stones crack, path opens anyway but alerts guards

### Hidden Secret
**Perception DC 15:** Find carved message: "The fool's moon laughs at linear time"
- Alternate solution: Activate randomly while laughing (for Fool Warlocks)

## Room 2: Cornfield Crossing

### Description
*A narrow pathway winds through dense, 8-foot corn stalks. The path is 5 feet wide and 60 feet long. Whispers emanate from the corn - voices of missing villagers calling for help.*

### The Scythe Pendulums
**Mechanical Trap:**
- Animated scythes swing across the path every 10 feet
- **Perception DC 14** to spot the trigger lines
- **Pattern:** Swing every 6 seconds (start of each round)

**Crossing Challenge:**
- **Method 1:** Time the swings (Acrobatics DC 13, 2 successes needed)
- **Method 2:** Disable mechanism (Thieves' Tools DC 14)
- **Method 3:** Tank through (1d8 slashing per blade, 6 total)
- **Failure:** 1d6 slashing + difficult terrain from entangling corn

### Scarecrow Ambush
**Trigger:** Loud noise or 2+ failures

**Enemies:** 1-2 Scarecrows (reskin with wicker theme)

**Wicker Scarecrow Statistics:**
- **AC:** 11
- **HP:** 36 (8d8)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 11 (0), **DEX:** 13 (+1), **CON:** 11 (0)
- **Attacks:** 
  - Claw: +3 to hit, 2d4+1 slashing
  - Terrifying Glare: WIS save DC 11 or frightened
- **Vulnerabilities:** Fire damage
- **Immunities:** Poison, charmed, exhaustion
- **Special:** False Appearance (motionless = indistinguishable from normal scarecrow)

### Environmental Features
- Corn provides total cover beyond 5 feet
- Setting corn on fire: Deals 1d6 fire damage/round to area but spreads
- Voices are illusions (Investigation DC 13 to recognize)

## Room 3: Chamber of the Harvest Ritual

### Description
*A 40-foot wide ritual circle carved into ancient stone. Thirteen sleepwalking villagers stand at ritual points, chanting. At the center, a massive pumpkin-headed creature feeds on wisps of life force while cultists maintain a complex ritual. The Horned King's symbol burns in green fire.*

### The Ritual Components
1. **The Pumpkin Head** (center): Main threat
2. **Cultist Fanatic** (north point): Maintaining the ritual
3. **2 Cultists** (east/west): Protecting the fanatic
4. **13 Villagers** (circle): Channeling life force
5. **Ritual Focus** (altar): Stone carved with horned crown

### Initiative and Lair Actions

**Initiative Order:**
1. Lair action (on initiative 20)
2. Pumpkin Head
3. Cultist Fanatic
4. Cultists
5. Party

**Lair Action - Sap Tide:**
- Thick sap erupts from the ground
- 10-foot radius centered on one party member
- CON save DC 12 or:
  - 1d6 necrotic damage
  - Difficult terrain for 1 round
  - Stuck to ground (movement 0) until action to break free

### The Pumpkin Head (CR 3)

**Statistics:**
- **AC:** 15 (natural armor)
- **HP:** 52 (8d8+16)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 16 (+3), **DEX:** 10 (0), **CON:** 14 (+2)
- **INT:** 6 (-2), **WIS:** 10 (0), **CHA:** 14 (+2)
- **Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning, piercing
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** Fire (if blessed)
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, frightened

**Actions:**
- **Multiattack:** 2 vine whips or 1 vine whip and 1 seed spit
- **Vine Whip:** +5 to hit, reach 15 ft., 1d8+3 slashing + grapple (escape DC 13)
- **Seed Spit:** Ranged +2, 30 ft., 1d6+2 bludgeoning + grow vines (restrained, DC 12 STR to break)
- **Harvest Scream (Recharge 5-6):** 15-foot cone, CON save DC 13 or frightened 1 minute

**Monster Secret:** Blessed fire removes damage resistance for 1 round

### Cultist Fanatic (Modified)
- **AC:** 13 (leather armor)
- **HP:** 33 (6d8+6)
- **Spells:** 
  - Cantrips: Sacred Flame (necrotic themed), Thaumaturgy
  - 1st: Command, Inflict Wounds
  - 2nd: Hold Person, Spiritual Weapon (sickle form)
- **Concentration:** Maintains Bane on party or Bless on Pumpkin Head

### The Tension Clock

**Ritual Completion Timer:** 4 Rounds
- **Round 1:** Villagers' eyes begin glowing green
- **Round 2:** Grain spontaneously combusts on altar
- **Round 3:** Horned King's shadow appears
- **Round 4:** Curse takes root (see consequences)

**Advancing the Clock:**
- Each round the ritual continues: +1
- Each failed disruption attempt: +1
- Fanatic uses action to accelerate: +1

## Disrupting the Ritual

### Primary Method - Break the Circle
**Requirements:** 3 cumulative successes
- **Check:** Religion or Arcana DC 13 as an action
- **Limit:** Each PC can contribute once per round
- **Support:** Other PCs can help (advantage) instead of rolling

**Creative Disruptions (auto-success):**
- Physically move 3+ villagers from positions
- Destroy the altar with 20+ damage
- Cast Dispel Magic (3rd level) on the circle
- Use the children's rhyme with blessed flame

### Secondary Methods
**Save the Villagers:**
- Touch with iron: Breaks trance for 1 round
- Lesser Restoration: Saves one permanently
- Moving them: STR check DC 12 (they resist)

**Target the Leadership:**
- Killing the fanatic: Clock stops advancing
- Killing Pumpkin Head: Villagers wake immediately

## Environmental Hazards

**The Ritual Circle:**
- Stepping on lines: 1d4 necrotic damage
- Center area: Difficult terrain from animated vines
- Destroying sections: Thunder damage, force damage, or dispel magic

**Collapsing Grove:**
If ritual reaches round 4 but party still wins:
- Trees begin falling: DEX saves DC 12 or 2d6 bludgeoning
- Escape skill challenge: 3 successes before 3 failures

## Victory Outcomes

### Complete Success (Clock at 0-2)
- All villagers saved
- Harvest fully purified
- Grove can be cleansed and reclaimed
- Reward: Full payment + Effigy Amulet + village's gratitude

### Partial Success (Clock at 3)
- Most villagers saved (1d4 remain affected)
- Harvest has minor blight (10% crop loss)
- Grove remains tainted
- Reward: Full payment + healing potions

### Costly Victory (Clock at 4)
- Villagers saved but traumatized
- Harvest curse weakens but persists (25% crop loss)
- Grove becomes permanently corrupted
- Sets up future adventure hooks
- Reward: Half payment + village's wariness

## Ending the Adventure

### Resolution Scene
*As the Pumpkin Head falls, its grotesque form collapses into mundane vines and rotting gourds. The sleepwalkers gasp awake, confused and terrified. The sickly green light fades, and normal moonlight returns to the grove.*

**Skill Checks for Aftermath:**
- **Medicine DC 10:** Tend to awakened villagers
- **Religion DC 12:** Properly consecrate the grove
- **Investigation DC 14:** Find cultist correspondence (future plot hooks)

### Return to Enoch
*The village greets you with tears of joy and relief. Mayor Mara personally thanks each of you, her nephew Timothy clinging to her side. The harvest festival resumes with newfound vigor, and the wicker effigies are burnt in a great bonfire.*

**Rewards Ceremony:**
- Public recognition as Heroes of the Harvest
- Payment from Mayor Mara
- Choice of one magic item from village vault
- Free lodging in Enoch whenever needed
- Letter of recommendation to other settlements

### Future Hooks
1. Who leads the Horned King cult?
2. Other groves exist - are they also corrupted?
3. The Ghostlight Express brings word of similar incidents
4. A fragment of the Pumpkin Head regenerates...

## DM Combat Management

### Initiative Tracking
```
20: Lair Action (Sap Tide)
[Roll for creatures and party]
Bottom of round: Advance Tension Clock
```

### Quick Reference
- Most checks: DC 12-13
- Damage: Minions d6, Pumpkin Head d8, Lair d6
- Resistance: Remember fire vulnerability
- Clock: Visible to players (use tokens/tracker)

### Scaling Difficulty
**Easier (3 players/new players):**
- Remove 1 cultist
- Pumpkin Head: Use average HP (45)
- Clock: 5 rounds instead of 4

**Harder (5-6 players/experienced):**
- Add 1 scarecrow
- Pumpkin Head: Max HP (64) + 10 temp HP
- Lair action: 1d8 damage
- Clock: 3 rounds
