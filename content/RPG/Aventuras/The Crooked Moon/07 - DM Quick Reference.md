# DM Quick Reference Guide

## 2-Hour Session Timeline

### Pre-Game (15 minutes before)
- [ ] Print/load handouts
- [ ] Set up battle map for finale
- [ ] Review character connections
- [ ] Prepare NPC name list
- [ ] Set visible tension clock

### Time Management
- **00:00-00:05** - Opening narration, festival scene
- **00:05-00:15** - Brief roleplay, rumors
- **00:15-00:25** - Bat hound combat
- **00:25-00:35** - NPC interactions, get quest
- **00:35-00:60** - Investigation phase
- **00:60-01:00** - Travel to grove, optional encounter
- **01:00-01:15** - Puzzle room
- **01:15-01:30** - Scarecrow corridor
- **01:30-01:50** - Final battle
- **01:50-02:00** - Resolution and rewards

## Essential Information Flow

### Must Convey in Act 1
- ✓ Villagers are disappearing
- ✓ Horned King cult is involved
- ✓ Grove is the destination
- ✓ Fire/iron are useful

### Must Convey in Act 2  
- ✓ Grove location (northeast)
- ✓ Multiple entrance options
- ✓ Ritual is time-sensitive
- ✓ Blessed fire is especially effective

### Must Convey in Act 3
- ✓ How to disrupt ritual
- ✓ Tension clock stakes
- ✓ Villagers can be saved

## Impromptu Rulings

**Player wants to do something not covered:**
- Reasonable and cool? DC 12
- Difficult but possible? DC 15
- Should work but boring? Suggest cooler alternative
- Game-breaking? "Yes, but..." with consequence

**Combat taking too long:**
- Enemies flee/surrender at half HP
- Environmental collapse forces movement
- Reinforcements arrive in X rounds (pressure)
- Offer surrender terms

**Players stuck on investigation:**
- NPC approaches with clue
- Overheard conversation provides info
- Failed check reveals different clue
- "You also notice..." addition

## Contingency Planning

### If Players Miss the Grove
**Backup Discovery Methods:**
- Sleepwalker procession passes by
- Cultist attacks in town
- Grove lights visible from anywhere
- NPC directly asks for help

### If Players Split Up
**Focus Management:**
- 5 minutes max per group
- Use messengers between groups
- Converge at grove entrance
- Create urgent event requiring reunion

### If TPK Likely
**Escape Valves:**
- Mayor's guards arrive
- Granny's blessing activates
- Cultists take prisoners instead
- Environmental hazard affects enemies too

### If Too Easy
**Difficulty Spikes:**
- Add 1 scarecrow
- Fanatic gets 2nd level spells
- Lair actions every round
- Clock advances faster

## Common Rules Questions

**"Can I use X skill instead?"**
Generally yes if justified. Same DC.

**"Does my background help?"**
Yes - advantage or lower DC by 2.

**"Can I help?"**
Yes - helper must be proficient or explain how.

**"What do I know about X?"**
Ask for relevant skill check, provide appropriate info.

**"Can I ready an action outside combat?"**
Convert to group initiative if needed.

## Spell Effects vs Challenges

**Detect Magic:** Shows ritual circle, cursed items, enchanted villagers
**Detect Evil:** Overwhelming evil from grove, fiend presence
**Charm Person:** Works on cultists, not on undead/constructs
**Sleep:** Ineffective on sleepwalkers (already asleep)
**Dispel Magic:** Temporarily disrupts ritual, frees one villager
**Remove Curse:** Permanently frees one villager
**Protection from Evil:** Advantage vs Pumpkin Head

## Emergency Cheat Sheet

**Player says "I'm bored":**
- Immediate combat/action
- Give them specific task
- Ask what would excite them

**"This is too hard":**
- NPC provides help
- Lower next DC by 2
- Reveal enemy weakness

**"I don't know what to do":**
- Summarize three options
- Ask other players for suggestions
- NPC makes direct request

**Running over time:**
- Skip to final battle
- Narrate investigation montage
- Reduce final combat rounds

**Running under time:**
- Add second wave of enemies
- Require gathering components
- Add moral dilemma with villagers
