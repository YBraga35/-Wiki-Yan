# Temple of the Five and the Oath of Peace

## Descent to the Oath

> In a sacred crypt beneath the temple, eternal green‑and‑silver flames cast otherworldly light. Upon a black onyx pedestal rests an ancient bronze tablet of glowing runes — pulsing irregularly, like a sick heart. Ethereal silver chains link it to five wall‑seals of the Five Goddesses and Gods. The chains are cracked.

## Chondrus

> In a side chamber, the tiefling envoy of Lutheria smiles with delicate malice. “Five hundred years of peace held by a single spell… all things end. But perhaps the Oath could be renewed — or revised. The Titans are not irrational. Under the right conditions, they can be… persuaded.”

## The Sense of Failing Peace

> A low hum emanates from the tablet; the air is cold; flickers of the Oath’s ancient signing play in the air. Those sensitive to magic feel the waning presence of the Five converge here.
