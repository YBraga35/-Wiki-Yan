# Session One — The Night Messengers: Dagmara’s Final Messages

_(Tales from the Shadows — Yllmourne Adaptation, Solo + DMPC Format)_

---

## 1. Session Objective

Introduce the **Agent of Maal** (player character) and their companion **Nessus Underbough**, a halfling Divine Soul sorcerer and former merchant. Establish Yllmourne’s atmosphere, Dagmara’s murder, and the first supernatural encounter tied to the Mudskipper plague. By the end, the player should feel:

- Enveloped by dread and curiosity — the _investigation hook_ has sunk in.
    
- Responsible for Dagmara’s justice.
    
- Connected to Nessus as a genuine companion.
    
- Intrigued by Maal’s will and the mystery of Faylene.
    

---

## 2. System & Table Adjustments (Solo + DMPC, 5e 2024)

### Encounter Balance

- **Party Composition:** 1 PC (Lv3) + 1 DMPC support (Lv3 Divine Soul).
    
- **XP Adjustment:** Encounters should be drawn from _The Night Messengers (Lv1-2)_ module but tuned using the _Two-Player Encounter Matrix_ — treat 2 adventurers as a 4‑player party of Lv1.
    
- **Enemy Count:** No more than 2–3 active combatants at once. Others join as waves or cinematic tension.
    
- **Fail‑safes:** If combat spikes, Nessus can cast _Sanctuary_ or _Fog Cloud_ to stall.
    

### Action Economy Rules of Thumb

- **Nessus** always acts second. He prioritizes support (e.g. _Bless, Healing Word, Guiding Bolt_).
    
- **Player Spotlight:** Never solve problems _for_ the PC. Nessus advises, assists, or deflects blows.
    
- **Maal’s Intervention:** When stakes are high, allow brief “divine intuition” (advantage on key checks) — this replaces a missing party’s Help actions.

## 4. Scene 1 — Dockside Arrival

### Read‑Aloud

> _The voyage from the mainland is mercifully short, yet three days at sea are enough to learn the sailors’ whispered gospel of Yllmourne — the city where the gulls no longer land._  
> _When the ship rounds the headland, the skyline looms: warehouses clinging to the shore like barnacles, and higher still, the Governor’s Palace — white against a bruised sky._  
> _The stench reaches you before the dock — rot, quicklime, and the sweet hint of decay masked by incense. The symbol of Maal grows warm against your chest. Justice called you here, and justice reeks of the dead._

_(pause for reaction — let the player describe their posture or equipment)_

### NPC: Bonita, the Wharfmaster

- **Appearance:** Weather‑worn woman with rough hands, exhausted eyes.
    
- **Personality:** Honest, grief‑stricken, clings to duty.
    
- **Goal:** Seeks someone who still believes in order — Dagmara’s death broke her faith.
    

**Dialogue Sample:**

> “Dagmara said Maal’s justice would cleanse this harbor. Now they say her lungs filled with saltwater. You’re the first priest I’ve seen who doesn’t look afraid to breathe here.”

Bonita provides:

- Dagmara’s room key.
    
- Rumor that _the eyes were taken_, same as plague victims.
    

### NPC: Sergeant Harlan

- **Tone:** Lawful but pragmatic — “no good comes from stirring shadows.”
    
- Provides Dagmara’s death report (stabbed, throat cut, eyes removed).
    
- Warns the Agent to stay away from the _Slippery Eel Tavern_.
    

**Transition:**

> _A low fog begins to roll from the sea, clinging to your boots as you cross the cobblestones. The city watches — silent windows, shuttered prayers._

## 5. Scene 2 — Dagmara’s Room

### Setting

Small rented room above the _Slippery Eel_. Smells faintly of brine and incense. Desk overturned, window ajar.

### Clues

| Check               | Discovery                                                                            |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| DC 12 Investigation | Water stains on the floor — not from outside. Something was _poured_ or _bled_ here. |
| DC 14 Arcana        | The scent of salt is necromantic residue; a ritual connected to the Shadow Realm.    |
| DC 10 Religion      | Symbol of Maal drawn on the wall with charcoal, smudged by a handprint.              |


### The Diary (Player Prop)

> _“The plague is not born of disease, but of breath withheld. The drowned walk among us — their eyes see the depths we refuse to face.”_  
> _“Faylene prays in secret. I must confront her before the tide rises again.”_

### Nessus’ Commentary

> “You know… I used to trade with clerics of Onotas. They said faith was profit, not penance. Dagmara — she was different, wasn’t she? She believed law could still matter here.”

**Purpose:** deepen emotional stake, set name _Faylene_ as first suspect.

## 6. Scene 3 — The Night Messengers

### Setting

That night, the Agent rests in prayer at Bonita’s safehouse. The candlelight flickers, and shadows stretch unnaturally long.

### Cinematic Description

> _The flame gutters. The air turns cold. From the window, fog presses inward — and from within it, three figures take shape, each limned in faint moonlight._

1. **Little Bess** — a drowned child clutching a sailor’s charm.  
    _“It came from below… the idol sang… and the ship screamed.”_
    
2. **Old Torvin** — skeletal harbor master wheezing seawater.  
    _“The plague isn’t sickness… it’s drowning turned inside out.”_
    
3. **Dagmara** — pale, throat slit, eyes gone.  
    She points toward the lighthouse, mouthing _“Faylene.”_
    

### Mechanics

- **Wisdom Save (DC 13)** vs. fear (or roleplayed terror).
    
- **Divine Insight:** On success, Maal grants a brief vision — a crimson tide lapping at the base of a distant lighthouse.
    
- **Boons:** Advantage on Insight against cultists in next session.
    

### Nessus’ Reaction

> “I felt them too. The air went heavy — like the moment before a deal goes bad. But if Dagmara spoke your name from beyond, then maybe we’re meant to finish what she began.”

**End Beat:** thunder rolls; a scream outside — another body pulled from the harbor, eyes missing.

> _Fade to black._

## 7. Pacing & Cinematic Rhythm

### Structure for a 3‑Hour Session

1. **Arrival & Introduction (30 min)** – establish tone, explore docks.
    
2. **Investigation at Dagmara’s room (60 min)** – exploration, roleplay, tension.
    
3. **Supernatural encounter (60–90 min)** – ghost sequence + reflection + cliffhanger.
    

### Techniques for Flow

- Keep combat cinematic — 3–4 rounds max.
    
- End each scene with an image or echoing line (e.g., “The symbol of Maal glows faintly, as if watching.”)
    
- Between scenes, let Nessus bridge transitions with light, grounded commentary — the _Watson_ to the Agent’s _Holmes_.
