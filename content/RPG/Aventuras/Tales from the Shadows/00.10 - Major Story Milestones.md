# Major Story Milestones

## Purpose & Design Philosophy

This campaign uses **milestone leveling** tied to narrative achievements rather than XP accumulation. Each milestone represents a significant story beat that advances the overarching conspiracy, develops character relationships, and reveals new layers of the planar intrigue.

**Design Principles**:
- Levels 1-4 focus on local investigation (mystery building)
- Levels 5-7 expand to political intrigue (naval conflict)
- Levels 8-12 escalate to heroic action (planar threat)
- Levels 13-20 culminate in cosmic resolution (divine tribunal)

**Milestone Triggers**: PCs achieve milestones by completing objectives, making discoveries, or reaching dramatic turning points - NOT by defeating specific enemies. Combat is often a consequence, not the goal.

## Act I: Investigation Phase (Levels 1-4)

### Milestone 1: Uncovering the First Victim's Secret
**Level 1 → 2**

- **Act**: Act I - Investigation Phase
- **Trigger**: PC successfully investigates Dagmara's death and discovers she was murdered (not random plague death) due to her knowledge of magical corruption in plague patterns
- **Revelation**: The plague is not natural disease - it has magical components. Dagmara documented early victims showing signs of curse magic rather than biological infection.
- **Consequences**:
  - PC gains Bonita as ally (she provides Dagmara's hidden journal)
  - Deputy Marshal Harlan officially partners with PC
  - Mayor Lockton becomes nervous and obstructive
  - Mudskippers begin appearing (undead plague victims)
- **NPC Reactions**:
  - **Bonita**: Tearfully grateful someone is investigating; becomes devoted ally
  - **Harlan**: Validates PC's authority; opens official investigation files
  - **Lockton**: Issues warnings about "causing panic" and "overstepping authority"
  - **One-Eyed Cy**: Sends word through intermediaries - wants to talk
  - **Sefton Bane**: Takes note of PC (doesn't act yet, but aware)
- **Faction Clock Changes**:
  - Shadow Conspiracy clock +1 (they're aware of investigation)
  - Civic Authority clock -1 (corruption being exposed)
  - Underground Alliance clock +1 (Cy interested in cooperation)
- **Player Reward**:
  - Level 2
  - Dagmara's journal (crucial evidence)
  - Reputation: "The One Who Listens" (NPCs trust PC to investigate thoroughly)
- **Foreshadowing**: Journal contains sketches of shadow-touched victims with strange marks - same marks later seen on Callista's curse and on victims of Sefton's magic
- **DM Notes**:
  - Make Dagmara's death feel personal through Bonita's grief
  - Emphasize PC's choice to investigate vs. accept easy answers
  - First hint that religious authority (Faylene) is connected somehow
  - Introduce concept of mudskippers rising as plague consequence

### Milestone 2: Surviving the Shadow Manifestation
**Level 2 → 3**

- **Act**: Act I - Investigation Phase
- **Trigger**: PC encounters first direct shadow creature manifestation during fog bank investigation on barrier islands. Survives either through combat, clever escape, or negotiation with The Ferryman.
- **Revelation**: Shadow realm is bleeding through into material world. The plague creates "thin spots" where creatures cross over. Not random - something is deliberately weakening barriers between planes.
- **Consequences**:
  - PC gains firsthand knowledge of shadow realm danger
  - Navula becomes accessible (she's been waiting for someone who's "seen the shadows")
  - Scholar Friedel arrives in Yllmourne (drawn by planar anomalies)
  - Shadow attacks increase in frequency
- **NPC Reactions**:
  - **Navula**: "So you've seen them. Good. Now you'll listen to what I have to say."
  - **One-Eyed Cy**: Reveals he lost his eye to shadow creature; shares knowledge
  - **Friedel**: Enthusiastically offers to help investigate (hides his deterioration)
  - **The Ferryman**: If encountered, cryptically offers "passage when the time comes"
  - **Faylene**: Increasingly frantic prayers seem to make shadow manifestations worse
- **Faction Clock Changes**:
  - Shadow Conspiracy clock +2 (manifestations accelerating)
  - Knowledge Seekers clock +1 (multiple researchers now involved)
  - Plague Witnesses clock +1 (survivors reporting shadow sightings)
- **Player Reward**:
  - Level 3
  - Shadow-touched item (weapon/armor that functions in shadow realm)
  - Contact with Navula (access to witch's knowledge)
  - Title: "Shadow-Marked" (NPCs who know about shadows recognize PC as experienced)
- **Foreshadowing**: The Ferryman's cryptic warning about "when the waters stop flowing" hints at Act IV's cosmic stakes. Shadow creatures seem to be searching for something specific.
- **DM Notes**:
  - Make this encounter genuinely frightening - establish shadow realm as serious threat
  - PC should feel outmatched, requiring creativity to survive
  - Introduce Ferryman as mysterious rather than immediately hostile
  - This shifts campaign from mystery investigation to supernatural horror

### Milestone 3: Preventing the Public Lynching
**Level 3 → 4**

- **Act**: Act I - Investigation Phase
- **Trigger**: PC uncovers plot to scapegoat innocent person (likely foreign merchant or Navula herself) for plague. Must prevent mob justice through evidence, persuasion, or intervention.
- **Revelation**: Fear is being deliberately cultivated. Someone wants Yllmourne destabilized and turning on itself. Lockton's corruption goes deeper than incompetence - he's actively facilitating chaos.
- **Consequences**:
  - PC's reputation solidified as protector (or becomes target if failed)
  - Sefton Bane takes direct notice (may send Pale Maiden to observe)
  - Community divides between PC's supporters and fearmongers
  - Harlan forced to choose between orders and justice (chooses justice)
- **NPC Reactions**:
  - **Harlan**: Defies Mayor's orders to support PC; permanent ally gained
  - **Lockton**: Openly hostile now; tries to have PC removed from investigation
  - **Bonita**: Treats scapegoat victim; confirms they're innocent
  - **Minqena**: If Library or foreign scholar targeted, becomes crucial ally
  - **Sefton**: (from distance) Impressed by PC's ability to sway crowds
- **Faction Clock Changes**:
  - Civic Authority clock -2 (Lockton's corruption partially exposed)
  - Underground Alliance clock +2 (Cy backs PC publicly; Wren spreads word)
  - Shadow Conspiracy clock +1 (Sefton adjusts plans around PC)
- **Player Reward**:
  - Level 4
  - Public support (can call on NPC allies for aid)
  - Official authority from Maal's temple to continue investigation
  - Enemy: Lockton now actively working against PC
- **Foreshadowing**: Evidence suggests outside agitators stirred mob - maritime connections to Hilbre Isle. First concrete link to Sefton Bane's involvement.
- **DM Notes**:
  - This is moral choice milestone - PC's ethics determine approach
  - Failed milestone doesn't end campaign; scapegoat's death becomes motivation
  - Excellent opportunity for PC to demonstrate character values
  - Transitions from investigation to active heroism
  - End of Act I - PC now recognized hero and target

## Act II: Political Intrigue Phase (Levels 5-7)

### Milestone 4: Exposing the Library Arson Plot
**Level 4 → 5**

- **Act**: Act II - Political Intrigue Phase
- **Trigger**: PC discovers and prevents plot to burn Library (destroying historical records of Sogmonnath's imprisonment). Confronts arsonists and traces conspiracy to Sefton's agents.
- **Revelation**: Someone wants to erase knowledge of how ancient threat was imprisoned. Historical records contain counter-ritual to current curse. Library contains maps of shadow realm thin spots.
- **Consequences**:
  - Minqena becomes permanent ally and research partner
  - Access to restricted Library sections (Sogmonnath's history revealed)
  - First direct confrontation with Sefton's mortal agents
  - Friedel's obsession intensifies (he wanted those books)
- **NPC Reactions**:
  - **Minqena**: Eternally grateful; opens entire Library to PC
  - **Friedel**: Increasingly unstable; may have been involved (DM choice)
  - **Professor Aldric**: Arrives to help catalog saved materials
  - **Sefton**: Sends message through captured agent: "An invitation to Hilbre Isle"
  - **Navula**: "So you found the old records. Now you know what sleeps in the mountain."
- **Faction Clock Changes**:
  - Knowledge Seekers clock +3 (Library preserved; research accelerates)
  - Shadow Conspiracy clock +1 (forced to use more direct methods)
  - Civic Authority clock -1 (Lockton's failure to protect Library)
- **Player Reward**:
  - Level 5
  - Historical documents about Sogmonnath
  - Library research access (can investigate any lore question)
  - Sefton's "invitation" (plot hook to Act II climax)
- **Foreshadowing**: Records show Sogmonnath was imprisoned by combined effort of Navula's predecessor, ancient Maal priests, and sacrifice of champion (parallel to Callista's situation). Breaking the seal requires specific ritual - exactly what Faylene's hexed prayers are doing.
- **DM Notes**:
  - Make library fire dramatic - racing against flames to save crucial texts
  - Captured arsonist can provide intel on Sefton (interrogation scene)
  - This milestone shifts focus from plague to larger conspiracy
  - Excellent moment to introduce Sogmonnath as name/legend

### Milestone 5: Breaking Faylene's Hex
**Level 5 → 6**

- **Act**: Act II - Political Intrigue Phase
- **Trigger**: PC identifies source of Faylene's corruption (hexed by Sefton's contract magic), gathers components for counter-ritual, and successfully frees her from compulsion.
- **Revelation**: Faylene's prayers weren't failing - they were being twisted into curse amplification. Sefton has been using her unwitting devotion to spread plague and weaken planar barriers. She's been his primary tool, not his enemy.
- **Consequences**:
  - Plague spread slows significantly (not stopped, but manageable)
  - Faylene becomes powerful ally with restored divine magic
  - Rotarist Church mobilizes to help (organized healing efforts)
  - Sefton escalates to direct action (no more subtle manipulation)
  - Bonita can finally make headway treating patients
- **NPC Reactions**:
  - **Faylene**: Devastated by revelation but grateful; swears oath to PC
  - **Bonita**: Medical breakthrough now possible; teams with Faylene
  - **Sefton**: Furious; sends Pale Maiden with kill order
  - **Navula**: Grudging respect for Church now that hex is broken
  - **Friedel**: Fascinated by hex mechanics; wants to study residual magic
- **Faction Clock Changes**:
  - Plague Witnesses clock +2 (healing becomes possible)
  - Shadow Conspiracy clock -2 (major setback; forced to change tactics)
  - Civic Authority clock +1 (Church restores public confidence)
- **Player Reward**:
  - Level 6
  - Faylene's divine blessing (permanent boon: advantage on saves vs. curses)
  - Church resources (healing, sanctuary, information network)
  - Enemy escalation: Pale Maiden now hunting PC
- **Foreshadowing**: Broken hex reveals Sefton used fey contract law - he's not fully human. His contracts have specific binding conditions that can be exploited. Faylene's restored prayers begin to reveal Sogmonnath's location in visions.
- **DM Notes**:
  - Make Faylene's realization heartbreaking - she's been helping kill her flock
  - Counter-ritual should require PC sacrifice (component, promise, or risk)
  - This is major victory that makes Sefton take PC seriously as threat
  - Excellent moment for PC to demonstrate compassion vs. judgment
  - Pale Maiden's first assassination attempt should occur soon after

### Milestone 6: Surviving the Pale Maiden's First Hunt
**Level 6 → 7**

- **Act**: Act II - Political Intrigue Phase
- **Trigger**: PC survives first assassination attempt by Pale Maiden. Either defeats her in combat (unlikely), escapes cleverly, or negotiates temporary truce. Survives "first encounter" mentioned in her challenge.
- **Revelation**: Sefton Bane is shadow fey hybrid with bound servants. He operates by fey contract law. Pale Maiden is testing PC, not simply executing - she wants worthy opponent, not easy kill.
- **Consequences**:
  - PC recognized as serious threat to shadow conspiracy
  - Pale Maiden becomes recurring antagonist (but complex one)
  - Need for stronger allies becomes obvious (leads to naval alliance)
  - Shadow fey court becomes aware of PC (complications)
- **NPC Reactions**:
  - **Pale Maiden**: "You survived. Interesting. We will meet again." [Vanishes]
  - **Sefton**: Intrigued rather than angry; considers recruiting PC
  - **Callista**: Recognizes Pale Maiden's curse style; offers to help train PC
  - **Navula**: "She's testing you. Shadow fey don't hunt - they cultivate. You're being grown into something."
  - **One-Eyed Cy**: Offers PC protection; "You're in deep now. Need proper backup."
- **Faction Clock Changes**:
  - Shadow Conspiracy clock +1 (PC now priority target)
  - Underground Alliance clock +2 (criminal network rallies to protect PC)
  - PC Reputation clock +3 (surviving shadow fey assassin is legendary)
- **Player Reward**:
  - Level 7
  - Pale Maiden's token (she leaves something behind - sign of respect)
  - Callista as companion (she offers to train/protect PC)
  - Knowledge of Sefton's true nature
- **Foreshadowing**: Pale Maiden's contract can be broken if PC discovers binding conditions. She hints at wanting freedom but can't act against Sefton directly. Her "three encounters" challenge suggests she's cultivating PC as champion who might free her.
- **DM Notes**:
  - First encounter should be terrifying but survivable
  - Pale Maiden should demonstrate superiority but choose not to kill
  - This establishes her as complex character, not simple villain
  - PC should realize they need allies for what's coming
  - Excellent transition to Thranengi naval conflict (need fleet to confront Sefton)

### Milestone 7: Breaking the Thranengi Blockade
**Level 7 → 8**

- **Act**: Act II - Political Intrigue Phase (transitioning to Act III)
- **Trigger**: PC organizes coalition (Wren's pirates, allied merchants, possibly naval support) to break Thranengi naval blockade preventing aid from reaching Yllmourne. Confronts Sefton's maritime empire directly.
- **Revelation**: Sefton has been using blockade to weaken Yllmourne while consolidating shadow fey power on Hilbre Isle. Thranengi officials are either bribed or hexed. His maritime empire is front for building shadow court in material world.
- **Consequences**:
  - Aid reaches Yllmourne (economic and medical relief)
  - Sefton forced to abandon subtle approach; retreats to Hilbre Isle
  - Naval battle establishes PC as military leader, not just investigator
  - Access to barrier islands and Hilbre Isle now possible
  - Act II ends; Act III begins with shift to heroic action
- **NPC Reactions**:
  - **Wren Toothtaker**: "Best damn naval battle I've ever fought! You're alright, Maal's agent!"
  - **Sefton**: Direct confrontation (likely shipboard); reveals shadow fey nature; issues formal challenge
  - **Lockton**: Forced to publicly acknowledge PC's heroism (privately seething)
  - **Harlan**: Promoted to Marshal; PC deputized with official authority
  - **One-Eyed Cy**: "You're not just investigating anymore. You're at war."
- **Faction Clock Changes**:
  - Shadow Conspiracy clock -3 (major strategic defeat)
  - Underground Alliance clock +2 (successful military coordination)
  - Civic Authority clock +2 (PC has undeniable legitimacy now)
  - Naval Power clock established (PC commands fleet assets)
- **Player Reward**:
  - Level 8
  - Naval command authority (can requisition ships)
  - Wren's loyalty (permanent ally)
  - Sefton's formal challenge (invitation to Hilbre Isle for "resolution")
  - Title: "Breaker of Chains" (freed Yllmourne from blockade)
- **Foreshadowing**: During battle, PC sees shadow islands appearing and disappearing in fog - planar barriers are failing. Sefton's retreat is strategic; he's preparing shadow court for full manifestation. Sogmonnath's awakening is imminent.
- **DM Notes**:
  - Epic naval battle - use skill challenges and mass combat rules
  - PC should feel like military commander coordinating forces
  - Sefton escapes but this is clear victory for PC
  - Transitions from intrigue to heroic fantasy tone
  - This is end of Act II - celebrate the victory before Act III escalates

## Act III: Heroic Rise Phase (Levels 8-12)

### Milestone 8: First Confrontation with Sogmonnath
**Level 8 → 9**

- **Act**: Act III - Heroic Rise Phase
- **Trigger**: PC ventures into Waterlogged Mountain and discovers Sogmonnath's prison. First direct interaction with ancient imprisoned entity. Must resist her influence and escape without breaking final seals.
- **Revelation**: Sogmonnath is awakening because Sefton has been deliberately weakening seals using plague deaths as sacrifices. She's ancient sorcerer-queen who attempted planar conquest centuries ago. Navula's predecessor helped imprison her. Current plague is echo of her original curse.
- **Consequences**:
  - PC understands true scope of threat (cosmic, not local)
  - Sogmonnath aware of PC; begins direct manipulation attempts
  - Need to find original imprisonment ritual becomes urgent
  - Time pressure intensifies (seals degrading daily)
- **NPC Reactions**:
  - **Sogmonnath**: "Maal's chosen. How delightful. Tell me, does your god of justice approve of eternal imprisonment without trial?"
  - **Navula**: "You saw her. Now you understand. This is what I've been preventing for two centuries."
  - **Sefton**: "She promised me power beyond mortality. All I had to do was feed the seals. You're ruining everything."
  - **Friedel**: Obsessed with speaking to Sogmonnath; increasingly dangerous
  - **Professor Aldric**: Horrified; helps research imprisonment ritual desperately
- **Faction Clock Changes**:
  - Shadow Conspiracy clock +3 (Sogmonnath active participant now)
  - Knowledge Seekers clock +2 (urgent research focus)
  - Plague Witnesses clock -1 (deaths accelerating as seals weaken)
  - Cosmic Stakes clock established (world-ending threat acknowledged)
- **Player Reward**:
  - Level 9
  - Fragment of original seal (can be studied)
  - Sogmonnath's "interest" (she contacts PC in dreams)
  - Knowledge of full conspiracy scope
- **Foreshadowing**: Sogmonnath mentions "the trial that was denied me" - plants seed for Act IV's tribunal. She's not simply evil; she was imprisoned without divine judgment. This is why Maal's agent is perfect opponent/advocate. She offers PC power in exchange for "proper trial."
- **DM Notes**:
  - Sogmonnath should be terrifying but also articulate and persuasive
  - She has valid grievance (imprisoned without trial) that complicates morality
  - PC should feel outmatched; retreat is appropriate response
  - This raises stakes from local plague to planar catastrophe
  - Introduces theme of justice vs. expedience (central to Act IV)

### Milestone 9: Freeing Callista or Losing Friedel
**Level 9 → 10**

- **Act**: Act III - Heroic Rise Phase
- **Trigger**: PC must choose: use counter-ritual components to cure Callista's shadow curse OR prevent Friedel from completing his transformation into shadow creature. Cannot save both with available resources.
- **Revelation**: Shadow corruption is contagious and progressive. Sefton's power comes from deliberately corrupting mortals and binding them to shadow court. Every cursed individual strengthens shadow realm's presence in material world. Sogmonnath's awakening accelerates all shadow transformations.
- **Consequences**:
  - **If Callista saved**: Loyal warrior ally gained; Friedel becomes shadow-corrupted mini-boss
  - **If Friedel saved**: Academic knowledge preserved; Callista's heroic sacrifice (she fights until transformation, PC must mercy-kill)
  - **If neither saved**: Both become enemies; PC gains different resources
  - Moral weight of choice affects PC's reputation and divine standing
- **NPC Reactions**:
  - **Callista** (if saved): "You gave me my life back. I am yours until death."
  - **Friedel** (if corrupted): "You abandoned me! I'll show you what I've learned in the shadows!"
  - **Navula**: "Hard choices define heroes. You chose. Live with it."
  - **Bonita**: Grief regardless of choice (both were patients she couldn't save alone)
  - **Pale Maiden**: Respect if PC made tactical choice; disdain if PC tried to save both and failed
- **Faction Clock Changes**:
  - Shadow Conspiracy clock +1 or +2 (gains corrupted servant)
  - PC Companions clock adjusted (different ally gained/lost)
  - Moral Authority clock established (PC's ethical standing)
- **Player Reward**:
  - Level 10
  - Companion loyalty (Callista) OR Academic knowledge (Friedel's research if saved)
  - Lesson about resource scarcity and consequence
  - Reputation shift based on choice
- **Foreshadowing**: This choice mirrors Act IV's tribunal - judging who deserves salvation. Sogmonnath watches PC's decision and comments on "the arbitrariness of mercy." Shows PC that even good intentions require hard choices.
- **DM Notes**:
  - Telegraph this choice in advance so PC can prepare emotionally
  - Both options should have clear benefits and costs
  - No "right" answer - consequences flow from PC's values
  - Corrupted character becomes recurring tragic antagonist
  - This tests PC's heart before cosmic stakes of Act IV

### Milestone 10: Claiming Shadow Court Sanctuary
**Level 10 → 11**

- **Act**: Act III - Heroic Rise Phase
- **Trigger**: PC infiltrates or assaults Sefton's shadow court on Hilbre Isle. Either defeats Sefton, forces truce, or exposes him to Thranengi authorities. Claims sanctuary/territory where shadow manifestations can be controlled.
- **Revelation**: Sefton built shadow court as anchor point for merging planes. Hilbre Isle has natural thin spot between realms. By controlling it, PC can regulate planar bleeding. Sefton's contracts with shadow fey can be inherited or broken by defeating him.
- **Consequences**:
  - **If Sefton defeated**: Shadow court servants (including Pale Maiden) potentially freed
  - **If Sefton survives**: Becomes recurring antagonist but weakened
  - PC gains base of operations with planar properties
  - Access to shadow realm for travel/research
  - Reduction in random shadow manifestations
- **NPC Reactions**:
  - **Pale Maiden**: If contract broken, offers service to PC or departs freed
  - **Sefton**: (if defeated) "You don't understand what you've done. I was CONTROLLING the merger. Without me, it will happen chaotically."
  - **Ferryman**: "You now hold the threshold. Guard it well, or all will cross who should not."
  - **Navula**: "Finally. A proper keeper. Your predecessor held that post for sixty years."
  - **Sogmonnath**: "Excellent. You're becoming powerful enough to be useful."
- **Faction Clock Changes**:
  - Shadow Conspiracy clock -4 (Sefton's infrastructure destroyed/claimed)
  - PC Power clock +3 (commands shadow court resources)
  - Planar Stability clock established (PC can influence barrier strength)
- **Player Reward**:
  - Level 11
  - Hilbre Isle sanctuary (controllable shadow realm access)
  - Shadow court servants (if contracts broken properly)
  - Planar travel capability
  - Title: "Threshold Guardian" or "Court-Breaker"
- **Foreshadowing**: Controlling shadow court gives PC leverage in Act IV tribunal - demonstrates ability to manage planar boundaries. Sefton's warning about "chaotic merger" is accurate; PC must now maintain what he built. Sogmonnath's interest intensifies; she sees PC as potential liberator.
- **DM Notes**:
  - This is major power spike moment - PC becomes planar authority
  - Sefton makes compelling argument that he was lesser evil
  - Pale Maiden's freedom (if achieved) should feel earned
  - Sanctuary provides home base for remainder of campaign
  - Shifts PC from reactive investigator to proactive guardian

### Milestone 11: Reinforcing or Shattering the Seals
**Level 11 → 12**

- **Act**: Act III - Heroic Rise Phase
- **Trigger**: PC must choose how to handle Sogmonnath's prison. Options: Reinforce seals (imprison her longer), Shatter seals (confront her directly), or Modify seals (prepare for controlled awakening and trial).
- **Revelation**: Original imprisonment was flawed - done in haste without proper judgment. Sogmonnath's grievance is legitimate (denied trial). Simply re-sealing perpetuates injustice. Confronting her directly risks planar catastrophe. Proper solution requires divine tribunal - which PC can petition Maal to convene.
- **Consequences**:
  - **Reinforce**: Temporary safety, perpetuates injustice, Sogmonnath becomes eternal enemy
  - **Shatter**: Immediate boss fight, high risk/reward, may free or destroy her
  - **Modify**: Triggers Act IV tribunal; proper resolution but uncertain outcome
  - Choice determines campaign's final act structure
- **NPC Reactions**:
  - **Navula**: Supports modification (she's been waiting for proper solution)
  - **Professor Aldric**: Advocates reinforcement (safest academically)
  - **Friedel** (if corrupted): Wants seals shattered (chaos serves him)
  - **Faylene**: Prays for divine guidance; supports tribunal option
  - **Sogmonnath**: Promises power for freedom; threatens destruction if re-sealed
- **Faction Clock Changes**:
  - Cosmic Stakes clock advances significantly regardless of choice
  - Different faction clocks depending on choice
  - Act IV structure determined
- **Player Reward**:
  - Level 12
  - Consequences based on choice
  - Transition to Act IV
  - Divine attention (Maal responds to petition)
- **Foreshadowing**: This choice IS the foreshadowing - determines whether Act IV is combat-focused (shatter), temporary victory (reinforce), or tribunal-focused (modify). PC's values throughout campaign should guide decision.
- **DM Notes**:
  - Present all three options clearly with known consequences
  - PC's previous choices should make one option feel most appropriate
  - This is point of no return - commits to campaign ending type
  - Recommended option: Modify (leads to tribunal, most narratively rich)
  - Regardless of choice, Act III ends with major confrontation

## Act IV: Cosmic Litigation Phase (Levels 13-20)

*Note: Following milestones assume "Modify seals → Tribunal" path. Adjust if PC chose differently.*

### Milestone 12: Summoning the Divine Tribunal
**Level 12 → 13**

- **Act**: Act IV - Cosmic Litigation Phase
- **Trigger**: PC successfully petitions Maal to convene high tribunal to judge Sogmonnath properly. Gathers required advocates, prepares evidence, and witnesses summons to planar courthouse.
- **Revelation**: Maal agrees Sogmonnath deserves trial. Tribunal will judge not only her crimes but also those who imprisoned her without due process. PC serves as Advocate of Justice, presenting evidence. Thellos/Naran serve as opposing advocates. Sogmonnath's fate (and PC's) depends on evidence and argumentation.
- **Consequences**:
  - Campaign shifts to investigation-trial hybrid
  - PC must gather evidence across Acts I-III events
  - Witnesses must be convinced to testify
  - Opposing advocates actively sabotage preparation
  - Stakes: If PC loses, Sogmonnath freed without restriction; if wins improperly, injustice perpetuates
- **NPC Reactions**:
  - **Maal**: "You petition for true justice. Be prepared - it may not match your expectations."
  - **Sogmonnath**: "Finally. A fair hearing. Prove I deserve imprisonment, Advocate."
  - **Thellos/Naran**: "We will demonstrate that imprisonment was necessary expediency. Justice sometimes requires hard choices."
  - **Navula**: "This is what I've hoped for. Proper resolution. But trials can go wrong."
  - **All NPCs**: Must decide whether to testify (some eager, some reluctant)
- **Faction Clock Changes**:
  - Tribunal Preparation clock established
  - Evidence Quality clock established
  - Witness Cooperation clock established
  - Opposition Sabotage clock established
- **Player Reward**:
  - Level 13
  - Divine mandate as Advocate of Justice
  - Access to planar courthouse
  - Authority to compel witness testimony
  - Support from Maal's temple (resources, research)
- **Foreshadowing**: Tribunal structure mirrors PC's investigation throughout campaign - gathering evidence, interviewing witnesses, uncovering truth. But now cosmic powers judge PC's work. Everything PC learned comes to bear.
- **DM Notes**:
  - This begins 8-level arc building to campaign climax
  - Shift tone from combat to investigation-social
  - PC's relationships with NPCs determine witness quality
  - Previous choices affect available evidence
  - Introduce opposing advocates as intelligent antagonists

### Milestone 13: Securing Key Witnesses (Part 1)
**Level 13 → 14**

- **Act**: Act IV - Cosmic Litigation Phase
- **Trigger**: PC successfully convinces 5 key witnesses to testify. Choices might include: Navula (Sogmonnath's ancient enemy), Faylene (curse victim), Bonita (plague evidence), One-Eyed Cy (shadow manifestation witness), Callista (curse survivor), Wilhelm (death toll), etc.
- **Revelation**: Each witness provides different evidence type. Need balanced testimony: historical context, contemporary crimes, systemic effects, personal harm, and potential redemption. Missing witness types weakens case.
- **Consequences**:
  - Witnesses become targets for opposing advocates
  - Must protect witnesses until trial
  - Each witness reveals new perspective on Sogmonnath
  - Some witnesses sympathize with her; complicates case
- **NPC Reactions**:
  - **Witnesses**: Varying levels of enthusiasm/reluctance
  - **Opposing advocates**: Begin targeting witnesses (intimidation, bribery, assassination attempts)
  - **Sogmonnath**: Interested in who PC chooses; reaches out to some witnesses directly
  - **Maal**: Observes but does not interfere (PC must succeed independently)
- **Faction Clock Changes**:
  - Witness Cooperation clock +5 (significant progress)
  - Opposition Sabotage clock +2 (they're interfering)
  - Tribunal Preparation clock +3 (case building)
- **Player Reward**:
  - Level 14
  - Witness protection resources
  - Initial testimony depositions (preview of evidence)
  - Deeper understanding of Sogmonnath's crimes and context
- **Foreshadowing**: Some witnesses hint that original imprisonment was done hastily during war. Context suggests Sogmonnath may have been stopped before full crimes committed - imprisoned for intent, not just action. Raises question: Can you imprison for what someone might do?
- **DM Notes**:
  - Roleplay witness recruitment - each has different motivations
  - Some witnesses require quests/favors to recruit
  - Protect witness encounters can be combat or social
  - Build sympathy for some aspects of Sogmonnath's position
  - Maintain moral complexity - no clear answer

### Milestone 14: Surviving Assassination at the Threshold
**Level 14 → 15**

- **Act**: Act IV - Cosmic Litigation Phase
- **Trigger**: Opposing advocates send powerful assassins (possibly corrupted Friedel, hired planar mercenaries, or manipulated allies) to eliminate PC before trial. Must survive assault on Hilbre Isle sanctuary.
- **Revelation**: Thellos/Naran fear fair trial will reveal their expedient imprisonment was unjust. They prefer Sogmonnath remain imprisoned without examination. They're not defending justice; they're defending their decision to bypass it.
- **Consequences**:
  - True nature of opposing advocates revealed (corrupt officials, not justice-seekers)
  - Evidence of divine misconduct (can be used at trial)
  - Sanctuary's defenses tested and strengthened
  - Some allies may be injured or killed protecting PC
- **NPC Reactions**:
  - **Pale Maiden**: If freed, defends PC; if still bound, fights PC (tragic)
  - **Callista**: Heroic defense; proves her redemption
  - **Sefton**: If alive, might help PC (enemy of my enemy) or exploit chaos
  - **Maal**: Provides divine intervention at crucial moment (once only)
  - **Sogmonnath**: Helps defend PC (she wants her trial)
- **Faction Clock Changes**:
  - Opposition Sabotage clock +4 (desperate measures)
  - Evidence Quality clock +2 (their attack IS evidence of corruption)
  - PC Survival clock established (death is real possibility)
- **Player Reward**:
  - Level 15
  - Evidence of opposing advocates' corruption
  - Divine intervention boon (Maal's direct aid)
  - Hardened sanctuary defenses
  - Proven loyalty of companions
- **Foreshadowing**: Attack demonstrates that powerful entities fear proper judgment. System is corrupt enough that divine officials attempt murder rather than face trial. Validates PC's pursuit of true justice. Also shows Sogmonnath isn't only one on trial - the system itself is.
- **DM Notes**:
  - Epic battle defending sanctuary
  - Companions should have heroic moments
  - Consider permanent consequences (NPC death possible)
  - This is midpoint of Act IV - raises stakes dramatically
  - Shift from preparing for trial to recognizing system is rigged

### Milestone 15: Uncovering the Original Conspiracy
**Level 15 → 16**

- **Act**: Act IV - Cosmic Litigation Phase
- **Trigger**: PC discovers hidden historical records proving Sogmonnath's original imprisonment was conspiracy. She was developing planar magic that threatened established divine power structure. Gods moved against her preemptively.
- **Revelation**: Sogmonnath's crime was pursuing knowledge that would have democratized divine power - allowed mortals to access planar magic without godly permission. She wasn't conquering planes; she was opening them. Gods called it "planar conquest attempt" and imprisoned her without trial to suppress the knowledge.
- **Consequences**:
  - PC must decide: Is this evidence admissible, or does it threaten cosmic order?
  - Sogmonnath's true nature revealed (revolutionary, not tyrant)
  - Some allies abandon PC (too dangerous to oppose gods)
  - Other allies join (attracted to truth-seeking)
  - Tribunal becomes referendum on divine authority itself
- **NPC Reactions**:
  - **Sogmonnath**: "Now you understand. They feared losing control. Justice was never the goal."
  - **Maal**: "I knew. I convened this tribunal because suppressing truth is not justice. Present your evidence."
  - **Thellos/Naran**: "You're threatening the foundations of cosmic order. Stand down."
  - **Navula**: Conflicted (she helped imprison Sogmonnath believing she was threat)
  - **Professor Aldric**: "This changes everything we know about planar magic history."
- **Faction Clock Changes**:
  - Evidence Quality clock +4 (crucial evidence obtained)
  - Cosmic Stakes clock +3 (threatening divine order)
  - Ally Stability clock -2 (some abandon PC)
  - Truth-Seeker clock +3 (attracted principled allies)
- **Player Reward**:
  - Level 16
  - Explosive evidence for trial
  - Understanding of planar magic principles (mechanical benefit)
  - Maal's private support (god backs truth)
  - Enemy: Thellos/Naran now personally hostile
- **Foreshadowing**: This evidence recontextualizes entire campaign. Plague wasn't Sogmonnath's revenge; it was side effect of degrading prison seals. Sefton wasn't her servant; he was trying to access her suppressed knowledge. PC has been solving conspiracy while uncovering larger one.
- **DM Notes**:
  - Major revelation - prepare players for paradigm shift
  - Sogmonnath shouldn't become simple hero (she's complex)
  - Gods aren't villains either (they had reasons, however flawed)
  - This raises question: What is justice when system itself is unjust?
  - PC must choose between stability and truth

### Milestone 16: The Trial Begins - Opening Arguments
**Level 16 → 17**

- **Act**: Act IV - Cosmic Litigation Phase
- **Trigger**: Tribunal formally convenes. PC presents opening arguments. Opposing advocates present theirs. Battle of rhetoric and evidence begins in planar courthouse before divine judges.
- **Revelation**: Tribunal format: PC presents evidence, opposing advocates counter, witnesses testify, judges question both sides, verdict rendered. Outcome depends on PC's preparation, evidence quality, and persuasiveness. Combat skills irrelevant; investigation and relationship-building determine success.
- **Consequences**:
  - Trial structure established (multiple sessions)
  - Public nature means cosmic powers watching
  - PC's performance affects reputation across planes
  - Evidence presented becomes public knowledge
  - Some revelations damage PC's allies (collateral consequences)
- **NPC Reactions**:
  - **Divine Judges**: Impartial but intimidating; represent different cosmic principles
  - **Sogmonnath**: Dignified defendant; surprisingly sympathetic
  - **Thellos/Naran**: Skilled orators; manipulative but effective
  - **Witnesses**: Nervous; performance varies
  - **Spectators**: Cosmic beings watching; their reactions influence judges
- **Faction Clock Changes**:
  - Trial Progress clock established
  - Judicial Favor clock established (swings based on arguments)
  - Public Opinion clock established (cosmic community watching)
  - Opposition Strategy clock established (they adapt to PC's tactics)
- **Player Reward**:
  - Level 17
  - Trial proceeding (progress toward resolution)
  - Reputation among cosmic powers
  - Understanding of divine judicial system
- **Foreshadowing**: Opening arguments establish that verdict is genuinely uncertain. Judges are impartial; outcome depends on evidence. Opposing advocates hint at "necessary evil" defense - imprisonment without trial prevented catastrophe. PC must prove alternatives existed.
- **DM Notes**:
  - Roleplay trial sessions in detail
  - Use skill challenges for arguments (Persuasion, Insight, Investigation)
  - Let players strategize evidence presentation order
  - Opposing advocates should be formidable but not cheating
  - Each trial session should feel like high-stakes negotiation

### Milestone 17: Turning a Key Witness
**Level 17 → 18**

- **Act**: Act IV - Cosmic Litigation Phase
- **Trigger**: PC convinces crucial opposing witness to switch sides OR successfully discredits opposing advocate's star witness. Major courtroom reversal.
- **Revelation**: Witnesses can be persuaded by truth, not just loyalty. Some testifying for opposing advocates do so from fear or misinformation. Presenting evidence that contradicts their understanding can turn them.
- **Consequences**:
  - Major shift in judicial favor toward PC
  - Opposing advocates scramble to recover
  - Turned witness provides new evidence
  - Remaining witnesses reconsider positions
  - Trial momentum swings decisively
- **NPC Reactions**:
  - **Turned Witness**: Often Navula or another PC ally who initially opposed Sogmonnath but now sees nuance
  - **Sogmonnath**: Grateful; opens up more about her intentions
  - **Thellos/Naran**: Angry; may attempt courtroom misconduct
  - **Judges**: Impressed by PC's persuasiveness
  - **Maal**: Quiet satisfaction (justice proceeding properly)
- **Faction Clock Changes**:
  - Judicial Favor clock +4 (major swing)
  - Trial Progress clock +3 (approaching conclusion)
  - Opposition Strategy clock -2 (they're losing ground)
  - Public Opinion clock +3 (cosmic audience impressed)
- **Player Reward**:
  - Level 18
  - Trial victory within reach
  - Deepened relationship with turned witness
  - Opposing advocates' desperation (potential vulnerabilities)
- **Foreshadowing**: Turned witness reveals that original imprisonment coalition had dissenters who were silenced. Not all gods agreed with expedient justice. Some divine powers will support PC's verdict, whatever it is. Cosmic order won't collapse from truth.
- **DM Notes**:
  - Make witness turning moment dramatic courtroom scene
  - Use actual evidence PC gathered throughout campaign
  - Opposing advocates should react intelligently
  - This is climax of investigation work paying off
  - Build toward final verdict session

### Milestone 18: Surviving the Final Sabotage
**Level 18 → 19**

- **Act**: Act IV - Cosmic Litigation Phase
- **Trigger**: Thellos/Naran attempt final desperate measure: assault on courthouse, manipulation of judges, or forcing mistrial. PC must defend trial's integrity without disrupting proceedings.
- **Revelation**: Opposing advocates are willing to destroy tribunal itself rather than accept unfavorable verdict. They represent corrupt divine bureaucracy valuing stability over justice. Their sabotage proves PC's central argument.
- **Consequences**:
  - Combat encounter or high-stakes intrigue
  - Opposing advocates exposed and removed/censured
  - New advocates appointed OR trial proceeds without opposition
  - Sogmonnath's vindication nearly certain now
  - But PC must still propose appropriate sentence/resolution
- **NPC Reactions**:
  - **Judges**: Furious at corruption; purge opposing advocates
  - **Maal**: "This is why I convened proper tribunal. The rot runs deep."
  - **Sogmonnath**: "Do you see now? This is what I faced. Arbitrary power disguised as order."
  - **All Allies**: Rally to defend trial (epic cooperative moment)
  - **Cosmic Spectators**: Shocked by divine corruption; public opinion shifts massively
- **Faction Clock Changes**:
  - Trial Progress clock +5 (sabotage attempt backfires)
  - Judicial Favor clock maxed (opposing advocates discredited)
  - Cosmic Reform clock established (systemic change possible)
  - PC Authority clock +4 (proven incorruptible)
- **Player Reward**:
  - Level 19
  - Trial victory achieved
  - Cosmic-level reputation
  - Authority to propose verdict/sentence
  - Support from reformed divine bureaucracy
- **Foreshadowing**: Sabotage attempt proves that justice requires vigilance. Powerful entities will corrupt any system if unchecked. PC's role isn't just advocate; it's reformer. Final milestone will determine what kind of cosmic order emerges.
- **DM Notes**:
  - Epic confrontation combining all PC skills
  - Allies should contribute meaningfully
  - Opposing advocates should be defeated but not killed (face justice themselves)
  - This validates PC's entire campaign arc
  - Sets up final choice: What justice for Sogmonnath?

### Milestone 19: Rendering Final Verdict
**Level 19 → 20**

- **Act**: Act IV - Cosmic Litigation Phase
- **Trigger**: PC presents final arguments and recommended verdict. Judges deliberate and render judgment. Sogmonnath's fate determined. Cosmic order reformed or reaffirmed.
- **Revelation**: True justice requires both accountability and mercy. Sogmonnath committed no crimes but posed genuine threat. Original imprisonment was unjust but prevented potential catastrophe. Perfect solution doesn't exist; PC must choose best available option.
- **Consequences**:
  - **Verdict Options**:
    1. **Exile**: Sogmonnath banished to outer plane; prevented from material world but free
    2. **Conditional Freedom**: Released under supervision; can teach planar magic with oversight
    3. **Service**: Bound to serve cosmic good for set period; redemption through action
    4. **Reformed Imprisonment**: Better conditions, regular review, path to eventual freedom
    5. **Full Exoneration**: Freed completely; original imprisonment condemned as unjust
  - Each option has different cosmic consequences
  - PC's choice affects campaign epilogue significantly
  - Divine bureaucracy reformed based on trial precedent
- **NPC Reactions**:
  - **Sogmonnath**: Responses vary by verdict; grateful for any genuine justice
  - **Maal**: "You have served justice well. This is the arbitration I hoped for."
  - **Judges**: Implement verdict and establish new precedents
  - **All Campaign NPCs**: React based on their values and PC's relationship
  - **Cosmic Powers**: Some pleased, some displeased; new political landscape
- **Faction Clock Changes**:
  - All clocks resolve based on verdict
  - Campaign Completion clock achieved
  - Legacy clock established (determines epilogue)
- **Player Reward**:
  - Level 20
  - Campaign resolution
  - Cosmic-level boon from Maal (permanent divine gift)
  - Authority as Arbiter (can adjudicate planar disputes)
  - Title: "The Arbiter's Voice" or "Reformer of Heaven"
  - Epilogue based on choices throughout campaign
- **Foreshadowing**: None - this is resolution. But verdict sets stage for potential sequel (cosmic politics, reformed divine bureaucracy, Sogmonnath's new role, etc.)
- **DM Notes**:
  - Let player choose verdict based on their values
  - No "right" answer; each option has merit and cost
  - Epilogue should honor PC's journey and choices
  - Celebrate investigation skills that made trial possible
  - Consider sequel hooks based on verdict's cosmic implications

- **Trigger**: Time skip (months to years) showing consequences of verdict. PC's new role in cosmic order. Yllmourne's recovery. Fates of all major NPCs.
- **Revelation**: PC's choices throughout campaign created lasting change. Yllmourne recovered (or didn't). Divine bureaucracy reformed (or resisted). Planar magic knowledge democratized (or controlled). Legacy established.
- **Consequences**:

  - PC's actions ripple across planes
  - NPCs live out consequences of PC's choices
  - New status quo established

- **Player Reward**:
  - Completed campaign
  - Epilogue customized to their choices
  - Option to continue in new role (sequel potential)
  - Satisfaction of justice truly served
- **DM Notes**:
  - Personalize epilogue to player's specific choices
  - Honor all relationships PC built
  - Show positive and negative consequences honestly
  - Leave door open for sequel if desired
  - Celebrate the investigation-to-justice arc completion

## Using This Guide

### Session Planning
- Review next 2-3 milestones before each session
- Prepare multiple paths to trigger milestone (player agency)
- Track faction clocks between sessions
- Adjust milestone difficulty based on PC performance

### Player Communication
- Share milestone structure in Session 0 (they know leveling is narrative)
- Don't reveal specific triggers (preserve discovery)
- Celebrate milestone achievements explicitly
- Allow players to influence milestone order (within reason)

### Adaptation Tips
- Milestones can be reordered (especially within same Act)
- Multiple sessions may be required per milestone
- Some milestones may be achieved simultaneously
- Failed milestones don't end campaign; consequences shift story
