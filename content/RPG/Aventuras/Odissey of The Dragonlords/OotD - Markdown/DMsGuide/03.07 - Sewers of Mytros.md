The sewers of Mytros were a great technical marvel that combined the urban planning skills of Vallus with the technical know-how of Volkan. Today the sewers of Mytros make it the cleanest city in Thylea, but after a couple centuries of use its people have grown accustomed and jaded to this marvel.
Now the sewers are in disrepair, as Acastus has focused more on parades, games, and equipment than on "boring" public maintenance. As a result they play host to a number of creatures, some dangerous, some not - but all a risk Vallus would rather not have to shoulder.
Tying in the Sewers The sewers have a number of useful features that could be of interest to the players:
● The Ghoul King has amassed a small hoard of valuable treasure. ● The Ghoul King's ghouls have been emerging into the city at night to hunt. At first they only attacked Stygian Row, but now that they have begun to broaden their pattern, the city is beginning to get nervous. ● The Nest of the Forgotten can be the source of one or more new companions for the party. ● The water elementals that have settled in the southern drainwell are clogging the sewers, leading to unfortunate flooding. ● Ismene Neurdagon is conducting evil experiments on people from the Nest of the Forgotten. Her activities, if discovered, could provide useful leverage to the rest of the Neurdagon family. ● The sewers offer a useful way to move about the city unseen. They can be a useful point of entry to the Colossus of Pythor, and a good way to disrupt the Lady of Coins's operations. ● Vallus asks the party to deal with the Ghoul King or the water elementals. However, these "seek and destroy" quests have an ulterior motive: Vallus is hoping the heroes will find the Nest of the Forgotten. Their heroic merit will be judged on how they react to this desperate settlement in need, with little to offer in return. If they prove worthy, she may give them a powerful boon.

When a thief afflicted with the Graverobber's Curse dies with great malice and fear in their heart, Lutheria gains claim over their souls. Sometimes she decides to return the cursed soul to its body, transforming the unfortunate creature into a ghoul. Unable to die and unable to slake their eternal hunger, ghouls roam the land by night, desperate to consume any living flesh it can find.

## Water Elemental Blockade
During the storm that wracked Mytros, a few water elementals took up residence in the storm drains of the sewers, displacing the ghouls that once hunted there. The elementals remain, even after the storm abates, generally menacing the sewer denizens and clogging the sewer pipes.

## Nest of the Forgotten
Thylea is a diverse place, but Mytros does not make it seem so. Its streets consist mainly of mortals, with minotaurs being confined to slavery and tieflings and half-orcs marginalized to slums like Stygian Row. Even then, there are some unfortunates that Thylean society places even further down the totem pole; for these folk, the only refuge is the Nest of the Forgotten.
The Nest of the Forgotten serves as a settlement of sorts for medusae, tieflings, half-orcs, and minotaurs. They shelter in a large chamber of the sewers, erecting small tents for privacy and cultivating mushrooms and other lightless plants for food. Any other supplies are purchased by tieflings and half-orcs, individuals who can venture out to certain parts of the city without being attacked or arrested.
The Nest of the Forgotten can serve as a rest point for players making their way through the dangerous sewers. They can also be used to underscore the danger and oppression that some races face in Thylea.

There is much to do in the Nest of the Forgotten, mainly pointing the party to other optional content:
● Jankas or Telekles can speak to the atrocities of the Minotaur Market. They will mainly appeal to the party's sense of honor or justice, but Jankas may offer a rare potion or two that he's been saving if they do something about it. ● Anteia and her fellow medusae can guard the Nest well enough, but there is little they can do for those that must venture out. The Nest loses several people to the marauding ghouls every year, and now the new arrival of elementals is pushing the ghouls to become even more aggressive. Dealing with either the Ghoul King or the elementals would be a great boon to the Nest. ● Halie is convinced her friend Achradina was kidnapped, and she is right. Achradina was kidnapped by Ismene Neurdagon to be, "repaired," and has been transformed into a gruesome machine. Achradina is a human friend who remained by Halie's side after her transformation; Halie is desperately loyal to her and will do anything to find her. Halie will be wrecked by her friend's fate, but she is the most likely NPC of the Nest to join the party out of gratitude and admiration. ● Chremon knows of the child sacrifices occurring in the Temple of Lutheria, but has so far been powerless to stop them. With the party's help, he may just be courageous enough to try. ● Anteia and the Nest once nursed to health a lonely but strong-willed medusa named Moxena. Moxena was an exile of her people, and the medusae of the Nest tried to encourage her to find peace and meaning within her curse. Moxena disagreed, and left the Nest to become the Lady of Coins. Now Moxena extorts the Nest with her knowledge of its location, threatening to reveal its existence to the wrong kinds of people. Anteia regrets having to ask it of one of her own kind, but she realizes that Moxena is a threat to all she is trying to protect. She can reveal the location of the colossus's secret entrance to the party.
