# Companions and Romance

**I am revising these mechanics to simplify the companion mechanics**

**The current mechanics, after a few campaigns of using them, are not ideal; it presents an additional mechanic for players to keep track of, and in several groups, I’ve found it becomes a hassle rather than a fun thing.**

**Future revisions will provide more concise advice for roleplaying the characters, other uses for them throughout the campaign, as well as a singular bonus for having them present when adventuring.** 

Befitting a module made by several former Bioware employees, *Odyssey of the Dragonlords* includes a series of rich characters, including several of whom may join the party on their adventures. This document was made to keep track of these various characters, present mechanics for running them without the need for adding additional characters in combat, as well as providing information on how to use the Companions more, including advice for additional encounters and romances. 

\---------------

Before including romance in your games, ensure that your players are comfortable with that sort of at the table. There are many resources out there that discuss relationships between players, players and NPCs, etc. Two I recommend are [The Lovers’ Guidebook](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/258160/The-Lovers-Guidebook--Player-Edition) (PWYW) and [The Lovers’ Handbook](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/258023/The-Lovers-Handbook) for their approach to consent, comfort levels, and recommendations and advice on running romance in games.

**Kyrah**  
**Encountered:** *The Sour Vintage*  
	  
The Goddess of Music initially puts forward the thinly veiled disguise that she is a mere poet in service to the Oracle. Once her identity is revealed, she may join the party as a companion, instead of merely observing the party’s adventures and recording them for her poem.

***Song of Heroism (1/Day).*** Kyrah performs a powerful song of bravery empowered by the Oath of Fellowship. Each Friendly creature gains Heroic Inspiration, which lasts for 1 minute, disappearing if it went unused.

***Loyalty Upgrade.*** If Kyrah has grown closer with the party, *Song of Heroism* can be used 2/Day.

***Romance.*** Kyrah is a playful lover. While she may flirt openly and often, she will not consider a relationship until her status as a Goddess is revealed and the party has accomplished several heroic deeds (likely by the time the Great Labours and the storm over Mytros are completed). Kyrah enjoys playing pranks on her partner and ribbing them. She will end any relationship she is in if her partner makes continued choices she views as immoral, not willing to find herself stuck in another situation as she did with Estor and his bloodlust. Kyrah will not consent to marry anyone unless they can beat her in a footrace.

**Loreus**  
**Encountered:** *The Mossy Temple*  
		 *The Embarkation of the Ultros (if he did not join at the Mossy Temple)*

Loreus is a young satyr who had a very sheltered upbringing. He is not typical of his kind. Rather than frolicking and dallying with nymphs like other satyrs his age, Loreus spends all of his time writing love poems and reading epic romances. He is fascinated by mortal men and women, especially those who resemble the great heroes of his favorite stories.

**Lovesick.** Loreus will do absolutely anything for the person he loves, no matter how dangerous or embarrassing. He will constantly seek new ways to prove his love, no matter how strange they might look to others.

***Love Poem (1/Combat).*** Once per combat encounter, you can add 1d8 to any d20 Test you make.

***Loyalty Upgrade.*** If Loreus’ affections are earnestly met and reciprocated, his *Love Poem* can now be used 2/Combat Encounter and the die size becomes 1d10.

Similarly, while Loreus’ actions should come from a place of sincerity in his displays of earnest adoration, make sure the player who is the recipient of this is amiable to a character having feelings for them and that it doesn’t make the player feel awkward. |

***Romance.*** Loreus is a romance option for characters of any species or gender. Loreas is driven by his emotions, both good and bad. A hero that treats him kindly and is shown to help Demetria’s victims inspires a deep affection in him.

Loreus treats the object of his affections with respect and reverence. With their consent, he will swear an Oath of Protection to them to remain by their side so long as they allow him. 

He provides sweet gestures, words of affirmation, and voices his continued belief in his lover, no matter the obstacle at hand.

**Bullbug**  
**Encountered:** *Events in Mytros: Optional Event \- Bullbug*  
*Minotaur Market Encounter (M21): Bullbug the Minotaur*

Bullbug is a pot-bellied minotaur gladiator with a sunny disposition and a penchant for drinking. Contrary to the reputation that minotaurs have in Mytros, he doesn’t have an evil disposition or a lust for blood. He has a different view on life than many of his kind, because he was raised by a human family who treated him like a son.

As a youth, Bullbug gained the distinction of being one of the few minotaurs to survive the sacrificial rituals in Mytros. He refused to kill the warriors he defeated, and so gained the sympathy of the family matron who took Bullbug into her home and raised him as a son. But then, several years ago, Bullbug's family were killed by the Order of Sydon, and he was sold into slavery.

Bullbug is not very bright, but he’s reliable in a fight and he’s loyal. He loves to make terrible jokes at the expense of his own species. When he makes an especially bad mistake, he usually blames it on his “stupid cow brain.”

***Bodyguard (1/Rest).*** Bullbug can reduce damage you would take (no action required). Roll 1d8 \+ your level, reducing the damage by the amount rolled (to a minimum of zero).

***Loyalty.*** You know roll 1d12 \+ your level when reducing damage. 

***Romance.*** Bullbug is likely to only be interested in minotaurs, centaurs, satyrs, and nymphs, but at your discretion, he may fall in love with any character who shows him genuine care and affection, though they may need to be very forthright in this, as he isn’t particularly perceptive. Bullbug approaches romance in an almost knightly fashion, though his attempts at romance are often more comedic than not, as he isn’t particularly good at setting a mood. He is a valiant defender, nonetheless, to his beloved.

**Darien**  
**Encountered:** The Amazon Prison (P7)

Darien is the rightful heir to the throne of the Amazons. Her mother, Queen Thesilea, ruled the island of Themis along with her two sisters. But when Darien was five years old, her mother was betrayed by her closest advisors, who had transformed into medusae. They killed the three matriarchs and assumed their identities. But before the final stroke fell, Darien's mother forced the medusae to swear not to harm her youngest daughter until she comes of age. Accordingly, Darien was thrown into prison, where she has remained for thirteen years.

In order to prevent Darien from revealing this dark secret, the new queen enchanted Darien's prison cell with the silence spell. Consequently, she has been unable to speak for over a decade. During that time, she has subsisted on the stories and scraps that her stimfay companion brings to her. She dreams of exacting vengeance on the medusae, and she trains every day in her cell, looking forward to the opportunity.

**Spirited.** Darien has spent thirteen years trapped in a cell, listening to stories of grand adventure. She is restless and enthusiastic about the possibility of going on her own adventures. Once she achieves vengeance for her mother, she is eager to join the heroes on their quest and explore the world beyond her island.

**Shy.** Darien has not had much human contact in the time that she's been trapped in her cell. She speaks haltingly and with some embarrassment about her limited social skills. She has never seen a man before, and she regards them with detached curiosity. She may develop secret feelings for female heroes.

***War Cry (1/Rest).*** Darien grants you Advantage on all attack rolls made on your turn.

***Loyalty.*** Darien’s Amazonian War Cry can now be used 2/Rest.

***Romance.*** Darien is interested only in women. She is somewhat naive and sheltered due to her long imprisonment and her views on romance are idealistic. She is shy and not likely to make the first move, though will be more open in her affections once they are known. If the Exile is in the party, Darien has long nursed a childhood crush on her and believes they were meant to become Lifemates. She only has eyes for the Exile, and will not reciprocate any feelings for any other character otherwise.

**Moxena**  
**Encountered:** *The Colossus of Pythor*

TN **medusa**

The Lady of Coins is a medusa from the island of Themis, the twin sister of the Amazon Queen. Her true name is Moxena, but she abandoned that name after a failed coup against her sister. She rebelled by joining with the Amazon faction who believed that the worship of Lutheria had corrupted the sisterhood. 

However, she had no intention of restoring the Amazons to a sisterhood of equals—she just wants to replace her sister as Queen and rule the island herself.

Having fled, she ended up in Mytros. Her political skills enabled her to establish the Cult of the Snake, a thieves’ guild that serves her with unswerving loyalty. She robs from the rich and then uses the money to purchase the freedom of minotaur slaves and other undesirables. However, she is not an altruist. She  intends to use the money that she collects to hire an army. Moxena wants vengeance on her sister, but she can easily be convinced to extend her quest to include vengeance against her sister’s patron, Lutheria.

**Pragmatic**. Moxena may desire revenge against her sister above all things, but she is practical about it. She will form alliances with anyone who can help her attain that goal.

**Prejudiced**. Moxena dislikes men as a rule and she treats them with patient but disdainful condescension. If possible, she will ignore male heroes and speak primarily to female heroes in the party.

***Relentless Pace.*** While Moxena is your companion, your Speed increases by 5 ft.

***Loyalty.*** The bonus Moxena grants your Speed becomes 10 ft.

***Romance.*** Moxena is, in my mind, a romance option not unlike characters such as Morrigan in Dragon Age and Miranda in Mass Effect. This note aside, Moxena is likely only going to be interested in women, or in men who show themselves to be powerful, witty, and who can break her perception of them as weak and useless. She desires strength, wealth, and power in equal degrees, and any affections she may hold for someone are always likely to come second to herself, unless you decide to play her as having a bit more of a tsundere like personality.

**Nessa**  
**Encountered:** Scorpion Island

Nessa is a centaur who belongs to a cursed tribe on Scorpion Island. For the past few years, she has led a band of scouts who patrol the island, herding the giant scorpions away from the shores. Like all members of her tribe, she must bond with a mortal rider before she comes of age, or she will transform into a giant scorpion. When the heroes meet her, Nessa has a month before she comes of age, and she is desperate to find a rider.

**Groovy.** Nessa is relaxed and down-to-earth. She likes to braid hair, make flower wreaths, and smoke the lotus flowers that are common on her island. She’s good with a javelin, but she thinks war is boring.

**Loyal.** Nessa will be loyal to the mortal rider who bonds with her. She will try to learn everything she can about her rider, as her dream is to be best friends with her rider forever.

***Like, Give Peace a Chance\! (1/Long Rest).*** Nessa casts *Calm Emotions* (DC 14).

***Loyalty.*** Nessa’s DC becomes 17\.

***Romance.*** Nessa is eager to experience things. She doesn’t approach romance with the same reverence that characters like Darien and Loreus do, instead being open to flings and casual affairs of the heart. It will take a while for her to realize that she desires something more serious, if she ever does. She is more open to exploring the concept of love with other centaurs, as is tradition for bonding on Scorpion Island.

**Pholon**  
**Encountered:** *Scorpion Island*	  
		 *Special Land Encounters: Pholon the Centaur*

Pholon is a centaur who once was part of the cursed tribe on Scorpion Island. Years ago, he bonded with a man named Ankaios, a human soldier whom he loved. The two of them had many adventures across Thylea, but sadly, Ankaios was recently killed in battle. Pholon must find a new rider before the next full moon, or else he will transform into a giant scorpion.

**Adolescent.** Pholon likes to party and drink mead. He’s always up for drinking, singing, or wrestling, especially with “his bros.” He’s good in a fight, but he thinks war is a real bummer.

**Loyal.** Pholon is loyal to the mortal rider who bonds with him, and will be determined to be the very best of friends with that person. He's supportive to a fault, and he volunteers for anything that he deems too dangerous for his favorite bros.

***Chug\! Chug\! Chug\! (1/Day).*** As a Bonus Action, you can consume two potions, or feed someone two potions as an Action.

***Loyalty.*** Whoever benefits from *Chug\!* gains Resistance to all damage until the end of your next turn.

Pholon seems to have a predisposition towards men, based on his past partner, but whether or not he is exclusively attracted to men is up to you in how you roleplay him. Pholon, like Nessa, is young and wild. He is taken in by beauty and by people who are especially exciting to him, especially if they like to drink and party as he does.

**Other Characters**

***Sulla Neurdagon.*** If the party goes through with the side quest *‘The Test of Beauty and Wisdom’* in Mytros, Sulla is likely to have fallen utterly in love with one or two party members. If they accept his proposal, they will find him to be a bit immature and obnoxious, but devout in his affections for them. Upon marrying Sulla and meeting their new father-in-law, Taran Neurdagon, the party member that married Sulla will be gifted five hundred platinum pieces, as a sort of dowry. While Sulla will not join them on their adventures, he will remain in Mytros and cheer them on in their heroic endeavours. He will likely send siren messengers to them on their adventure, each accompanied with letters detailing his adoration accompanied by gifts of no small worth.

***Kalistor.*** Kalistor has a weakness for powerful builds and is quick to fall for a hero who has an 18+ Strength score. He will also likely develop an interest in those who actively oppose the Cult of Lutheria, whom he is adamantly working against in the city of Mytros. 

***Anora.*** Anora is wise beyond her years (the module doesn’t state her age, but I assume she is in her early twenties)  She is ready to lead Estoria, but is also willing to die for it. She is likely to develop an interest in party members who choose to sacrifice themselves in place of her. A party member that romances Anora may be able to marry her and rule Estoria together after the Battle of Mytros, provided that they did not enter a pact with Sydon and Lutheria. 

***Vallus.*** Vallus has a particular weakness for charming, handsome, and ambitious men. She may be reluctant to engage in another relationship after what happened with Acastus, but this is more out of a desire to not be as arrogant as she was going into that relationship, rather than reluctance to be in another relationship. She may compliment and more subtly flirt with a male party member who meets the qualities described above, during the *Summoned by the Queen* quest, but may also require a more proper courtship before she opens her heart to anyone.

***Pythor.*** Pythor falls in love with literally every beautiful mortal who crosses his path. He just can’t help himself, any more than a moth can help flying towards the candle flame. He is respectful but persistent in his attentions; jubilant in victory and gracious in defeat. Should someone show an interest in Pythor, he will make advances as well. He is respectful of limits, but is very forward in his affections. Pythor may attempt to shake his drunkenness for a character that presents it as an obstacle to being with him. A character who romances Pythor risks drawing Hexia’s ire. 

**WIP:** Pythor is not an excellent love interest based on the interaction if a Demigod is present, and it may only serve to complicate issues with Hexia \- especially if it directs her attention to another of Pythor’s lovers rather than confronting her husband’s progeny
