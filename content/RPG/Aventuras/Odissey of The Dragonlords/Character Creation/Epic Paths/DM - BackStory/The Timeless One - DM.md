# The Timeless One

You are the child of Versi the First, the Titan of Prophecy. Having foreseen the downfall of she and her divine siblings at the hands of Sydon and Lutheria, she sent you to the Island of Time until you had the opportunity to defeat the traitorous twins. You spent untold centuries away from the world until five years ago when you returned to Thylea’s mainland and grew to adulthood with an Oldwood tribe that still honours your mother. This new Thylea is strange and many creatures not of the Titans now walk it. They build cities and honour new gods, but they are not your enemy. Sydon and Lutheria hate them fiercely, and you know the cruelty that the Twin Titans can inflict. Perhaps these settlers can even help you in your quest.

**For players who love:** The story of Zeus and Cronus, Javik (Mass Effect)  
**Restrictions:** You must be a native to Thylea  
**Adventure Hook:** You have learned that there is a new Oracle, one that bears your mother’s name. Hopefully she can divine what happened to your family.

## Heroic Tasks

* Recover the Staff of Kentimane
* Commune with Thylea
* Find the Lost Titans

## Divine Blessing
*Blessing of Prophecy:* When you finish a long rest, roll one d20 and record the number rolled. You can replace any attack roll, saving throw, or ability check made by you or a creature that you can see with this roll.

# Integrating the Timeless One

## Meeting the Oracle
The Oracle foresaw two things: first, the Timeless One must recover the **Staff of Kentimane** from the depths of the **Mossy Temple**, and second, that they must bring the staff to the **Island of the Golden Heart** to commune with Thylea. She cannot say what truly befell the Lost Titans, for her father jealously guards that secret, but knows that she and her siblings were named after them as a final insult. She believes, however, that knowledge of their fates waits for the Timeless One at the sacred tree.

> "Cousin, strange cousin, you are an anomaly. Lost to the sands of time, yet returned to Thylea in the here and now. This was fated to be, foreseen by my namesake - your mother, Versi the First. I wish I could say what happened to her, cousin, but my father jealously guards such secrets. All that I can say is that Sydon named his children after his lost siblings as some sort of final insult. Still, I have seen you fulfill her prophecy, now given to me. For your hand to guide Thylea's new dawn, you must seek out the Hand of Kentimane that sleeps in the heart of the Oldwood, and take it to the Island of the Golden Heart. There, you shall commune with our grandmother, Thylea herself."

## The Mossy Temple
The **Staff of Kentimane** is sequestered away inside of **Demetria’s** sacred tree, placed there when the tree was just a sapling, long before it gave rise to Demetria herself.

The tree is located in area **T10: The Den of Lush Flowers**. The fey in that area came to the temple, in addition to hoping to try and talk down Demetria, because they had anticipated the arrival of the Timeless One. They are followers of **Versi the First** and they inform the Timeless One that the item they require is within Demetria’s sacred tree, her heartwood having grown around it, but they warn that cutting the tree down could kill Demetria. Cutting down the tree causes it to bleed, and if Demetria is still alive, her screams ring through the Mossy Temple.

For her part, Demetria knows that ‘some relic’ was placed in the tree before her time, but she rarely enters her tree, instead preferring to sleep in her lavish chambers. The Timeless One can potentially convince Demetria to enter the tree and remove the staff herself, possibly after defeating her in combat and refusing to take her life, or through other means the player may come up with.

## The Wisdom of Vallus
Before departing aboard the *Ultros*, Queen **Vallus** will inform the Timeless One that they should seek out the **Lotus Witch**, as she hoards lost knowledge from across the ages. Vallus may also suggest the **Fates** as a source of knowledge, but warns that they are fickle and cruel, even compared to the Lotus Witch, and have historically been biased in favour of the Twin Titans.

## Communing with Thylea
Bringing the **Staff of Kentimane** will allow the Timeless One to commune with the Mother Goddess, **Thylea**. During the communion, Hands of Kentimane and branches from Thylea’s tree reach out to form a cocoon around the Timeless One as Thylea grants them visions of the history of the Elder Titans, the birth of the Titans, and other early days of Thylea, including the moment when Thylea decided to become the land itself. These visions are accompanied by feelings of hope and profound sorrow, emanating from Thylea as she remembers her children.

After the communion, the Staff of Kentimane receives the ability to cast *Bigby’s Hand*. After communing with Thylea, the Golden Heart drops a single large, but not heavy, golden fruit at the Timeless One’s feet. This fruit is needed to brew an elixir to heal the Lost Titans, a message conveyed through a brief series of images to the Timeless One and anyone that touches the fruit.

## Seeking Knowledge
Either the **Lotus Witch** or the **Fates** can tell the Timeless One that their aunt ‘slumbers beneath Fire Island’ and that their mother *“Strains against chains, blinded on an island in the Nether Sea alongside two of her forgotten siblings.”* They also relay that the Timeless One will need their uncle Taleius’ lost weapon from Themis (*“Hidden in the belly of the beast the usurper queen keeps as her pet”*) and the Staff of Kentimane to free the other Lost Titans.

## Golden Elixir
Both the **Lotus Witch** and the **Fates** are also capable of aiding the Timeless One in turning the golden fruit into a brew that can alleviate the suffering of the Titans, but only if they answer a number of the Lotus Witch’s questions or riddles, or perform a task for the Fates (or otherwise strongarm the hags into a deal). Alternatively, a PC proficient in herbalism and or alchemy tools may be capable of doing so themselves.

The Golden Elixir has no benefit to anyone other than the Titans, and the single fruit is enough to sustain each of the Lost Titans and more. It can be bottled or carried in a barrel or whatever the player’s may prefer; a Lost Titan needs only a sip to regain some of their strength and alleviate the mental anguish that has driven some of them to madness.

## Finding and Freeing the Lost Titans
Finding the Lost Titans is one thing - freeing them is another.

* **Chalcia, Titan of Swiftness** - The Lost Titan of Swiftness is the ‘Sleeping God’ under Fire Island.
    * Initially, Chalcia’s dormant powers grant the staff the ability to cast *Expeditious Retreat* (1 charge).
    * Freeing Chalcia grants the Staff of Kentimane the ability to cast *Haste* (3 charges).

    Freeing Chalcia is a matter of stopping the sacrifices on Fire Island. Once a day has passed without a sacrifice on the island, Sydon’s seal, that of the Empyreal Eye, will become visible in the caldera. The Timeless One will hear their mother’s voice and receive a vision of themselves jumping into the lava. Once the Timeless One jumps in, a pair of Kentimane’s arms leap from the Staff and encircle them. They sink low enough in the volcano for the arms to rip a hole large enough for Chalcia to escape from the chamber of fumes and molten fire she has been trapped within. This should be a dramatic moment, possibly destroying part of the caldera in the process, as fiery Chalcia emerges, holding her sister’s progeny in her hands.

    Chalcia is nervous and worried and fidgety, but she delights in meeting her sister’s progeny, having heard of them but never having met them. She intends to make her way to the Golden Heart, where she will rest and recover.

* **Talieus, Titan of Craft** - The Lost Titan of Crafting has been subjected to pulling Lutheria’s Barge in the Nether Sea.
    * Initially, Talieus’ dormant powers grant the staff the ability to cast *Expeditious Retreat* (1 charge).
    * Freeing Talieus grants the Staff of Kentimane the ability to cast *Animate Objects* (5 charges).

    Saving Talieus requires reaching **Hypnos**. Doing so requires his sword, **Titansbane**, to cut him free from his chains, wraps, and sewn orifices. Talieus travels to the Island of the Golden Heart in hopes of recovering from his torment.

* **Hergeron, Titan of Strength** - The Lost Titan of Strength was beaten by Sydon and imprisoned in the Tower of Praxys.
    * Initially, Hergeron's dormant powers grant the staff the ability to cast *Heroism* (1 charge).
    * Freeing Hergeron grants the Staff of Kentimane the ability to cast *Power Word Fortify* (7 charges).

    Hergeron can be freed by using the Staff of Kentimane. By slamming the Staff atop the adamantine spikes, the Timeless One summons a series of the Hands of Kentimane to free Hergeron and to hold up the tower in Hergeron’s place. He then disappears, heading for the Golden Heart.

* **Yala, Titan of Beauty** - The Lost Titan of Beauty has been petrified on Titan Island.
    * Initially, Yala’s dormant powers grant the staff the ability to cast *Command* (1 charge).
    * Freeing Yala grants the Staff of Kentimane the ability to cast *Yolande’s Regal Presence* (5 charges).

* **Goloron, Titan of Wisdom** - The Lost Titan of Wisdom has been petrified on Titan Island.
    * Initially, Goloron’s dormant powers grant the staff the ability to cast *Identify* (1 charge).
    * Freeing Goloron grants the Staff of Kentimane the ability to cast *Legend Lore* (5 charges).

* **Versi, Titan of Prophecy** - The Timeless One’s mother has been partially petrified on Titan Island.
    * Initially, Versi’s dormant powers grant the staff the ability to cast *Augury* (2 charges).
    * Freeing Versi grants the Staff of Kentimane the ability to cast *Clairvoyance* (as an action, 3 charges, seeing and hearing simultaneously occur).

    The reunion between mother and child should be very dramatic and touching. Though half-mad, Versi still recognizes the coming of her child. She is incredibly proud of her beloved child, and attempts to embrace them, though her arms are too deeply petrified.

    Freeing Versi, Yala, and Goloron on Titan Island requires the combined use of the Staff of Kentimane and Titansbane. Combined, the two tools can be used to ‘chisel’ the stone off of the Lost Titans. A single swing upon each Titan causes the stone to fall off their bodies.

    If the brew is ready, the Titans readily drink from it and the Timeless One’s aunt and uncle dote on them, while they share a tender reunion with their mother. The three intend to head for the Golden Heart.

    Before departing, Versi the First may request to speak with her niece, Versi the Oracle. The two smile at one another, and Versi the First thanks Versi the Oracle for guiding Thylea.

## Interacting with the Twin Titans
The Titans act disdainfully towards their sister’s progeny and are even boastful about their victories over their siblings.

**Lutheria** will haunt the Timeless One’s dreams with visions of their time with their mother, corrupting the dreams into nightmares, or imparting visions of the torment the Lost Titans have endured over the ages.

Meanwhile, at **Sydon’s** behest, the Order of Sydon declares the Timeless One to be a blasphemous heretic that worships ‘dead gods’ that fell to the might of the Lord of Storms.

## The Island of Time
The Timeless One spent ages on the **Island of Time**; revisiting it should be special. The gynosphinx on the island was a friend to **Versi the First** and looked after the Timeless One during their stay on her behalf. She will greet the Timeless One as an old friend that she has not seen in ages; she may even have maternal feelings for them. She will warn them, however, that she can no longer protect them against the lord of the island.

## Meeting Lutheria in Hypnos
If the Timeless One has already reunited with their mother, **Lutheria** will mock them and lament that she wasn’t there to witness such a ‘heart-wrenching display.’ If the Timeless One has managed to free their mother, Lutheria will be both furious and somewhat unnerved.

## Meeting Sydon in Praxys
If the party has freed **Hergeron**, likely the last Lost Titan needed, **Sydon** will be furious. He is less likely to accept any terms for a new Oath of Peace now, and calls his daughter Chalcia to aid him in slaying the party. He still retreats early in the fight, leaving for the Battle of Mytros.

## The Battle of Mytros
At any point during the Battle of Mytros, the Lost Titans can group together and with their powers, grant the party the effects of a short rest; this can only happen once.

During the event ‘Kentimane Awakens,’ the Elder Titan arrives as a defender of ‘old Thylea’. The Lost Titans will help slow their father down and impede his progress through the city.

After the Battle of Mytros, they retire to their mother’s golden island to live in peace and solitude as they recover from their aeons of torment.

## Divine Boon
If the Timeless One dies, they come under the effects of the *True Resurrection* spell as they are engulfed by a pair of the Hands of Kentimane. They re-emerge from the hands moments later, bathed in Thylea’s golden light. This can happen only once.

# Staff of Kentimane
*Staff, Legendary (Requires Attunement by the Timeless One)*

A relic of the Titans, this staff is covered in grasping, earthen hands reminiscent of its namesake.

The Staff of Kentimane has 10 Charges. It regains 1d8+2 charges each day at dawn. While holding it, you can use an action to expend a number of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC.

*Upon first obtaining the staff, it has the power to cast the following spells:*
* *Mage Hand* (0 Charges; creates an earthen hand like on the staff)
* *Command* (1 charge)
* *Expeditious Retreat* (1 charge)
* *Augury* (2 charges)
* *Borrowed Knowledge* (2 charges)
* *Enlarge / Reduce* (2 charges; Enlarge only)
* *Kinetic Jaunt* (2 charges)
* *Vortex Warp* (2 charges)

*The following spell is added after communing with Thylea:*
* *Bigby’s Hand* (5 charges; resembles a Hand of Kentimane)

*The following spells are added after freeing each Titan:*
* *Haste* (3 charges, free Chalcia)
* *Animate Objects* (5 charges, free Talieus)
* *Legend Lore* (5 charges, free Goloron)
* *Arcane Eye* (4 charges, allows hearing as well as sight; free Versi)
* *Yolande’s Regal Presence* (5 charges, free Yala)
* *Power Word Fortify* (7 charges, free Hergeron)
