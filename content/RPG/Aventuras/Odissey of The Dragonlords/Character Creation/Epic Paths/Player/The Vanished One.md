# The Vanished One

You are one of the Dragonlords, the legendary outside explorers who came to Thylea and faced the Titans. You were present when the tiny settlement of Mytros was founded and for many battles in the First War, but you’ve been missing for almost five hundred years. During a dangerous expedition, you were severely injured and your oathsworn dragon was felled. You were saved by, and fell in love with, an irresistibly charming nymph named Versi, who nursed you back to health. You’ve spent the last five centuries in paradise with her, until one day Versi, known to most of the land as the Oracle, claimed Thylea needed you once more.

## For players who love:
- Odysseus
- Captain America

## Restrictions:
- You must be a mortal explorer from distant lands.

## Adventure Hook:
- You are the Oracle’s consort.

# Heroic Tasks

## Tasks to accomplish:
- To find if you have any descendants and, if so, reunite with them.
- To reclaim your title as a Dragonlord by finding a dragon egg which you can raise to be your new companion.
- To get back your fabled equipment, made from the scales willingly given by your former dragon companion. Choose one item from the following list:

### Available items:
- **Boots of Speed**
- **Cloak of the Pseudodragon** (as per Cloak of the Bat, but allows flight in all light and the transformation into a pseudodragon instead of a bat)
- **Dragon Scale Mail** (any armour or scale type)

# Divine Blessing

## Blessing of the Dragonlords:
You gain your oathsworn dragon’s damage resistances, damage immunities, and senses (blindsight, darkvision, and its passive perception if it is greater than yours). Your dragon becomes an adult, if it was not already, and you can use its legendary resistance feature (but this counts towards the total number of uses per day).

# Centuries of Leisure

The time that you have spent with Versi has created gaps in your memory. You are no longer the great Dragonlord that you once were. You must relearn all of the skills lost to you.
