# Chapter 3: Summoned by the King — Player Overview

This chapter begins when royal dragonriders summon the party to Mytros while a great storm rages overhead. From there, the heroes confront the court, the city, and choices that will ripple across Thylea.

## Tarchon’s Summons

> In the distant skies, three winged forms approach — young copper dragons. Red‑cloaked centurions ride them. The dragons descend and touch down. Three captains dismount and approach with calm determination. Their leader speaks: “I am Tarchon. We come on behalf of King Acastus to escort you to his palace. He requests an audience.”

## The Royal Gate

> As you near Mytros, its vast walls and soaring temples crown the base of Mount Volkan. A sprawling harbor stretches along the coast — the jewel of Thylea.
>
> But something is wrong. Black, swirling clouds loom above. Gale winds lash banners and batter ships. In the eye of the hurricane, a colossal face coalesces — Sydon, Lord of Storms. His gaze sweeps the city, then locks onto you. Lightning cracks from the heavens.
