# Octant Faylene Chicarian

## Snapshot
- **Role**: Rotarist prelate overseeing Yllmourne’s faithful; tragic antagonist hexed into fueling the plague.
- **Motivation**: Save her flock and prove her decades of devotion were not in vain; seeks absolution for imagined failures.
- **Leverage**: Freed will once hex is broken, deep loyalty to community, guilt over unintended harm.
- **Stat Baseline**: `Priest` (MM p.348) augmented with hex-flavored spells (`bestow curse`, `spirit guardians`, `blindness/deafness`); post-liberation upgrade to CR 7 high priest with restorative miracles.
- **Deep Dive**: Extended notes in [[npc/apend.01 - Integrated NPCs Complete Guide#Octant Faylene Chicarian|Integrated NPCs]].

## Arc by Act
- **Act I – Beleaguered Shepherd**: Ally on paper, but her sermons unintentionally spread the Mudskipper affliction (see [[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)#Scene 4: The Abandoned Lighthouse|Banquet Murder]] thread).
- **Act II – Hex Revelation**: Investigation uncovers Sefton’s contract twisting her prayers; rescue mission in [[02.00 - Act II - Political Intrigue (Lv5-7)#Adventure 8: The Hexed Court|Act II]].
- **Act III – Redeemed Strategist**: Partners with Bonita and Navula to design counter-ritual cleansing the city cistern ([[03.00 - Act III - Rise of the Hero (Lv8-12)#The Ritual of Cleansing|Act III]]).
- **Act IV – Divine Witness**: Provides theological backbone during Maal’s tribunal and helps reconcile religious factions ([[04.00 - Act IV - Cosmology and Faith (Lv13-20)|Act IV]]).

## Relationship Web
- **Sefton Bane**: Unwitting thrall; once freed, her testimony is critical to dismantling his court ([[npc/01.02 - Sefton Bane (EN)|Sefton dossier]]).
- **Bonita Sandberg**: Mutual respect as healers; Bonita is first to suspect something is wrong ([[npc/01.05 - Bonita Sandberg (EN)|Bonita dossier]]).
- **Navula**: Distrust rooted in dogma; reconciliation scene offers strong roleplay payoffs ([[npc/01.04 - Navula (EN)|Navula dossier]]).
- **Agent of Maal**: Beacon of hope; she seeks absolution through the Agent’s investigation.
- **Mayor Lockton**: Political ally turned critic as her reputation crumbles.

## Scene Hooks
- **Confessional Breakdown**: Private meeting where Faylene begs the Agent for guidance—grant advantage on Insight checks if comforted.
- **Hex Feedback**: During climax of Act II, her prayers lash out as psychic damage waves; PC can absorb or redirect with Religion DC 16.
- **Restoration Vigil**: Post-rescue, she leads joint ritual with Maal’s clergy, providing Bless-type buff for Act III missions.

## Mechanical Notes
- While hexed, aura imposes disadvantage on Medicine checks within 10 ft.; removing curse lifts penalty and grants aura of protection (+1 saves vs. disease).
- Track her Stability Score (start 3). Each public failure reduces by 1; reaching 0 triggers breakdown requiring immediate intervention.
- Rewards: access to Rotarist archives, divine boons (reroll failed save vs. fear once per long rest).
