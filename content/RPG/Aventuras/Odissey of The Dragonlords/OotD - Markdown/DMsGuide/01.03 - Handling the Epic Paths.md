This section describes how to integrate each of the epic paths into the story. Each epic path includes a secret divine blessing that can be used to bring the hero back to life. Before running any of the epic paths, you should read the Epic Paths section in the Creating Heroes appendix.

## Sharing Epic Paths

The epic paths are designed to give each player their own unique motivations and moments to shine. They work best if they are not shared.

However, if your players wish to share an epic path, the best way to do this is to create characters who are siblings or otherwise closely related. They will effectively play as partners who share the same story. In this case, each should receive their own rewards, and you may want to differentiate them by adjusting the items for their chosen classes.

# Epic Paths

## The Bastard

King Acastus received a prophecy when he was young. The Oracle claimed that she saw no great deeds in his future and that everything he did accomplish would be overshadowed by his progeny, who was one day destined to take his life. Acastus has lived in fear of death and obscurity ever since. Afraid, Acastus ensured he would never sire a child by keeping a close watch on his concubines and remaining distant from his wife.

The Bastard's mother was Acastus' favourite concubine. He gave her a family heirloom - a unique coin, which she wore as a pendant. One day, she happily told Acastus that she was pregnant, hoping that he would legitimize the child. Instead, he plotted to have her executed. Vallus learned what was happening and could not abide by it. She spirited the Bastard's mother away, informing her that she was exiled, knowing that the woman was too charmed by Acastus to believe the truth of his betrayal. Only one other person witnessed this exile, the King's mother, Idylla. Vallus forced her to swear an oath that she would not reveal what she had seen to Acastus.

After many years of searching for the Bastard and their mother, Acastus found them. He ordered one of his men to take their dragon to the Bastard's home and burn it down with them inside. Fate protected the Bastard, but their mother was not so fortunate.

### Meeting the Oracle

> "You carry an unkind name that burns deep to your noble soul, Bastard. I admit that the identity of your father and your mother's killer is obscured to my sight, but I can see greatness throughout your long-reaching family tree. The token of his that you carry, once given to your mother, is known as a burnished dragonlord coin, and I can see that it was one of the first to be minted. Take it to the City of the Dead and present it to the silent keeper of that place. He will show you the way to the tomb where a member of your blood restlessly sleeps."

### Divine Boon

If the Bastard is ever killed, the spirit of their mother will appear over their body and weep. As her ghostly tears fall on them, they immediately return to life at full health. After being revived, the Bastard is under the effects of the Bless spell for one minute, no concentration required. This can only happen once.

## The Cursed One

An ancient ancestor of the Cursed One offended the Twin Titans, and as recompense, the Titans worked together to weave a curse that would confound the ancestor's bloodline forevermore, drawing upon the eldritch powers of the Maw of Typhon to create the curse, binding it within the blood of the ancestor, whom they trapped in the Maw.

Only by delving into the Maw of Typhon and destroying their ancient ancestor can the Cursed One end the affliction on themselves, their family, and their bloodline.

### Meeting the Oracle

> "You, Cursed One, whose veins run black with the foulest of hexes, and punishments are disproportionate to the crimes committed ages ago. I see that you must travel to the City of the Dead, there you must speak with the silent guardian that watches over all of Telamok. He shall reveal the nature of your curse, for he was there when the curse was placed upon your blood."

### Divine Boon

If the Cursed One is ever killed, Damon will teleport in and raise them via a True Resurrection spell that he can cast only once and only on the Cursed One, as an Oath sworn to their ancestors; if needed, he will also aid in the battle that slew the Cursed One.

## The Demigod

The Demigod is a child of Pythor, the God of Battle. Pythor has spent centuries obsessing over his failures. He believes that the Demigod is his path to redemption. Pythor wants the Demigod to perform the tasks that he himself failed to accomplish—and to take his place as the God of Battle.

### Meeting the Oracle

> "You are a beauteous thing, but a flower cannot bloom if its stem wilts. So too must you, to see your destiny through, go to your divine parent, Pythor, now King of Estoria. He wallows in his wine and his grief, long having lost the luster of his glory days. You shall forge the weapon he could never wield, defeat his ancient foe, and save his true love. In doing so, you shall walk a path that would allow you to outshine your father in heroism, and perhaps even join him in godhood."

### Divine Boon

If the Demigod is ever killed, Pythor will appear within 1 hour and surrender a portion of his divine spark to raise them back to life. This can only happen once, and Pythor suffers one level of permanent exhaustion as a result.

## The Doomed One

When the Doomed One was born, Lutheria demanded that they be sacrificed along with many other children as part of an annual wine festival. Of all the parents of these children, only the Doomed One's refused. Consequently, Lutheria slaughtered the Doomed One's parents and directed her entire army of servants to torment the child endlessly as a practical joke.

### Meeting the Oracle

> "You whose birth was cursed, whose joyous day of coming to the world was spoiled, I can see the torment that has been laid at your feet, but my sight is obscured from seeing the perpetrator of your misfortunes. Speak with my sister, Demetria, where she weeps and rages in the Oldwood. She has a connection to that which is fated to be your ultimate doom."

### Divine Boon

If the Doomed One is killed, they will immediately come under the effects of the spell True Resurrection. This can happen only twice. No matter how the Doomed One is raised from the dead, they will remain dead forever after dying for the third time. This restriction ends once Lutheria is dead.

## The Dragonslayer

The dragon responsible for the tragedy that destroyed the Dragonslayer’s childhood home is the gold dragon known as Helios.

### Meeting the Oracle

> "I see that your destiny is bound to blood and glory. You shall be the scourge of the evil dragons that plague mortals and fey alike. To become this glorious figure, and to right the wrong that was done to you and yours, you must seek the one who is bound in the flames of the great forge. She can provide insight into the weapon destined to slay the setting sun."

### Divine Boon

If the Dragonslayer is ever killed, they will be visited by Morta (the night hag) from the Island of the Fates. She will resurrect the Dragonslayer and reveal that their destiny is not to die here. This can only happen once.

## The Exile

Thirteen years ago, a group of three Amazonian sisters were tempted by Lutheria with great power should they act on the ambitions of the eldest sister and usurp the throne. In a single night of bloodshed, the Queen and her main supporters were murdered as the sisters stole their lives and identities. For this deed, the three sisters were transformed into medusae, as both a 'reward' from Lutheria and a curse for their treachery and regicide.

The Exile was aided in escaping by the youngest sister, Moxena. When the eldest sister ordered Moxena to clear out the deposed queen's living quarters, she found the soon-to-be Exile as a defenseless child. Rather than killing her as ordered, she snuck the child onto a boat and pushed it out to sea, trusting in Fate to protect her.

### Meeting the Oracle

> "Your Highness. Thank you for coming before me. I can see the path to your throne is a treacherous one indeed, but you needn't walk it alone. In the head of god, there is a woman. She could offer you either aid, or be the first villain to fall to your duly deserved revenge. Beyond her, I see that you must right the wrong the Amazons committed long ago. Only if they stand by the people of Thylea against the Titans can the Twins be felled. To retake your throne, you must not only exact brutal vengeance, but prove your worth lies beyond your blood."

### Divine Boon

If the Exile dies, the ghost of her mother, the true Thesilea VI, immediately appears above her, righteous and proud. She grabs hold of her daughter and lets out an Amazonian battlecry that rouses the Exile from her final slumber. The Exile comes under the effects of the True Resurrection spell. This can only happen once.

## The Gifted One

The Gifted One is the grandchild of Sydon, who fell in love with a mortal sorceress decades ago. Eventually, Lutheria convinced her brother that such a romance was corrupting him. Sydon murdered the sorceress, unaware that she had already given birth to a daughter; the Gifted One is the child of this daughter.

### Meeting the Oracle

> "I am pleased to have one with such a high destiny before me. I see you are destined for greater things yet—to ride at the head of a flight of reborn dragons... but have you ever wondered at the origin of your great gifts? Have you ever pondered where they came from? I will reveal it to you. You and I, you see, are kin. My father, Lord of All, once loved a mortal sorceress. She was your grandmother. My father's sister-wife, Lutheria, convinced him to murder his mortal lover. You must avenge her, and in doing so, her spirit will guide you to your great destiny... Now, allow me to gift you something of hers—a keepsake I know she would wish you to have far more than I."

### Divine Boon

If the Gifted One is ever killed, their body will disintegrate. Vallus will then use a True Resurrection spell to bring them back to life. She then uses her magic to return the Gifted One to their companions. This can only happen once.

## The Haunted One

No one believes the Haunted One’s family existed—not even the gods. Only the Fates know the truth. The Haunted One was prophesied to “break the power of death.” When Lutheria learned of this, she hunted them down and slaughtered their entire family.

But Mytros, the Goddess of Dawn, intervened. Before Lutheria could deal the killing blow to the Haunted One, Mytros used divine magic and erased the entire existence of the Haunted One from memory. Lutheria forgot her purpose and returned to her throne.

Only the Fates remember the prophecy or the existence of the Haunted One and their family. Mytros saw that the prophecy could only come true if all traces of the Haunted One’s history were forgotten. The souls of their family remain trapped in Lutheria’s scythe.

### Meeting the Oracle

> "My condolences for your loss, O Haunted One. Though I cannot see the fates that befell your loved ones, I can see their memories clinging to you like so many ghosts. I know now that the silver light of dawn protected you from the wrath that fell so terribly upon your family. Yet, they need not haunt you forever. You may yet save your beloved family, but to do so, you must recover the artifacts of your (house/tribe). Seek my sister, Demetria, where she resides in the darkest part of the forest, for her servants have delivered to her a relic, stolen from your fallen family."

### Divine Boon

Once attuned to a family artifact, the Haunted One can escape death once. If the Haunted One is killed, the spirits of their loved ones will possess the hero so that they can continue their battle as if they had just taken a short rest. This can only happen once.

## The Lost One

The Lost One is a hero who came from the outer world as a survivor of a shipwreck or as an explorer seeking fame and fortune. Unlike the other epic paths, this works well if more than one player decides to create a character with this path. If more than one player creates a character with this path, their rewards should be added to the lost treasure of the Dragonlords.

### Meeting the Oracle

> "I sense distant shores upon you. Your very venture here to these lost lands is an odyssey in and of itself. But your journey has only just begun. Seek you a way to return home or to carve out a place for yourself here in forgotten Thylea? I cannot say. What I do know, fabulous wealth was brought to this land by the last great arrivals, the Dragonlords of yore. You must recover their lost treasure - with those boons in hand, you will find a way to sail back through the Forgotten Sea, or to make yourself a legend here in these lands."

### Divine Boon

Once the Ultros has been claimed, if the Lost One is killed, then Estor will appear and possess the body of the Lost One, bringing them back to full health and powers as if they had just completed a full rest. The player can play as the possessed hero and try to extricate themselves from the situation where they were defeated. This can only happen once.

## The Masterpiece

The Masterpiece was created by Volkan during the First War, though they never saw action during that conflict, as the Mithral Forge was shut down during the war. Volkan kept the Masterpiece inactive in his forge in Estoria for several centuries, tinkering away, trying to perfect them without access to his full range of tools in the Mithral Forge. But now that the Oath of Peace has begun to wane, Volkan has decided it is time to unleash his Masterpiece on the Twin Titans.

### Meeting the Oracle

> "What a tragedy it is, not only to be chained to war and strife by destiny, but to have been born to know nothing but conflict as well. Masterpiece, I see you fulfilling your glorious purpose, present at the battle that must be, but so too do I see that you shall forever remain unfulfilled, even once you have achieved your purpose. To negate this hollow future, you must seek out a soul, and make it yours. Before your quest can begin in earnest, you must return to where you were created, and speak with she who dwells within the fires of the forge - she will be able to guide you further."

### Divine Boon

If the Masterpiece is ever killed, Volkan will appear within one hour and surrender a portion of his divine spark to raise them back to life. This can only happen once, and Volkan suffers one level of permanent exhaustion as a result.

## The Myrmidon

While the Dragonlords were the champions of the First War, wars are not won by individuals alone. To support the Dragonlords, legions of hoplites were recruited. The first of these legions, the Myrmidons, sported the symbol of an ant upon their banner as they faced the armies of the Titans. You have lived a simple life as a soldier of Mytros, but you feel it is your duty and destiny to reclaim your family's lost honour.

### Meeting the Oracle

> "I see that the blood of the Myrmidons runs through your veins. You, like your ancestors before you, shall unite Thylea against the Titans that threaten to destroy her. You must rally the people, recover the ancient symbols of your order, and be anointed their general by the new Dragonlords. Once done, you shall stand at the forefront of the army in the great battle that must be."

### Divine Boon

If the Myrmidon is ever killed, the spectral image of their ancestor comes into view and stands with a shield and spear over the body of the Myrmidon. At the start of the Myrmidon's next turn, they come under the effects of the Resurrection spell. This can only happen once.

## The Psychopomp

The souls of the dead in Thylea never see the Elysian fields or the wastes of Asphodel; every single mortal instead ends their journey in Lutheria's grasp. Those sacrificed to Lutheria find themselves locked away within her crystal scythe, their soul slowly drained of its power, until eventually they are snuffed out. This is the fate in store for the Psychopomp's loved one, unless they are saved.

### Meeting the Oracle

> "Psychopomp, I see that you have lost your heart, the light of your life that kept you above water when the spirits threatened to drown you in their sorrows. To reunite with your love, you must brave the depths again. Seek out the guardian of Telamok, for he has studied the nature of death for centuries and made unto himself an undying being of great power. He can guide you in how to better use your gifts. With this knowledge, you must undergo a katabasis, a descent into the Underworld, for it is there that you will find your love. Beware though, for the primordial depths of the Nether Sea cannot be navigated alone. You will need a guide... I see a spurned servant of Lutheria currently in Mytros - he would do anything for a chance to return there."

### Divine Boon

If the Psychopomp is killed, they immediately receive the benefits of a True Resurrection spell and return to full health, albeit with a ghostly pallor and transparent form. For the next minute they have resistance to all damage except force and radiant damage, and can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain (they suffer 1d10 force damage if they end their turn inside a creature or object), after which their form returns to normal. This can only happen once.

## The Repentant One

You were once a powerful and proud sphinx. For your actions, you were banished from the homeland of the sphinxes. Your actions and fate amused the Lady of Dreams, who hoped she had found a kindred spirit. She decided to test you as one of her practical jokes. In her mortal guise she vexed you beyond belief, but when you went to curse her, she revealed herself and in her fury at your lack of 'a sense of humour' she stripped away your powers and rendered you a mere mortal. Now, awash with terrible humility and regret, you must attempt to rebuild your strength and undo what your pride has wrought.

### Meeting the Oracle

> "The Merciless Furies alone know the harm you have wrought on mortals seeking their own destinies, o' Repentant One. Exiled, transfigured, and brought low, you have still found your way here, on a path towards absolution. This desire for forgiveness marks you as different from another of your kind, also banished from your homeland. Beneath the stars of the mighty scorpion, she has worked a wicked curse. Undoing it will lead you down the path towards healing your wounded soul, if not undoing all the grief and strife you have rendered unto Thylea's children."

### Divine Boon

If the Repentant One dies, their past self emerges from the weave of time, appearing as a great and powerful sphinx. They cast the True Resurrection spell on their present day self, restoring them to life, before returning to their own time. This can only happen once.

## The Timeless One

You are the child of Versi the First, the Titan of Prophecy. Having foreseen the downfall of she and her divine siblings at the hands of Sydon and Lutheria, she sent you to the Island of Time until you had the opportunity to defeat the traitorous twins. You spent untold centuries away from the world until five years ago when you returned to Thylea's mainland and grew to adulthood with an Oldwood tribe that still honours your mother.

### Meeting the Oracle

> "Cousin, strange cousin, you are an anomaly. Lost to the sands of time, yet returned to Thylea in the here and now. This was fated to be, foreseen by my namesake your mother, Versi the First. I wish I could say what happened to her, cousin, but my father jealously guards such secrets. All that I can say is that Sydon named his children after his lost siblings as some sort of final insult. Still, I have seen you fulfill her prophecy, now given to me. For your hand to guide Thylea's new dawn, you must seek out the Hand of Kentimane that sleeps in the heart of the Oldwood, and take it to the Island of the Golden Heart. There, you shall commune with our grandmother, Thylea herself."

### Divine Boon

If the Timeless One dies, they come under the effects of the True Resurrection spell as they are engulfed by a pair of the Hands of Kentimane. They re-emerge from the hands moments later, bathed in Thylea's golden light. This can happen only once.

## The Vanished One

The Oracle, Versi, has lived for millennia and has never loved anyone—until she laid eyes on the Vanished One. She will do anything to ensure the Vanished One lives, but her jealousy knows no bounds.

### Meeting the Oracle

> "My heart, the fates are cruel to part us so soon after you came to my rescue. Before I speak of your great destiny, I would ask that you make an Oath to me, here and now, that once you have fulfilled your destiny and averted the Doom of Thylea... that you will return to me. And that our hearts may sing as one forever."
> 
> (let the Vanished One answer)
> 
> "I have always had a 'softness' for mortals... but I had no idea what it meant to love, not truly, before I laid eyes upon you, (Vanished One's name). I will speak now of your destiny: you are fated to see your order reborn—I see you, once more wearing your famed armor and crown. You must recover them. Look for them in the Necropolis of Telamok. Old friends await you there."

### Divine Boon

If the Vanished One dies, Versi will appear within 1 hour and kiss them, restoring them to life. This can only happen once.
