## Session Summary

This detailed account chronicles a D&D session where the mists of fantasy intertwine with the threads of narrative, revealing a world of gods, heroes, and intertwined destinies. Players Guilherme and Gibby1978 brought life to Pantos and Kallistos, respectively, under the masterful guidance of Dungeon Master Yan Braga.

The session begins with DM Yan Braga contextualizing players Guilherme and Gibby1978 about the world of Thylea, the stage for their adventure. Thylea, inspired by Greek mythology, emerges as an isolated island, trapped in an eternal twilight of ancient times, where gods and heroes walk the same paths.

The DM then recites a poetic invocation, a call to the muses of memory and faith, to guide the narrative of the Odyssey of the Dragonlords. Through words laden with symbolism, we are transported to a somber scenario where the Titans Sydon and Lutheria plot Thylea's destruction, while the gods struggle to maintain the tenuous Order of Peace.

In a rustic and welcoming tavern, the Sour Vintage, located west of the city of Estoria, our heroes meet. Pantos and Kallistos, summoned by Kyrah, the Oracle's messenger, await destiny's call. The tavern, with its peeling paint walls and the cozy warmth of the fireplace, offers temporary refuge from the approaching storm.

Guilherme, playing Pantos, describes his character as a medium-statured being, adorned with an imposing horned helmet whose central tip radiates bright blue. He carries a throwing weapon richly detailed in blue, a shield on his back, and a curious tail adorned with his own hair.

Gibby1978, in turn, brings Kallistos to life, an imposing figure of striking beauty, with angular features and violet eyes that sweep the environment with a mixture of wonder and curiosity. His blue-black hair, like obsidian, seems to dance to the rhythm of an invisible wind. He wears a steel-blue tunic adorned with bronze scales and a crimson cape that contrasts with the tavern's cozy atmosphere.

Aetala, the hospitable tavern keeper, offers the heroes wine or herbal tea while they await Kyrah's arrival. Kallistos, with a gesture of delicacy, requests a meat dish, revealing certain nervousness before the variety of options.

Meanwhile, Guilherme, as Pantos, asks for green tea, demonstrating an appreciation for herbal beverages. The interaction between the two characters reveals distinct personalities, yet united by a common purpose.

The tavern's atmosphere transforms with Kyrah's arrival, a black-haired woman in traveler's garments, carrying a heavy satchel full of scrolls. With a welcoming smile, she introduces herself as the Oracle's messenger and reveals the destiny awaiting Pantos and Kallistos: to stop an ancestral beast corrupted by the Titans, threatening Thylea's peace.

Kyrah explains that the Order of Peace, once a bulwark against the Titans' influence, has weakened, and the beast is just one of many signs that a new war approaches. She warns them not to offer the beast's sacrifice to any of the Titans, as this could upset the balance of power among the gods.

With a generous gesture, Kyrah presents Pantos and Kallistos with two white arrows, symbols of hope and mementos of their journey's beginning. She also offers her services as a scribe, promising to record their deeds in verse and prose.

The tavern becomes a stage for debates and interactions, where heroes reveal their personalities and worldviews. Kallistos, with his thirst for knowledge, seeks to understand Thylea's history and the complex relationships between gods and mortals. Pantos, meanwhile, demonstrates certain naivety and an aversion to violence, seeking peaceful solutions to conflicts.

Night advances and the tavern becomes a cauldron of stories and rumors. Kallistos, with his discernment ability, tries to separate truth from fiction, seeking to understand the people of Thylea's desires and fears. Pantos, with his natural charisma, enchants those present with his music and stories, lifting the tavern's spirits.

One patron, drunk and desperate, confronts the heroes, accusing them of cowardice for hiding behind the tavern's walls while the beast terrorizes the countryside. Kallistos, with empathy and compassion, tries to calm the man, promising to do everything in his power to restore peace and security to Thylea.

Kyrah, observing the interaction, recognizes the heroes' goodness and courage but warns them not to be carried away by emotion, reminding them that the path to Thylea's salvation is long and arduous.

To lighten the mood, Kyrah proposes a dice game, where luck and strategy intertwine. Pantos, with a stroke of luck, becomes the game's king, demonstrating unexpected talent for strategy.

Night comes to an end and the heroes prepare to sleep, each in their own way. Kallistos, in his gleaming armor, offers prayers to the gods, seeking strength and guidance for the journey that begins. Pantos, in his simplicity, drinks green tea and surrenders to dreams, seeking inspiration from the muses.

The following morning, Kyrah awakens them with a radiant smile, announcing the arrival of hunters who will guide them in their search for the beast. Javon, the oldest and most experienced hunter, warns the heroes about the forest's dangers and the beast's ferocity.

With the sun breaking on the horizon, Pantos, Kallistos, and Kyrah join the hunters and depart in search of their destiny, toward the challenges and dangers awaiting them in Thylea.

The session ends with players sharing their impressions about the adventure, praising the engaging narrative, captivating characters, and immersive atmosphere. The session, rich in detail and emotions, foreshadows an epic and unforgettable journey where fantasy and reality merge in a tale of heroism and redemption.
