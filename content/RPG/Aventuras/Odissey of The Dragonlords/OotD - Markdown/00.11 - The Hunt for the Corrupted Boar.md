# The Hunt for the Corrupted Boar

## The Dawn Departure

The morning arrives cold and wrapped in a dense fog. The heavy dew turns the grass into a damp carpet that muffles their steps. Kyrah and the two hunters, Taneias and Javon, wait outside the tavern, their faces tense but determined. The smoke from their breaths mixes with the mist.

> _"The boar leaves a trail of destruction impossible to ignore,"_ murmurs Taneias, adjusting his bow. _"But it's smart. Cruel. It's already killed hunters better than us."_

---

## The Path Through the Devastated Lands

### Description of the Journey

As the group moves north, entering the hills of the Heartlands, the devastation becomes inescapable. What were once prosperous farms are now smoldering ruins. Wooden fences are shattered, fields of crops trampled into mud mixed with dried blood.

**Sensory Details:**

- **Sight:** Trees with trunks snapped in half, deep tusk marks on stones and earth, crows feeding on half-eaten corpses.
    
- **Smell:** The sickening scent of rotten flesh mixed with turned earth and something more—a sulfuric, almost demonic odor.
    
- **Sound:** The unnatural silence. No birds sing, no insects buzz. Only the wind whistling through the empty ruins.
    
- **Touch:** The air feels heavier here, as if the land itself is weeping.
    

### Specific Locations Along the Way

#### 1. The Thornwood Farm (2 hours of travel)

A small family property, completely destroyed. The main house has been torn down, its walls collapsed. In the barn, you find:

- **Disturbing Discovery:** The bodies of a family (parents and two children) have been dragged into the barn and partially devoured. A journal, open next to the mother, reveals her last words: _"We sacrificed a sheep at the entrance of the property as an offering. It ate the sheep... and then it attacked us anyway. The gods have abandoned us."_
    

**Personal Moment for the Vanished One:** Among the wreckage, you find a medallion with the symbol of the Dragonlords—identical to the one you carried in life. The family must have kept it as a protective relic. It didn't work.

#### 2. The Desecrated Shrine of Mytros (4 hours of travel)

A small stone altar dedicated to the Goddess of the Dawn has been completely destroyed. The statues have been toppled and broken, and there are deep tusk marks in the stone offerings.

**Personal Moment for the Exile:** You recognize the patterns in the wreckage—this altar was built by Amazonian refugees who fled Themis generations ago. There are faded paintings of warriors on the broken stones. Your people once worshiped here, seeking Mytros's blessing before battles.

**Optional Encounter:** A **ghost** of a priestess (use the stats for a **Spectre**, but she is not initially hostile) appears, lamenting:

> _"The Titans sent us this beast to punish us... but why? What have we done but survive? Help us. Make this creature pay."_

She may grant inspiration to a character who swears to avenge her.

#### 3. The Dead Hunters' Camp (6 hours of travel)

You find the remains of a group of experienced hunters who tried to ambush the boar. There are at least eight bodies, some torn apart, others trampled beyond recognition.

**Investigation (DC 12):** Reveals they set up an elaborate trap with nets and pits, but the boar simply destroyed them. Marks on the trees show that the creature hunted them down methodically, one by one.

**Personal Moment for the Demigod:** Among the belongings, you find a letter addressed to "Pythor, the King of Estoria"—one of the dead hunters was a veteran who served with your father in the First War. The letter was never sent: _"My Lord, I do not fear death in battle. But this creature... it is not natural. It kills for pleasure, not for need. The Titans send us monsters that reflect their own cruelty."_

## Suggested Combat Encounters

### Random Encounter Table

Roll 1d8 every 2 hours of travel:

|d8|Encounter|
|---|---|
|1-3|No encounter|
|4|Cursed Crows|
|5|Desperate Survivors|
|6|Cultists of Sydon|
|7|Starving Wolves|
|8|Omen of the Boar|

---

### 1. Cursed Crows

**Creatures:** 2d6 **Swarms of Ravens** (use the stats for **Swarm of Ravens** from the MM, but with 27 HP each)

These crows have been corrupted by the boar's presence. Their eyes glow red, and they voraciously attack any living creature.

**Tactic:** They focus on wounded characters, trying to peck out eyes and throats.

**Personal Moment for the Cursed One:** The crows seem particularly drawn to you. During combat, you hear whispers in your mind: _"Cursed... like us... together in the darkness..."_ (Roll your curse die after the combat).

---

### 2. Desperate Survivors

**Creatures:** 1d4+2 **Bandits** (but they are not initially hostile)

These farmers are starving and desperate, hiding in a cave. They might:

- **Ask for help:** Beg for food and an escort to Estoria.
    
- **Give information:** They saw the boar pass by here last night, heading north.
    
- **Become hostile:** If the party refuses to help or acts arrogant.
    

**Personal Moment for the Exile:** One of the farmers is an exiled Amazon who recognizes you. She was banished from Themis for refusing to participate in the rituals of Lutheria. She kneels: _"My queen... you live! There is hope for us yet."_

**Reward for Helping:** If the party helps them, they reveal a shortcut through the hills (reduces travel time by 1 hour).

---

### 3. Cultists of Sydon

**Creatures:** 1 **Priest** (Braeca, the leader) + 4 **Acolytes**

These cultists are also hunting the boar—but to take it alive as an offering to Sydon.

> _"Stand aside, heretics! This beast belongs to the Lord of Storms. To interfere is blasphemy!"_

**Tactic:** Braeca casts **Hold Person** on the frontline characters, while the acolytes use **Sacred Flame**.

**Personal Moment for the Vanished One:** Braeca recognizes you from historical records: _"Impossible... you should have been dead for centuries! Sydon will know of this—the past returns to haunt the present!"_

**Treasure:** Braeca carries a **Potion of Healing** and a **Holy Symbol of Sydon** (can be sold for 25 gp or destroyed as an act of defiance).

---

### 4. Starving Wolves

**Creatures:** 1 **Dire Wolf** (alpha) + 2d4 **Wolves**

The boar's presence has driven away all the normal game. These wolves are desperately hungry and attack the party.

**Tactic:** The Dire Wolf tries to knock down the weakest-looking character, while the smaller wolves flank.

**Personal Moment for the Demigod:** During the fight, you feel a strange connection to the alpha wolf. Make a Persuasion check (DC 15) or Animal Handling check (DC 12) to calm it. If successful, the wolf recognizes the divine spark within you and backs down, taking its pack with it. Pythor once tamed mountain wolves in a similar fashion.

---

### 5. Omen of the Boar

**Not a combat, but a tense encounter**

You find fresh tracks from the boar—enormous, deep, and still smoking with an unnatural heat. The sulfuric smell is unbearable here.

**Wisdom (Perception) Check DC 14:** You notice the boar passed through here less than an hour ago. Its tracks lead to a clearing ahead.

**Everyone must make a Wisdom Saving Throw DC 12:**

- **Failure:** You have a brief, disturbing vision—the boar standing on mountains of corpses, its red eyes fixed on you. You are **frightened** for 1 minute.
    
- **Success:** You feel only a deep chill.
    

**Personal Moment for the Cursed One:** The curse in your blood reacts to the boar's corrupted presence. You feel physical pain (1d4 necrotic damage) but also gain advantage on your next tracking check against the creature. The curse recognizes another victim of the Titans.

## Dreams and Visions During Rest

If the party takes a short or long rest before finding the boar:

### Dream of the Demigod (Child of Pythor)

You dream of your father in his past glory—young, strong, leading armies against the gygans. But then the scene changes. Pythor ages rapidly, his body bending under the weight of centuries of failure. He looks at you through the dream:

> _"My child, I failed. I failed to protect those I loved. I failed to defeat my enemies. Do not fail as I have failed. Be stronger. Be what I could not be."_

You wake up with tears on your face. **Gain inspiration.**

---

### Dream of the Cursed One

You dream of your ancestors—a long line of tormented faces, all carrying the same curse as you. They reach for you through the darkness, pleading:

> _"Break the chains... free us... do not let our agony be eternal..."_

At the end of the dream, you see a gigantic figure—half Sydon, half Lutheria—laughing as it weaves black threads through the generations of your family.

You wake up in a cold sweat. **Roll your curse die.**

---

### Dream of the Vanished One

You are back on the battlefield of the First War. Around you, Dragonlords mounted on dragons fight against hordes of gygans. You recognize faces—comrades long dead. They shout your name, asking for orders.

But then everything freezes. A voice echoes:

> _"You have returned... but the world moved on without you. They no longer need the Dragonlords. They do not need you."_

You wake up with the poignant question: is there still a place for you in this new world?

**If you share this dream with the others, gain inspiration.**

---

### Dream of the Exile

You dream of Themis—not as it is now, but as it should be. Your mother is alive, seated on the rightful throne. The Amazons sing battle hymns, not to Lutheria, but to the Great Mothers. You are by her side, not as a hidden child, but as the recognized heir.

Then, the scene shatters. Medusae with the faces of the impostors enter the throne room. Your mother is killed again. And you wake up.

> _"This is the world that was stolen from you. Reclaim it."_

**Gain advantage on your next Intimidation or Persuasion check against the false queens.**

## The Boar's Lair

When you finally track the boar to its lair, read the description of the area **"The Boar's Lair"** from the original document, but add these details:

### Tactical Preparations

Before entering the cave, Taneias and Javon suggest:

**Taneias:** _"That creature is smart. If we go into the cave, we'll be cornered. We need to draw it out."_

**Javon:** _"Or set traps. Rope, stakes, anything to slow it down when it charges."_

**Kyrah:** _"Remember—this is not just a hunt. It is a test. Show the gods that you are worthy."_

The players can now prepare traps as described in the original document (DC 12 Survival for each).

### Moment Before the Confrontation

Give each player a chance to make a final statement or action before the battle:

- **The Demigod can ask for Pythor's blessing** (roll Religion DC 12 - success grants advantage on the first attack roll).
    
- **The Cursed One can try to channel their curse into strength** (roll Arcana DC 14 - success allows rerolling the first 1 on an attack or check).
    
- **The Vanished One can recall ancient tactics against beasts** (roll History DC 12 - success grants a temporary +2 bonus to AC).
    
- **The Exile can invoke the warrior spirit of the Amazons** (roll Intimidation DC 12 - success imposes disadvantage on the boar's first attack roll).
    

---

## After the Battle

When the boar falls, its final words echo in everyone's minds (as per the original document). But add these personalized reactions:

**To the Cursed One:** The boar fixes its dying eyes on you: _"I see the mark on them... Sydon and Lutheria... they made us, you and I... monsters for their entertainment..."_

**To the Demigod:** _"Child of the failed one... you carry the blood of a coward who could not stop me..."_

**To the Vanished One:** _"Ghost of the past... you do not belong in this age... go back to your tomb..."_

**To the Exile:** _"Queen without a throne... what difference do you make? The Titans always win in the end..."_

---

## Final Reflection

After burying or burning the bodies in the boar's cave (as per the custom Kyrah mentions), give the players a moment to process:

- What did this boar represent to each of them?
    
- How do they feel about the gods who sent this creature?
    
- Are they ready for what comes next?
    

**Kyrah observes:** _"You have proven your worth. But this is only the beginning. The Titans sent this beast as a warning. The real war is yet to come."_

---

This journey to the boar is not just about tracking and combat—it's about establishing the central themes of the campaign: fate vs. free will, the weight of the past, the cruelty of the Titans, and the determination of heroes to challenge the gods.
