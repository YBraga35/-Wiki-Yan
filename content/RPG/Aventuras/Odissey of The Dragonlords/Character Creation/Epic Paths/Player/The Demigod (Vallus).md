**Demigod (Vallus)**   
You are the mortal child of Vallus, the goddess of Wisdom. From an early age you have demonstrated wisdom beyond your years. You have been kept in secret by your mother. She has kept you hidden for reasons she has not explained to you. While her love for you is great it is also stifling. Vallus has kept you sheltered in Mytros your whole life and out of the public eye. You long to go out and explore and see the world. You want to know who your father is and how you can make your mark on the world.

**For players who love:** Rapunzel   
**Restrictions**: You must be a native of Thylea  
**Adventure Hook**: You have been summoned by the Oracle who knows of your divine heritage and thinks you have a part to play in the upcoming prophecy. Your mother realizing how important this is to all of Thylea has let you finally venture forth from Mytros.

**Heroic Tasks**

* Find your father  
* Discover your mother's secrets  
* Claim your heritage.Pick one greater magic item from this list:

  * **Sunblade** *(any weapon)*  
  * **Pendant of Sun (Necklace of Fireballs with 3 charges refreshing 1d3 per day DC 15\)**   
  * **Dragon Scale Mail(any armor)**

**Divine Blessing**  
\-Blessing of Wisdom: Increase your Wisdom by 2 and your maximum Wisdom to 22\.
