# Opening Hooks and Introduction

## Opening Scene: The Agent Arrives

**Recommended Delivery**: Read this aloud or paraphrase dramatically at the start of Session 1.

---

*The voyage from the mainland was mercifully short, but even three days at sea felt like an eternity when your destination was Yllmourne. The sailors whispered about the city the entire journey—how the plague turned men's lungs to brine, how ships vanished in the harbour without a trace, how even the gulls had learned to avoid the docks.*

*As your vessel rounds the headland, you see it: Yllmourne, the Pearl of Lhodos, now tarnished beyond recognition. The city sprawls across the coastline in layers—dockside slums pressed against stone warehouses, merchant quarters climbing the hillside, and above it all, the Governor's Palace and the Temple of Onotas, gleaming white against the grey sky like bones picked clean.*

*But it's the smell that hits you first. Even from this distance, the harbour reeks of rotting fish, unwashed bodies, and something else—something medicinal and wrong. The faint, acrid tang of quicklime used to bury the Mudskipper's victims.*

*Your hand instinctively moves to the holy symbol of Maal resting against your chest. The God of Life, Death, and Law sent you here for a reason: Dagmara is dead. Your fellow Agent was found three days ago in an alley behind the Slippery Eel tavern, her throat cut, her eyes missing. The local authorities ruled it a robbery gone wrong. But Dagmara was no fool—she would not fall to common cutpurses. And her last message to the Temple spoke of corruption, plague-vectors, and a name: **Faylene**.*

*As the ship docks and you step onto the slime-slicked pier, you feel it immediately: this city is sick. Not just with plague, but with something deeper. Something that sets your divine senses screaming.*

*Welcome to Yllmourne. The investigation begins.*

---

**After the Opening**: Allow players to describe their first actions. Are they heading straight to Dagmara's body? Seeking allies? Investigating the tavern? Use their choices to determine which hook(s) to emphasize.

## Character Background Integration

### The Agent of Maal's Role

As the primary protagonist (whether a single PC or the party's shared background), the Agent of Maal has clear motivations:

**Primary Mission**: Investigate Dagmara's murder. Determine who killed her, why, and whether it connects to her investigation into Yllmourne's corruption.

**Secondary Missions**:
- Assess the Mudskipper plague's true nature (natural disease or supernatural curse?)
- Identify whether Yllmourne's authorities can be trusted or are complicit
- Report findings to Maal's Temple for potential divine intervention

**Character Hooks**:
- **The Devoted Investigator**: You trained alongside Dagmara at the Temple. Her death is personal.
- **The Replacement**: You were supposed to come to Yllmourne *with* Dagmara, but were delayed. Her death is your failure.
- **The Specialist**: Maal's Temple sent you because you have expertise Dagmara lacked (plague investigation, urban politics, planar threats).
- **The Outsider**: You've never been to Yllmourne. You're deliberately chosen for your lack of local connections—no one can compromise you.

### Party Integration Options

**If the Party are All Maal Agents**:
- Frame as a task force sent after Dagmara's death
- Each PC has different specialties (investigator, healer, warrior, scholar)
- Tension can arise from different interpretations of Maal's law

**If Only One PC is the Agent**:
- Other PCs are hired assistants, local allies, or have personal stakes in Yllmourne
- The Agent is the "quest giver" but should respect others' expertise
- **Sample Ties**:
  - **The Local Guide**: Grew up in Yllmourne's slums; knows the docks intimately
  - **The Exiled Noble**: Fled Yllmourne years ago when Sefton rose to power; seeking redemption
  - **The Plague Doctor**: Came to study the Mudskipper; discovered it's not natural
  - **The Sailor**: Survived a mysterious ship sinking; wants answers
  - **The Former Servant**: Worked for Cy or Sefton; knows too much and was forced out

**If No One is Formally a Maal Agent**:
- The party are investigators hired by the Temple (mercenary angle)
- Dagmara's ghost/divine vision appears to them (supernatural hook)
- They stumble into the mystery accidentally and become invested

### Dagmara's Character (for Flashbacks/Context)

**Dagmara Stoneheart** (Deceased Human Cleric of Maal, Level 5)
- **Appearance**: Stocky, middle-aged woman with close-cropped grey hair and calloused hands from decades of gravedigging before her calling
- **Personality**: Gruff, darkly humorous, uncompromising about truth but merciful in judgment
- **Last Known Actions**: Investigating plague origins; interviewed Navula (plague-touched healer); tracked shipments to the Library; mentioned "Faylene" and "rotten from the pulpit"
- **Cause of Death**: Throat cut with professional precision (assassin, not thug); eyes removed post-mortem (symbolic or ritualistic?)

**DM Note**: Use Dagmara in flashbacks, discovered journal entries, or even ghostly visions to provide cryptic clues. She should feel like a mentor even in death.

## Initial Hooks

Provide players with multiple entry points into the investigation. Based on their choices in the opening scene, emphasize 2-3 of these hooks most strongly.

### Hook 1: The Murder Scene

**Location**: Alley behind the Slippery Eel tavern (dockside slum)

**The Scene**:
The alley is roped off with mildewed rope, but no guards remain—budget cuts. Dagmara's body was removed to the city morgue, but her blood still stains the cobblestones in a dark, rust-brown pool. Chalk outlines where she fell.

**Clues Available**:
- **DC 12 Investigation**: Find Dagmara's holy symbol in the gutter—it was torn from her neck but not taken (not a robbery)
- **DC 14 Investigation**: Scuff marks suggest she fought back; defensive wounds on the chalk outline notes
- **DC 15 Medicine**: The throat wound was precise, professional—this was an assassination, not a mugging
- **DC 16 Perception**: A single grey thread caught on a nail—fine wool, expensive (noble's cloak or priest's vestment?)
- **Divine Sense or Detect Magic**: Faint necromantic aura lingers—something was done to her body after death (the eye removal)

**NPC Encounter**:
**Gritch** (neutral evil goblin informant) lurks nearby. For 5 gp, he reveals:
- "Saw a hooded figure leave the alley that night—tall, walked like quality folk"
- "Dagmara was asking questions about the Library, about shipments from the docks"
- "Word is, she pissed off someone important. That's how you end up eyeless in the shit-district."

**Connection**: Leads to Hook 3 (mysterious ship sinkings—what was being shipped?) or Hook 2 (investigating the morgue/body).

### Hook 2: The Morgue and the Mudskipper

**Location**: Yllmourne Public Morgue (overcrowded warehouse near the docks)

**The Scene**:
Dozens of plague victims lie wrapped in stained sheets, waiting for mass burial. The air is thick with quicklime dust and decay. The mortician, **Helga Ironfoot** (neutral good dwarf), is overwhelmed and grateful for any help.

**What Helga Knows**:
- Dagmara's body is here; Helga can show the party (body is preserved due to Gentle Repose cast by the Temple)
- **The Eye Removal**: Done with surgical precision, post-mortem; Helga has no idea why
- **Dagmara's Belongings**: Her journal was "confiscated by city authorities" (actually stolen); her traveling pack remains with minor gear

**Examining Dagmara's Body**:
- **DC 13 Medicine**: Confirms assassination; no signs of poison or disease
- **DC 15 Religion**: The eye removal matches old rituals to blind the dead so they cannot witness secrets in the afterlife (someone feared what Dagmara saw)
- **Speak with Dead** (if cast): Dagmara can answer 5 questions:
  1. *"Who killed you?"* — "I... couldn't see their face. Hooded. Smelled of incense."
  2. *"Why were you killed?"* — "I learned too much... the plague... not natural..."
  3. *"What did you discover?"* — "Shipments... wrong cargo... Faylene knows..."
  4. *"Who is Faylene?"* — "Prelate... Onotas... rotting the faith from within..."
  5. *"What should we do?"* — "Trust Navula... find the source... stop the sinking..."

**The Mudskipper Plague**:
While at the morgue, Helga shows them plague victims:
- Lungs fill with seawater-like fluid; victims drown on dry land
- Corpses smell of brine and rot
- **DC 14 Nature or Medicine**: This isn't a normal disease—the fluid is literally *seawater*, not edema
- **DC 16 Arcana**: Faint transmutation magic lingers on victims—this is a curse, not contagion

**Connection**: Leads to Hook 4 (curse manifestations) or Hook 5 (seeking Navula, the plague-touched healer).

### Hook 3: Mysterious Ship Sinkings

**Location**: Harbourmaster's Office or dockside taverns

**The Situation**:
Over the past two months, seven ships have sunk in Yllmourne's harbour—always at night, always without survivors, always carrying specific cargo. The harbourmaster, **Torvin Saltbeard** (lawful neutral human), is desperate for answers.

**What Torvin Knows**:
- Ships sank suddenly, no storms or obvious damage
- Cargo manifests showed diverse goods: grain, textiles, wine, but also "Library shipments" and "Temple supplies"
- Survivors? None. Bodies wash up days later, lungs full of seawater (Mudskipper-like symptoms)
- Rumours of sabotage by Sefton's smugglers to eliminate competition—but Sefton denies involvement

**Investigation Options**:
- **Interview Sailors** (DC 12 Persuasion): "Unnatural fog rolls in before the sinkings"; "Heard singing from the water"; "Ships just... opened up, like something clawed them from below"
- **Examine Wreckage** (DC 14 Investigation): Hull timbers show claw marks—too large for any known sea creature
- **Diving Expedition** (requires magic or equipment): Find a sunken ship; cargo holds are empty except for seaweed and a strange barnacled idol (faint aura of conjuration magic—summons something from the deep)

**Connection**: Leads to Hook 7 (tracking shipments) or reveals connection between plague and maritime sabotage.

### Hook 4: Curse Manifestations - Callista's Haunting

**Location**: Callista's estate (merchant quarter, hillside)

**The Situation**:
**Callista Whitesail** (neutral good human merchant) is being haunted. Mirrors fog over in her presence, reflections move independently, and she's plagued by visions of drowning. She's desperate for help and will pay well.

**What Callista Knows**:
- The haunting started two weeks ago, after she attended a religious service led by Prelate Faylene
- She feels like she's being "watched through her own eyes"
- Dreams of dark water, something pulling her down
- Her business has suffered; clients think she's cursed

**Investigation**:
- **DC 13 Arcana**: Callista is under a hex—divination and enchantment magic combined
- **DC 15 Religion**: The hex's structure matches Onotanian rituals, but twisted (corrupted religious magic)
- **Detect Magic**: Strong enchantment aura on Callista; a scrying sensor follows her
- **Remove Curse** temporarily alleviates symptoms but doesn't break the hex (the caster is too powerful)

**Following the Thread**:
- Callista mentions Prelate Faylene blessed her specifically during the service
- If players stake out Callista's home, they see a **cloaked figure** watching from the shadows (one of Faylene's Rotarist agents)

**Connection**: Directly implicates Faylene (mentioned in Dagmara's last message); leads to investigating the Onotanian Temple.

### Hook 5: Navula's Grotto - The Plague-Touched Healer

**Location**: The Grotto (sea cave below the docks, accessible at low tide)

**The Situation**:
**Navula** (neutral good plague-touched human) is a former healer now afflicted with the Mudskipper. Instead of dying, she survived but transformed—her lungs adapted to breathe both air and water. She lives in a cave, treating other plague victims with limited success.

**Meeting Navula**:
- She's initially fearful—authorities tried to burn her alive as a "plague-spreader"
- If approached peacefully, she's eager to help; she respected Dagmara
- **What Navula Knows**:
  - The plague is a *curse*, not a disease; it's selective, targeting specific people
  - "I got sick after handling cargo from the *Serpent's Gift*—one of the sunken ships"
  - "There are children in the slums showing early symptoms; authorities are quarantining them to die"
  - "Dagmara believed the curse originated from something in the water, something summoned"

**Navula's Quest**:
She begs the party to save the quarantined children (leads into **Adventure 2: Hunters of Tenebrous Plain** adaptation).

**Connection**: Provides crucial info about plague's supernatural nature; introduces theme of "authorities sacrificing the innocent."

### Hook 6: The Library Under Threat

**Location**: Yllmourne Free Library (neutral ground, old stone building)

**The Situation**:
The Library is one of the few neutral spaces in Yllmourne, serving scholars, mages, and historians. **Elara Moonshadow** (neutral good half-elf Head Librarian) is panicking: the Onotanian Royal Guard has been "inspecting" the Library, claiming it houses heretical texts. She fears they plan to burn it down.

**What Elara Knows**:
- Dagmara spent her last three days researching in the restricted archives
- She was looking into "planar incursions," "historical curses," and "Yllmourne's founding"
- The Royal Guard, led by **Commander Vareth**, keeps demanding access to the restricted section
- Elara suspects someone powerful wants specific knowledge destroyed

**Investigation Options**:
- **Examine Dagmara's Research** (DC 10 Investigation): Find her notes on historical curses tied to sea deities and references to "The Sodality" (mysterious cult)
- **Defend the Library**: If the Royal Guard attacks, players must decide whether to fight (antagonizes Onotas faith) or negotiate
- **Research the Curse**: Access to the archives grants advantage on future checks about the plague, Faylene, or planar threats

**Connection**: Establishes the Library as a key asset; introduces Commander Vareth as an antagonist; plants seeds for **Adventure 7: The Lost Treasure of Paletree Rise**.

### Hook 7: Tracking the Shipments

**Location**: Dockside warehouses

**The Situation**:
Dagmara's notes (if recovered) or Speak with Dead mention "wrong cargo." Investigating the sunken ships' cargo manifests reveals discrepancies: items listed that don't match typical merchant goods.

**Investigation**:
- **DC 14 Investigation**: Cross-referencing manifests shows all sunken ships carried shipments labeled "Temple Supplies" or "Library Materials"
- **Interview Dock Workers** (DC 13 Persuasion): "Aye, strange crates—heavy, inscribed with symbols, smelled like brine even before they hit the water"
- **Bribe a Smuggler** (20 gp): "Those crates? They came from a ship called the *Abyssal Choir*. Flies Sefton's colours but don't answer to him. Creepy bastards."

**The *Abyssal Choir***:
A ship crewed by cultists serving Naran and Thellos. It's currently docked in a hidden cove (discovered later in Act II). The cargo contains cursed idols that summon water elementals to sink ships.

**Connection**: Reveals the planar conspiracy; ties shipments to both the Library (knowledge destruction) and plague (cursed cargo).

### Hook 8: Political Intrigue - Cy vs. Sefton

**Location**: The Governor's Palace or Seafarers' Rest (Sefton's HQ)

**The Situation**:
Both **Governor Cy** (legitimate but ineffectual ruler) and **Admiral Sefton** (criminal warlord) want the party's help, for different reasons.

**Cy's Offer**:
- Publicly, he wants the murders and sinkings solved to restore order
- Privately, he wants dirt on Sefton to justify a crackdown
- Offers official authority, resources, and payment

**Sefton's Offer**:
- Claims the sinkings are hurting *his* smuggling business too
- Suspects Cy's administration is incompetent or corrupt
- Offers underworld contacts, muscle, and "alternative solutions"

**The Dilemma**:
Players must navigate both factions without committing fully to either (yet). Accepting help from one may antagonize the other.

**Connection**: Establishes the political landscape; both leaders are potential allies or obstacles depending on players' approach.

## Establishing Yllmourne

### Sensory Details

Use these atmospheric touchstones to make Yllmourne feel real:

**Sights**:
- Slums: Rotting wood, patched sailcloth roofs, open sewers, quarantine zones marked with red paint
- Docks: Rusted chains, barnacled pilings, fog that never quite lifts, gulls circling ominously
- Merchant Quarter: Faded grandeur, boarded-up shops, anxious merchants haggling
- Temple District: White marble stained with soot, Onotanian priests in crimson robes, desperate crowds seeking blessings

**Sounds**:
- Constant lapping of waves mixed with coughing (the Mudskipper)
- Bells tolling for the dead (hourly)
- Gulls' cries (but they sound wrong—too harsh, almost mocking)
- Tavern music (always minor keys, always melancholic)

**Smells**:
- Brine, rot, quicklime, cheap incense, unwashed bodies, cooking fish, medicinal herbs

**Emotions**:
- Paranoia: Everyone suspects everyone
- Desperation: The plague is slowly killing the city
- Exhaustion: Authorities are overwhelmed
- Defiance: Some still fight to save Yllmourne

### Key NPCs to Meet First

**Session 1-2 Introductions**:
1. **Helga Ironfoot** (Morgue): Ally, provides medical/forensic expertise
2. **Navula** (Grotto): Tragic figure, source of plague information
3. **Torvin Saltbeard** (Harbourmaster): Bureaucratic ally, provides maritime info
4. **Gritch** (Informant): Seedy contact, sells rumours
5. **Elara Moonshadow** (Library): Ally, provides research access

**Session 3-4 Introductions** (as investigation deepens):
1. **Prelate Faylene** (Onotas Temple): Outwardly helpful, secretly villainous
2. **Governor Cy**: Well-meaning but overwhelmed
3. **Admiral Sefton**: Charismatic criminal, morally grey
4. **Callista Whitesail**: Victim of conspiracy, potential ally

## First Session Structure

### Recommended Flow

**Session 1: "Arrival and First Blood" (3-4 hours)**

1. **Opening Scene** (15 min): Arrival in Yllmourne, establish atmosphere
2. **Player Introductions** (20 min): Each PC describes themselves, their reaction to the city
3. **Hook Selection** (10 min): Players decide their first move
4. **Investigation Phase 1** (60 min): Explore chosen hook (e.g., visit morgue, interview witnesses)
5. **First Combat/Threat** (40 min): Encounter plague-touched thugs, Rotarist agents, or summoned creature (establish danger)
6. **Downtime & Theorizing** (20 min): Players rest, discuss findings, plan next steps
7. **Cliffhanger** (5 min): Reveal a new threat or shocking discovery (e.g., Dagmara's journal is found, mentions player's name)

**Session 2: "Deepening the Mystery" (3-4 hours)**

1. **Recap** (5 min): Refresh players on discoveries
2. **Follow-Up Investigations** (90 min): Pursue 2-3 additional hooks
3. **NPC Encounter** (30 min): Meet a key figure (Faylene, Cy, or Sefton)
4. **Revelation** (20 min): Connect at least two threads (e.g., shipments + plague = cursed cargo)
5. **Hook into First Full Adventure** (15 min): Transition into **Adventure 1: The Night Messengers**

## Player Agency

### Embracing Chaos

**Do**:
- Let players choose which hooks to pursue; they don't need to investigate all of them
- Allow creative solutions (e.g., using magic to scry instead of legwork)
- Reward players connecting dots you didn't explicitly plan
- Adapt NPC loyalties based on player actions

**Don't**:
- Force a specific investigation order
- Punish players for "missing" clues—have NPCs remind them or provide redundant info
- Railroad them toward specific conclusions
- Make the mystery unsolvable without DM hints

### When Players Go Off-Script

**Example**: Players decide to break into Faylene's private chambers in Session 1, before you planned to introduce her.

**Good Response**:
- Allow the attempt; set high DCs for stealth/breaking in
- If they succeed, reward them with early clues (Faylene's journal, cursed components)
- Escalate consequences (Faylene sends assassins, turns public opinion against them)
- Adjust later adventures to account for early discovery

**Bad Response**:
- "Faylene's chambers are magically sealed; you can't enter yet."
- Punish them with instant capture/death for being clever

### The Investigation Journal

**Player Tool**: Encourage players to keep a shared journal (physical or digital) tracking:
- NPCs met (name, role, trustworthiness)
- Locations visited
- Clues discovered
- Working theories

At the start of each session, invite one player to recap using the journal. This reinforces their agency and helps you understand their mental model of the mystery.

## Conclusion

The opening hooks are designed to pull players into Yllmourne's web of intrigue through multiple entry points. By Session 3, they should feel:
- **Invested**: Dagmara's murder and the plague are personal
- **Overwhelmed**: There's too much to investigate (this is intentional—it feels real)
- **Capable**: They've solved at least one mystery and earned an ally or two
- **Hooked**: They want to know what's really happening in this cursed city

From here, transition into [[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)|Act I's full adventures]], where the surface mysteries begin to reveal deeper conspiracies.
