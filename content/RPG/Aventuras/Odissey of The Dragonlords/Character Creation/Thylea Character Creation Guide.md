# ⚔️ _Character Creation Guide for Thylea_

_For use with the Odyssey of the Dragonlords setting_

Welcome, hero. Your journey through the lands of Thylea begins with a choice — one that defines your destiny, binds you to ancient oaths, and places your name among legends.

This guide walks you through the unique character creation process for _Odyssey of the Dragonlords_, using **only** the approved campaign materials. You will not require the standard _Player’s Handbook_, as the world of Thylea has its own races, deities, equipment, and heroic narrative structure.

---

## 🔱 Step 1: Choose Your Epic Path

Your Epic Path is the cornerstone of your character’s destiny. It is more than a background — it determines how the world reacts to you, how prophecy shapes your tale, and the unique boons and burdens you carry.

> 📘 Refer to:
> 
> - _Odyssey of the Dragonlords – Player's Guide_
>     
> - _Guide to Xophos_ (for additional Epic Paths)
>     

Each Epic Path offers:

- A unique **prophecy** that unfolds over the campaign
    
- Special **starting abilities or items**
    
- Roleplay hooks tied to the gods, titans, and mortal factions
    

**Available Epic Paths include** (but are not limited to):

- The Doomed One
    
- The Vanished One
    
- The Lost Heir
    
- The Cursed One
    
- The Demi-God (and more)
    

> 💡 _Choose your Path first — it will guide the rest of your choices._

---

## ⚔️ Step 2: Choose Your Class

Once your destiny is chosen, select a **Class** to define your skills, powers, and role in the party. Thylea uses the standard D&D 5e class framework but with thematic adaptations for this unique mythological setting.

> 📘 Refer to:
> 
> - _Odyssey of the Dragonlords – Player's Guide_
>     
> - _Guide to Xophos_ (for setting-specific archetypes)
>     

All core 5e classes are allowed **as long as they are adapted to the Thylean setting.** Some classes have special narrative or mechanical adjustments — for example:

- Paladins and Clerics draw divine power from the **Five Gods**, the **Twin Titans**, or lesser deities of the Thylean Pantheon.
    
- Bards may use poetic traditions, epic chanting, or magical instruments inspired by Thylean culture.
    
- Warlocks may form pacts with ancient dragons, primordial spirits, or the forgotten gods.
    

> 🛠️ _Multiclassing is allowed but must be justified in-character and thematically coherent._

---

## 🧬 Step 3: Choose Your Species (Race)

Thylea distinguishes sharply between **native races** and **colonist races**, each with their own cultural significance and narrative consequences.

> 📘 Refer to:
> 
> - _Races of Thylea (Adaptation Guide)_
>     
> - _Odyssey of the Dragonlords – Player's Guide_
>     
> - _Guide to Xophos_
>     

### Native Species of Thylea:

- **Centaurs** – Proud warriors and swift travelers
    
- **Satyrs** – Joyful tricksters and lovers of freedom
    
- **Minotaurs** – Honorable survivors of ancient slavery
    
- **Nymphs** – Spirits bound to nature or places of power
    
- **Siren** – Enigmatic winged songcasters with tragic pasts
    
- **Myrmekes**, **Fey-Blooded**, and others
    

### Colonist Species (Adapted from 5e):

- **Humans** – Settlers from foreign lands, divided into various cultures
    
- **Elves, Dwarves, Halflings, Tieflings** – All present but viewed through the lens of colonization and foreign descent
    
- Use the **Race Adaptation Guide** to interpret these options in a Thylean context
    

> ⚠️ Avoid default racial lore from Forgotten Realms or other settings. Thylea has its own history.

---

## 🧭 Step 4: Determine Background

Backgrounds influence your character’s past and skills. You may use the standard ones **as long as they fit the setting**, or choose Thylea-specific ones found in:

> 📘 _Odyssey of the Dragonlords – Player's Guide_

Examples:

- **Acolyte of the Five Gods**
    
- **Veteran of the First War**
    
- **Initiate of the Oracle**
    

> 📝 _Customize your background traits to reflect your Epic Path or personal journey._

---

## 🛡️ Step 5: Assign Ability Scores

Use **either Point Buy** (27 points) **or the Standard Array** (15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8).  
Rolling for stats is discouraged unless approved by the DM.

Remember:

- Apply species bonuses after choosing ability scores
    
- Prioritize your class’s primary attributes, but don’t neglect roleplay potential
    

---

## 🧰 Step 6: Select Equipment

Thylea has **unique weapons, armors, and trinkets** based on its mythological roots.

> 📘 Refer to:
> 
> - _Odyssey of the Dragonlords – Player's Guide_ (Equipment Section)
>     
> - Artifacts and magical items may be discovered through quests or oaths.
>     

Avoid standard equipment packs unless adapted to the setting. Instead, choose gear that fits your background, class, and origin within Thylea.

---

## 🕊️ Step 7: Connect to the World

Before your first session, consider:

- What is your relationship to the **gods** or **titans**?
    
- How does your **Epic Path** affect your goals and fears?
    
- Are you known or anonymous? Are you loved or feared?
    
- What oaths or burdens do you carry?
