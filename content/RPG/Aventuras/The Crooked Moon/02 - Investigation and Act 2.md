# Act 2 - Folklore and Trails (30-35 minutes)

## Objective
Players must gather at least 2 solid clues to locate and access the Effigy Grove. Present multiple paths simultaneously to maintain pacing.

## Investigation Locations

### 1. Temple of the Harvest

**External Description:**
*The temple is a modest stone building with corn sheaves carved into the lintels. Candlelight flickers within, and you can smell incense mixed with dried herbs.*

#### Brother Aldric - Harvest Domain Acolyte
**Appearance:** Young man, nervous, ink-stained fingers, harvest domain symbol

**Initial Interaction:**
"Oh, thank the Harvest Mother you're investigating! I've been blessing seeds day and night, but something's corrupting them. Look—" *He shows seeds that sprout black, twisted vines when blessed.*

**Religion/History Check DC 12:**
- Shows ancient map of the Effigy Grove (handout)
- Explains the grove was sealed after children disappeared 50 years ago
- The Horned King was worshipped there in darker times

**Map Details Revealed:**
- Three clearings connected by wicker-lined paths
- A ritual circle at the heart
- Warning: "Lunar phases ward the way"

**Additional Clue (Insight DC 14):**
Brother Aldric is holding back - his teacher went to investigate alone yesterday and hasn't returned.

### 2. The Blacksmith's Forge

**Scene Description:**
*The forge still glows hot, but the blacksmith works frantically on iron horseshoes and nails, muttering prayers with each hammer strike.*

#### Gunnar the Smith
**Appearance:** Massive arms, soot-covered, exhausted

**Initial Reaction:**
"Iron! It's the only thing that keeps them at bay. I've been working non-stop since the Jameson boy walked right past my shop at midnight, eyes rolled back white."

**Key Information (no check needed):**
- Sleepwalkers all head northeast toward the old grove
- They don't respond to anything except iron touch (breaks the trance briefly)
- Started three nights ago during the new moon

**Crafting Opportunity:**
- Can silver or bless weapons (10 minutes, 10gp per weapon)
- Sells iron filings (5gp) - thrown as action, reveals invisible fey/fiends

### 3. The Abandoned Farmstead

**Approaching the Farm:**
*The Briggsley farmstead sits empty, door ajar. Corn stalks in the field form unnatural patterns - concentric circles with a path leading northeast.*

**Investigation DC 12:**
- Inside: Signs of struggle, burnt wicker figures
- Journal entry: "They come at the third bell. Must stay awake. The scarecrows watch."
- Cellar: Ritual circle drawn in salt, partially scuffed

**Survival/Nature DC 13:**
- Tracks of multiple people walking in unison
- All barefoot, moving with identical gait
- Trail leads toward the grove

**Hidden Cache (Investigation DC 15):**
- Silver sickle (counts as silvered weapon)
- Vial of blessed oil (can bless one weapon for 1 hour)
- Note: "If you find this, DON'T follow the lights. The pumpkins aren't decorations - they're WATCHERS."

### 4. Town Records Office

**The Dusty Archive:**
*Scrolls and ledgers line the walls. The clerk dozed off at his desk, soft snoring mixing with rustling papers moved by an unfelt wind.*

**History Check DC 12 (advantage if helped by clerk):**
- **50 Years Ago:** "The Autumn of Weeping" - seven children vanished
- Grove was sealed by a traveling cleric
- Mentions "The Tithe of Grain and Blood"

**Critical Discovery (Investigation DC 14):**
Map showing the grove has three entrances:
1. Main path (heavily warded, likely guarded)
2. The Crow's Route (through the canopy, requires climbing)
3. The Underway (old root cellar tunnels, cramped but hidden)

## Optional Encounter - Cultist Scouts

### Trigger
If players spend too long investigating or make obvious preparations

### The Confrontation
**Setup:** 2-3 Cultists in wicker masks watching the roads

**Cultist Statistics:**
- **AC:** 12 (leather armor)
- **HP:** 9 (2d8) each
- **Attack:** Sickle +3 to hit, 1d6+1 slashing
- **Spells:** Command (1/day), Inflict Wounds (1/day)
- **Special:** When killed, body sprouts strangling vines (5ft difficult terrain)

**Stealth Approach (Group DEX DC 13):**
- Success: Bypass or surprise them
- Failure: Alert cultists, reinforcements in grove

**Interrogation (if captured):**
- "The Pumpkin Head must feed!"
- "The sleepers are the tithe!"
- "You're too late - the ritual began at moonrise!"

## The Sleepwalking Procession

### Encounter Trigger
As players prepare to leave for the grove

**The Scene:**
*A line of villagers shuffles past - men, women, children - all walking with eyes rolled back, humming a discordant harvest tune. They carry grain sheaves and don't respond to calls.*

**Options:**
1. **Follow them:** Leads directly to grove (bypasses some dangers)
2. **Stop them:** Iron touch or Dispel Magic breaks trance for 1 minute
3. **Wake them:** Lesser Restoration or Remove Curse saves one permanently

**If Questioned (briefly awakened):**
"The harvest... must feed the harvest... the king calls... the pumpkins watch..."

## Gathering Supplies

### Available Resources

**From Temple:**
- Holy water (2d6 radiant damage to fiends/undead, 25gp)
- Scrolls of Detect Evil and Good (50gp)
- Borrowed holy symbol

**From Blacksmith:**
- Iron weapons (+1 damage vs fey/fiends, 2x base cost)
- Iron filings (blinds fey for 1 round, DC 12 CON save)
- Wicker-cutting shears (advantage on escaping entangle)

**From Herbalist:**
- Antitoxin (advantage on poison saves, 50gp)
- Moonflower paste (darkvision 30ft for 1 hour, 25gp)
- Burned sage bundles (disadvantage on stealth, advantage vs possession)

## Information Summary for Players

By Act 2's end, players should know:
1. **Location:** The Effigy Grove, northeast of town
2. **Threat:** The Pumpkin Head and Horned King cultists
3. **Urgency:** Ritual in progress, villagers in danger
4. **Weakness:** Fire (especially blessed), iron, disrupting the ritual
5. **Approach:** Multiple paths available (main, trees, tunnels)

## Transition to Act 3

### The Final March
*As you approach the grove's edge, the twisted moon seems to pulse with sickly light. The humming grows louder - dozens of voices in dark harmony. Through the trees, you glimpse flickering pumpkin light and smell burning grain mixed with something sweet and rotten.*

*Three paths diverge:*
- **The Wicker Road:** Wide but decorated with hanging effigies
- **The Crow's Route:** Through the twisted canopy above
- **The Underway:** A root cellar entrance, cramped and dark

*Time is running out. The harvest ritual has begun.*

## DM Pacing Notes
- Players need only 2 solid clues to progress
- If stuck, have NPCs provide information
- Keep investigations brief (5 minutes per location)
- Introduce urgency through sleepwalkers or sounds from grove
- Optional combat should be quick (3 rounds maximum)
