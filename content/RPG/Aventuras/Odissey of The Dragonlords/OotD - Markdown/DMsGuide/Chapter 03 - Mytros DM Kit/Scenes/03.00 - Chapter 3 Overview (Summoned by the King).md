# Chapter 3: Summoned by the King — Overview

This document provides an English rendering of the PT material “03.00.md,” focused on the core opening of Chapter 3: the summons, approach to Mytros under Sydon’s storm, and the first contact with Tarchon.

## Opening Quote
> “You may be a goddess, my queen, but you fail to understand the hearts of your subjects. I alone know what is best for my people. Do not question me.”
>
> — King Acastus to Queen Vallus, Goddess of Wisdom

## Tarchon’s Arrival (p. 86)

> In the distant skies, three winged shapes approach. Sunlight glints off their scales — young copper dragons. Rumors spoke of Mytros’ dragons, but these are far more splendid than imagined. Red‑cloaked centurions ride them — high‑ranking officers. The dragons descend; three captains dismount and approach with determined eyes. Their leader steps forward: “I am Tarchon. We come on behalf of King Acastus to escort you to his palace. He requests an audience.”

- Tarchon (NG Captain) arrives with two other captains on young copper dragons; they will not attack; they appeal to honor/duty and disclose Acastus’ expectations.

## The Royal Gate (p. 87)

> Approaching Mytros, its immense scale dominates the senses: walls and temples across Mount Volkan’s foundations; a great harbor stretching for miles. The jewel of Thylea.
>
> But something is wrong. Massive, swirling black clouds loom; gales howl; the storm’s eye reveals Sydon’s scowling visage. His eyes lock onto you. A crackling bolt of lightning is hurled down.

Have the heroes roll initiative; the lowest suffers a lightning bolt (save applies). Use a second bolt at the second‑lowest if desired. Then continue into the palace‑focused flow.
