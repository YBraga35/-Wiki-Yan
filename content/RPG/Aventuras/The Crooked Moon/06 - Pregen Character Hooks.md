# Pregen Character Hooks and Special Interactions

## Character-Specific Connections to Enoch

### 1. Kael Ironleaf - Graveborn Fighter (Barrow Guard)
**Connection:** Previously guarded the ancient barrows near Enoch; recognizes similar burial practices in the grove
**Personal Hook:** One of the missing villagers is his living descendant (great-great-nephew)
**Special Knowledge:** Can sense undead/constructs within 30 feet in the grove
**Unique Interaction:** The wicker scarecrows hesitate before attacking him (ancient guardian recognition)

### 2. Lira Shadow-Reed - Silkborn Rogue (Sinner)
**Connection:** Owes a debt to Mayor Mara for pardoning a past theft
**Personal Hook:** Recognizes one cultist as former criminal contact
**Special Knowledge:** Knows secret smuggling tunnels that connect to grove's Underway
**Unique Interaction:** Can automatically succeed on one Deception check with cultists by claiming to join them

### 3. Dorn Grainblessed - Harvestborn Cleric (Harvest Domain)
**Connection:** Blessed the seeds for this year's planting ceremony
**Personal Hook:** His blessing ritual was corrupted - feels personally responsible
**Special Knowledge:** Can sanctify normal fire into "harvest flame" (10-minute ritual)
**Unique Interaction:** Sleepwalking villagers respond slightly to his holy symbol (stop walking for 1 round)

### 4. Nemi Wickerwhisper - Threadborn Druid (Circle of Wicker)
**Connection:** Apprenticed to a wicker-worker who disappeared last harvest
**Personal Hook:** Can hear whispers from wicker effigies warning of danger
**Special Knowledge:** Knows the true name of the Pumpkin Head ("Gordhallow")
**Unique Interaction:** Can command scarecrows for 1 round with opposed CHA check

### 5. Mira Algaz - Bogborn Druid (Old Ways)
**Connection:** Helped cleanse Enoch's water supply last season
**Personal Hook:** The corruption is spreading to the swamp lands she protects
**Special Knowledge:** Can identify which villagers are most deeply cursed
**Unique Interaction:** Regeneration trait works at double rate in the grove (roll 2 HD)

### 6. Jian Pest-Mist - Plagueborn Monk (Pestilent Haze)
**Connection:** Seeking redemption by healing what plague destroyed
**Personal Hook:** Recognizes the ritual's disease-like spread pattern
**Special Knowledge:** Can identify weak points in the ritual circle (advantage on disruption checks)
**Unique Interaction:** Immune to the Pumpkin Head's Harvest Scream

### 7. Seraph Slate - Stoneborn Paladin (Castigation)
**Connection:** Church sent them to investigate heretical activities
**Personal Hook:** Previous mentor tried to cleanse this grove and never returned
**Special Knowledge:** Divine Sense reveals exact number of fiends/undead in grove
**Unique Interaction:** Smite damage causes Pumpkin Head to reveal its weakness

### 8. Thalan Omenwalker - Gnarlborn Ranger (Grim Harbinger)
**Connection:** Has been tracking strange omens pointing to Enoch
**Personal Hook:** Spirit hound barks warnings near cursed areas
**Special Knowledge:** Can track sleepwalkers without rolling
**Unique Interaction:** Favored enemy (fiends) applies to Horned King cultists

### 9. Vexa Two-Voices - Curseborn Warlock (Great Fool)
**Connection:** The Great Fool finds the Horned King's seriousness amusing
**Personal Hook:** Can hear the Horned King and Great Fool arguing in their mind
**Special Knowledge:** Knows the ritual's fatal flaw (revealed through mad laughter)
**Unique Interaction:** Can cause cultists to laugh uncontrollably (WIS save DC 13)

### 10. Velren Hornmark - Relicborn Warlock (Horned King)
**Connection:** Serves a different aspect of the Horned King - sees cultists as heretics
**Personal Hook:** Must prove their interpretation is correct to maintain pact
**Special Knowledge:** Can read the cultists' ritual directly (no check needed)
**Unique Interaction:** Fanatic will try to convert rather than kill them

### 11. Lyra Crimson - Azureborn Sorcerer (Crimson Dynasty)
**Connection:** Family has ancient blood-debt to Enoch from founding days
**Personal Hook:** Visions show a family member among the cultists
**Special Knowledge:** Can see life force being drained from villagers
**Unique Interaction:** Blood magic can sever one villager's connection instantly

### 12. Eryndor Fogbound - Deepborn Wizard (Occultism)
**Connection:** Researching the Horned King cult for forbidden knowledge
**Personal Hook:** Has corresponded with the cult leader (doesn't know it)
**Special Knowledge:** Recognizes all ritual components and their purposes
**Unique Interaction:** Can speak the Horned King's true name to stun cultists (1 round)

### 13. Caldus Athanor - Human Wizard (Philosophy)
**Connection:** Seeking practical application of theoretical harvest principles
**Personal Hook:** Mentor theorized about "perfect harvest ritual" - this is a corruption
**Special Knowledge:** Knows alchemical counter to the cursed seeds
**Unique Interaction:** Can philosophically debate cultists into inaction (CHA contest)

### 14. Gruk of Cinders - Ashborn Barbarian (Experiment)
**Connection:** Experimental serums were derived from Enoch's herbs
**Personal Hook:** Rage serums are reacting to grove's corruption
**Special Knowledge:** Can smell the Pumpkin Head from 100 feet away
**Unique Interaction:** While raging, immune to difficult terrain in grove

### 15. Sska Gleam - Stoneborn Fighter (Barrow Guard)
**Connection:** Carved the memorial stones for last year's deceased
**Personal Hook:** Recognize names of missing as those who should be dead
**Special Knowledge:** Can read ancient grave-markers in grove revealing truth
**Unique Interaction:** Undead see them as "death itself" and avoid them when possible

### 16. Brunna Dewfall - Harvestborn Bard (Whistles)
**Connection:** Performed at last three harvest festivals
**Personal Hook:** Knows personal secrets about half the missing villagers
**Special Knowledge:** Can counter the cultists' chant with proper harvest songs
**Unique Interaction:** Bardic Inspiration works at double effect against fear in grove

### 17. Kor Halfhand - Graveborn Paladin (Castigation)
**Connection:** Lost their hand defending Enoch from undead years ago
**Personal Hook:** Promised the previous mayor to always protect Enoch
**Special Knowledge:** Missing hand tingles near necromantic magic (detects ritual circle)
**Unique Interaction:** Can use Lay on Hands to permanently break sleepwalking curse

### 18. Mira Duskslip - Reborn Rogue (Sinner)
**Connection:** Previously died in Enoch and was reborn here
**Personal Hook:** Remembers dying during a similar incident decades ago
**Special Knowledge:** Knows location of previous cult's hidden cache
**Unique Interaction:** Death has touched them - Pumpkin Head hesitates to attack

### 19. Thale Starbright - Azureborn Sorcerer (Crimson Dynasty)
**Connection:** Star-readings predicted catastrophe in Enoch
**Personal Hook:** Celestial bloodline reacts violently to Horned King's presence
**Special Knowledge:** Can use starlight to reveal invisible ritual markings
**Unique Interaction:** Sorcery points restore when disrupting ritual (1 point per success)

### 20. Torag Dawnbrand - Stoneborn Cleric (Harvest Domain)
**Connection:** Rival priest to Brother Aldric - better claim to solve this
**Personal Hook:** Secret: Their failed blessing last year started this curse
**Special Knowledge:** Knows proper counter-ritual from guilty study
**Unique Interaction:** Can channel divinity to make harvest flame burn without fuel

## Group Dynamics and Combinations

### Powerful Synergies

**The Harvest Priests** (Dorn + Torag)
- Combined blessing creates stronger harvest flame
- Theological debates provide comic relief
- Can perform greater restoration together

**The Death Touched** (Kael + Kor + Sska)
- Form "Guardian Triad" - advantage on saves vs necromancy
- Share knowledge of ancient burial practices
- Can sense each other if separated

**The Wicker Workers** (Nemi + scarecrows)
- Nemi can "speak" to scarecrows, learning cult secrets
- Possibility of turning scarecrows against cultists
- Knows weaknesses of all wicker constructs

**The Patron Conflict** (Vexa + Velren)
- Their patrons' argument provides tactical information
- Can perform "theological combat" against fanatic
- Together can break the Horned King's influence

### Conflicting Interests

**Knowledge Seekers** (Eryndor + Caldus)
- Might want to study ritual before stopping it
- Could accidentally strengthen the curse
- Provide essential information but at wrong times

**The Cursed** (Curseborn + Plagueborn)
- Their conditions might resonate with grove's curse
- Risk of corruption/possession
- Strongest against curse but most vulnerable

## Quick Roleplay Prompts

### Act 1 - Festival
- How does your character participate in (or avoid) the festivities?
- What catches your attention about the "normal" celebration?
- How do you react when the bells ring out?

### Act 2 - Investigation
- What method of investigation suits your character?
- Who in the village would you naturally trust/distrust?
- How do you prepare for entering the grove?

### Act 3 - The Grove
- What aspect of the grove resonates with your nature?
- How does your character face their fears?
- What would make you hesitate to stop the ritual?
