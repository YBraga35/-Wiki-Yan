# Act 1 - Fair and Bells (20-25 minutes)

## Opening Scene - The Harvest Festival

### Initial Description
*The autumn air carries the scent of fresh-baked bread and mulled cider as you arrive in Enoch's town square. Colorful ribbons flutter from every post, and wicker dolls hang from doorways - traditional harvest guardians meant to protect the coming crops. The Crooked Moon hangs low and bright in the afternoon sky, casting long shadows despite the sun.*

*Farmers compare prize pumpkins while children chase each other with corn-husk swords. A fiddler plays near the well as couples dance. Yet something feels off - dogs whine at shadows, and the older folk clutch iron tokens, whispering prayers.*

### Festival Activities (1-2 rounds of roleplay)
Let players engage briefly with:

**Harvest Games:**
- Pumpkin carving contest (DEX check DC 12)
- Apple bobbing (CON check DC 10)
- Corn maze race (WIS/Survival DC 12)
- Ring toss (DEX DC 10, win stuffed wicker doll)

**Quick Rumors (from villagers):**
1. "The Briggsley family hasn't been seen for three days..."
2. "Old Henrik swears he saw straw men walking at midnight."
3. "The mayor's been acting strange, locking herself in the town hall."
4. "Found burnt wicker beneath my window this morning. Bad omen, that."
5. "The Singer Granny won't sing the old harvest songs anymore. Says they're cursed."
6. "Saw lights in the old Effigy Grove. Nobody's gone there since grandfather's time."

## The Attack - Bells and Bats

### Trigger (after 5-10 minutes)
*The church bells suddenly ring out - not the measured toll of the hour, but a frantic, discordant clanging. Screams erupt from the festival crowd as dark shapes descend from the twisted moon's light.*

### Combat Encounter
**Enemies:** 2 Bat Hounds (CR 1 each)

**Bat Hound Statistics:**
- **AC:** 13
- **HP:** 22 (4d8+4) each
- **Speed:** 30 ft., fly 60 ft.
- **STR:** 14 (+2), DEX:** 16 (+3), **CON:** 12 (+1)
- **Attack:** Bite +5 to hit, 1d6+3 piercing + 1d4 necrotic
- **Special:** Pack Tactics, Keen Hearing
- **Monster Secret:** Harvest bells cause disadvantage on their next attack

**Environmental Elements:**
- Festival stalls provide half cover
- Civilians flee (difficult terrain in some squares)
- Wicker effigies seem to turn their heads to watch

### Post-Combat Investigation

**Investigation Check (DC 12):**
- **Success:** Find burnt wicker dust beneath overturned stalls, bearing a strange symbol (horned crown)
- **15+:** Also notice seeds carved with tiny runes scattered about
- **Critical Success:** Recognize the symbol matches old warnings about the "Circle of Wicker"

**Medicine/Nature Check (DC 10):**
- The bat hounds aren't natural - their blood is mixed with sap
- Wounds show signs of plant-like regeneration

## Meeting the Mayor

### Mayor Mara of Enoch
*A stern woman in her fifties with grey-streaked hair pulled tight, Mayor Mara emerges from the town hall, crossbow in hand. Her usual composed demeanor cracks with worry.*

**Appearance:** Practical dress, iron chain necklace, calloused hands

**Initial Reaction:**
"Thank the harvest you were here! But please, no talk of curses or cults - we can't afford panic. These are just... wild animals. Nothing more."

**Persuasion/Intimidation DC 12:**
- Admits three families have disappeared
- All were found sleepwalking toward the old grove
- She's posted guards but they fall asleep on duty

**Insight DC 14:**
- She's terrified but trying to maintain control
- Knows more about the grove than she's saying
- Protective of someone (her nephew is missing)

## The Singer Granny

### Granny Willa - Keeper of Old Songs
*In a rocking chair by the well sits an ancient woman, her milky eyes staring at nothing. Children usually gather for her stories, but now they keep their distance.*

**Appearance:** Weathered face, gnarled hands, carries a worn songbook

**Performance/History DC 12:**
"You want the old rhyme? The one we don't sing anymore?"

*She rocks slowly and whispers:*

```
"When wicker men walk under crooked moon's light,
And pumpkin heads glow with unholy sight,
Bring harvest flame blessed by morning's first dew,
For only sacred fire can see you through.

The Horned King hungers for autumn's first yield,
Blood and grain sown in cursed field,
Three circles turning, three voices must cry,
Or sleepers wake never, and harvesters die."
```

**Follow-up Questions:**
- The Effigy Grove was sealed 50 years ago
- The Horned King is an old god of failed harvests
- Harvest flame = blessed fire that harms his servants
- Her grandson is among the missing

## Act 1 Clues Summary

By the end of Act 1, players should have:
1. **The Threat:** Cultists of the Horned King are active
2. **The Symbol:** A horned crown mark (identifies cultists/locations)
3. **The Rhyme:** Hints about fire weakness and the ritual
4. **The Stakes:** Villagers are disappearing, time is running out
5. **The Direction:** Something is happening at the Effigy Grove

## Transition to Act 2

*As the sun sets behind the crooked moon, Mayor Mara approaches you privately.*

"I'll pay you 50 gold each if you can quietly investigate this. The Harvest Acolyte at the temple might have old maps of the grove. And... and if you find my nephew Timothy, there's another 50 gold in it. Please - Enoch can't survive a failed harvest."

## DM Notes for Pacing
- Keep festival interaction brief (5-10 minutes max)
- Launch combat quickly to maintain energy
- Have 2-3 NPCs ready with key information
- If players miss clues, have NPCs approach them
- End on a clear call to action
