# The Crooked Moon - Harvest Festival One-Shot

## Campaign Setting
**World:** Druskenvald - A gothic fairy-tale realm where the departed souls arrive on the Ghostlight Express to live again under the twisted moon's pale light.

**Province:** Enoch - An agricultural heartland where harvest traditions run deep and old superstitions hold power. The province celebrates the Festival of Sowing, but this year's celebration will be unlike any other.

## Adventure Synopsis
During Enoch's Harvest Festival, the celebration turns to horror when wicker effigies come alive and villagers begin disappearing. A cult of the Horned King attempts to sow a harvest curse to feed a terrible Pumpkin Head. The party must follow folklore clues, cross a cursed grove, and interrupt the ritual before the curse takes permanent root.

## Session Details
- **System:** D&D 5e
- **Level:** 3
- **Players:** 3-6
- **Duration:** 2 hours
- **Tone:** Light folk horror, omens, rural tradition

## Content Warnings
- Cult activity
- Mind control/curses
- Body horror (mild - animated scarecrows)
- Missing persons

## Quick Start Guide for DMs
1. Print/open handouts 01-08
2. Mark DCs on cards (mostly DC 12-13)
3. Choose 6-8 NPC names from the village
4. Review lair actions and tension clock on sticky notes
5. Have monster stats ready (Bat Hounds CR 1, Scarecrows, Pumpkin Head CR 3)

## Act Structure
- **Act 1 - Fair and Bells (20-25 min):** Festival opening, attack, initial clues
- **Act 2 - Folklore and Trails (30-35 min):** Investigation, gathering information
- **Act 3 - Grove and Ritual (55-60 min):** Dungeon crawl, final confrontation

## Victory Conditions
- Save the sleepwalking villagers
- Disrupt the ritual circle (3 cumulative successes)
- Prevent the harvest curse from taking root (4-round tension clock)

## Rewards
- 80-180 gp total
- 1-2 Healing Potions
- Effigy Amulet (1/day Protection from Evil and Good vs fiends/constructs of the Horned King)
- Favor of Enoch (social advantage in the region)
