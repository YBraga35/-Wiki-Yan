# NPCs – Great Games and Arena

Key figures who appear in or influence the Games, Arena, Debates, and social events, with affiliation, table use, and references.

## Court/Divine
- King Acastus (Mytros): public legitimacy; offers and bargains (Antikythera); provokes Scions in ceremonies. Use: audiences, royal box, theatrical interruptions.
- Queen Vallus (Wisdom): sponsors “clean” quests; grants boons for compassion. Use: official requests, judging character (Nest/Sewers).
- Kyrah (Trickery/Music): present at Games and socials; sets tempo, playful provocations. Use: anchor inspiring scenes, narrative challenges.
- Pythor (Battle): symbolic presence; Science Fair judge (looks for battle applications). Use: crown combat achievements.
- Volkan (Forge): Science Fair judge (innovation); crafting contact. Use: encourage creative solutions.
- Versi (Oracle): neutral arbiter of the Games; validates results. Use: mediate disputes and announce winners.

## Order of Sydon and Cults
- Commander Gaius: ideological antagonist; public pressure. Use: tension in stands, duels of honor.
- Chondrus (Lutheria): intrigue, vault heist, nocturnal temptations. Use: corruption hooks and moral choices.
- Zephyrus (Helios): rising cult. Use: social encounters, religious invitations, street conflicts.

## Academy of Mytros
- Dean Akimedes: formal bridge to PCs (gifted/scholars). Use: invitations, sponsorships.
- Machaon (abjurer), Chalcodon (transmuter), Kerodotos (priest), Atreus (captain): debate quartet. Use: debates, classes, workshops.
- Planemaster Orena (siren warlock): planar projects and navigation. Use: hire PCs / be hired to crew the Ultros.

## Delegations, Athletes, and Gladiators
- Amazonians: captain Rena Pipea; Antigone (priest). Use: rivalry, honor, Exiled politics.
- Aresians: Kyriakos Leafdew (monk), Shieldmaster Paris (captain). Use: discipline, martial philosophy.
- Scions of Sydon: Talius, Hergeron, Yala, Chalcia, Goloron. Use: antagonism on track and in stands.
- Estoria/Freecity Alliance: Egan (veteran), other regional athletes. Use: tradition and fair play.
- Cy (Aresia): Games superstar; hated by Mytros. Use: apex rival on the field.
- Gladiators and MCs: see formats and roles in `03.08 - Proximo's Guide - Discord.md` (Arena, Grand Melee, Beast Breaking, Chariots, King of the Mountain, Harpy Cages, Clash of the Titans).

## Grey Contacts
- Lady of Coins (Moxena): Sacred Hospitality at the Colossus; espionage. Use: tempting offers and risky alliances.
- Captain Horus (Lady of Coins): contracts (incl. Amazonians). Use: moral dilemmas.
- Taran Neurdagon / Ismene Neurdagon: commerce and secrets. Use: tie‑ins with Nest/Sewers.
- Princess Anora: social presence (romance). Use: court scenes, dances, closeness with the people.
- Thericles: local athletic figure/mentor. Use: training and track insights.

## Table Usage Tips
- Assign 1–2 “zones” per NPC (Royal Box, Stands, Field, Festival) and a “conversation topic” for each (see `03.02 - Great Games Using Party Planning.md`).
- Link each NPC to 1 actionable conflict: invitation, blackmail, sponsorship, duel, favor.
- Show visible consequences in Arena and streets (cheers, boos, rumors) as PCs interact.
