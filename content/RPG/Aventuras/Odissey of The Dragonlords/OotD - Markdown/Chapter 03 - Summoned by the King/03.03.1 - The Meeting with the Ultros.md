# The Meeting with the Ultros

Use these scenes for the journey to the Dead Falls and the first encounter with the legendary vessel.

## Audience with Acastus

> “My scouts claim they saw the Ultros at the Dead Falls. If you bring it under your command, we will possess the only ship fit for the Forgotten Sea — a task for true heroes.”

## The Sour Vintage (First Night)

> The inn glows warm in the hills. Inside: the scent of aged wine, roast meats, and exotic spice. Aetala recognizes you: “The Oracle’s Chosen! You eat, drink, and sleep for free under this roof. My patrons insist.”

Rumors:
- A fisherman saw a black‑sailed ship sailing up the River Lethe against the current — “as if it knew the way.”
- The Dead Falls lead toward the Underworld — “strange things happen there; the rocks scream like harpies.”
- An elder repeats a mantra: “Heroes do not fear the dead!”

Kyrah (if present):

> “I never liked those stories. Estor Arkelander was a destroyer. That ship is a tomb.”

## Camp Before the Falls (Second Night)

> The roar of the Dead Falls fills the valley. Mist rises from the chasms, curling like ghostly fingers. The air grows cold.

Proceed to the confrontation and the Ultros’ awakening as your campaign dictates.
