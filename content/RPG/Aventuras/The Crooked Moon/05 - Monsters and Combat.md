# Monsters and Combat Encounters

## Primary Adversaries

### Pumpkin Head (CR 3)
*A grotesque fusion of plant and undeath, this creature stands 8 feet tall with a carved jack-o'-lantern head that weeps burning sap. Its body is made of twisted vines and autumn vegetation.*

**Pumpkin Head**
- **Armor Class:** 15 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 52 (8d8 + 16)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 16 (+3), **DEX:** 10 (+0), **CON:** 14 (+2)
- **INT:** 6 (-2), **WIS:** 10 (+0), **CHA:** 14 (+2)
- **Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning, piercing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** Fire damage (especially blessed fire)
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, exhaustion, frightened, poisoned
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Understands Common and Sylvan but can't speak
- **Challenge:** 3 (700 XP)

**Special Traits:**
- **False Appearance:** While motionless, indistinguishable from a scarecrow
- **Harvest Hunger:** Gains 5 temporary HP when a creature fails a save against its abilities
- **Blessed Fire Weakness:** When damaged by blessed fire, loses damage resistances until the start of its next turn

**Actions:**
- **Multiattack:** Two vine whip attacks or one vine whip and one seed spit
- **Vine Whip:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage, and target must make DC 13 STR save or be grappled (escape DC 13)
- **Seed Spit:** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +2 to hit, range 30 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage, and vines grow around target. DC 12 STR save or restrained for 1 minute (can repeat save at end of turns)
- **Harvest Scream (Recharge 6):** Each creature within 15-foot cone must make DC 13 CON save or be frightened for 1 minute. Frightened creatures see visions of failed harvests and starvation. Can repeat save at end of turns.

**Tactics:**
- Opens with Harvest Scream to frighten multiple enemies
- Uses reach to stay out of melee
- Grapples healers/casters first
- Retreats if reduced to 1/4 HP

### Bat Hound (CR 1)
*These unnatural creatures combine the worst aspects of bats and hunting dogs. Their bodies are covered in patchy fur and leathery wings, with oversized ears and milky white eyes.*

**Bat Hound**
- **Armor Class:** 13
- **Hit Points:** 22 (4d8 + 4)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., fly 60 ft.
- **STR:** 14 (+2), **DEX:** 16 (+3), **CON:** 12 (+1)
- **INT:** 3 (-4), **WIS:** 12 (+1), **CHA:** 6 (-2)
- **Skills:** Perception +3, Stealth +5
- **Damage Immunities:** Poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Poisoned
- **Senses:** Blindsight 60 ft., passive Perception 13
- **Languages:** —
- **Challenge:** 1 (200 XP)

**Special Traits:**
- **Echolocation:** Can't use blindsight while deafened
- **Keen Hearing:** Advantage on Perception checks that rely on hearing
- **Pack Tactics:** Advantage on attacks if ally is within 5 feet of target
- **Bell Weakness:** When church bells ring or handheld bells are rung within 30 feet (action), has disadvantage on next attack

**Actions:**
- **Bite:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage plus 3 (1d4) necrotic damage. Target must make DC 11 CON save or have max HP reduced by necrotic damage taken. Reduction lasts until finishing a long rest.

**Tactics:**
- Attack in pairs for pack tactics
- Focus on isolated targets
- Flee when reduced to 1/4 HP
- Avoid bell towers

### Wicker Scarecrow (Modified)
*These animated constructs of wicker and straw move with jerky, unnatural motions. Their button eyes glow with green fire, and wisps of smoke trail from their joints.*

**Wicker Scarecrow**
- **Armor Class:** 11
- **Hit Points:** 36 (8d8)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 11 (+0), **DEX:** 13 (+1), **CON:** 11 (+0)
- **INT:** 10 (+0), **WIS:** 10 (+0), **CHA:** 13 (+1)
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** Fire
- **Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning, piercing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Immunities:** Poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned, unconscious
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Understands the languages of its creator but can't speak
- **Challenge:** 1 (200 XP)

**Special Traits:**
- **False Appearance:** While motionless, indistinguishable from ordinary scarecrow
- **Flammable:** When taking fire damage, catches fire and takes 1d4 fire damage at start of each turn until someone uses action to douse flames

**Actions:**
- **Multiattack:** Two claw attacks
- **Claw:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (2d4 + 1) slashing damage
- **Terrifying Glare:** Target within 30 feet must make DC 11 WIS save or be frightened until end of scarecrow's next turn. If save fails by 5+, also paralyzed from fear. Target immune for 24 hours after save.

**Tactics:**
- Use False Appearance for ambush
- Open with Terrifying Glare
- Focus on frightened targets
- Avoid fire-wielding enemies

## Cultist Forces

### Cultist Fanatic (Modified for Horned King)
*This zealot wears elaborate robes decorated with antlers and grain sheaves. Their eyes burn with fanatic devotion to the Horned King.*

**Horned King Fanatic**
- **Armor Class:** 13 (leather armor)
- **Hit Points:** 33 (6d8 + 6)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 11 (+0), **DEX:** 14 (+2), **CON:** 12 (+1)
- **INT:** 10 (+0), **WIS:** 14 (+2), **CHA:** 14 (+2)
- **Skills:** Deception +4, Persuasion +4, Religion +2
- **Senses:** Passive Perception 12
- **Languages:** Common, Sylvan
- **Challenge:** 2 (450 XP)

**Spellcasting:** 4th-level spellcaster, spell save DC 12, +4 to hit
- **Cantrips:** Sacred Flame (appears as green fire), Thaumaturgy
- **1st level (4 slots):** Command, Inflict Wounds, Bane
- **2nd level (3 slots):** Hold Person, Spiritual Weapon (appears as spectral sickle)

**Actions:**
- **Multiattack:** Two attacks with ritual sickle
- **Ritual Sickle:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) slashing damage
- **Harvest Curse (1/Day):** One creature within 30 feet must make DC 12 WIS save or have disadvantage on death saves for 24 hours

**Tactics:**
- Maintains Bane on party or Bless on allies
- Uses Spiritual Weapon to attack while casting
- Commands allies to "Harvest!" (attack)
- Attempts to escape if allies fall

## Environmental Hazards

### Animated Sap Trap
*Triggered by pressure plates or lair actions*
- **Effect:** 10-foot radius, thick sap erupts
- **Save:** DEX DC 13 to avoid
- **Failure:** Speed reduced to 10 feet for 1 minute
- **Damage:** 1d6 acid damage at start of turn if stuck

### Wicker Shard Explosion
*Triggered by wrong puzzle answers*
- **Effect:** 15-foot cone of sharp wicker
- **Save:** DEX DC 13 for half damage
- **Damage:** 2d6 piercing + 1d4 necrotic

### Scythe Pendulum Trap
*Mechanical trap in corridors*
- **Perception:** DC 14 to spot trigger
- **Effect:** +8 to hit, 2d8 slashing damage
- **Disable:** Thieves' tools DC 14

### Strangling Corn Maze
*Environmental hazard*
- **Effect:** Corn animates to entangle
- **Save:** STR DC 12 to break free
- **Damage:** 1d4 bludgeoning per round while restrained

## Combat Encounter Balance

### Easy Encounters (for pacing)
- 2-3 Basic Cultists
- 1 Wicker Scarecrow
- 1 Bat Hound solo

### Medium Encounters
- 2 Bat Hounds (opening scene)
- 1 Scarecrow + 2 Cultists
- 1 Fanatic + 1 Cultist

### Hard Encounter (finale)
- 1 Pumpkin Head + 1 Fanatic + 2 Cultists + Lair Actions

### Deadly Encounter (if needed)
- Add 1 Scarecrow to finale
- Pumpkin Head starts with 10 temporary HP
- Lair actions deal 1d8 damage

## Monster Tactics Guide

### Intelligence Levels
- **Pumpkin Head (INT 6):** Cunning but bestial, focuses on biggest threat
- **Scarecrows (INT 10):** Follow orders, basic tactics
- **Cultists (INT 10):** Coordinate attacks, protect fanatic
- **Fanatic (INT 10):** Strategic spell use, commands allies

### Morale Guidelines
- **Cultists:** Flee at 1/2 casualties or if fanatic falls
- **Scarecrows:** Fight until destroyed
- **Bat Hounds:** Flee at 1/4 HP
- **Pumpkin Head:** Fights to the death
- **Fanatic:** Attempts escape at 1/4 HP

### Environmental Usage
Enemies should use environment:
- Cultists hide behind cover
- Scarecrows emerge from corn
- Pumpkin Head uses reach from difficult terrain
- Bat Hounds attack from above

### Initiative Order Template
```

```

### Damage Types Present
- **Slashing:** Most physical attacks
- **Piercing:** Bat hound bites
- **Necrotic:** Draining attacks, ritual damage
- **Fire:** Player weapons (hopefully), vulnerabilities
- **Psychic:** Fear effects

### Conditions Commonly Applied
- **Frightened:** Harvest Scream, Terrifying Glare
- **Grappled:** Vine whips
- **Restrained:** Seed spit, corn maze
- **Poisoned:** None (but players might expect it)

### Monster Secrets (Reward Investigation)
1. **Pumpkin Head:** Blessed fire removes resistances
2. **Bat Hounds:** Church bells cause disadvantage
3. **Scarecrows:** Completely vulnerable to fire
4. **Cultists:** Regular villagers under robes - some might surrender
