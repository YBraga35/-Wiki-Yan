# Gaius’s House in Mytros

## Location
Commander **Gaius’** residence stands in the **Noble District of Mytros**, among prestigious properties near the famed **Neurdagon Estate** — two blocks from **Taran Neurdagon’s** opulent villa.

## The Noble District
Merchant‑aristocratic heart of Mytros. At the center, the **Grand Agora** with twelve‑foot statues of the Five around the edge and a new, taller **statue of Sydon** at the center — a clear symbol of the Order’s rise.

## Architecture
Gaius’ residence reflects his rank within the **Order of Sydon**: classical Thylean with distinct martial flourishes. The lot is modest compared to Neurdagon’s manor, yet sizable enough to project status.

### Exterior
- Main Portico: six white‑marble Doric columns veined in blue — the waters of Sydon. The triangular pediment shows Sydon’s trident cleaving a stylized storm.
- Front Garden: formal and martial — cypresses trimmed into geometric formations; flowerbeds laid out like tactical diagrams; a central fountain with a sculpture of Argyn, the silver dragon, in flight.
- Walls and Security: low limestone walls (3 ft) for demarcation, iron gates with storm motifs. Two Order **centurions** keep discreet watch.

### Interior
- Entry Hall: spacious atrium with a mosaic floor depicting Sydon’s battle against the “Usurpers.” Ceremonial Order armors displayed on polished bronze stands.
- Receiving Room: Gaius’ official salon — framed maps of Thylea, tapestries of the Order’s victories, a large black‑oak table piled with military papers and official correspondence.
- Private Study: shelves of intelligence reports, strategic plans, and a personal arms collection; a panoramic window over the city for keeping watch on Mytros’ movements.
- The Armor Room: centerpiece — a purpose‑built chamber to display the **Scales of Persephone** under an octagonal skylight that creates dramatic lighting over the armor.

## The Scales of Persephone – Display
A circular, reverent chamber of polished marble and niches harboring lesser relics from Dragonlord tombs.

- Display: the Scales are mounted on a white‑marble mannequin on a raised circular dais. Interlaced dragon scales emit a soft, ethereal glow, patterns shifting as sunlight filters through the skylight.

Magical Features (as observed)
- Responsive Glow: pulses brighter when Elion nears — recognizing its true bearer.
- Violent Rejection: when Gaius attempts to don it, the armor repels him with magical discharges, leaving him stunned.
- Arcs of Energy: small bolts leap from the scales toward Elion if he comes close.
- Residual Memories: echoes of Elion’s past adventures may flash within the armor.

Sketch the family/household and how they frame Gaius in the city: loyal retainers, pious adherents of Sydon, and a domestic culture of strict discipline.
