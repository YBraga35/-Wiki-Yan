# Integrated NPCs - Complete Guide

## Integration Philosophy

The key to successfully adapting Tales from the Shadows to Yllmourne lies in **contextual NPC integration**. Rather than wholesale replacement or simple addition, each NPC decision should serve the narrative, honor established Yllmourne lore, and create a cohesive campaign world.

### Decision-Making Framework

When encountering a Tales from the Shadows NPC, ask:

1. **Does Yllmourne already have someone in this role?** (Consider substitution)
2. **Would combining traits create a more interesting character?** (Consider fusion)
3. **Does this NPC bring something entirely new to the setting?** (Consider addition)
4. **How does this decision impact existing relationships and factions?**

### Three Integration Approaches

**SUBSTITUTE**: Replace TftS NPC with existing Yllmourne character who serves the same narrative function. Use when Yllmourne already has a well-developed character who fits the role perfectly.

**FUSE**: Combine characteristics from both the TftS NPC and an Yllmourne character to create an enhanced version. Use when both characters have complementary traits that strengthen each other.

**ADD**: Introduce the TftS NPC as a newcomer to Yllmourne. Use when the character brings unique elements that don't exist in the setting, or when they can arrive during the economic boom period.

## Complete NPC Profiles

### One-Eyed Cy
**Source**: Yllmourne (existing) + TftS Shadowmaster (fusion)

- **Function**: Crime lord, arena master, shadow intelligence broker
- **Traits**: Pragmatic, surprisingly honorable, calculating, secretly terrified, protective of "his" city
- **Objective**: Maintain criminal empire while preventing shadow incursion from destroying Yllmourne
- **Secret**: Has witnessed shadow realm bleeding through during fog; his lost eye was taken by shadow entity
- **Leverage**: Loyalty to Yllmourne's survival; fear for his workers; greed can be exploited but he won't betray the city
- **Appearance & Voice**: Grizzled man in his 50s, eyepatch over left eye, scarred hands from pit fighting days. Gravelly voice that commands respect. Dresses in well-tailored but practical clothes - successful but not ostentatious.
- **Wants from PC**: Investigate plague source threatening his people; eliminate threats to Yllmourne's stability; keep his shadow knowledge secret from authorities
- **Campaign Arc**:
  - Act I: Reluctant informant providing underworld intelligence
  - Act II: Reveals shadow realm knowledge when threat escalates
  - Act III: Becomes unlikely ally against planar conspiracy
  - Act IV: Testifies at tribunal about shadow incursions
- **3 Clear Phrases**:
  1. **What he wants**: "Keep Yllmourne standing - even rats abandon a sinking ship, but this is MY ship"
  2. **How he reacts to PC**: "Maal's agent, eh? Well, the gods haven't cared about the Belly before, why start now? Unless... you've seen them too. The things in the fog."
  3. **What he fears**: "I've seen what happens when the shadows get hungry. Lost more than my eye that night."
- **Stat Block Reference**: Use **Bandit Captain** (MM p.344) with +2 CR adjustment: add Legendary Resistance (1/day), Fighting Style: Unarmed Fighting, and Intimidating Presence action
- **Relationships**:
  - Former lover of Dagmara (her death haunts him)
  - Business rivalry with Sefton Bane (mutual respect, underlying threat)
  - Employs Wren Toothtaker for maritime smuggling
  - Grudging respect for Mayor Lockton (both "manage" Yllmourne)
  - Distrusts Faylene (religious interference in business)
  - Secret source: Navula (she told him about shadows)

### Sefton Bane (Maritime Tyrant + Queen of Night Fusion)
**Source**: Yllmourne (existing) + TftS Queen of Night and Magic (fusion)

- **Function**: Maritime empire ruler, shadow fey court master, primary recurring antagonist
- **Traits**: Charismatic, cruel, aesthetically obsessed, bound by arcane contracts, utterly convinced of his supremacy
- **Objective**: Expand maritime control while cultivating shadow fey power; ultimately seeks to merge shadow realm with material world under his rule
- **Secret**: Is half-shadow fey (mother was fey, father human merchant); his maritime empire is cover for building shadow court on Hilbre Isle
- **Leverage**: Vanity can be exploited; bound by fey contract law (must honor bargains); genuinely loves his shadow court subjects
- **Appearance & Voice**: Devastatingly handsome man in his early 40s with too-pale skin and eyes that reflect purple in certain light. Dresses in black silk and silver jewelry. Voice is melodious, almost hypnotic, but with underlying coldness.
- **Wants from PC**: Test PC's worthiness as opponent; recruit PC to shadow court; eliminate PC if they threaten his plans; force PC into binding contract
- **Campaign Arc**:
  - Act I: Distant threat; PC investigates his smuggling operations
  - Act II: Direct confrontation as political antagonist blocking aid to Yllmourne
  - Act III: Revealed as shadow fey hybrid orchestrating planar bleeding
  - Act IV: Potential redemption arc OR final boss before tribunal (DM choice)
- **3 Clear Phrases**:
  1. **What he wants**: "This world is drowning in mediocrity. I will rule a court where beauty, power, and darkness reign eternal."
  2. **How he reacts to PC**: "Maal's little investigator. How delightful. Tell me, when you uncover all the ugly truths, will your god still claim you?"
  3. **What he fears**: "Being ordinary. Being forgotten. Being powerless like my father was."
- **Stat Block Reference**: Acts I-II use **Archmage** (MM p.342) with fey type; Acts III-IV use custom Shadow Fey Noble (CR 13): Archmage base + Shadow Step, Fey Ancestry, Innate Spellcasting (shadow-themed), Legendary Actions
- **Relationships**:
  - Rival to One-Eyed Cy (competing for Yllmourne influence)
  - Despises Mayor Lockton (sees him as weak bureaucrat)
  - Fascinated by PC (worthy opponent)
  - Commands The Pale Maiden (shadow fey assassin)
  - Secret patron: Receiving power from imprisoned Sogmonnath
  - Manipulates Faylene through hexed contract

### Octant Faylene Chicarian
**Source**: Yllmourne (existing) - enhanced with shadow magic elements

- **Function**: Religious leader of Rotarist Church, unknowing curse vector, tragic antagonist
- **Traits**: Devout, exhausted, desperate for results, magically manipulated, increasingly unstable
- **Objective**: Save Yllmourne through religious devotion; please the gods; atone for unknown sins
- **Secret**: Has been hexed by Sefton Bane; her prayers are twisted into curse amplification; she's unwittingly spreading plague
- **Leverage**: Faith can be restored once freed from hex; genuinely wants to help; maternal love for congregation
- **Appearance & Voice**: Woman in her 50s with prematurely gray hair, exhausted eyes, hands that shake slightly. Wears Rotarist vestments that seem too heavy for her frame. Voice wavers between powerful sermon delivery and fragile whispers.
- **Wants from PC**: Proof that religious devotion isn't failing; solution to plague; forgiveness for whatever she's done wrong (she senses something is wrong)
- **Campaign Arc**:
  - Act I: Sympathetic religious leader trying to help
  - Act II: PC discovers her hex; becomes rescue mission
  - Act III: Breaking her curse becomes crucial to stopping plague
  - Act IV: Freed Faylene becomes powerful ally and witness
- **3 Clear Phrases**:
  1. **What she wants**: "I have served the gods faithfully for thirty years. Why do my prayers go unanswered?"
  2. **How she reacts to PC**: "Maal sent you? Finally. FINALLY. Tell me what I've done wrong. Tell me how to fix this."
  3. **What she fears**: "That every prayer I've spoken has been poison. That I've damned them all."
- **Stat Block Reference**: Use **Priest** (MM p.348) with additional curse-themed spells; post-liberation upgrade to **High Priest** (custom CR 7 with divine restoration powers)
- **Relationships**:
  - Victim of Sefton Bane (doesn't know it)
  - Seeks Mayor Lockton's support (he's avoiding her)
  - Distrusts Navula (pagan witch)
  - Knew Dagmara (prayed together; guilt over her death)
  - PC becomes her salvation

### Minqena Yorusi (Librarian + Scholar Fusion)
**Source**: Yllmourne (existing) + TftS Cornelius Bowditch (fusion)

- **Function**: Head librarian, planar magic researcher, information nexus
- **Traits**: Brilliant, obsessive, socially awkward, morally flexible regarding knowledge, protective of books
- **Objective**: Preserve and expand library's collection; understand planar mechanics; prevent book burning
- **Secret**: Has discovered records of Sogmonnath's imprisonment and believes awakening her might solve the plague (catastrophically wrong)
- **Leverage**: Threaten the library and she becomes enemy; offer rare books and she'll help; appeal to her logic
- **Appearance & Voice**: Half-elf woman in her 60s (looks 40s), ink-stained fingers, wears reading glasses on chain, always has at least three books nearby. Speaks in rapid academic tangents, often forgets social niceties.
- **Wants from PC**: Access to Navula's grimoires; protection from arsonist plot; help researching planar rifts; someone who understands importance of knowledge preservation
- **Campaign Arc**:
  - Act I: Information provider for plague investigation
  - Act II: Arson plot victim; PC must save library
  - Act III: Provides crucial planar ritual research (some dangerously wrong)
  - Act IV: Expert witness on historical precedents
- **3 Clear Phrases**:
  1. **What she wants**: "Every book burned is a thousand voices silenced forever. I will not let ignorance win."
  2. **How she reacts to PC**: "Oh good, someone who asks real questions. Look at this - third century planar mechanics, pre-Sundering. You see the implications?"
  3. **What she fears**: "Fire. Always fire. They burned Alexandria, they burned Baghdad, they will not burn my library."
- **Stat Block Reference**: Use **Archmage** (MM p.342) but replace combat spells with ritual/research magic; non-combatant who uses environmental advantages (books as cover, animated furniture, teleportation circles in library)
- **Relationships**:
  - Academic respect for Friedel (newcomer scholar)
  - Frustration with Mayor Lockton (cuts library funding)
  - Fascinated by Navula (wants her books)
  - PC becomes trusted research partner
  - Unwittingly provides Sefton information (he's library patron)

### Navula (Witch + Oma Rattenfanger Fusion)
**Source**: Yllmourne (existing) + TftS Oma Rattenfanger (fusion)

- **Function**: Swamp witch, knowledge keeper, morally gray informant, shadow realm expert
- **Traits**: Ancient (appears 60s, actually 200+), darkly humorous, transactional, unexpectedly kind, lonely
- **Objective**: Maintain balance between realms; prevent shadow realm from consuming material world; find worthy apprentice before death
- **Leverage**: She'll help if paid fairly (prefers secrets over gold); threaten coastal ecosystem and she becomes enemy; show genuine respect and she softens
- **Appearance & Voice**: Weathered woman with sea-green eyes and hair like kelp, covered in barnacles and tidal marks. Lives in grotto surrounded by magical fungi and shadow-touched creatures. Voice sounds like waves over rocks - rhythmic, ancient, inevitable.
- **Wants from PC**: Someone to pass knowledge to; payment in secrets or rare components; prevention of planar collapse; death with dignity when time comes
- **Campaign Arc**:
  - Act I: Mysterious informant about shadow creatures
  - Act II: Reveals curse mechanics and Sogmonnath's history
  - Act III: Teaches PC shadow realm navigation
  - Act IV: Dies peacefully knowing PC will maintain balance (or becomes permanent ally if PC earns it)
- **3 Clear Phrases**:
  1. **What she wants**: "The tides turn, dearie. Shadow and light, life and death. Someone needs to remember how to keep them separate."
  2. **How he reacts to PC**: "Maal's chosen, are you? Good. The Arbiter's agent might actually listen. Come in, we'll trade - you bring me what you've learned, I'll tell you what it means."
  3. **What she fears**: "Dying before I pass this on. The young ones don't listen anymore. Don't learn the old ways. Then the shadows come and everyone screams 'why didn't someone warn us?' Someone did. You didn't listen."
- **Stat Block Reference**: Use **Archmage** (MM p.342) modified with hag abilities: at will *disguise self*, 3/day *bestow curse*, 1/day *geas*. Lair actions in grotto (swamp terrain control).
- **Relationships**:
  - Warned One-Eyed Cy about shadows (he lost his eye ignoring her advice)
  - Ancient rival to Sogmonnath (fought her during imprisonment)
  - Distrusted by Faylene (pagan vs. church)
  - Wants Minqena's library access (trade relationship)
  - Sees PC as potential successor

### Scholar Friedel
**Source**: TftS (add as newcomer)

- **Function**: Obsessed researcher, cautionary tale, potential villain or ally
- **Traits**: Brilliant, manic, ethically compromised, desperate for validation, deteriorating
- **Objective**: Unlock shadow realm magic to prove academic theories; gain immortality through shadow transformation; be remembered as greatest scholar
- **Secret**: Already partially transformed into shadow creature; dying from exposure to forbidden texts; will betray anyone for knowledge
- **Leverage**: Offer collaboration and he's useful ally; threaten his research and he becomes dangerous; show him he's wrong and he breaks down
- **Appearance & Voice**: Man in his 30s who looks 50s, pale skin with shadow veins visible, constantly writing in journals, hasn't slept properly in months. Speaks in enthusiastic rambles interrupted by coughing fits. Ink stains on everything.
- **Wants from PC**: Access to library's restricted section; Navula's grimoires; protection from academic rivals; test subject data (ethically questionable)
- **Campaign Arc**:
  - Act I: Arrives as helpful expert on shadow creatures
  - Act II: Increasingly unstable as shadow corruption progresses
  - Act III: Becomes mini-boss OR tragic sacrifice (DM choice based on PC relationship)
  - Act IV: If redeemed, provides crucial testimony; if corrupted, serves as warning
- **3 Clear Phrases**:
  1. **What he wants**: "Do you understand what we could learn? The shadow realm holds secrets that would revolutionize everything we know about magic!"
  2. **How he reacts to PC**: "You're investigating the plague? Perfect! I have seventeen theories about shadow-plane parasitic possession. Let me show you my notes—"
  3. **What he fears**: "Dying before I finish my work. Being forgotten. Being WRONG about everything."
- **Stat Block Reference**: Acts I-II use **Mage** (MM p.347); Act III use **Shadow Sorcerer** (custom CR 8: Mage base + shadow spells, advantage in dim light, resistance to necrotic, shadow step ability)
- **Relationships**:
  - Competing with Minqena for library access (she's wary of him)
  - Fascinated by Navula (she refuses to teach him)
  - Manipulated by Sefton (doesn't realize it)
  - Sees PC as research partner (until PC opposes him)
  - Gravedigger Wilhelm reports strange behavior to PC

### Captain Wren Toothtaker
**Source**: Yllmourne (existing) - enhanced with shadow realm exposure

- **Function**: Pirate captain, naval expert, comic relief, unexpected ally
- **Traits**: Charismatic, cowardly when outmatched, loyal to crew, surprisingly clever, loves dramatic storytelling
- **Objective**: Get rich, stay alive, protect her crew, avoid Sefton's control
- **Leverage**: Threaten crew and she fights desperately; offer gold and safe harbor and she's reliable; appeal to her pride and she takes risks
- **Appearance & Voice**: Woman in her 40s with collection of gold teeth (hence nickname), weather-beaten skin, elaborate coat made from many stolen jackets. Voice booms with theatrical flair - every sentence is a performance.
- **Wants from PC**: Protection from Sefton's fleet; share of treasure; recognition as "greatest captain on the seas"; immunity for past crimes
- **Campaign Arc**:
  - Act I: Smuggling informant with useful maritime intelligence
  - Act II: Provides naval transport during Thranengi blockade
  - Act III: Her crew has encountered shadow islands; crucial navigation knowledge
  - Act IV: Provides testimony about planar maritime anomalies
- **3 Clear Phrases**:
  1. **What she wants**: "I'm not greedy, Maal's agent. I just want enough gold to retire comfortably. Say, a small island's worth?"
  2. **How she reacts to PC**: "You need a ship? Well well well. Wren Toothtaker, captain of the finest vessel that ever sailed, fled from, or accidentally sank another ship. Your servant, sir/madam."
  3. **What she fears**: "Sefton Bane. Man's not natural. Seen things in fog near his islands... things with too many teeth. Rather not discuss it sober."
- **Stat Block Reference**: Use **Bandit Captain** (MM p.344) modified for naval combat: swimming speed 40ft, advantage on ship-based checks, crew provides bonus actions
- **Relationships**:
  - Employed by One-Eyed Cy (smuggling operations)
  - Terrified of Sefton Bane (fled from his ships)
  - Drinking buddies with many NPCs (information network)
  - Respects PC's courage (if PC earns it through bravery)
  - Flirts with everyone (unsuccessfully)

### Bonita Sandberg (Dagmara's Colleague)
**Source**: TftS Bonita + Yllmourne Dagmara (fusion)

- **Function**: Surviving healer with crucial knowledge, moral compass, tragic witness
- **Traits**: Compassionate, traumatized, determined, increasingly paranoid, ethically rigid
- **Objective**: Honor Dagmara's memory by finding cure; prevent more deaths; expose the conspiracy; survive long enough to matter
- **Secret**: Has Dagmara's final journal documenting early plague victims and noting magical corruption; knows Dagmara suspected Faylene but didn't understand why
- **Leverage**: Sworn oath to do no harm (can't be forced to hurt others); desperate to solve plague (will take risks); guilt over surviving when Dagmara died
- **Appearance & Voice**: Woman in her 30s with exhausted eyes and hands stained from constant medical work. Wears practical healer's clothing, always carries medical bag. Voice is soft but firm - compassionate bedside manner hiding steel core.
- **Wants from PC**: Justice for Dagmara; help protecting patients; someone who believes her theories; protection from whoever killed Dagmara
- **Campaign Arc**:
  - Act I: Key witness providing medical evidence about plague
  - Act II: Assassination target (PC must protect); reveals journal
  - Act III: Helps develop counter-curse working with freed Faylene
  - Act IV: Medical expert witness at tribunal
- **3 Clear Phrases**:
  1. **What she wants**: "Dagmara knew something. They killed her for it. I will not let her die for nothing."
  2. **How she reacts to PC**: "Thank the gods. Someone from Maal's temple. The city guard won't listen - they say I'm hysterical. But you'll investigate properly, won't you?"
  3. **What she fears**: "That I'll be next. That I'll fail another patient. That Dagmara's death meant nothing."
- **Stat Block Reference**: Use **Priest** (MM p.348) modified for medical skills: healing spells maximized, Medicine expertise, can stabilize dying creatures as bonus action
- **Relationships**:
  - Dagmara's best friend and colleague (survivor's guilt)
  - Treats One-Eyed Cy's people (he protects her)
  - Suspects Faylene (wrong about intent, right about connection)
  - Distrusts Mayor Lockton (he's dismissed her concerns)
  - PC becomes her protector and investigative partner

### Mayor Myles Lockton
**Source**: Yllmourne (existing) - enhanced with corruption elements

- **Function**: Civic leader, corrupt official, coward, information bottleneck
- **Traits**: Bureaucratic, cowardly, self-preserving, occasionally sympathetic, out of his depth
- **Objective**: Maintain appearance of control; protect his position; avoid blame for plague; survive politically and physically
- **Secret**: Has accepted bribes from Sefton to delay aid; knows more about plague source than he admits; terrified of losing power
- **Leverage**: Threat of exposure destroys him; promise of safe exit makes him cooperate; appeal to buried conscience sometimes works
- **Appearance & Voice**: Portly man in his 50s with thinning hair, expensive but rumpled clothing, constantly sweating. Voice wavers between bluster and pleading.
- **Wants from PC**: Make plague disappear quietly; protect his reputation; find scapegoat for failures; be remembered as hero not coward
- **Campaign Arc**:
  - Act I: Obstructionist authority figure
  - Act II: Potential ally if threatened enough; provides access to records
  - Act III: Either redeemed (helps PC) or exposed (removed from office)
  - Act IV: Testifies about civic conspiracy (reluctantly)
- **3 Clear Phrases**:
  1. **What he wants**: "I'm trying to prevent panic. Do you understand what happens when people panic? Riots. Economic collapse. I'm protecting this city."
  2. **How he reacts to PC**: "Maal's temple sent you? That's... unnecessary. We have everything under control. No need for divine intervention."
  3. **What he fears**: "Being blamed. Being remembered as the mayor who let Yllmourne fall. Being poor again."
- **Stat Block Reference**: Use **Noble** (MM p.348) with no combat ability; relies entirely on guards and political maneuvering
- **Relationships**:
  - Bribed by Sefton Bane (in deep)
  - Avoids Faylene (her failure reflects on him)
  - Dismisses Bonita's warnings (inconvenient)
  - Fears One-Eyed Cy (criminal knows his secrets)
  - PC represents existential threat to position

### Callista Morwyn (Cursed Champion)
**Source**: TftS (add as newcomer)

- **Function**: Cursed warrior seeking redemption, potential companion, combat specialist
- **Traits**: Noble, haunted, disciplined, gradually corrupting, proud but desperate
- **Objective**: Break curse before shadow transformation completes; atone for past failures; die with honor if cure impossible
- **Secret**: Curse triggers in combat - the more she fights, the faster she transforms; avoiding combat means letting others die; no-win situation
- **Leverage**: Honor code prevents betrayal; curse makes her desperate; promise of redemption earns total loyalty
- **Appearance & Voice**: Tall woman in her 30s with champion fighter's build, shadow-mark spreading from left shoulder (visible corruption), haunted eyes. Wears practical armor, keeps weapons immaculate. Voice is controlled but you can hear the strain.
- **Wants from PC**: Cure for shadow curse; worthy death if cure fails; someone who sees the person not the curse; redemption through service
- **Campaign Arc**:
  - Act I: Arrives seeking Navula's cure; becomes temporary companion
  - Act II: Curse worsens; moral dilemma about using her in combat
  - Act III: Breaking Sefton's shadow magic may cure her OR she sacrifices herself heroically (DM choice based on PC actions)
  - Act IV: If cured, becomes permanent ally and witness
- **3 Clear Phrases**:
  1. **What she wants**: "I was a champion. People cheered my name. Now I'm becoming the very thing I swore to fight. Help me end this - either the curse or my life."
  2. **How she reacts to PC**: "Maal's agent? The Arbiter might actually answer prayers for justice. Mine have gone unanswered for two years."
  3. **What she fears**: "Losing myself completely. Waking up one day as a shadow monster wearing my face. Hurting innocents."
- **Stat Block Reference**: Use **Champion** (VGtM p.212) modified with shadow curse: gains shadow step and necrotic damage, but takes psychic damage after combat (progressing corruption)
- **Relationships**:
  - Seeks Navula's help (witch is considering it)
  - Cursed by shadow fey (Sefton's court connection)
  - Sparring partner to PC (if martial character)
  - Inspiration to Bonita (seeing her fight curse gives hope)
  - Friedel wants to study her curse (she refuses)

### Deputy Marshal Harlan Cross
**Source**: New character (filling TftS Captain of Guard role)

- **Function**: Law enforcement ally, Maal devotee, parallel investigator
- **Traits**: Dutiful, frustrated, incorruptible, politically naive, loyal to law over politics
- **Objective**: Solve plague murders through proper legal channels; support PC's investigation; resist pressure to close cases prematurely
- **Leverage**: Appeal to justice and he's steadfast; threaten innocent citizens and he prioritizes their safety; very by-the-book
- **Appearance & Voice**: Man in his 40s with military bearing, graying temples, hands that instinctively rest on truncheon. Wears marshal uniform with pride. Voice is measured, formal, but warms when discussing justice.
- **Wants from PC**: Collaboration on investigation; evidence he can use legally; backup when confronting powerful suspects; validation that proper procedure matters
- **Campaign Arc**:
  - Act I: Official investigation partner; provides legal authority
  - Act II: Increasingly sidelined by Mayor; chooses PC over politics
  - Act III: Helps organize defense of Yllmourne
  - Act IV: Legal expert witness; represents civic justice
- **3 Clear Phrases**:
  1. **What he wants**: "The law exists to protect everyone - rich and poor, powerful and weak. I won't abandon that principle."
  2. **How he reacts to PC**: "Finally. Someone who wants to investigate rather than cover up. Maal sent you at exactly the right time."
  3. **What he fears**: "That justice is impossible in a corrupt system. That doing things properly means letting guilty parties escape."
- **Stat Block Reference**: Use **Veteran** (MM p.350) with law enforcement modifications: expertise in Investigation/Insight, can deputize others, has arrest authority
- **Relationships**:
  - Frustrated by Mayor Lockton (orders to stand down)
  - Respects One-Eyed Cy grudgingly (honorable in his way)
  - Works with Bonita (protecting her as witness)
  - Allied with PC (finally has proper backup)
  - Maal devotee (sees PC as divine validation)

### Gravedigger Wilhelm
**Source**: TftS (add as newcomer)

- **Function**: Humble witness, pattern recognition, everyman perspective
- **Traits**: Observant, simple, kind, fatalistic, darkly humorous
- **Objective**: Bury the dead with dignity; warn people about what he's seeing; keep graveyard orderly despite impossible workload
- **Leverage**: Respects those who respect the dead; frightened but won't abandon duty; knows graveyard secrets
- **Appearance & Voice**: Older man (60s) with weathered hands and perpetually dirty fingernails, strong despite age. Wears simple gravedigger's clothes. Voice is rough from years of outdoor work, accent marks him as local.
- **Wants from PC**: Acknowledgment that plague victims deserve proper burial; help dealing with undead rising; recognition of his observations
- **Campaign Arc**:
  - Act I: Provides crucial timeline of plague deaths
  - Act II: Reports undead activity (mudskippers rising)
  - Act III: Graveyard becomes battleground; Wilhelm helps defend
  - Act IV: Simple testimony about human cost of conspiracy
- **3 Clear Phrases**:
  1. **What he wants**: "Every person deserves a proper burial. I've buried 247 this month. Each one had a name. Each one mattered."
  2. **How he reacts to PC**: "You're here about the murders? Good. I'll show you the graves. Tell you which ones didn't stay buried."
  3. **What he fears**: "Running out of space. Running out of strength. The dead not staying dead."
- **Stat Block Reference**: Use **Commoner** (MM p.345) with high Wisdom; non-combatant but has intimate knowledge of graveyard layout and tunnel systems
- **Relationships**:
  - Knew Dagmara (buried her personally; still angry)
  - Reports to Harlan Cross (suspicious deaths)
  - Observes Friedel's strange graveyard visits
  - Respects PC (someone finally listening)
  - Simple man caught in cosmic horror

### The Pale Maiden
**Source**: TftS (add as Sefton's enforcer)

- **Function**: Shadow fey assassin, recurring mini-boss, mysterious threat
- **Traits**: Silent, efficient, utterly loyal, alien mindset, beautiful and terrifying
- **Objective**: Serve Sefton Bane; eliminate threats to shadow court; test worthy opponents before killing them
- **Leverage**: Bound by fey contract to Sefton (breaking it frees her); has warrior's honor code; curious about mortality
- **Appearance & Voice**: Impossibly pale woman who seems to absorb light, dark eyes like pools of ink, moves with predatory grace. Wears black silk that flows like shadow. Rarely speaks; when she does, voice sounds like wind through dead trees.
- **Wants from PC**: Worthy duel before death; understanding of mortality (she's immortal); orders from Sefton (eliminate PC or recruit them)
- **Campaign Arc**:
  - Act I: Mysterious assassin (glimpsed in shadows)
  - Act II: Direct confrontation (PC likely retreats/survives)
  - Act III: Potential redemption if PC breaks her contract OR final assassination attempt
  - Act IV: If freed, testifies against Sefton; if corrupted, final boss fight
- **3 Clear Phrases**:
  1. **What she wants**: "My lord commands your death. But you interest me. Survive three encounters, and I will speak with you."
  2. **How she reacts to PC**: [Mostly silent. Tilts head curiously when PC does something unexpected. Vanishes mid-conversation.]
  3. **What she fears**: "Freedom. What does one do when the binding contract ends? What purpose remains?"
- **Stat Block Reference**: Use **Assassin** (MM p.343) modified with fey abilities: shadow step at will, charm person 3/day, immunity to enchantment, advantage in dim light. CR 11.
- **Relationships**:
  - Bound to Sefton Bane (contract servant)
  - Rival to Callista (corrupted vs. cursed warrior)
  - Hunts Friedel (he knows too much)
  - PC as worthy opponent (respects skill)
  - Doesn't understand mortals (finds them fascinating)

### Professor Aldric
**Source**: TftS-inspired (add as newcomer)

- **Function**: Academic mentor, curse specialist, exposition provider
- **Traits**: Intellectual, ethically concerned, cautious, professorial, genuinely helpful
- **Objective**: Study Yllmourne's magical outbreak professionally; help find solution; publish groundbreaking paper (secondary)
- **Leverage**: Academic curiosity drives him; ethical guidelines limit methods; can be convinced with evidence
- **Appearance & Voice**: Distinguished man in his 60s with white beard, wearing academic robes from University of Thrane, constantly taking notes. Voice is warm, professorial, tends to lecture.
- **Wants from PC**: Access to investigation sites; collaboration on research; safety while conducting fieldwork; credit for discoveries (not greedy, just academic pride)
- **Campaign Arc**:
  - Act I: Arrives offering expertise on magical plagues
  - Act II: Provides crucial theoretical framework for curse mechanics
  - Act III: Helps design counter-ritual
  - Act IV: Expert witness on magical theory
- **3 Clear Phrases**:
  1. **What he wants**: "This outbreak represents unprecedented magical phenomenon. If we document it properly, we might prevent future occurrences."
  2. **How he reacts to PC**: "Maal's investigator? Excellent. I've studied divine magic theory for forty years. Your perspective combined with my research could solve this."
  3. **What he fears**: "That rushing to solutions causes more harm. Academic rigor exists for good reason."
- **Stat Block Reference**: Use **Mage** (MM p.347) specialized in divination/abjuration; non-combatant researcher with extensive theoretical knowledge
- **Relationships**:
  - Professional respect for Minqena (library collaboration)
  - Concerned about Friedel (recognizes obsession warning signs)
  - Fascinated by Navula (wants to interview her)
  - Mentors PC (provides magical education)
  - Counterpoint to Friedel (ethics vs. obsession)

### The Ferryman
**Source**: TftS (add as supernatural manifestation)

- **Function**: Mysterious transporter, shadow realm guide, ominous portent
- **Traits**: Cryptic, inevitable, neither good nor evil, bound by ancient rules, knows too much
- **Objective**: Ferry souls and travelers between realms; maintain balance; collect payment in memories or secrets
- **Leverage**: Bound by rules (must transport those who pay properly); can be bargained with; knows hidden truths
- **Appearance & Voice**: Tall figure in hooded cloak that obscures face, appears during fog banks near barrier islands, poles a small boat that shouldn't float. Voice echoes strangely, as if coming from underwater.
- **Wants from PC**: Payment for passage (never gold - always memories, secrets, or promises); respect for ancient customs; acknowledgment of his role
- **Campaign Arc**:
  - Act I: Ominous encounter (PC sees him, doesn't understand)
  - Act II: First bargain (PC needs shadow realm information)
  - Act III: Crucial transport during climactic confrontation
  - Act IV: Final payment reveals he's been guide all along
- **3 Clear Phrases**:
  1. **What he wants**: "All must pay the ferryman. What will you trade? Gold has no value where we're going."
  2. **How he reacts to PC**: "Maal's chosen. I have ferried many who claimed divine purpose. Few returned. Will you?"
  3. **What he fears**: [Cryptically] "The day the waters stop flowing. When there are no more crossings to make. The silence."
- **Stat Block Reference**: Use **Death Knight** (MM p.47) modified: no combat focus, instead has teleportation and planar travel abilities. Cannot be killed, only bargained with.
- **Relationships**:
  - Known to Navula (old acquaintance)
  - Fears Sogmonnath (she tried to bind him once)
  - Respects Maal (arbiter of cosmic order)
  - PC as latest passenger (curious about their fate)
  - Exists outside normal faction structure

## Using This Guide

### Campaign Preparation

1. **Read all profiles** before Session 0 to understand interconnections
2. **Choose integration approach** for any additional TftS NPCs you incorporate
3. **Adjust relationships** based on PC backstory and choices
4. **Prepare 3-5 NPCs** deeply for each session rather than trying to master all

### During Play

- **Consult "3 Clear Phrases"** before roleplaying to capture essence quickly
- **Track relationship changes** - PC actions should alter NPC attitudes
- **Use relationship web** to determine who knows what and when
- **Let NPCs pursue objectives** independently of PC - create living world

### Adaptation Notes

- **Scale stat blocks** appropriately for party level
- **Adjust redemption arcs** based on PC's ethical choices
- **Kill NPCs when dramatically appropriate** - don't protect favorites artificially
- **Let relationships surprise you** - if PC forms unexpected bond, run with it

- See **07 - Major Story Milestones** for when NPCs trigger major plot events
- See **08 - Narrative Flow** for pacing NPC revelations across campaign
- See **09 - Adapted Locations** for where NPCs can be encountered
- See **03 - Faction Dynamics** for how NPC organizations interact
