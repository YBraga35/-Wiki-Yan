## The Debates
The heroes are invited to participate in a series of philosophical debates at the Hall of Debate. Machaon, Chalcodon, Kerodotos, and Atreus all wish to test the intellectual mettle of the so-called "Saviors of Thylea."
Qualifying.
Anyone with proficiency in Persuasion qualifies automatically. Everyone else can attempt to qualify with a persuasion, performance, history, or insight check DC 13 (dc should be 8 + party level)

### In Game Mechanics
Once during the contest they can make a skill check relevant to the riddle (bullshitting encouraged) a skill check of 13+ results in a clue, a 20+ results in the answer.
### Prize
A lovely golden ring. Once per day, recharges at dawn, you may cast invisibility on your self by slipping the ring onto your finger. Requires attunement.
Pocket Dimension Monster Battle.
Literally summoned or trained animals and magical beasts killing each other. (non lethally) Familiars are allowed to attack during this event. Owners can only use a trainer action at the beginning of each round. Like lair actions, you may not repeat the same trainer action two rounds in a row. Boost - your pokemon gains 5 temp HP. ● Rally - if your pokemon is brought to 0 health, it can zombie save to avoid death. ● Attack! - the next attack by your pokemon crits on a natural 18,19, or 20. ●
Spectators: Anyone who is not a trainer in an active battle is a spectator. At the beginning of each round a spectator can choose one of the following things to occur: Cheer - Give a pokemon a +1 bonus to any d20 roll and damage roll it makes, stackable. ● Jeer - Give a pokemon a -1 penalty to any d20 roll it makes, stackable. ● Or Inspire - give a pokemon a 1d6 inspiration die. It can also be used to boost damage. ● Only 1 die per spectator per battle.
