# Chapter 1: Heroes of the Prophecy

### Arrival at the Temple of the Oracle

Though others failed, you slew the corrupted boar that plagued the lands west of Mytros. Your names are everywhere celebrated as the Heroes of the Heartlands. Now, you have come to the Temple of the Oracle to claim your reward. Kyrah has promised that you will learn more of your destiny from the water nymph, Versi, the legendary Oracle who brokered the Oath of Peace. But Kyrah has heard nothing from her in two weeks, and now she fears that something may have happened.

### O1. The Chasm of Whispering Steam

The air grows heavy and humid as you descend into the chasm, the familiar scent of pine and damp earth giving way to the sharp, metallic tang of sulfur and heated rock. The walls of the canyon weep with mineral-rich water, creating slick, rust-colored streaks on the stone. A perpetual, eerie mist clings to the ground, muffling sound and reducing visibility. The only consistent sounds are the soft hiss of countless steam vents and the drip-drip-drip of water echoing in the oppressive silence.

The path is treacherous. As you navigate the chasm floor, you notice the steam vents pulse rhythmically. A weathered stone near the start of the chasm has a barely legible inscription:

> _"The Titan’s breath is a jealous rage. Heed the short warning, respect the long sigh, and flee from his final roar."_

A DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) check reveals the pattern: a short burst, a long hiss, then a violent eruption. To pass safely, you must time your movements. Failure requires a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw to take half of **2d6 fire damage** from a geyser blast. Four **steam mephits**, summoned by the hag Heleka, lurk in the mist, attempting to lure you into the geysers.

### O2. The Temple Portico

Through the swirling mist, the temple facade emerges like a phantom. Carved directly from the canyon wall, its scale is humbling. Above the entrance, a magnificent frieze depicts Sydon, the Lord of Storms, as a wrathful judge. Standing guard are four soldiers from the Order of Sydon. Their leader, a grim-faced priest named **Braeca**, steps forward.

> _"Hold, strangers. This is a holy place, now being cleansed of its... impurities. The Oracle has fallen silent, a clear sign that the Five Gods have abandoned her to the righteous fury of Sydon. Turn back, before you offend the Lord of Storms further. The 'Chosen Ones' of a silent Oracle are chosen for nothing but ruin."_

### O3. The Temple Chamber

The air inside is thick with the smell of incense, stale blood, and fear. The grand chamber is desecrated. A great bronze statue of Sydon mocks the chamber's original purpose, its shadow falling over a murky reflection pool. Braeca and four more soldiers have cornered the temple attendants near a sacrificial bier. The bodies of several acolytes lie in pools of their own blood. An elderly priest, **Proteus**, his face a mask of grief and fury, shouts at Braeca:

> _"You fools! This is not piety, it is blasphemy! You defile this sacred place while a monster from the depths tortures our lady! Heleka—that's the sea hag's name! She delights in every scream, for she knows it torments the Oracle's spirit! Sydon's justice? This is Lutheria's cruelty!"_

The rectangular pool at the statue's base has a faint, magical shimmer. On the floor near it, a riddle is inscribed:

> _"I have no voice, but I show the truth. I have no eyes, but I reflect the world. I drink the light but am forever dark. To see beyond, you must first offer a sacrifice not of blood, but of brilliance."_

**Solution:** Casting a spell that produces light onto the pool will cause it to flare brightly, showing a brief, horrifying vision of Heleka torturing an acolyte in the grotto below.

> _“I am the Lord who commands silence, yet I speak through thunder.  
> My wrath brings death, yet my absence brings despair.  
> I am justice without mercy, and truth no one wishes to hear.  
> What am I?”_

**Answer:** The Storm / The Judgment / The Cruel Truth / The Unyielding Reality

> I am fed by water and mortal memory,

**Answer:** Prophecy / The Oracle / The Vision / Eternal Memory

**Answer:** Fate / Destiny / Divine Truth / Prophecy


### O5. The Attendants' Chambers

These seven rooms are where the temple attendants sleep. All are sparse and tidy, containing little more than robes, boots, and prayer scrolls. In one hallway, the key to the locked stairway door hangs on a hook. Heleka has enchanted four flying swords and left them hanging on the wall next to the key. The swords remain motionless until the key is touched, at which point they come alive and attack.

Treasure: Proteus's room is the one exception. He has a small chest that contains six potions of healing.

### O6. Mimic Door

The door at the bottom of the stairway looks like strong oak with Sydon's wrathful face carved into it. It is actually a **Mimic** loyal to Heleka. When someone touches it, the mimic will attack, with the 'carved' face of Sydon biting whoever touched it.

### O7. The Oracle's Grotto

Beyond the mimic, you enter a grotto of profound natural beauty, now twisted into a theater of cruelty. Liquid blue light shimmers across slick, rocky walls. The room is lit by torches and filled with the vaporous mist of steaming hot springs. You can hear water cascading into an underground river at the rear of the cave. One of the rugs in the center of this grotto is actually a **Rug of Smothering**. It will attack anyone who tries to move deeper into the cave.

### O8. The Oracle's Chamber

At the far end of the grotto, the scene is horrifying. The beautiful Oracle, **Versi**, is held suspended over the rushing river by a **water weird** that seems to be an extension of the river itself. Her golden raiment is soaked, and her face is a portrait of anguish.

In the chamber behind her, the hideous, blue-skinned sea hag **Heleka** cackles as she slowly tortures a terrified acolyte.

> _"Hush now, little priestling. Your cries are music to my ears, and a dagger in your lady's heart. She sees your every tear, feels your every cut. Oh, she's powerful, the Oracle is! Powerful enough to watch you die, and do nothing!"_

As you confront Heleka, Versi's voice echoes in your minds:

> _"She is a creature of the jealous depths... born of spite and shadow. To unbind the river's grasp, you must answer its heart... What does the Storm Lord fear most, yet create with every passing wave?"_

**Answer:** Silence. Or Calm. Speaking the answer aloud will cause the water weird to lose its cohesion for 1d4 rounds, freeing Versi to aid you.

### The Oracle's Prophecy

Once Heleka is defeated and Versi is rescued, she thanks you. Her voice is weak but filled with an ancient power.

> _"I see... a mountain's heart of fire, where weapons of the gods sleep, waiting for a dwarf's touch... I see... a horn, carved from the mother of dragons, that does not call to beasts, but to the echoes of destiny... I see... a tomb where the first lord lies, but his greatest treasures are not buried with him..._
> 
> _Three labors... three threads in a tapestry of doom. Pull one, and the others may unravel. Only when you have held the fire, drunk the echo, and claimed the lost legacy... only then will the path to the forgotten sea be known to you. But beware... for the path is sailed by a ghost, captained by a sin that can never be atoned._
> 
> _But woe unto thee, for I have seen the end of all things. My father's anger cannot be quelled, and his sister schemes even now. Your quest may yet fail and, if it should, the sky will rain black fire, and the doom of Thylea will come."_

### The Great Labors & Leaving the Temple

Both Proteus and Kyrah help interpret the Oracle's vision. The three labors are clear:

1. **Light the Fires of the Mithral Forge.**
    
2. **Drink From the Horn of Balmytria.**
    
3. **Claim the Weapons of the Dragonlords** from the Necropolis of Telamok.
    

Before you leave, Proteus gives each of you one **potion of healing**.

> _"The future of Thylea may be bleak, but there is always hope. Make yourselves a beacon of light as we enter this time of darkness."_

After Proteus departs, Kyrah offers a different perspective:

> _"Listen to me — forget what Proteus said. This is your chance to win fame beyond your wildest dreams. Fame is the path to immortality."_

She recommends traveling to **Estoria** first, as the Horn of Balmytria should be there on display. She also gives you the golden arrows she promised as your reward.
