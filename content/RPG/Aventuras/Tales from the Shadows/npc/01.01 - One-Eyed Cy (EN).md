# One-Eyed Cy

## Snapshot
- **Role**: Dockside crime lord, arena master, pragmatic broker of shadow intelligence.
- **Motivation**: Keep Yllmourne intact while protecting his people and profits; views shadow incursions as existential threat.
- **Leverage**: Loyalty to the Belly districts, fear of the shadow entity that took his eye, buried care for dagmara.
- **Stat Baseline**: Start from `Bandit Captain` (MM p.344), add Legendary Resistance (1/day), Unarmed Fighting style, and an `Intimidating Presence` action to reflect veteran pit-fighter status.
- **Deep Dive**: See [[npc/apend.01 - Integrated NPCs Complete Guide#One-Eyed Cy|Integrated NPCs]] for extended hooks.

## Arc by Act
- **Act I – Informant Under Duress**: Provides underworld intelligence when convinced the Agent can curb the Mudskipper plague; scene anchor in [[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)#Scene 2: Plague Hill Investigation|Act I]].
- **Act II – Reluctant Ally**: Shares first-hand accounts of shadow bleed and supports tribunal efforts to save Kael; reappears in [[01.20 - Tribunal Script (EN)|Kael’s Trial]] as political weight.
- **Act III – Coalition Builder**: Funds blockade-breaking preparations if shown the Choir threatens trade; key contact in [[03.10 - Naval Briefing (EN)|Night of the Blockade]].
- **Act IV – Tribunal Witness**: Testifies about shadow incursions and Sefton’s contracts during the cosmology arc [[04.00 - Act IV - Cosmology and Faith (Lv13-20)|Act IV]].

## Relationship Web
- **Dagmara Stoneheart**: Former lover; her death drives his cooperation with the Agent. See [[npc/01.07 - Dagmara Stoneheart (EN)|Dagmara dossier]].
- **Sefton Bane**: Sophisticated rival whose shadow court threatens Cy’s territory; oscillates between cold diplomacy and sabotage ([[npc/01.02 - Sefton Bane (EN)|Sefton dossier]]).
- **Bonita Sandberg**: Funds her clinic security out of respect for Dagmara ([[npc/01.05 - Bonita Sandberg (EN)|Bonita dossier]]).
- **Navula**: Secret informant about shadow tides; their uneasy alliance keeps him ahead of planar threats ([[npc/01.04 - Navula (EN)|Navula dossier]]).
- **Faylene**: Distrust rooted in Rotarist interference with the docks; becomes open hostility once her hex is revealed ([[npc/01.03 - Octant Faylene (EN)|Faylene dossier]]).

## Scene Hooks
- **Negotiating for Proof**: Demand concrete plague evidence before he risks men (Persuasion/Insight vs. DC 15) — use during early Act I.
- **Arena Favor**: Offers manpower if the Agent clears his pit-fighting slate of Choir infiltrators (custom encounter tuned via [[00.12 - Encounter Matrix (EN)|Encounter Matrix]]).
- **Tribunal Testimony**: Will take the stand if guaranteed protection from Onotanian reprisals (ties into faction clocks in [[apend.01 - Special Mechanics Guide#Faction Clocks|Special Mechanics]]).

## Mechanical Notes
- Treat his crew as `Veterans` and `Bandits` with Morale checks keyed to his HP (if he drops below 25%, minions disengage).
- Grant Cy a once-per-session `Shadow Sense` (advantage on Perception/Insight vs. planar threats) learned from Navula.
- Rewards: Information, smuggling routes, safe houses; rarely coin unless trust proven.
