# Maal Doctrine — Theological Reference

Use this sheet to articulate Maal’s faith for the English-language table, mapping parallels to Christian concepts while honouring Yllmourne cosmology and 2024 rules updates.

## Core Tenets
- **Triad of Stewardship**: Life, Death, and Law are sacred trusts. Justice is the equilibrium between them.
- **Doctrine of Witness**: Every judgment must be witnessed by mortal and divine eyes—hence Maal’s agents travel the world.
- **Sanctified Remembrance**: The dead retain agency through lawful rites; necromancy used for truth-seeking is sanctioned when sanctioned by Maal’s clergy.

## Parallels & Divergences (Christian Framing)
- **Parallels**:
  - Emphasis on covenantal law (echoes of Old Testament legalism).
  - Martyr narratives inspire faith reaffirmation, similar to saints.
  - Ritual confessions resemble sacramental penance.
- **Divergences**:
  - Maal accepts controlled necromancy as part of justice.
  - Salvation is achieved through adherence to law and service, not grace alone.
  - Divine miracles are transactional—boons granted when commandments are met.

## Liturgical Touchstones
- **Tribunal of Three Lights**: Public judgments invoking Maal’s aspects (Life = Mercy, Death = Finality, Law = Verdict).
- **Oaths of Sand & Salt**: Agents swear to preserve order on land (sand) and sea (salt) — incorporate into session intros.
- **Relics**: Dagmara’s broken scales, Maal’s sigils shaped like interlocking triangles; use as visual props.

## Roleplaying Prompts
- Present Maal’s clergy as composed, even compassionate, yet uncompromising when law is threatened.
- Encourage the player to weigh mercy vs. retribution; reward lawful mercy with visions/boons, stern warnings if line is crossed.
- Frame debates with Tor Underleaf around balancing law with community care, reinforcing religious nuance.
