# Session One Briefing — Arrival in Yllmourne

Use this document to kick off the paid solo campaign in English. It blends the Portuguese material from [[00.02 - Opening Hooks and Introduction|Opening Hooks]] with premium-table expectations.

## Table Etiquette & Expectations
- **Session language**: English (player resides in Canada). Keep Brazilian flavor in NPC names but speak them clearly once, then offer phonetic shorthand if needed.
- **Premium pacing**: structure session into three distinct scenes with intentional transitions. Provide recap beats after each scene to affirm understanding and spotlight player agency.
- **Safety & tone**: confirm Lines & Veils with the player. Horror beats lean on tension, not gore; focus on investigative dread and moral ambiguity.

## Opening Read-Aloud (Polished)

> *The voyage from the mainland is mercifully short, yet three days at sea is enough to learn the sailors’ whispered gospel of Yllmourne. They speak of a plague that turns lungs to brine, of ships that vanish in the harbour, of gulls that have learned to avoid the docks entirely.*  
> *When the ship rounds the headland, the city sprawls ahead—dockside slums pressed against stone warehouses, merchant terraces clinging to the hillside, and above it all the Governor’s Palace and the Temple of Onotas, gleaming white against a bruised sky.*  
> *The smell hits next: rotting fish, unwashed bodies, quicklime. Beneath it rides something colder—the sense that the city itself is holding its breath.*  
> *Your hand finds the symbol of Maal resting against your chest. Dagmara is dead. Her eyes were taken. Her message named corruption, plague vectors, and a traitor called **Faylene**. Maal has sent you to bring justice.*  
> *The plank thuds onto the slime-slick pier. Yllmourne watches. The investigation begins.*

Encourage the player to describe their arrival posture (holy symbol, armor details, luggage). Offer the DMPC’s presence immediately if desired (see below).

## Scene Structure

### Scene 1 — Dockside Orientation
- **Objectives**: establish plague ambience, introduce Mudskipper rumors, foreshadow faction tensions.
- **Key NPCs**: Wharfmaster Bonita (grieving ally), Dock Sergeant Harlan (lawful contact).
- **Mechanics**: Insight/Perception checks reveal fear + corruption cues; Medicine checks note plague symptoms (no infection yet).
- **Links**: [[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)|Act I dossier]] › “Scene 1: The First Haunting.”

### Scene 2 — Dagmara’s Trail
- **Objectives**: secure Dagmara’s journal, hint at religious conspiracy.
- **Key NPCs**: Bonita, church acolytes, inquisitorial agent shadowing the PC.
- **Evidence**: Present sanitized copy of Dagmara’s notes (create in English later); lean on [[npc/apend.01 - Integrated NPCs Complete Guide|NPC Guide]] for personalities.
- **Links**: [[00.10 - Major Story Milestones|Milestone 1]] for consequence mapping.

### Scene 3 — Night Messengers Vision
- **Objectives**: deliver ghostly warnings, seed lighthouse hook, introduce name “Faylene.”
- **Tone**: supernatural horror, personal stakes — reference [[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)#Scene 1: The First Haunting|Night Messengers]].
- **Mechanics**: Wisdom saves vs. fear; provide Maal-aligned boon (advantage on Insight vs. cultists next day).

## DMPC Integration (Halfling Divine Soul)
- Offer introduction during Scene 1 as a friendly face awaiting the Agent—childhood friend **Toren “Tor” Underleaf** (adjust name as desired) returning from trade voyage when Dagmara died.
- **Role**: support caster; emphasise logistical help (arranging lodging, smoothing customs). Keep spotlight on player by having Tor ask legal-theological questions rather than deliver answers.
- **Mechanical positioning**: begin at level 3 with protective/buff spell load (bless, shield, healing word, sanctuary). Avoid heavy damage spells in early sessions.
- **Narrative tension**: Tor wrestles with Maal’s strict doctrines vs. halfling pragmatism—use as lens for Christian parallels (law vs. grace).

## Religious Framing
- Present Maal as god of Life, Death, and Law whose clergy debates justice through ritual tribunals. Draw gentle parallels to Christian canon law and concepts of divine judgment, while clarifying differences (Maal sanctions necromantic investigations as part of lawful duty).
- Session prompt: ask the player to recall a past tribunal they witnessed, then echo that emotion when confronting Dagmara’s corpse.

## Premium Table Touchpoints
- **Props**: share a digital handout of Dagmara’s journal cover page (English summary) post-session.
- **Music/Sound**: ambient harbour track + distant church bells; switch to low chanting during Night Messengers vision.
- **Recap Template**: end session with “Finding, Dilemma, Next Step” summary; log it inside [[00.00 - Master Matrix (EN)|Master Matrix]] under Maintenance Notes.
