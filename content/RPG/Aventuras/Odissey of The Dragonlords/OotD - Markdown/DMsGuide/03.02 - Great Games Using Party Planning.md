● 1 athletic and social event per day ● In the morning do preliminaries ○ Anyone can participate in prelim ○ Cost 10gp to try ○ Only 2 athletes per faction can go on to the main athletic event ● Place the 12 participants on the field for the main athletic event ● Activate the next social event in the Main Event Sequence ● Shuffle any remaining Guests into groups of 2-5 and spread them around the Zones and give them a topic of conversation ● Allow players who aren’t in the match, using support magic, or drawn into an special event to choose a group to approach(if they want) ● Run the Athletic event ● Resolve the social event and any other social interactions the players initiate ● End of Day
## Factions
6 Main Factions have come to participate in the games. They have Standard and Elite Athletes depending on the competition and are shown as (+X, +Y) where standard athletes get +X to the check and maybe a bless spell and Elite Athletes get +Y to checks and often advantage from Enhance Ability or something similar. 1. The Mytrosian Athletic Society(+4, +6) 2. The Thylean Freecity Alliance(aka Estoria, Xanderia, Pythus etc)(+4, +6) 3. The Aresians(+5, +8) 4. The Amazonians(+5, +8) 5. The Scions of Sydon(Sydons demigod children)(+8, +12) a. Assume they are wearing some sort of bracelet or something to suppress their legendary actions and immunities or it will just feel unfair as they are all Young Empyreans 6. The Chosen of the Oracle(the players)
