# Dagmara's Journal — Condensed Translation

Recovered from Wharfmaster Bonita and translated for the Agent of Maal. Use this as an in-character handout summarizing Dagmara Stoneheart’s final notes. Each entry references corroborating sources in the campaign files.

## Timeline Highlights

### Day -7 — Plague Pattern Breaks
- Dagmara charts Mudskipper cases and notes **necromantic leakage** inconsistent with natural disease.
- Marks victims bearing spiral wave brands identical to symbols later tied to the *Abyssal Choir* ([[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)#Scene 2: Plague Hill Investigation|Plague Hill Investigation]]).
- Marginalia: “Law breached at the waterline. Someone weaponizes faith.”

### Day -5 — Faylene’s Sermon
- Attends Prelate Faylene’s vigil; records doctrinal deviations stressing “purity through suffering.”
- Notes hex residue clinging to congregants, suspects ritual tampering with Maal’s justice ([[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)#Scene 4: The Banquet Murder|Banquet Murder]]).
- Adds warning: “If Faylene is compromised, Onotanian support of Cy is a mask.”

### Day -4 — Lighthouse Reconnaissance
- Tracks contraband idols to abandoned lighthouse; observes Rotarist cultists summoning tidal elementals ([[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)#Scene 4: The Abandoned Lighthouse|Abandoned Lighthouse]]).
- Records sigil match between idols and mudskipper lesions; identifies name “Faylene” carved inside ritual circle.
- Secures a mirror shard infused with illusion magic (later vital evidence).

### Day -3 — Library Correspondence
- Confers with Minqena about restricted tomes on plague curses; learns Dagmara requested access to memory vaults ([[npc/apend.01 - Integrated NPCs Complete Guide|Integrated NPC Guide]] → Minqena profile).
- Entry lists “Sodality of Still Waters” and “Abyssal Choir” side by side, followed by “two masters: Naran & Thellos.”

### Day -2 — Ship Manifests
- Interviews dockhands; cross-references manifests citing vessel **Abyssal Choir** running food shipments that never made landfall ([[00.02 - Opening Hooks and Introduction#Harbor Rumors|Harbor Rumors]]).
- Journal sketches include warped hulls and ghostly rigging, annotated “sinks by choice, not chance.”
- Predicts blockade escalation “within tendays” if cult remains unchecked.

### Day -1 — Confessional Visit
- Visits Callista Whitesail at Faylene’s invitation; detects mirrored aura indicative of hex experimentation ([[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)#Scene 3: Mirror Realm Dive|Mirror Realm]]).
- Writes: “Callista is host to prototype curse. If released, entire court falls.” Leaves warning to alert Cy discreetly.

### Day 0 — Final Entry
- Notes being followed by cloaked figure branded with spiral wave.
- Records plan to meet Bonita near Slippery Eel with compiled evidence.
- Final line smudged by seawater: “If I fail, Maal’s next agent must expose Faylene in tribunal. Watch the mirrors.”

## Key Findings & Cross-References

- **Cult Integration**: Rotarist cells, the *Abyssal Choir*, and shadow agents coordinate rituals; see [[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)#Investigation Hooks|Investigation Hooks]] and [[00.11 - Narrative Flow and Storytelling#Foreshadowing Guide|Foreshadowing Guide]].
- **Primary Antagonist**: Dagmara names Faylene five times, labeling her “architect,” “hexmonger,” and “false confessor.”
- **Planar Suspicion**: Repeated mentions of “Naran/Thellos hands steering tide”—aligns with Act II revelations ([[02.00 - Act II - Political Intrigue (Lv5-7)#Cliffhanger|Act II Cliffhanger]]).
- **Action Requests**: Urges successor to “secure Kael’s release,” “redeem Callista,” and “hunt the Choir’s harbor.”
