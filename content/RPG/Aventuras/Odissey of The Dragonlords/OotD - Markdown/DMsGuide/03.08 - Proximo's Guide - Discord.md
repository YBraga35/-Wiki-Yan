## the Great Games
The Great Games have a history nearly as violent as the Aresian campaigns. Itʼs just a bunch of bloodthirsty barbarians tearing each other apart over the smallest dispute. Naturally, Iʼm referring to the spectators not the athletes.
## History of the Games
The Games have been held every 10 years since the signing of the Oath of Peace. Priests, nobles, and others oﬀer sacriﬁces to both the Five Gods and the Titans at the start of each Games. This marks the 50th anniversary of the Games, and is rumored to be the most grandiose Great Games ever. In recent years the games have become a political tool used by various factions to demonstrate their dominance. Because of this, the Games are oﬃciated by a neutral party - Versi, the Oracle, whose judgement is respected by all.
