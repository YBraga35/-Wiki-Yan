# The Bastard

You grew up knowing little more than your mother’s love. Yours was a hard life, but you had each other, and you had the many stories your mother told you of your father - though she never revealed the identity of the man. Your mother bemoaned that you were both denied the lives you were owed by your birthright, as you were both kept from your father by his jealous wife. You returned home one day to find your house ablaze. Forcing your way inside, you managed to pull your mother out of the fires. With her dying breaths, she revealed that your home was attacked by someone, and told you to find your father, giving you the last token of him that she still possessed: a single burnished coin.

**For players who love**: Oedipus, Jon Snow  
**Restrictions**: You must be a mortal from Thylea  
**Adventure Hook**: The Oracle claims to have knowledge as to the identity of your father, and that of your mother’s murderer.

## Heroic Tasks

* Find your father
* Avenge your mother’s murder
* Claim the heirlooms of your father’s fathers. Pick one lesser and one greater magic item from this list:
    * (lesser) *Stone of Good Luck*
    * (greater) *Bloodshed Blade (any weapon)*
    * (greater) *Nine Lives Stealer (any weapon)*
    * (greater) *Dancing Sword (any weapon)*
    * (greater) *Scimitar of Speed (any weapon)*

## Divine Blessing

*Blessing of Leadership.* You may order your allies to attack. Each creature of your choice you can see and that can hear you may use their reaction to make an attack or cast a cantrip. The blessing cannot be used again until after a long rest.

# Integrating the Bastard

King Acastus received a prophecy when he was young. The Oracle claimed that she saw no great deeds in his future and that everything he did accomplish would be overshadowed by his progeny, who was one day destined to take his life. Acastus has lived in fear of death and obscurity ever since. Afraid, Acastus ensured he would never sire a child by keeping a close watch on his concubines and remaining distant from his wife.

The Bastard’s mother was Acastus’ favourite concubine. He gave her a family heirloom - a unique coin, which she wore as a pendant. One day, she happily told Acastus that she was pregnant, hoping that he would legitimize the child. Instead, he plotted to have her executed. Vallus learned what was happening and could not abide by it. She secreted the Bastard’s mother away, informing her that she was exiled, knowing that the woman was too charmed by Acastus to believe the truth of his betrayal. Only one other person witnessed this exile, the King’s mother, Idylla. Vallus forced her to swear an oath that she would not reveal what she had seen to Acastus.

After many years of searching for the Bastard and their mother, Acastus found them. He ordered one of his men to take their dragon to the Bastard’s home and burn it down with them inside (any neighbours the Bastard may have had were threatened or bribed into silence, were killed, or otherwise did not see the deed carried out). Fate protected the Bastard, but their mother was not so fortunate.

## Meeting the Oracle
Versi reveals that while the exact identity of the Bastard’s father is obscured to her sight, she sees greatness stretching back in their family line. She recognizes the coin as being a *burnished dragonlord coin,* and that judging by its age, it was one of the first of such to ever be minted. At this point, the coin gains the abilities of a regular *burnished dragonlord coin.* Versi believes it may have special properties beyond what the coins typically possess, though it is currently ‘dormant.’ Versi encourages the Bastard to show the coin to Damon in the Necropolis at Telamok, and states that *“A member of your blood sleeps restlessly in the necropolis.”*

> "You carry an unkind name that burns deep to your noble soul, Bastard. I admit that the identity of your father and your mother’s killer is obscured to my sight, but I can see greatness throughout your long-reaching family tree. The token of his that you carry, once given to your mother, is known as a burnished dragonlord coin, and I can see that it was one of the first to be minted. Take it to the City of the Dead and present it to the silent keeper of that place. He will show you the way to the tomb where a member of your blood restlessly sleeps.”

## Meeting Damon
Damon will not take the special coin from the Bastard, instead he will direct them to bring it to the tomb of Telamok Arkelander, effectively revealing that one of the Bastard’s ancestors resides in that tomb.

## The Tomb of Telamok
When the Bastard enters this area, the sarcophagus containing Telamok’s undying corpse begins to stir. Once opened, he reaches out towards the Bastard, gesturing for the coin. At his touch, the coin gains the properties of a *Stone of Good Luck.* He spits, full of dust, and claims that the Bastard is not ‘his’ but is instead part of his *“fool brother’s lineage.”* He will then attack, focusing on the Bastard.

## House Arkelander
The Bastard, or other party members, may try to learn more about the Arkelander family after confronting Telamok’s undead form. Any native Thylean would likely know that Estoria was named for Estor Arkelander. A History check, or asking anyone such as Pythor or Aesop, reveals that the sole surviving member of the Arkelander line is King Acastus of Mytros.

## Summoned by the King
After the matter of the storm is dealt with, Acastus will dismiss the party - save for the Bastard, with whom he wishes to speak to privately. You may wish to run the event *‘Summoned by the Queen’* separate to this meeting, as Vallus would prefer to speak to the party without the Bastard being present (see below for more on Vallus’ treatment of the Bastard).

If the Bastard wears the coin openly, Acastus immediately recognizes it. Otherwise, he notes the resemblance between himself and the Bastard, and/or of the Bastard and their mother, and has thus connected the dots, so to speak. He will ask the Bastard about the coin, and relays to them that he gave it to the *“love of his life.”*

Acastus acts overjoyed to have his child returned to him and feigns acceptance of them. He will explain that Vallus had their mother banished due to her incredible jealousy and that he searched for her for years without success. If the Bastard tells Acastus about their mother’s death, Acastus feigns heartbreak and lies, saying that he expects that Vallus used her godly powers to rain down fire upon the house, knowing he was close to finding his true love. He asks the Bastard to keep quiet about this theory, however, as Vallus is popular in the city and, of course, is a god.

Before Acastus will officially recognize the Bastard, he asks them to deal with the matter of the Ultros, after which he promises to shower them with the love and glory that they so rightfully deserve. Secretly, he hopes his ancestor will slay his progeny.

## Estor's Frustration
Upon meeting Estor, the ghost immediately recognizes the Bastard as being of his blood. He laments Acastus’ cowardice in sending his own child to deal with him rather than coming to meet his fate himself. Regardless, he will state that the Bastard should consider ‘bringing honour to the family’ by captaining the Ultros.

## Return to Acastus
Upon returning to Mytros, Acastus will feign delight at the Bastard’s success. Secretly, he is furious, doubly so if the Bastard has become captain of the Ultros. He will then make a show of presenting the Bastard to the city, allowing them to join him on the balcony of the palace and flying over the city on Icarus. During the speech he gives to Mytros’ people, he will attempt to claim that the Bastard’s great deeds are a result of the blood they share. *Be careful when wording this*, as it should not be *too* obvious that Acastus is just using the Bastard. If asked for his weapon, Acastus will state that he will ‘give it to them when the time is right,’ and that he still has need of it to protect Mytros.

## The Wisdom of Vallus
Vallus feels a measure of shame for her failure to protect the Bastard’s mother and as such largely avoids interacting with the Bastard. During the Embarkation, however, Vallus will quickly pull the Bastard aside. She quietly states that, *“All will be revealed beneath the stars of the Warrior,”* and that *“Someone who knows the truth waits for you there,”* to the Bastard. This refers to the Island of Exiles and Idylla Arkelander.

## Meeting Idylla
Upon meeting the Bastard, Idylla immediately recognizes them as being Acastus’ child, noting their resemblance to her son, or the Bastard’s resemblance to their mother. She will be overjoyed and immediately begin ingratiating herself to them. She invites the Bastard to her home on the island, where she reveals the whole story of the Bastard’s background to them, including the truth behind Acastus’ treachery and his plans to have their mother murdered - something she honestly claims that she vehemently tried to dissuade him of - and about the real reason for their mother’s ‘exile’ and her oath to Vallus to try and protect the Bastard. She reveals that Acastus spent years hunting them down, and that when he learned Idylla knew what happened to them all along, he had her exiled (this is only *part* of the reason behind her exile, but it *is* the reason Acastus allowed it to occur).

Just before her exile, she secured a number of luxury goods to take with her and smuggled something special alongside them: a silver dragon egg. She states that it is from the same brood as Icarus, and will reveal where Acastus truly got his dragons. She may use the egg to convince the Bastard to allow her to join them on the Ultros (see below) if they seem reluctant to bring her with them.

Idylla pushes to join the Bastard on their journey, offering her considerable influence, her contacts, political skills, and plays on their family ties to convince them to bring her aboard the Ultros. If they agree, she dotes on them frequently and provides advice periodically. She attempts to persuade the Bastard to take vengeance on Acastus, and to claim the throne - all in an attempt to return herself to power and to be an advisor at their side should she take the throne.

## The Assassination Attempt
Acastus learns of the Bastard’s trip to the Island of Exiles one way or another, either because he has spies on the Island of Exile (who may possess a *Sending Stone*), or because he has one of his false Dragonlords tail the Ultros and regularly report back to him on their progress. Understanding that his ruse has failed, he decides to act. Acastus sends a number of his false Dragonlords (two or three Mytrosian centurion **captains** with maximum hit points riding on **young brass dragons**) to find and kill the Bastard and the rest of the heroes. The dragons may flee if their riders are slain. You may wish to have the dragons use their breath weapons on the ballista of the ship, instead of solely targeting the PCs, as that may overwhelm the party, depending on their level.

## Confronting Acastus
Idylla cautions against confronting Acastus too soon. She claims that they must focus on their destiny - and the Titans - first and that when the time is right they will return to Mytros and take both the throne *and* vengeance. If Idylla is not present, then Kyrah, Versi, or another NPC can provide similar advice on concentrating on their journey rather than returning to Mytros.

During the Battle of Mytros, the time for vengeance will finally have come. During the *Mad King* event, Acastus will reveal everything he has done during his ravings. After the battle, he will apologize for what he has done to his child, finally feeling some semblance of remorse. He gives the Bastard their greater artifact (this weapon replaces Acastus’ Nine Lives Stealer, if that option was not chosen, and if the Nine Lives Stealer was chosen, it returns to having its maximum number of charges) and begs his child to save his city with his dying breaths.

If Tarchon and the other false Dragonlords that served Acastus still live at the end of the Mad King encounter, they swear their fealty to the Bastard.

## Claiming Their Legacy
If Vallus survives the Battle of Mytros she may abdicate the throne to the Bastard. As Acastus’ heir - and potentially at Idylla’s urging - the Bastard may ascend to the throne. This may conflict with the Haunted One epic path. DMs should use discretion when handling who takes the throne of Mytros, and ensure both the players and the characters are okay taking on the role of ruler of the city.

## Divine Boon
If the Bastard is ever killed, the spirit of their mother will appear over their body and weep. As her ghostly tears fall on them, they immediately return to life at full health. After being revived, the Bastard is under the effects of the *Bless* spell for one minute, no concentration required. This can only happen once.
