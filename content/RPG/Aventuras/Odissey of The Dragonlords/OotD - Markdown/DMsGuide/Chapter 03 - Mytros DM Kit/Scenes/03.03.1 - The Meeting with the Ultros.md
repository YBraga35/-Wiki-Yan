# “The Meeting with the Ultros” – Expanded Cinematic Script

## ACT I: DEPARTURE — The Road to the Dead Falls

### Opening Scene: Audience with the King

The Throne Hall of Mytros radiates power and history. Great white‑marble pillars hold up the vaulted ceiling, golden late‑afternoon light staining the polished stone like painted glass. King **Acastus** sits upon his throne, imposing, an old map resting on his hands.

> Read‑Aloud:
>
> “King Acastus fixes you with an intense gaze as you approach. His pale‑blue eyes seem to scrutinize not only your bodies, but your souls. He extends a hand, offering an aged map, its edges worn by time.
>
> ‘My scouts claim they saw the Ultros at the Dead Falls. A legendary ship, lost for centuries. If you can bring it under your command, we will have the only vessel capable of sailing the Forgotten Sea. A task for true heroes — not cowards.’
>
> He pauses; for a heartbeat, his eyes fix on some distant point, as if seeing something none of you can. The faintest tremor crosses his face before he regains composure.”

Conduction Note: underline that Acastus is acting strange — as if he knows more than he says. Kyrah, if present, remains notably quiet.

### Journey to the Falls: Through the Heartlands

Two days’ travel. Rocky hills, damp groves, a palpable hush.

#### First Night — The Sour Vintage Tavern

After a full day’s walk, the party reaches the halfway tavern: warm breeze, the smell of fresh bread and spices.

> Read‑Aloud:
>
> “The Sour Vintage appears like a blessing among the hills. Warm lights glow through round windows; laughter and cheerful voices spill outside. Inside, heat and the aroma of aged wine, roast meat, and exotic spices embrace you like a welcome hug.
>
> The innkeeper, **Aetala**, recognizes you instantly. Her eyes light up.
>
> ‘The Oracle’s Chosen! Welcome, welcome! You’ll eat, drink, and sleep for free under this roof. My patrons insist on it.’
>
> She gestures toward a table of merchants, shepherds, and warriors who raise their cups in your honor.”

Rumors at the Tavern:
- A grey‑haired fisherman claims to have seen the ship sailing up the River Lethe at midnight, black sails, no visible crew. “It sailed against the current — as if… as if it knew the way.” He drinks nervously.
- A traveling merchant whispers that the Dead Falls is a path to the Underworld — to **Lutheria, Lady of Dreams**. “Strange things happen there. The rocks scream like harpies. Those who reach the end never return.”
- An older woman lays a hand on a hero’s shoulder and recites an old mantra, almost a prayer: “Heroes do not fear the dead!” She repeats it three times, as if to convince you — or herself.

Kyrah Moment (if present):

> “I do not love the sword for its cut, nor the arrow for its speed, nor the warrior for their glory. I love what they defend.”
>
> She pauses, eyes fixed on the horizon.
>
> “The Ultros… I never liked those stories. Estor Arkelander was a destroyer. That ship is a floating tomb.”

If the group mentions Estor specifically, she tenses — shoulders stiffen, chin quivers.

#### Second Night — Camp Before the Falls

The distant roar of the Dead Falls fills the valley. The air grows colder. Mists rise from the chasms, curling like ghostly fingers around the rocks.

> Read‑Aloud:
>
> “The roar of the Dead Falls swallows the valley as you draw near. It is not the cheerful sound of water — it is a deep, constant bellow, as if the earth itself were grinding its teeth.”
