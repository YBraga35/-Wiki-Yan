# Meeting the Lady of Coins (Exiled)

## The Approach

> A discreet messenger slips close during a quiet moment as you watch the city recover. In a soft, respectful voice: “Your Highness, the Lady of Coins invites you under Sacred Hospitality — Guest Friendship — by the customs of the Great Mothers.” He leaves a silver coin engraved with a serpent.

## The Colossus of Pythor

> Inside the colossal statue, ancient chains and mechanisms line the walls. Steps echo like heartbeats as you climb. Small apertures offer sweeping views of Mytros until you reach the head.

> Within the round chamber, silk curtains drape the walls; two round windows face the sea. A silk hammock cradles a figure wearing a platinum helm.

## Moxena

> She removes her helm. Despite the years — and her transformation into a medusa — something in her eyes remains: the same compassion shown when she rescued a child long ago.
>
> “My little princess… how you’ve grown. Thirteen years since I set you on that boat, trusting Fate to guide you.”

Confession and Oath

> “I was there the night your mother, Queen Thesilea, was slain. Ordered to erase every trace, I found you hiding in a wardrobe. I could not obey. I fled, and built a network to one day restore what was lost.”
>
> “Before the Great Mothers and the Furies, I swear my life and resources to your cause — if you can forgive my part in that night.”
