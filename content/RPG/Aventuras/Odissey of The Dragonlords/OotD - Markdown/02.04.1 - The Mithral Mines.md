# Master's Guide: The Heart of the Mountain (The Mithral Mines)

This guide details the Third Great Work, focusing on the journey to the Mithral Mines, the exploration of its forgotten halls, and the important moral choices the heroes will face. The goal is to turn this mission into a crucial turning point in the campaign, where the players define their stance against cruelty and in favor of restoration and hope.

## Part I: Hooks in Estoria - The Call of the Forge

The adventure to the Mithral Mines doesn't begin with a map, but with the echo of silenced hammers and the hope of a people. In Estoria, the heroes will find the catalysts for this journey.

### The Copper Mine and the Tavern: The Dwarves' Lament

In the noisy, smoky environment of the Dragon's Tooth Tavern, the heroes might encounter a group of dwarves from Clan Rurik. They are the last descendants of the Mithral smiths and now work in Estoria's modest copper mine, but their hearts yearn for the gleam of true metal.

> **Description:** At a dark table, dwarves drink in bitter silence. Their faces, marked by hard labor, carry a deep melancholy. One of them, a dwarf with a grizzled beard and calloused hands named **Borin**, slams his mug on the table with restrained force.

If the heroes approach, Borin will share his people's song, a lament that he or a dwarven bard could sing with a deep, pain-filled voice.

> [!title] Elegy of the Lost Mines
> 
> In mountains high, where the wind is a wail,
> 
> Echoes the memory of a people in travail.
> 
> Mithral Mines, once their cherished home,
> 
> Now lie in shadows, under a concealing dome.
> 
> The clinking of hammers, the chisel's sweet song,
> 
> Forged wealth in silver, and in bitterness strong.
> 
> But a dark day descended, the Titan came down,
> 
> With grim Lutheria, fate itself did drown.
> 
> The earth, it trembled, and the gates gave way,
> 
> Flames and shadows took their homes that day.
> 
> Fathers and sons, in chains or in dust,
> 
> Lost forever in the abyss's lonely trust.
> 
> The clan, now broken, wandered in its grief,
> 
> Seeking shelter, but finding no relief.
> 
> Now at the Tooth, they labor without peace,
> 
> With scars that time will never release.
> 
> Oh, fallen brothers, in darkness you're kept,
> 
> Your bones cry out, a promise we've accepted.
> 
> For every skull, for every face,
> 
> We'll bury our sorrow in a hallowed place.
> 
> Mithral Mines, cursed they may be,
> 
> But in dwarven hearts, they'll live eternally.
> 
> For even in pain, on nights of the scourge,
> 
> The flame still flickers, in the darkness, it surges.

**Dialogue with Borin:**

> "Mithral! They speak of the wealth of Estoria's copper, but it's dross compared to what we had. Our mines... our home... cursed by Lutheria. She unleashed a beast of fire and death, a three-headed hound from the underworld, to silence our forges. Many of our kin fell there. Their bones are scattered through those halls, without a proper burial. But the Great Forge of Mithral is still there. Dormant. They say the Forge Guardian, an ancient salamander who helped Volkan himself forge weapons for the gods, still lives, trapped in the heart of the mountain. To reignite that forge... would be to reignite the soul of our people."

**The Request and the Reward:**

Borin then makes a solemn request to the heroes.

> "We don't ask you to face the beast for us. But if your paths lead you into the darkness of our old home, we ask that you honor our dead. Bring back the skulls of our fallen kin, so we may give them the funeral rites they deserve. For each skull you return, our clan will reward you with **10 gold pieces**. It's not much, but it's all we have. It is a matter of honor."

### The Hall of Volkan: A God's Request

**Volkan**, the God of Forges disguised as a grumpy dwarven blacksmith, will recognize the heroes as the Chosen of the Oracle.

> **Description:** The Hall of Volkan is a place of heat and ceaseless work. The air smells of coal and metal. Volkan, sweat shining on his brow, hammers a piece of iron on his anvil. Upon seeing the heroes, he stops, and his eyes, usually irritable, gain a glint of ancient interest.

**Dialogue with Volkan:**

> "The Chosen... It's about time. I've heard the dwarves whining into their mugs. The Mithral Mines... My greatest creation in Thylea, silenced by Lutheria's envy. That forge was not meant to lie cold. It was made to create wonders, to arm heroes against the darkness. If you are who the prophecy says you are, then you will go there. Rekindle the flames. Defeat whatever guards those halls. Do this, and when the forge burns again, I myself will go there to forge for you weapons worthy of your destiny."

**Trigger for Epic Paths:**

- **For the Demigod (Child of Pythor):** Volkan, their grandfather, will be more direct. "Your father... he had great ambitions, but little patience. He started a weapon, but never finished it. Only in the Mithral Forge can it be completed. Bring me his hammer and the right components, and I will forge you a weapon that will make your father proud."
    
- **For the Dragonslayer:** "You seek vengeance against a dragon? Common weapons will not suffice. The Forge Guardian, the salamander, helped create weapons that wounded the Titans. She knows the secrets to forging steel that can pierce ancient scales. Go. Learn from her."
    

## Part II: The Journey through the Gray Peaks

The journey from Estoria to the mines is arduous, climbing through rocky peaks and facing biting winds. The journey should last at least two days.

### The Abandoned Road

> **Description:** This long, abandoned road is rocky and covered with roots and thorny bushes. It winds westward along the granite cliffs of the Estorian tributary. North of the road are the vast steppes, and beyond them, the misty blue mountains. The sky is gray and cold during the day, but at night the clouds retreat to reveal a blanket of stars.

### Encounter: The Centaur Patrol

At sunset, the heroes encounter a centaur patrol.

> **Description:** The thunder of hooves can be heard from the north. Twelve riders approach. They wear bronze helms and carry long spears tied with fluttering scarlet ribbons. As they draw closer, you realize they are not horsemen, but centaurs—nomadic warriors with the torsos of men and the bodies of horses.

The chief, **Hukar**, warns the heroes of the dangers ahead.

> "Outsiders. If you're heading to the Great Falls, be careful. Filthy creatures, troglodytes, inhabit the caves there. They emerge at night to hunt. Two of my kin, Kaluk and Hirak, disappeared near those rocks. I fear the worst. The tunnels are too treacherous for our kind."

**Pholon's Challenge:**

After the conversation, Pholon, a melancholic warrior, steps forward, mourning the loss of his partner, Lykaon.

> "You fight as one body with many heads. Like we... were. I am Pholon. And I need to know if your strength comes from muscle or from spirit. I challenge your champion. No death. Only honor."

If the heroes prove honorable, Pholon will ask to join them on their journey to the Isle of Scoprion.

## Part III: The Great Falls of Arkelon

> **Description:** This rocky promontory offers a spectacular view of the Great Falls of Arkelon. The river plummets from a steep cliff and roars into a vast, semicircular lake, raising a white mist. The sound of the water is almost deafening, and the fading daylight shines on the water like red gold. Four massive stone hand statues surround the lake. A wooded path descends to the misty riverbank, where the collapsed entrance to the mines can be seen.

The main entrance is blocked, but a simple investigation reveals a secret path behind the waterfall.

## Part IV: The Mithral Mines - Upper Caverns

### F1. The Secret Path

> **Description:** This rocky ledge climbs from the riverbank and passes through a short tunnel, which diverts an endless cascade of water from the Great Falls. The ledge narrows as it ascends, stretching for a quarter of a mile along a steep cliff of smooth, black rock. Beyond the ledge, a shimmering curtain of translucent water falls to crash on the rocks below. A cold, white mist blurs your vision, and the roar of the water drowns out all other sounds.

**Encounter: Troglodyte Foragers**

> **Description:** A stench rises from below as four filthy humanoids climb onto the path ahead. Each carries a net filled with writhing fish. Upon seeing you, the troglodytes drop their fish and chatter amongst themselves. The stench is almost unbearable. You notice a hungry glint in their eyes.

Three **troglodytes** and one **troglodyte brute** attack immediately.

### F2. Forked Tunnel

> **Description:** This natural tunnel runs for fifty feet at a slight incline. The floor is wet, and moss grows on the walls and ceiling. The ground is well-worn, covered in the tracks of the filthy creatures from the waterfall. The tunnel eventually forks. To the left, it continues into a network of natural caves. A terrible smell comes from this direction, along with echoing screams of pain and horror. To the right, the tunnel becomes a masonry passage.

There is a chance of encountering a troglodyte patrol here.

### F3. Troglodyte Cave Entrance

> **Description:** The tunnel descends into the earth. From the darkness, a stench rises worse than anything you have ever known. The shrill sound from the depths of the caves suddenly ceases.

Four **troglodytes** are on guard, camouflaged against the walls.

### F4. The Prison Pit

> **Description:** A natural staircase descends into a cavern lit by the flickering fire of a campfire. The partially burned body of a centaur is spitted over the fire. On the other side of the room is a pen made of petrified wood. It currently holds a centaur who is being tormented by half a dozen disgusting lizards. The creatures poke the centaur with sharp sticks and croak with pleasure when he cries out in pain.

Four **troglodytes** and two hidden **troglodyte brutes** attack. The captured centaurs are **Kaluk** and **Hirak**. Hirak is on the spit and will die in five rounds if not healed.

### F5-F9: The Lairs and the King

- **F5. The Lairs:** Remains of bones and filth cover the floor. Two **troglodytes** and two **brutes** are sleeping here and will try to ambush the heroes.
    
- **F6. Icy Cavern:** A cold wind blows from a tunnel that descends into the Underdark. Three **troglodytes** and a **brute** guard the passage.
    
- **F7. The Hatchery:** (As previously described) The moral climax, where the heroes decide the fate of the troglodyte eggs and hatchlings under Kyrah's judgmental gaze.
    
- **F8. Garbage Pit:** A foul pit that requires a Constitution saving throw to enter. **Treasure:** Buried in the filth are a **mithral breastplate**, a **suit of mithral chain mail**, an **adamantine shortsword**, and an **adamantine spear**.
    
- **F9. The Troglodyte King:**
    
    > **Description:** This chamber has rough stone steps that rise to a platform. On the platform, a huge troglodyte sits on a crude throne, wearing a golden diadem. He grins maliciously and addresses you in his tongue: "How's it going, sweet things?"
    > 
    > The Troglodyte King (a brute with 19 Intelligence and maximum HP) is bored and challenges the heroes to a game of riddles before trying to eat them. He is accompanied by two brutes and a female troglodyte. **Treasure:** The king's diadem of intellect and a mithral ingot he uses as a scepter.
    

## Part V: The Mithral Mines - Lower Levels

### F10-F16: Dwarven Halls

- **F10. Broken Entrance:** A petrified statue of a two-headed **death dog** guards a bridge. It awakens 10 minutes after the heroes arrive.
    
- **F11. Cockatrice Pens:** Broken cages and statues of a troglodyte and a death dog. A **cockatrice** is loose and attacks. The death dog awakens after one minute.
    
- **F12. Barracks:** The scene of a recent battle. Two **cockatrices** attack. Three statues of **death dogs** awaken after 10 minutes, one every two rounds.
    
- **F14. The Hall of Dwarves:**
    
    > **Description:** This massive hall is supported by pillars, many of them broken. The corpse of a gigantic three-headed dog lies in the center, with smaller two-headed dogs gnawing at its carcass. Skeletal remains of dwarves are scattered everywhere.
    > 
    > Three death dogs attack. **Treasure:** One of the 25 dwarven skeletons wears a suit of mithral chain mail. The skulls can be collected for Borin's quest.
    
- **F17. The Deep Shaft:**
    
    > **Description:** This masonry tunnel ends at the edge of a massive natural chasm in the earth. Veins of silvery ore can be seen in the sides of the shaft. Old mining equipment lines the walls, but the wooden platforms are rotten. The surrounding area has been scarred by grooves in the stone, as if a giant claw had emerged from the shaft.
    > 
    > The shaft descends for a mile into the Underdark.
    
- **F18. The Dwarven Lift:**
    
    > **Description:** This room appears to be some kind of dwarven elevator. It is a ten-foot cube of metal made of bronze, with menacing dragon heads carved into the walls.
    > 
    > A deadly fire trap that is disabled by the password "dragon" in Dwarvish, information that Kyrah or Volkan can provide.
    

## Part VI: The Heart of the Forge

### F19-F22: The Forge and its Guardians

- **F19. The Armory:** Toppled weapon racks and broken equipment. **Treasure:** A **+1 mithral xiphos** is buried under the rubble.
    
- **F20. The Lava Pool:**
    
    > **Description:** This room is dominated by a large circular tank of molten lava in the center. Something is swimming beneath the bubbling magma. An ornate bronze sphere sits near the lava pool.
    > 
    > The Forge Guardian (a salamander) is trapped here by a cold device. She hates the dwarves and will only reignite the forge if the heroes destroy her contract, which is held by Cerberus.
    
- **F21. The Furnace:**
    
    > **Description:** This room appears to be a dead furnace. Four large stone tanks are lined up against the walls. In the center of the room, there is a circular hole three feet wide.
    > 
    > The Young Cerberus guards this place. He is a servant of Lutheria and is starving. **Treasure:** 56 pp, 125 gp, 389 sp, an adamantine trident, six adamantine darts, and the salamander's contract tablet.
    > 
    > **Event: Awakening the Forge:** Once Cerberus is defeated and the contract destroyed, the Guardian reignites the forge. This attracts the troglodyte horde led by the king for a final attack.
    
- **F22. The Secret Dock:**
    
    > **Description:** This chamber leads to a secret dock, located behind the Great Falls. The stone dock extends behind the thundering waterfall. The room is filled with rotting sacks, barrels, and crates. There is an ornate bronze lever on the other side of the room.
    > 
    > This lever activates a dam that creates an opening in the waterfall, allowing the Ultros to dock here later.
    

## Part VII: Consequences, Piety, and Rewards

The players' actions in the Mithral Mines have a profound impact on their legacy.

### The Choice: Creation vs. Destruction

- **Reigniting the Forge:** A moment of pure triumph that aligns the heroes with Volkan.
    
- **The Hatchery:** The choice here defines the group's character. Sparing the young aligns them with creation and mercy. Destroying them, with the cruelty of Estor.
    

### Piety Table: The Heart of the Forge

The actions here resonate strongly with **Volkan (Creation, Craft)** and **Kentimane (Destruction, Strength)**.

|   |   |   |   |
|---|---|---|---|
|**Action**|**Piety with Volkan**|**Piety with Kentimane**|**Justification**|
|**Successfully reignite the Mithral Forge**|**+5**|0|Creating something wonderful in Volkan's name.|
|Use the forge to repair a broken item|+1|0|Respecting craftsmanship.|
|**Destroy the hatchery and the young Troglodytes**|**-3**|**+2**|Creating something of poor quality (a legacy of cruelty); Burning a settlement.|
|**Spare the young Troglodytes**|**+2**|-1|Showing mercy instead of wanton destruction; Publicly displaying compassion.|
|Defeat Cerberus with clever tactics|+1|0|A decisive and creative victory.|
|Defeat Cerberus through brute force|0|+1|Achieving a great victory through strength.|

### Companions and Rewards

- **Pholon:** If accepted, he becomes a loyal companion.
    
- **The Mithral Forge:** Now reactivated, it becomes a potential base and the place to forge items from the Epic Paths.
    
- **Secret Anchorage (F22):** The discovery of the anchorage connects this location to the rest of the campaign via the _Ultros_.
