## Preliminary Games
You will need this: https://fexlabs.com/5ejump/ The first few days are these simple, traditional games. Think of the real-world Olympics. Complete with running the torch from the Sirens’ Gate all the way to the top of Volkan’s Mountain and the center of the great colosseum.
### How to play
This is a simple character audit or a single skill check. Followed by a storytelling challenge.
PCs can auto-qualify if they meet certain requirements for each event. Else, they may attempt an associated skill check to see if they would have succeeded and qualified for “The Grand Spectacles.”
During the preliminary games, no magic may be used. Competitors all wear bracers that glow if magic is being utilized. Cheaters are disqualified and publicly shamed, for life.
Placing in events
If a PC auto-qualifies and no other PCs attempt an event, that PC automatically comes in first place. If multiple PC’s auto qualify, whoever has the most qualifications is first, then second, etc. If there is a tie, roll-off on the associated skill challenge.
If a PC does not auto-qualify but succeeds, AND no other PC’s auto-qualified; check the result of their skill check: 20+ first 16+ second 12+ third That’s right even the lowliest PC can get 3rd place just by qualifying.
### Medals
The medals are cheaply-made, but they are prized by the people of Mytros and can easily be sold to merchants in Stygian Row. The real prize is fame and glory!
Preliminary Medals
For each category- First Place: 100 dr Second Place: 50 dr Third Place: 25 dr
### Tales of Victory
Anyone who auto-qualifies or successfully makes a check to qualify is allowed to describe to the group how the event went, if multiple PCs participated, the first-place PC starts the story, the second-place PC continues it, etc. Exceptional storytelling can earn “Olympic Gold.” Olympic Gold is like DM inspiration, but can only be used during the Great Games. Unlike DM Inspiration, you can have more than one Olympic Gold at a time.

## The Grand Spectacles
With the Preliminary games complete, the champions from those events prepare for outrageous events that push mortals to their very limits. Champions are encouraged to use and seek out magical assistance. That is correct, in these challenges, magic and supernatural abilities are not only allowed but encouraged. The challenges these athletes will face are sometimes deadly, but the glory gained from victory is that much sweeter.
Each of the Grand Spectacles is said to pay tribute to one of the gods/titans.
No haste spells allowed.
Other competitions will list allowed or banned magics and modes of movement.
If you are looking for good DCs for every try 8+average party level. So for example my party was level 6 so I just set all the DCs to 14.

### The Athletes provided by the book
Every settlement in Thylea sends its best athletes to compete in the Great Games. Competing this year are the following athletes: Cy (LN warrior monk) from the kingdom of Aresia. He is famed across Thylea for his accomplishments in previous Games. The citizens of Mytros hate him.
Egan (LG veteran) representing Estoria. He is an older competitor with more than a dozen Games under his widening belt.
Falana (CG noble) is a glamorous bard from Estoria. She brings a legion of adoring teenaged fans from the outlying settlements who cheer themselves hoarse.
Hatria (CE amazon warrior) is a warrior and the first-ever Amazon to compete in any Games. She is aloof and treated coldly by the other competitors.
Jorges (LE captain) of the Order of Sydon. He is arrogant and pompous and has an intense dislike for the heroes, particularly those who join the Games.
Leyland (CG gladiator) represents Mytros and is a favorite of the home crowd. He is considered one of the greatest athletes of this generation.
Pancras (CG oread) is a nymph from the distant northern steppes. He is quite an exotic figure, and is treated with curiosity by the other athletes.
Spira (NG soldier) is an underdog who suffered an injury five years ago and is only now recovered enough to participate. The crowd loves her. She has a Strength score of 16 (+3) and Athletics of +5.
