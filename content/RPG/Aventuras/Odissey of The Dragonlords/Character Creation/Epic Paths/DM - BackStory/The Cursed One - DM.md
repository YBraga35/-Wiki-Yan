# Integrating the Cursed One

An ancient ancestor of the Cursed One offended the Twin Titans, and as recompense, the Titans worked together to weave a curse that would confound the ancestor’s bloodline forevermore. Drawing upon the eldritch powers of the Maw of Typhon, they created the curse and bound it within the blood of the ancestor, whom they trapped in the Maw.

Only by delving into the **Maw of Typhon** and destroying their ancient ancestor can the Cursed One end the affliction on themselves, their family, and their bloodline.

---

## Nature of the Curse

Work with the Cursed One’s player to decide upon the nature of the curse. It should have a connection to their abilities as a character, either through their race, class, subclass, or other features.

For example:  
- A druid’s curse might be that they are destined to one day become trapped in animal form and go bestial, but until then they can control their transformations to some extent.

---

## Meeting the Oracle

**Versi** tells the Cursed One that they must speak with **Damon**, the ancient lich who guards the **Necropolis at Telamok**, to learn more about the nature of their curse.

> "You, Cursed One, whose veins run black with the foulest of hexes, and punishments are disproportionate to the crimes committed ages ago.  
> I see that you must travel to the City of the Dead. There you must speak with the silent guardian that watches over all of Telamok.  
> He shall reveal the nature of your curse, for he was there when the curse was placed upon your blood.”

---

## Meeting Damon

**Damon** knew the Cursed One’s ancestor and was present when the curse was laid. He communicates through visions and images that:

- The curse is unlike any other in Thylea.
- It was devised jointly by **Sydon** and **Lutheria**.
- The Cursed One must find a **black tower** beneath the **stars of the Scorpion**, atop which blooms a **lotus**.

> This tower is the domain of the **Lotus Witch**, who knows how to break the curse.

He also gifts them a long-lost family heirloom: a **Gem of Brightness**.

---

## Meeting the Lotus Witch

The **Lotus Witch** reveals:

- The curse's magic originates from **Typhon Island**.
- Only by diving into the **volcanic heart** of the island will the Cursed One be granted their wish.
- To survive the fiery depths, they must possess a **magic item powerful enough** to protect them from fiery death.

---

## The Greater Artifact

The Lotus Witch will only give the required artifact if the Cursed One:

- Successfully answers **enough of her riddles**.

If they fail, they must choose to **slay the Lotus Witch**.

---

## Typhon Island

When the Cursed One reaches **Typhon Island**, they can leap into the volcanic core.

- If they possess the **artifact from the Lotus Witch**, they and their allies appear **unharmed** on the **ash plains of Hades**.

There, they encounter their **ancestor**, now ancient and weathered, who reveals:

> "My death will end the curse."

But as the Cursed One attempts to strike, the laughter of the Titans echoes — the ancestor **transforms into a Typhon**.

- Once the Typhon is defeated, the ancestor returns to their true form and may then be slain.

---

## Ending the Curse

Once the ancestor is slain:

- All curses on the Cursed One’s family **end**.
- Their **Gem of Brightness** is fully **recharged**.
- The Cursed One **awakens back on Typhon Island**.

---

## Divine Boon

If the Cursed One is ever **killed**, **Damon** will appear and cast **True Resurrection** — usable **only once**, and **only on the Cursed One**, as part of an **Oath sworn to their ancestors**.

If needed, he may also aid in the battle that slew the Cursed One.
