# Yllmourne Overview

Concise location index for recurring sites across the campaign. Use this as a jumping point into act dossiers or appendices.

- **Governor’s Rise** — Seat of Cy’s authority, tribunal venue, political battleground. Site brief: [[places/01.02 - Governor's Rise (EN)|Governor’s Rise]].
- **Temple Quarter** — Rotarist stronghold and spiritual fault line. Site brief: [[places/01.03 - Temple Quarter (EN)|Temple Quarter]].
- **Hilbre Isle** — Sefton’s fortress island and blockade staging ground. Site brief: [[places/02.01 - Hilbre Isle (EN)|Hilbre Isle]].
