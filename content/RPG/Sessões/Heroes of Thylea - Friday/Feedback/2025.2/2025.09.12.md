# Session Zero Summary - Heroes of Thylea (Friday)

## Main Topics Covered

**-  Inspiration and Golden Stars System** - A homebrew reward system linking player feedback to mechanical benefits

**-  Flanking Rules** - Discussion about using +2 bonus instead of advantage for balance

**-  Death Save Mechanics** - Clarification that death save failures reset after combat ends

**-  Deity Worship System** - Simplified approach where each player chooses one primary god

**-  Piety Mechanics Implementation** - Using rules adapted from Mythic Odyssey of Theros

**-  Character Development Goals** - Interest in exploring moral complexity and deeper campaign lore

**-  Campaign Setting \& Tone** - Epic mythological storytelling in Odyssey of the Dragonlords

## Key Highlights

The most significant discussion centered around your **homebrew inspiration system** where players earn golden stars through end-of-session feedback and peer recognition. Three golden stars convert to inspiration points that can be used for rerolls with advantage, healing, or recovering spell slots.

There was also meaningful conversation about **campaign themes**, with players expressing interest in morally complex storytelling where traditional "good" and "evil" designations may be more nuanced than they initially appear.

The group agreed on practical rule modifications like **flanking giving +2 instead of advantage** for better game balance, and clarified that **death save failures reset between encounters** rather than carrying over.
