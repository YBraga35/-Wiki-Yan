# Oracle Temple - Quick Guide (60 min Streamlined)

> **🎯 Purpose**: Rescue Versi from Heleka FAST. This is the REVISED version cutting filler dungeon crawl from 8 rooms to 4 core encounters.

**Original Version**: `01.1 - Heroes of the Prophecy (Enchanced) [EN].md` (2+ hours, 8 locations)
**This Version**: 60 minutes, 4 core encounters, maintained emotional impact

---

## ⚡ STRUCTURE OVERVIEW

| Encounter | Time | Challenge Type | Stakes |
|-----------|------|----------------|--------|
| **1. Entrance & Steam Vents** | 10 min | Environmental hazard | Fire damage, Herkus clue |
| **2. Sydon Statue Puzzle** | 15 min | Puzzle/Combat | Shortcut or fight gargoyles |
| **3. Acolyte Rescue Timer** | 5 min | Investigation | Save acolyte = help in boss fight |
| **4. Heleka Boss Fight** | 30 min | Combat + Rescue | Save Versi, stop torture |

**Total Runtime**: 60 minutes (vs. 2+ hours original)

---

## 🗺️ DUNGEON MAP (SIMPLIFIED)

```
ENTRANCE → Steam Vents (T1) → Ruined Halls (T2-T3) → Sydon Statue (T4)
                                                            ↓
                              Acolyte Chamber (O8) ← Oracle Chamber (O7)
                                        ↓ (shortcut if puzzle solved)
                               Heleka Fight (O7)
```

**Removed from Original**:
- Flying swords (random encounter)
- Mimic door (filler)
- Rug of smothering (filler)

**Kept**:

- Heleka fight (boss)
- Acolyte rescue (stakes)

## 📍 ENCOUNTER 1: ENTRANCE & STEAM VENTS (10 MIN)

### Setup
Temple exterior is crumbling but still magnificent. Steam hisses from cracks.

### Boxed Text
> The Oracle's temple rises before you, carved into the mountainside. Once grand, it now shows centuries of neglect. Columns have fallen. Vines strangle the marble. But worst of all: you hear SCREAMING from within.
>
> A woman's voice, raw with pain and defiance: "I will NEVER tell you! The heroes will come! They WILL!"
>
> Kyrah's face goes pale. "That's Versi. We're too late—no, we're JUST in time. Hurry!"

### Environmental Hazard: Steam Vents (T1)
**Challenge**: Navigate entrance hall without triggering steam vents

**Mechanics**:
- Perception DC 12: Notice vents in floor
- Dexterity (Acrobatics) DC 13 OR Intelligence (Investigation) DC 13: Find safe path
- **Failure**: 2d6 fire damage from steam blast
- **Group Check**: If half the party succeeds, they guide others

**Clue - Herkus's Message**:
On the wall, written in druidic (or nature-themed imagery):
> "The tree mother weeps. The beast-woman tortures the seeing-lady. Stop the claws before the light dies."

**Translation**: Demetria (Mossy Temple foreshadowing) is corrupted. Heleka (sea hag = "beast-woman") is torturing Versi. Time is running out.

### DM Notes
- **Urgency**: Each round here, Versi screams again (countdown timer starts)
- **Epic Path Moment**: If any PC has Nature/Druidic connection, they understand the message instantly
- **Foreshadowing**: Mention "clawed footprints in dried blood" leading deeper

## 📍 ENCOUNTER 2: RUINED HALLS & SYDON STATUE (15 MIN)

### Transition
> You rush through collapsed hallways. Broken statues of ancient heroes line the walls—Dragonlords from 500 years ago. If any PC is the Vanished One, they see THEIR OWN STATUE (foreshadowing).
>
> Ahead: a massive chamber. At its center stands a 20-foot statue of SYDON, Lord of Storms, trident raised.

### Sydon Statue Puzzle (T4)
**The Challenge**: Statue blocks the direct path. Two options:

#### Option A: Solve the Puzzle (No Combat)
**Riddle** (inscribed at statue's base):
> "I am the storm that never sleeps,
> The lord of tides, the one who reaps.
> Kneel before me, mortals all,
> Or by my hand you'll surely fall.
> But speak my TRUE name—not the one I wear—
> And I shall let you pass with care."

**Answer**: "**Versi's Curse**" or "**Tyrant**" or "**Talieus's Shame**"
- Sydon's TRUE name before godhood was Talieus's shame (he's ashamed of his father)
- Alternative: Identify him as "the tyrant" (rebellion theme)

**Success**: Statue steps aside, reveals shortcut to Oracle Chamber
**Failure (3 wrong guesses)**: Gargoyle guardians animate

#### Option B: Fight the Gargoyles (Standard Route)
If players ignore puzzle or fail it:
- **2 Gargoyles** attack
- AC 15, HP 52 each
- While fighting: Versi screams again (urgency maintained)

**Reward for Puzzle Success**: Skip gargoyle fight, arrive at O7 faster (saves 10 min)

### DM Notes
- **Thematic**: Establishes Sydon as arrogant tyrant
- **Choice**: Smart players save time; combat-focused players still have fun
- **Vanished One Moment**: Their statue is nearby, younger, heroic pose

## 📍 ENCOUNTER 3: ACOLYTE RESCUE (5 MIN)

### Setup (If Shortcut Taken OR After Gargoyles)
> Through the next archway, you hear sobbing. A young acolyte, barely 16, is chained to a stone pillar. Her robes are torn, face bruised. She looks up with terrified hope.
>
> "Help me, please! The hag said she'd come back for me next! The Oracle—she's in there—" *points to sealed door* "—the hag is hurting her!"

### Quick Rescue
- Chains: DC 12 Strength OR DC 10 Thieves' Tools
- Acolyte (Name: Sera) is traumatized but functional
- **If saved**: She knows Heleka's weakness (see Boss Fight)

### Critical Information from Sera
"The hag... she HATES beautiful things. She tried to drown me in the mirror pool, but the Oracle's magic stopped her. If you can destroy her reflection, it weakens her!"

**Heleka's Weakness**: If her reflection in the mirror pool (O7) is shattered, she loses AC 2 and regeneration.

### Timer: Versi's Condition
**How long since players entered temple?**
- **Under 10 minutes**: Versi is injured but fighting
- **10-20 minutes**: Versi is exhausted, close to breaking
- **20+ minutes**: Sera dies from wounds; Versi gives FALSE prophecy (broken spirit)

## 📍 ENCOUNTER 4: HELEKA BOSS FIGHT (30 MIN)

### Oracle Chamber (O7) - Description
> You burst through the door into a circular chamber. At its center: a pool of

 perfectly still water reflecting the cracked ceiling above. On the far side, chained to a stone altar, is VERSI THE ORACLE.
>
> She is a vision even in agony—regal, ancient, beautiful. But her face is twisted in pain. Standing over her, clawed hands raised, is HELEKA THE HAG.
>
> Heleka is grotesque: green skin, claws dripping with Versi's blood, teeth filed to points. She LAUGHS when she sees you.
>
> "Oh, the little heroes! Right on time. Your Oracle refused to tell me the prophecy. Perhaps watching you DIE will loosen her tongue!"

### Heleka, Sea Hag (Modified Stats)
- **AC**: 14 (12 if reflection destroyed)
- **HP**: 52
- **Attacks**:
  - **Claws**: +4 to hit, 2d6+2 slashing
  - **Death Glare** (Recharge 5-6): One target within 30 ft, Wisdom save DC 13 or frightened + 2d10 psychic damage
  - **Horrific Appearance**: First creature to see her makes DC 11 Wisdom save or frightened (1 min)
- **Special**: Regeneration 5 HP/turn (stops if reflection destroyed)

### Battlefield Elements
1. **Mirror Pool** (Center):
   - Heleka's reflection visible
   - Attack reflection: AC 10, 10 HP
   - **If destroyed**: Heleka screams, loses AC 2, loses regeneration

2. **Versi (Captive)**:
   - Chained to altar
   - Has 15 HP remaining (out of 50)
   - Can cast *Bless* on heroes (bonus action) if freed
   - Chains: DC 15 Strength OR DC 12 Thieves' Tools

3. **Acolyte Sera** (If Rescued):
   - Throws holy water at Heleka (1d4 rounds)
   - +1 to party's attack rolls (inspiration)

### Tactics
- **Round 1**: Heleka uses Horrific Appearance, then Death Glare on healer/caster
- **Round 2-3**: Claws on whoever approaches Versi
- **Round 4+**: If bloodied, Heleka tries to drag Versi into mirror pool (drowning attempt)

### Objectives (Party Can Split)
- **Defeat Heleka** (primary)
- **Free Versi** (secondary, helps in fight)
- **Destroy Reflection** (optional, makes fight easier)
- **Protect Sera** (optional, if rescued)

### Heleka's Death
> Heleka shrieks as your final blow lands. She collapses, claws scrabbling at the stone. "You... you've won NOTHING! The Titans wake! Sydon will DROWN you all!"
>
> She dissolves into brackish water, leaving only seaweed and the smell of rotting fish.

## 🎭 VERSI'S PROPHECY (10 MIN)

### After Combat
If Versi is alive and unbroken:

> Versi gasps as you free her from the chains. She's weak, but her eyes burn with prophetic fire.
>
> "Thank you, my heroes. I knew you would come. I have SEEN your paths."

### FRAGMENTARY Prophecy (NOT Info-Dump)
Versi does NOT reveal everything. This is the REVISED version:

> "I see... fragments. The Fates are rarely clear, even to me.
>
> **Three trials await you**—fire, bone, and steel. A forge... a weapon? A tomb... but whose? And a temple, green and terrible, where a mother weeps.
>
> **A ship with no crew**, captained by memory and regret. You must claim it, but beware the one who steers.
>
> **The seas will test you**. Islands of wonder and terror. And at the end... the Titans themselves."

### Individual Prophecies (PRIVATE)
"I must speak with each of you alone. What I have seen is... personal. Dangerous if spoken aloud."

**DM**: Take each player aside (or pass notes) and give them their Epic Path prophecy (see `01.2 - Oracle Destiny Reveal [EN].md`).

**Examples**:
- **The Demigod**: "Go to Estoria. Your father drinks to forget you. Forge the weapon he could never wield."
- **The Vanished One**: "You must swear to return to me when this is done. I... I have never loved before. Not like this." (Oath moment)

### DM Notes
- **Fragmentary = Mystery**: Players must DISCOVER what trials mean
- **Private moments**: Build individual investment
- **Versi's vulnerability**: She's shaken, nearly broken, grateful

## ⏱️ TIMER SYSTEM (OPTIONAL BUT RECOMMENDED)

Track rounds since entering temple:

| Rounds | Versi's Condition | Sera's Condition | Consequence |
|--------|-------------------|------------------|-------------|
| 0-5 | Fighting back | Alive, terrified | Standard fight |
| 6-10 | Weakening | Alive, wounded | Versi at 10 HP |
| 11-15 | Breaking | Critical | Versi at 5 HP; Heleka has advantage |
| 16+ | Broken | Dead | Versi gives FALSE prophecy (TPK bait) |

**How to Track**: Each encounter = ~5 rounds. If players take too long (argue, split party, explore every room), advance the timer.

## ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. **DON'T run the full 8-room dungeon**
   - Use this streamlined version (4 encounters)

2. **DON'T let Versi info-dump the entire campaign**
   - Fragmentary prophecy only
   - Players must discover meaning

3. **DON'T make the rescue feel easy**
   - Versi is INJURED, traumatized
   - Heleka nearly broke her

4. **DON'T skip individual Epic Path moments**
   - Private prophecies are CRITICAL
   - Builds player investment

5. **DON'T forget Kyrah**
   - She should be horrified by Heleka
   - Tends to Versi after fight
   - Still mysterious (doesn't reveal goddess status yet)

## 🎯 SUCCESS METRICS

**This encounter is successful if**:
- [ ] Combat felt urgent (Versi in danger)
- [ ] Players made tactical choices (reflection? free Versi? fight?)
- [ ] Individual prophecies sparked player curiosity
- [ ] Temple felt dangerous but conquerable
- [ ] Runtime was ~60 minutes (not 2+ hours)

**Red flags to watch**:
- Players seem bored (too many empty rooms)
- Heleka died in 2 rounds (too easy; add minions)
