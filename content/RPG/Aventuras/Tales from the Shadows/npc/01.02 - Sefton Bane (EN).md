# Sefton Bane

## Snapshot
- **Role**: Shadow-fey admiral ruling Hilbre Isle; charismatic antagonist steering the blockade and planar conspiracy.
- **Motivation**: Forge a perfected court by merging the Shadow Realm with Yllmourne under his command.
- **Leverage**: Vanity, binding fey contracts, obligation to his court; his feelings toward worthy opponents can be exploited.
- **Stat Baseline**: Acts I–II `Archmage` (MM p.342) reflavored as fey; upgrade to custom Shadow Fey Noble (CR 13+) with Legendary Actions and Shadow Step in Acts III–IV.
- **Deep Dive**: Full design in [[npc/apend.01 - Integrated NPCs Complete Guide#Sefton Bane (Maritime Tyrant + Queen of Night Fusion)|Integrated NPCs]].

## Arc by Act
- **Act I – Distant Threat**: Operates through smugglers and shadow contracts; clues seeded in [[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)|Act I]] ship sinkings.
- **Act II – Political Adversary**: Hostile force at Seafarers’ Rest gala and blockade mastermind; key beats in [[02.00 - Act II - Political Intrigue (Lv5-7)|Act II dossier]].
- **Act III – Shadow Regent**: Directly commands the *Abyssal Choir* operations; naval confrontation options mapped in [[03.10 - Naval Briefing (EN)|Night of the Blockade]].
- **Act IV – Final Reckoning**: Either faces tribunal justice or climactic duel tied to Sogmonnath’s cage ([[04.00 - Act IV - Cosmology and Faith (Lv13-20)|Act IV]]).

## Relationship Web
- **One-Eyed Cy**: Respects but seeks to supplant; their contest shapes dock politics ([[npc/01.01 - One-Eyed Cy (EN)|Cy dossier]]).
- **Octant Faylene**: Hex-bound pawn; uses her prayers to spread curse without her knowledge ([[npc/01.03 - Octant Faylene (EN)|Faylene dossier]]).
- **Navula**: Ancient rival—her knowledge threatens his plans ([[npc/01.04 - Navula (EN)|Navula dossier]]).
- **Agent of Maal**: Intrigued adversary; offers temptations of power to test conviction.
- **Sogmonnath**: Secret patron empowering shadow court; his pact details appear in [[03.00 - Act III - Rise of the Hero (Lv8-12)#Discovering Sogmonnath|Act III]].

## Scene Hooks
- **Contract Duel**: Challenges the Agent to negotiate under fey contract law; success grants blockade intel, failure binds PC to perilous service.
- **Shadow Court Soirée**: Invitations to Hilbre’s masquerades reveal Choir officers; leverage during infiltration missions.
- **Blockade Ultimatum**: His envoy demands Yllmourne capitulation—spin up as skill challenge aligning with [[03.10 - Naval Briefing (EN)|Legal/Guerrilla paths]].

## Mechanical Notes
- Equip Sefton with signature spells (`shadow blade`, `mislead`, `dream`, `power word stun`) and lair actions on Hilbre (mirror doubles, shadow tether).
- Elite minions: `Shadow Demon`, `Gloomstalker Rangers` (custom), and the assassin *Pale Maiden*.
- Bargains impose mechanical costs (e.g., accept +2 bonus on Persuasion against his court at cost of vulnerability to psychic vs. Sefton).
