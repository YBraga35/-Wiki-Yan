# Act I: Horror on the Docks (Levels 1-4)

## Act Overview

**Theme**: *"The Surface Conspiracy"*

Act I introduces players to Yllmourne's immediate crises: Dagmara's murder, the Mudskipper plague, and the mysterious ship sinkings. Through six adapted *Tales from the Shadows* adventures, the party uncovers evidence of supernatural corruption, exposes surface-level conspirators, and earns their first victories against the darkness plaguing the city.

**Narrative Arc**:
- **Act I Opening**: Dagmara is dead; the plague spreads; ships sink nightly
- **Rising Action**: Investigation reveals curses, cults, and corrupted officials
- **Climax**: Public trial prevents a scapegoat's lynching; heroes expose initial conspirators
- **Resolution**: The plague's immediate threat is contained, but deeper questions remain (Who cursed the city? Why target the Library? Who commands the ship-sinkers?)

**Faction Clock Focus**:
1. **The Plague** (6-clock): Advances each week without intervention; reversed by breaking curse sources
2. **Cy's Legitimacy** (4-clock): Improves when heroes publicly support law and order
3. **Sefton's Dominance** (6-clock): Improves when heroes use underworld contacts or violence
4. **Library Preservation** (4-clock): Advances when heroes defend knowledge or thwart Royal Guard
5. **Planar Incursion** (8-clock): Slowly ticks up; players won't understand this clock until Act II

**Level Progression**:
- Start at **Level 1**
- Reach **Level 2** after completing Adventure 1
- Reach **Level 3** after completing Adventures 2-3
- Reach **Level 4** after Act I Climax (the public trial)

## Adventure 1: The Night Messengers

### Original TftS Summary
In the Shadow Realm, villagers are plagued by spectral "night messengers" who deliver cryptic warnings before disasters. The heroes must discover why the dead are rising and what they're trying to communicate.

### Yllmourne Adaptation: Dagmara's Final Messages

**Hook**: The night after the party arrives in Yllmourne, **three ghostly figures** appear to different party members in dreams or visions. Each is a victim connected to Dagmara's investigation, and each delivers a cryptic warning before fading.

**Level Range**: 1-2 (introductory investigation)

### Location Mapping

| TftS Location | Yllmourne Equivalent |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Shadow Realm village | Dockside slums (Mudskipper quarantine zone) |
| Haunted cemetery | Yllmourne mass graves (Plague Hill) |
| Spectral bridge | The Drowned Pier (where victims wash ashore) |
| Necromancer's tower | Abandoned lighthouse (used by cultists) |

### NPC Integration

**Keep Original**:
- **The First Messenger** (TftS): A child who drowned. In Yllmourne, this becomes **Little Bess**, a dock-worker's daughter who died in the first ship sinking. Her ghost appears soaking wet, coughing brine.

**Substitute**:
- **The Village Elder** (TftS) → **Old Torvin** (Yllmourne): Former harbourmaster, died of Mudskipper. His ghost is skeletal, lungs rattling with seawater.

**Add**:
- **Dagmara's Ghost**: Appears briefly to the Agent of Maal, eyes missing (as her corpse was mutilated). She cannot speak clearly (her throat was cut), but points desperately toward the lighthouse.

**Fuse**:
- **The Betrayed Lover** (TftS) + **Callista's Reflection**: A merchant betrayed by a business partner becomes Callista's "mirror-self"—a ghostly version of her that appears in reflections, begging for help.

### Key Scenes

**Scene 1: The First Haunting**
- **Trigger**: Party rests after initial investigations
- **Event**: Each PC experiences a vision/dream of one messenger
- **Clues Delivered**:
  - Little Bess: *"It came from below... the singing... the idol sang and the ship screamed..."*
  - Old Torvin: *"The plague isn't sickness... it's drowning... someone summoned the tide..."*
  - Dagmara: Points toward the lighthouse; mouths the word *"Faylene"*

**Scene 2: Plague Hill Investigation**
- **Location**: Mass graves outside the city
- **Encounter**: Gravediggers (potential witnesses); zombie plague victims (2-4 **zombies**, CR 1/4 each) clawing out of shallow graves
- **Clues**:
  - **DC 13 Investigation**: Recent graves disturbed from below, not above (something is *animating* the dead, not raising them)
  - **DC 15 Religion**: Necromantic energy, but the signature is wrong—it's *incidental* necromancy, a side effect of the plague curse

**Scene 3: The Drowned Pier**
- **Location**: Abandoned dock where ship sinking victims wash ashore
- **Encounter**: **2 Shadows** (CR 1/2 each) manifested from drowned sailors' despair
- **Clues**:
  - Bodies show claw marks matching those on sunken ship hulls
  - One body clutches a barnacled wooden idol (Tiny object, faint conjuration magic)
  - **DC 12 Arcana**: The idol is a beacon—it *calls* something from the depths

**Scene 4: The Abandoned Lighthouse**
- **Location**: Old lighthouse on rocky outcrop, supposedly abandoned
- **Reality**: Used by **Rotarist cultists** (servants of Faylene) to perform rituals
- **Encounter**:
  - **3 Cultists** (CR 1/8 each, use **cultist** stat block)
  - **1 Cult Fanatic** (CR 2, leader)
  - If the idol from Scene 3 is brought here, it begins pulsing, calling a **Water Elemental** (CR 5—too strong for level 1 PCs; they must flee or banish it)
- **Clues**:
  - Ritual notes mention "cleansing Yllmourne of heretical knowledge"
  - Correspondence signed with a symbol of Onotas (but twisted, wrong)
  - A list of targets: the Library, Dagmara, Navula, Callista

**Scene 5: Confronting the Messengers**
- **Resolution**: If players solve the mystery (identify the cultists, retrieve the idol, stop the ritual), the ghosts appear one final time to thank them
- **Reward**: Dagmara's ghost grants a **blessing**: once per long rest, the Agent of Maal can reroll a failed Wisdom saving throw (lasts until Act I concludes)

### Investigation Hooks

**Primary Mystery**: Why are the dead rising as messengers?
- **Answer**: The plague curse creates necromantic "leakage"; restless spirits try to warn the living about the curse's source

**Secondary Mystery**: What connects the messengers' warnings?
- **Answer**: All victims died due to the planar conspiracy—ship sinkings (Little Bess), plague (Old Torvin), assassination (Dagmara)

**Tertiary Hook**: Who operates the lighthouse?
- **Answer**: Faylene's Rotarists use it as a secondary ritual site; shutting it down hinders the conspiracy

### Faction Clock Impact

- **The Plague**: -1 tick (stopping the lighthouse ritual slows plague spread slightly)
- **Cy's Legitimacy**: +1 tick (if players report findings to Cy, he publicly praises their work)
- **Library Preservation**: +1 tick (cultists' target list reveals the Library is in danger)
- **Planar Incursion**: No change (players don't yet understand the planar connection)

### Rewards

**Treasure**:
- 50 gp from cultists' lockbox
- Barnacled idol (can be sold for 25 gp or studied for +2 bonus on future Arcana checks about water elementals)
- Ritual notes (provide advantage on future Investigation checks about the Rotarists)

**Experience**: Milestone to **Level 2** upon completing this adventure

## Adventure 2: Hunters of Tenebrous Plain

### Original TftS Summary
Hunters in the Shadow Realm are being picked off by a monstrous creature. The heroes must track it to its lair and discover it's a cursed victim, not a mindless beast.

### Yllmourne Adaptation: The Plague-Touched Children

**Hook**: Navula begs the party to rescue **plague-touched children** quarantined in the slums. Authorities plan to burn the quarantine zone to "contain the spread," but the children aren't contagious—they're *transforming*.

**Level Range**: 2-3

### Location Mapping

| TftS Location                     | Yllmourne Equivalent                               |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Tenebrous Plain (misty grassland) | Mudskipper Flats (tidal marshland below the slums) |
| Hunter's lodge                    | Quarantine barracks (guarded by city watch)        |
| Monster's lair (cave)             | Sea cave complex (Navula's extended grotto)        |

### NPC Integration

**Keep Original**:
- **The Cursed Hunter** (TftS): A tragic figure transformed by curse. In Yllmourne, this becomes **Jorah**, a dockworker transformed into a **sea spawn** (VGtM, CR 1)—he's hostile but pitiable.

**Add**:
- **The Children** (5 NPC kids, ages 6-12): Showing early Mudskipper symptoms but stable. They're terrified, hiding in the caves with Navula.
- **Captain Reld** (lawful neutral human guard): Ordered to burn the quarantine zone; genuinely believes it's necessary to save the city. Can be persuaded (DC 16 Persuasion) or must be fought.

**Substitute**:
- **The Monster Hunter** (TftS) → **Gritch** (the goblin informant): He's been tracking the "creature" for bounty money; will help the party for a cut.

### Key Scenes

**Scene 1: The Quarantine Zone**
- **Location**: Slum district, roped off with red flags
- **Situation**: Captain Reld and 6 **guards** prepare torches and oil
- **Challenge**: Party must negotiate, sneak the children out, or create a distraction
- **Social Encounter**:
  - **DC 14 Persuasion**: Convince Reld the children aren't contagious (requires evidence, like Navula's testimony or a Medicine check)
  - **DC 16 Intimidation**: Threaten legal consequences (works better if party has Cy's backing)
  - **Combat Option**: 1 **veteran** (Captain Reld) + 6 **guards** (CR 1/8 each)—narratively messy, antagonizes authorities

**Scene 2: The Mudskipper Flats**
- **Location**: Tidal marsh, ankle-deep water, thick fog
- **Encounter**: **2 Sea Spawn** (CR 1 each) hunting—one is Jorah, the other a stranger
- **Twist**: If the party subdues Jorah without killing him, he gasps out warnings: *"Run... the tide... the Choir... they're coming..."*
- **Clues**:
  - Sea spawn are former plague victims who fully transformed
  - The transformation is accelerated by proximity to the cursed idols (from Adventure 1)
  - Jorah's body has a brand: a spiral wave symbol (mark of the *Abyssal Choir* cult)

**Scene 3: Navula's Grotto (Extended)**
- **Location**: Deeper cave system beyond Navula's usual hideout
- **Discovery**: The children have built a makeshift shelter; Navula treats them with herbal remedies
- **Challenge**: The children are scared, won't trust strangers easily
  - **DC 12 Persuasion or Medicine**: Earn their trust by helping treat symptoms
  - One child, **Pip**, has visions due to the curse—he draws pictures of "the singing ship" (the *Abyssal Choir*)

**Scene 4: The Hunting Party**
- **Complication**: Gritch arrives with **2 hired thugs**, planning to capture the children for bounty
- **Encounter**: **Gritch** (use **bandit captain** stats, CR 2) + **2 thugs** (CR 1/2 each)
- **Moral Choice**: Gritch is just trying to survive; he can be bribed (30 gp), intimidated (DC 14), or beaten
- **Outcome**: If the party spares Gritch, he becomes a recurring ally/informant

**Scene 5: Securing Sanctuary**
- **Resolution**: The party must find a safe place for the children
- **Options**:
  - **The Library**: Elara agrees to hide them (advances Library Preservation clock)
  - **The Temple of Maal**: If a temple exists in Yllmourne, priests shelter them
  - **Navula's Grotto**: Reinforce it with magic wards (requires spellcaster skill)
  - **Cy's Palace**: Convince the Governor to grant asylum (DC 15 Persuasion, requires political capital)

### Investigation Hooks

**Primary Mystery**: What's causing the transformations?
- **Answer**: The plague curse + proximity to idols = mutation into sea spawn

**Secondary Mystery**: Who branded Jorah?
- **Answer**: The *Abyssal Choir* cult kidnaps plague victims for rituals

**Tertiary Hook**: What do Pip's visions mean?
- **Answer**: He's psychically linked to the *Abyssal Choir*'s ship; his drawings provide a map (used in Act II)

### Faction Clock Impact

- **The Plague**: -2 ticks (saving the children proves the plague can be managed, not just feared)
- **Cy's Legitimacy**: +1 tick if party works with authorities; -1 tick if they fight guards
- **Sefton's Dominance**: +1 tick if party uses Gritch's underworld contacts
- **Library Preservation**: +1 tick if children are hidden there

### Rewards

**Treasure**:
- 75 gp from Gritch (if he's convinced to pay)
- Navula's gratitude: She brews 3 **potions of water breathing** for the party
- Pip's drawings: Provide +2 bonus on future checks to locate the *Abyssal Choir*

**Experience**: Milestone to **Level 3** after completing Adventures 2-3

## Adventure 3: The Weeper in Shadow

### Original TftS Summary
A sorrowful spirit haunts a forest, causing plants to wither. Heroes discover the spirit was wronged and must right the injustice to lay it to rest.

### Yllmourne Adaptation: The Waterlogged Mountain

**Hook**: Fishermen report that **Waterlogged Mountain** (a forested hill overlooking the harbour) is "crying"—streams run black, trees weep sap, and anyone who enters feels overwhelming grief. A spirit is corrupting the land.

**Level Range**: 2-3

### Location Mapping

| TftS Location | Yllmourne Equivalent |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Shadow forest | Waterlogged Mountain forest |
| Weeping willow grove | Corrupted spring (source of Yllmourne's freshwater) |
| Spirit's grave | Shrine to a forgotten sea deity |

### NPC Integration

**Keep Original**:
- **The Weeper** (TftS): A ghost wronged in life. In Yllmourne, this becomes **Mira Saltborn**, a priestess of a sea goddess who was murdered 50 years ago for opposing Yllmourne's founding (the city was built on a sacred site).

**Add**:
- **Forester Thom** (neutral good human ranger): Local who's been trying to stop the corruption; provides guidance but is out of his depth.

**Fuse**:
- **The Corrupted Dryad** (TftS) + **Mira's Rage**: Mira's grief has partially corrupted a dryad guardian, creating a hostile but tragic encounter.

### Key Scenes

**Scene 1: The Grieving Forest**
- **Location**: Outer forest of Waterlogged Mountain
- **Atmosphere**: Trees drip black sap; animals flee; DC 12 Wisdom saving throw or suffer 1 level of exhaustion from overwhelming sadness
- **Encounter**: **2 Twig Blights** (CR 1/8 each) and **1 Vine Blight** (CR 1/2)—corrupted by Mira's grief
- **Clues**:
  - Carvings on trees: old prayers to a goddess named **Thessan** (sea/storm deity)
  - The corruption flows from the central spring

**Scene 2: The Corrupted Spring**
- **Location**: Heart of the forest, a natural spring now choked with black algae
- **Encounter**: **Corrupted Dryad** (use **dryad** stat block, CR 1, but hostile and able to cast *inflict wounds* instead of healing spells)
- **Roleplay**: The dryad is in agony, begging to be freed; she's bound to Mira's grief
- **Clues**:
  - The spring feeds Yllmourne's water supply—if the corruption spreads, the entire city is poisoned
  - Mira's ghost is bound to a shrine deeper in the forest

**Scene 3: The Forgotten Shrine**
- **Location**: Hidden stone shrine, overgrown with vines
- **Discovery**: Shrine to Thessan, defaced during Yllmourne's founding; Mira's skeleton lies before it, throat cut
- **Encounter**: **Mira's Ghost** (use **specter** stat block, CR 1) appears
- **Roleplay**:
  - Mira weeps, unable to communicate clearly
  - She was murdered by Yllmourne's founders for protesting the city's construction on Thessan's sacred ground
  - Her grief corrupts the land because her death was never acknowledged

**Scene 4: Laying Mira to Rest**
- **Challenge**: The party must decide how to give Mira peace
- **Options**:
  - **Restore the Shrine**: DC 15 Religion check, 1 hour of work, cleansing ritual
  - **Speak with Dead**: Let Mira tell her story; witnessing her truth brings peace
  - **Public Acknowledgment**: Promise to reveal her story in Yllmourne (requires follow-through in future sessions)
  - **Combat**: Destroy the ghost (narratively unsatisfying; corruption returns in days)

**Scene 5: Cleansing the Spring**
- **Resolution**: Once Mira is at peace, the spring clears
- **Reward**: The dryad is freed, grants the party **goodberries** (1d4+4, replenish daily for a week)
- **Long-Term**: The water supply is saved; citizens notice the improvement (builds trust)

### Investigation Hooks

**Primary Mystery**: What's causing the corruption?
- **Answer**: Mira's unresolved murder and desecration of Thessan's shrine

**Secondary Mystery**: Why does this matter now?
- **Answer**: The planar incursion is awakening old curses and forgotten grudges

**Tertiary Hook**: Who is Thessan?
- **Answer**: A sea goddess predating Onotas's dominance in Yllmourne; worshippers were suppressed

### Faction Clock Impact

- **The Plague**: -1 tick (clean water helps resist disease)
- **Cy's Legitimacy**: +1 tick if party publicizes Mira's story (shows government accountability)
- **Library Preservation**: +1 tick if party researches Thessan in the Library (justifies preserving "heretical" knowledge)
- **Planar Incursion**: -1 tick (laying old grudges to rest weakens planar agents' leverage)

### Rewards

**Treasure**:
- Mira's **holy symbol of Thessan** (acts as +1 focus for clerics/druids of sea deities)
- 100 gp worth of ancient coins from the shrine
- Dryad's blessing: 1d4+4 **goodberries** per day for 7 days

**Experience**: Contributes to **Level 3** milestone

## Adventure 4: A Death Among Pale Roses

### Original TftS Summary
A murder at a noble's garden party; heroes must solve the locked-room mystery while navigating high-society intrigue.

### Yllmourne Adaptation: Murder at Cy's Court

**Hook**: Governor Cy hosts a diplomatic banquet to ease tensions between factions. Mid-feast, **Lord Harren** (a Cy loyalist) drops dead—poisoned. All exits were guarded; the killer is among the guests. Cy begs the party to solve this before it ignites factional war.

**Level Range**: 3

### Location Mapping

| TftS Location | Yllmourne Equivalent |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Noble's estate | Governor's Palace, Grand Hall |
| Rose garden | Palace courtyard with fountain |
| Locked study | Harren's private chambers |

### NPC Integration

**Keep Original**:
- **The Jealous Rival** (TftS): In Yllmourne, this is **Sefton**, who had motive (Harren opposed him) but is innocent (for once).

**Add**:
- **Governor Cy**: Host, desperate to avoid scandal
- **Prelate Faylene**: Guest, secretly the true culprit (uses a proxy to administer poison)
- **Callista Whitesail**: Guest, still under Faylene's hex; her curse makes her seem suspicious

**Substitute**:
- **The Detective Rival** (TftS) → **Captain Reld** (if he survived Adventure 2): Investigating alongside the party, potentially helpful or obstructive

### Key Scenes

**Scene 1: The Banquet Hall**
- **Situation**: 20+ guests, servers, guards; Lord Harren collapses mid-toast
- **Challenge**: Interview witnesses before Cy locks down the palace
- **Clues**:
  - **DC 12 Medicine**: Harren was poisoned within the last 10 minutes (fast-acting)
  - **DC 14 Investigation**: Harren's wine glass has residue (not present in other glasses)
  - **Witnesses**: Several saw Callista near Harren's glass (she's being framed)

**Scene 2: Interrogations**
- **NPCs to Question**:
  - **Sefton**: Admits he hated Harren but has an alibi (was arguing with Cy during the poisoning)
  - **Callista**: Panicked, knows she's being framed, begs for help (her hex is intensifying)
  - **Faylene**: Calm, offers to "pray for guidance," subtly directs suspicion toward Callista
  - **Servants**: One (a Rotarist plant) is nervous; DC 13 Insight reveals he's lying
- **Evidence**: The poisoned wine was delivered by a servant who "received special instructions from a hooded guest"

**Scene 3: The Locked Chamber**
- **Location**: Harren's private study, locked from inside
- **Discovery**: Harren was investigating the Rotarists; his notes implicate Faylene
- **Encounter**: **1 Invisible Stalker** (CR 6—too strong for direct combat) left by Faylene to destroy evidence
- **Challenge**: Stealth/puzzle to retrieve notes without triggering the stalker; DC 15 Arcana to dispel its orders

**Scene 4: The Accusation**
- **Climax**: The party must publicly accuse the killer at Cy's court
- **Options**:
  - **Accuse Callista** (incorrect): She's arrested; Faylene's conspiracy continues; players lose trust
  - **Accuse the Servant** (partial truth): He's guilty of delivery but not the mastermind; executed, Faylene escapes scrutiny
  - **Accuse Faylene** (correct but dangerous): Requires strong evidence (Harren's notes + servant testimony + Callista's hex); Faylene denies it, but Cy is suspicious
- **Outcome**: If Faylene is correctly accused, she cannot be arrested (Cy fears angering Onotas faith) but is now an open enemy

**Scene 5: Aftermath**
- **Resolution**: Cy rewards the party, grants them official investigator status
- **Callista**: If cleared, becomes a grateful ally; funds party's investigations
- **Faylene**: If exposed, accelerates her plans (sets up Act II escalation)

### Investigation Hooks

**Primary Mystery**: Who poisoned Harren?
- **Answer**: Faylene, via a Rotarist servant, to silence her opposition

**Secondary Mystery**: Why frame Callista?
- **Answer**: Faylene is hexing her as a test case for wider curses; framing her removes a political rival

**Tertiary Hook**: What was Harren investigating?
- **Answer**: Links between the Rotarists and the *Abyssal Choir*

### Faction Clock Impact

- **Cy's Legitimacy**: +2 ticks if killer is correctly identified (restores faith in justice)
- **Sefton's Dominance**: +1 tick if party frames Callista (chaos benefits him)
- **Library Preservation**: +1 tick if Harren's notes are saved (evidence of conspiracy)
- **Planar Incursion**: +1 tick if Faylene is exposed (she escalates her plans in response)

### Rewards

**Treasure**:
- 200 gp reward from Cy
- Harren's **+1 rapier** (elegant, engraved with roses)
- Callista's gratitude: She funds the party, providing 500 gp over time

**Experience**: Contributes to **Level 3** milestone

## Adventure 5: Reflections and Remembrance

### Original TftS Summary
A mirror curse traps victims in reflective surfaces; heroes must free them by resolving their regrets.

### Yllmourne Adaptation: Breaking Callista's Hex

**Hook**: After the banquet murder, Callista's hex worsens—she's trapped in mirrors, unable to escape her reflections. The party must enter the "mirror realm" to break Faylene's curse.

**Level Range**: 3

### Location Mapping

| TftS Location | Yllmourne Equivalent |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Cursed manor | Callista's estate |
| Mirror realm (pocket dimension) | Hex-created demiplane |
| Shattered mirror hall | Faylene's ritual chamber (psychic projection) |

### NPC Integration

**Keep Original**:
- **The Mirror Victim** (TftS): Callista herself, trapped and tormented

**Add**:
- **Callista's Reflection**: A twisted, hostile version representing her fears and regrets
- **Faylene's Shadow**: A psychic projection of Faylene monitoring the hex

**Fuse**:
- **The Mirror Mage** (TftS) + **Faylene's Power**: The architect of the curse

### Key Scenes

**Scene 1: The Manifestation**
- **Trigger**: Callista collapses at her estate; her body turns glass-like
- **Challenge**: The party must perform a ritual to enter the hex
  - **DC 14 Arcana**: Identify the hex's structure
  - **Components**: A mirror, salt circle, Callista's blood (willing)
- **Entry**: Party is pulled into a psychic mirror dimension

**Scene 2: The Twisted Estate**
- **Location**: A warped version of Callista's home, everything reversed/distorted
- **Encounter**: **3 Shadows** (CR 1/2 each) manifested from Callista's guilt
- **Clues**:
  - Mirrors show Callista's regrets: business rivals she ruined, loved ones she neglected
  - Faylene's voice echoes: *"Repentance is the only path..."*

**Scene 3: Confronting the Reflection**
- **Encounter**: **Callista's Dark Reflection** (use **Banshee** stat block, CR 4)
- **Twist**: The reflection cannot be destroyed—only reconciled
- **Resolution**: Party must convince Callista (DC 15 Persuasion) to forgive herself
  - Success: Reflection merges peacefully, weakening the hex
  - Failure: Combat continues; defeating the reflection breaks the hex violently (harms Callista, 4d6 psychic damage)

**Scene 4: Faylene's Trap**
- **Location**: Central mirror hall, Faylene's psychic projection appears
- **Encounter**: **Faylene's Shadow** (use **Priest** stat block, CR 2, but with illusory duplicates)
- **Dialogue**: Faylene mocks the party, claims she's "saving Yllmourne from heretics"
- **Challenge**: Defeating the shadow doesn't harm Faylene but reveals her ritual components (a cursed mirror shard)

**Scene 5: Exiting the Hex**
- **Resolution**: With the hex weakened, the party and Callista can escape
- **Reward**: Callista is freed but scarred; she owes the party her life
- **Evidence**: The cursed mirror shard is physical proof of Faylene's guilt

### Investigation Hooks

**Primary Mystery**: How is Callista cursed?
- **Answer**: Faylene blessed her with a hex-laced prayer during the banquet

**Secondary Mystery**: Why target Callista?
- **Answer**: She's politically neutral, wealthy, and influential—perfect test subject for mass hexes

**Tertiary Hook**: What is Faylene's endgame?
- **Answer**: She plans to hex all of Cy's supporters simultaneously (Act II revelation)

### Faction Clock Impact

- **Cy's Legitimacy**: +1 tick (Callista becomes a vocal Cy supporter after rescue)
- **Library Preservation**: +1 tick (Callista donates to the Library in gratitude)
- **Planar Incursion**: -1 tick (breaking the hex disrupts planar agents' plans)

### Rewards

**Treasure**:
- Cursed mirror shard (evidence against Faylene, can be weaponized with DC 18 Arcana check)
- Callista's gift: **Cloak of Protection**
- 150 gp

**Experience**: Contributes to **Level 3** milestone

## Adventure 6: Big Briar's Blessings

### Original TftS Summary
A druid offers blessings that turn into curses; heroes must stop him before he corrupts the entire region.

### Yllmourne Adaptation: The Cursed Barrier Island

**Hook**: Fisherfolk report that **Briar Isle** (a barrier island) now grants "blessings"—those who visit return healed but changed. Within days, they transform into plant-like creatures. A corrupted druid, **Big Briar**, serves the planar conspiracy.

**Level Range**: 3-4

### Location Mapping

| TftS Location | Yllmourne Equivalent |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Corrupted grove | Briar Isle's central thicket |
| Druid's sanctuary | Ancient stone circle |
| Blessed victims' village | Fishing hamlet on the coast |

### NPC Integration

**Keep Original**:
- **Big Briar** (TftS): A well-meaning druid corrupted by planar forces. In Yllmourne, he's been deceived by an agent of Thellos into believing his transformations are "evolutions."

**Add**:
- **Transformed Villagers**: 8-10 NPCs partially transformed into **Needle Blights** (CR 1/4)

**Substitute**:
- **The Druid's Rival** (TftS) → **Forester Thom** (from Adventure 3): He's been tracking Big Briar, seeking help

### Key Scenes

**Scene 1: The Afflicted Village**
- **Location**: Coastal hamlet, half-abandoned
- **Situation**: Remaining villagers show early symptoms (bark-like skin, flowering hair)
- **Witnesses**: Describe Big Briar as "generous" and "touched by the gods"
- **Challenge**: Convince villagers to avoid the island (DC 14 Persuasion) or forcibly quarantine them

**Scene 2: Voyage to Briar Isle**
- **Travel**: Requires a boat; waters are treacherous (DC 13 group Survival check or face storm)
- **Encounter**: **1 Swarm of Quippers** (CR 1) and **2 Reef Sharks** (CR 1/2 each) attracted by blood in the water

**Scene 3: The Corrupted Grove**
- **Location**: Dense, overgrown forest; paths shift and confuse (DC 12 Survival to navigate)
- **Encounter**: **4 Needle Blights** (CR 1/4 each) and **2 Vine Blights** (CR 1/2 each)—former villagers
- **Clues**: The transformations are irreversible without high-level magic; mercy killings may be necessary

**Scene 4: The Stone Circle**
- **Location**: Ancient druidic site, now corrupted by planar energy
- **Encounter**: **Big Briar** (use **Druid** stat block, CR 2, with extra *entangle* and *spike growth* prepared)
- **Roleplay**: Big Briar genuinely believes he's "ascending" people; he's delusional, not malicious

## Act I Climax: The Public Trial

### Setup
After six adventures, the party has accumulated evidence:
- Faylene's involvement (Adventures 1, 4, 5)
- The *Abyssal Choir* cult (Adventures 1, 2, 6)
- Planar corruption (Adventures 3, 6)

However, a **scapegoat**—an innocent dockworker named **Kael**—has been arrested for Dagmara's murder. The authorities (pressured by Faylene) plan a quick execution to "close the case."

### The Trial Scene

**Location**: Public square, before Governor Cy and a crowd of hundreds

**Stakes**:
- If Kael is executed, the conspiracy continues unchallenged
- If the party exposes the truth, they make powerful enemies

**Structure**:
1. **Opening**: Kael is brought in chains; crowd bays for blood
2. **Prosecution**: Faylene (acting as spiritual counsel) presents "evidence" (planted)
3. **Defense**: Party may speak on Kael's behalf
4. **Evidence Phase**: Party presents their findings
5. **Judgment**: Cy rules based on evidence quality and public opinion

### Skill Challenge

**Goal**: Convince Cy and the crowd of Kael's innocence and hint at the deeper conspiracy

**DC Targets**:
- **DC 12**: Raise reasonable doubt about Kael
- **DC 15**: Implicate the Rotarists
- **DC 18**: Publicly name Faylene (dangerous)

**Checks** (party can attempt 3-5 before Cy rules):
- **Persuasion**: Sway the crowd with emotional appeals
- **Investigation**: Present physical evidence (idols, ritual notes, mirror shard)
- **Intimidation**: Threaten legal consequences for false accusations
- **Insight**: Read Cy's reactions, tailor arguments to his concerns

**Successes Needed**: 4+ successes = Kael freed, investigation officially sanctioned
- 2-3 successes = Kael freed but party earns Faylene's enmity
- 0-1 successes = Kael executed, party discredited

### Outcomes

**Full Success** (4+ successes):
- Kael is freed and becomes an ally
- Cy grants the party official investigator status and resources
- Faylene is publicly humiliated but not arrested (she retreats to plot Act II revenge)
- **The Plague** clock: -2 ticks (public knows the truth)
- **Cy's Legitimacy**: +3 ticks (justice is served)

**Partial Success** (2-3 successes):
- Kael is freed but controversy remains
- Party gains fame but also enemies
- Faylene accelerates her plans
- **Cy's Legitimacy**: +1 tick
- **Planar Incursion**: +1 tick (forced into the open, the conspiracy escalates)

**Failure** (0-1 successes):
- Kael is executed
- Party is warned to stop investigating
- Must operate underground in Act II
- **Cy's Legitimacy**: -1 tick (innocent man killed)
- **Sefton's Dominance**: +1 tick (Cy looks weak)
