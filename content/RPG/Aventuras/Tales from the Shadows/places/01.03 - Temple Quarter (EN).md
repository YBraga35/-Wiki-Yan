# Temple Quarter — Rotarist Precinct

## Overview
Founded around the alabaster Basilica of Onotas, the Temple Quarter blends sanctified plazas with narrow cloisters, reliquary vaults, and hidden catacombs. It is the spiritual heart of Yllmourne—and the site where Faylene’s corrupted prayers amplify the Mudskipper curse until the hex is broken.

Use alongside [[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)#Scene 4: The Abandoned Lighthouse|Act I lighthouse arc]], [[02.00 - Act II - Political Intrigue (Lv5-7)#Adventure 8: The Hexed Court|Hexed Court]], and [[00.20 - Maal Doctrine (EN)|Maal Doctrine]] references.

## Cinematic Beats
- **Evening Vespers (Act I)**: Bells toll; golden lamplight spills onto stone mosaics depicting Onotas’ mercy. Rotarist acolytes chant as Faylene’s shadow lengthens unnaturally—a visual hint of her involuntary cursework.
- **Hex Backlash (Act II)**: During the confrontation, the quarter vibrates with discordant hymns. Stained glass fractures, bleeding radiant and shadow energy across worshippers. Ideal for the climax exposing Sefton’s hex.
- **Ecumenical Vigil (Act III)**: After Faylene’s redemption, describe joint ceremonies of Rotarists, Maalites, and Thranengi shamans purifying fonts and cisterns, tying into [[03.00 - Act III - Rise of the Hero (Lv8-12)#The Ritual of Cleansing|Ritual of Cleansing]].
- **Triumphal Procession (Act IV)**: As tribunal verdict approaches, faithful march with lanterns and scales, chanting Maal’s maxims. The district becomes a living symbol of restored balance.

## Story Hooks by Act
- **Act I**: Sneak into restricted reliquaries to confirm Faylene’s involvement; navigate moral dilemma when Rotarist clergy plead for help amid suspicion.
- **Act II**:
  - Recover the cursed mirror shard used on Callista (links to [[01.20 - Tribunal Script (EN)|trial evidence]]).
  - Track hex glyphs across shrines to locate Sefton’s embedded agents; each glyph removed reduces Plague clock per [[apend.01 - Special Mechanics Guide#Mudskipper Plague Clock|Special Mechanics]].
- **Act III**: Host reconciliation summit between Faylene and Navula; success grants divine support for final rituals.

## Mechanical & Atmosphere Notes
- Sanctified ground grants advantage on Wisdom saves to faithful characters; corrupted zones invert effect until cleansed.
- Catacomb chases: treat cloisters as dim light with echoing acoustics—Stealth checks have disadvantage unless silence spells used.
- Introduce Rotarist liturgy cards as quick props; players quoting them in roleplay may earn inspiration when arguing doctrine.
