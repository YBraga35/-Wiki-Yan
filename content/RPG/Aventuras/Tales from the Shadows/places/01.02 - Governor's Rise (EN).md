# Governor’s Rise — Civic Heights

## Overview
Governor’s Rise crowns Yllmourne’s cliff face with marble terraces, a fortified courthouse, and the Governor’s palace. It is where official justice clashes with backroom politics; the setting for Kael’s tribunal, sessions with Mayor Myles Lockton, and negotiations between Cy and Sefton’s envoys.

Core scenes live in [[01.20 - Tribunal Script (EN)|Kael’s trial]], [[02.00 - Act II - Political Intrigue (Lv5-7)|Act II intrigue]], and [[03.10 - Naval Briefing (EN)|blockade planning]].

## Cinematic Beats
- **Marble Approach (Act I)**: As the Agent climbs the serpentine stair, polished statues of Maal’s arbiters loom overhead, their eyes shadowed by morning mist. Guards exchange wary looks, unsure whether to salute or challenge the newcomer.
- **Tribunal Tension (Act I Climax)**: Describe packed galleries, citizens pressed against wrought-iron railings, incense smoke curling under skylights while rain drums outside. Cy’s gavel cracks like thunder.
- **Political Storm (Act II)**: During diplomatic meetings, gusts off the sea slam shutters; papers scatter across the war-table as Sefton’s envoys demand concessions. Showcase Lockton’s frayed nerves—sweat beading despite the chill.
- **Siege Mentality (Act III)**: After blockade escalation, the palace courtyard fills with sandbag berms and signal fires. Distant cannon echoes reach the terrace, and runners arrive breathless bearing casualty reports.
- **Judgment Dawn (Act IV)**: The plaza becomes a ceremonial ground; banners of Maal, Rotarists, and the Merchant League hang side by side. Sunlight breaks through clouds as the tribunal convenes.

## Story Hooks by Act
- **Act I**: Secure official backing by convincing Lockton and Harlan; find hidden evidence caches in the palace archives (potential heist sub-scene). Leads directly into [[01.20 - Tribunal Script (EN)|trial]] skill challenge.
- **Act II**: 
  - Broker compromises between Cy and Sefton—success influences [[03.10 - Naval Briefing (EN)|blockade outcome]].
  - Counter infiltration by Sefton’s spies posing as clerks; reveal using contested Insight vs. Deception.
- **Act III**: Establish headquarters for privateers; use Governor’s Rise as negotiation venue for Thranengi alliance (see [[03.10 - Naval Briefing (EN)#Thranengi Alliance|scenario]]).

## Mechanical & Atmosphere Notes
- Skill challenge arena: allow PCs to collect “Influence tokens” by winning debates, presenting evidence, or aiding civilians—each token grants +1 to next Persuasion or Insight check within the Rise.
- Security details: treat palace guards as `Knights` with `Protection` fighting style; accessing archives requires DC 17 Investigation plus credentials.
- Environmental hazards: slick marble (Dex save DC 12 in storms) and steep terraces (falling risk) add stakes to chase scenes.
