# NPCs and Villagers

## Major NPCs

### Mayor Mara of Enoch
**Role:** Quest giver, authority figure
**Age:** 52
**Appearance:** Grey-streaked hair in tight bun, practical wool dress, iron chain necklace, weathered hands from years of farming before politics

**Personality:**
- Protective of village reputation
- Practical to a fault
- Secretly terrified but maintains composure
- Would rather handle things quietly than cause panic

**Motivations:**
- Save her nephew Timothy (age 8, among the missing)
- Prevent economic disaster from failed harvest
- Maintain order and prevent mob panic

**Key Information:**
- Knows about the sealed grove but won't volunteer it
- Has 200gp in emergency funds for heroes
- Father was involved in sealing grove 50 years ago
- Keeps his journal hidden (contains ritual to reseal grove)

**Roleplaying Notes:**
- Speaks in short, clipped sentences when stressed
- Touches iron necklace when nervous
- Voice: Firm but with underlying tremor

**Sample Dialogue:**
- "Wild animals. Nothing more. We can't afford panic."
- "Find my Timothy and I'll double your reward."
- "The harvest must succeed. Enoch depends on it."

---

### Granny Willa - The Singer
**Role:** Lore keeper, source of prophecy
**Age:** 89 (claims to be over 100)
**Appearance:** Milky eyes, gnarled hands, always has her worn songbook

**Personality:**
- Cryptic but helpful
- Genuinely cares for children
- Bitter about being ignored until crisis
- Sharp mind despite appearance

**Motivations:**
- Preserve old traditions (even dark ones)
- Protect her grandson Tomlin
- Pass on knowledge before she dies

**Key Information:**
- Knows all the old songs and their true meanings
- Witnessed the last grove incident as a child
- Can teach the "Harvest Flame" blessing (10-minute ritual)
- Her songbook contains map to grove's weak points

**Roleplaying Notes:**
- Rocks constantly in her chair
- Speaks in sing-song rhythm
- Intersperses speech with humming
- Voice: Cracked but melodious

**Sample Dialogue:**
- "Oh, you want THAT song? The one we don't sing?"
- "Fire and iron, dearie. Always fire and iron."
- "The Horned King remembers even if we forget."

### Brother Aldric - Harvest Domain Acolyte
**Role:** Religious authority, map provider
**Age:** 24
**Appearance:** Ink-stained fingers, simple brown robes with wheat embroidery, nervous energy

**Personality:**
- Scholarly but inexperienced
- Genuinely faithful
- Tends to over-explain
- Guilt-ridden about his teacher's disappearance

**Motivations:**
- Prove himself worthy of his position
- Find his missing mentor
- Protect the temple's reputation

**Key Information:**
- Has ancient map of Effigy Grove
- Can bless weapons (10-minute ritual)
- Knows about lunar ward puzzle
- Secretly has crush on baker's daughter

**Roleplaying Notes:**
- Fidgets with holy symbol
- Quotes scripture when nervous
- Adjusts non-existent spectacles
- Voice: Eager, slightly stammering

**Sample Dialogue:**
- "According to the Third Harvest Codex— oh, you just need the map?"
- "Master Durwin went alone. I should have stopped him."
- "The seeds are corrupted! Look how they grow!"

### Gunnar the Smith
**Role:** Weapon supplier, practical advice
**Age:** 41
**Appearance:** Massive arms, permanent soot stains, exhausted eyes, leather apron

**Personality:**
- Gruff but caring
- Superstitious (rightfully so)
- Works through problems physically
- Protective of his family

**Motivations:**
- Keep his family safe
- Maintain iron ward supply
- Prevent panic through preparation

**Key Information:**
- Witnessed sleepwalkers firsthand
- Can silver or cold-iron forge weapons
- Knows iron breaks enchantments
- Son almost joined sleepwalkers

**Roleplaying Notes:**
- Punctuates speech with hammer strikes
- Never stops working while talking
- Sweats constantly
- Voice: Deep, gravelly, tired

**Sample Dialogue:**
- "Iron's the answer. Always has been."
- "*CLANG* They walked right past. *CLANG* Eyes white as milk."
- "Take this. On the house. Bring my neighbors back."

## Cultist Organization

### The Circle of Wicker
**Leadership:** High Acolyte (unnamed, fled to grove)
**Members:** 12-20 locals, varying dedication
**Symbol:** Horned crown in twisted circle

**Motivations:**
- Desperate: Failed harvests mean starvation
- Corrupted: Promised power by the Horned King
- Coerced: Family members held hostage
- True Believers: Think they're saving the village

**Typical Cultist**
- **AC:** 12 (robes)
- **HP:** 9 (2d8)
- **Skills:** Deception +4, Religion +2
- **Equipment:** Concealed sickle, wicker charm, handful of cursed seeds

**Cultist Passwords/Phrases:**
- "The harvest hungers"
- "By root and crown"
- "The tithe is willing"

## NPC Interaction Quick Reference

### Information Flow Priority
1. **Essential:** Grove location, fire weakness, ritual nature
2. **Important:** Multiple entrances, sleepwalker patterns, iron effects
3. **Helpful:** Map details, blessed weapons, village history
4. **Flavor:** Personal stakes, relationships, fears

### NPC Knowledge Matrix
| NPC | Grove Location | Ritual Details | Weakness | History |
|-----|---------------|----------------|----------|---------|
| Mayor | Knows, hides | Suspects | No | Yes |
| Granny | Yes | Yes | Yes | Everything |
| Aldric | Map only | Some | Some | Book learning |
| Gunnar | Direction | No | Iron only | Recent events |

### Escalation Triggers
- **After 30 minutes:** Sleepwalker sighting
- **After 45 minutes:** Cultist confrontation
- **After 60 minutes:** Villager taken while party watches
- **After 75 minutes:** Grove lights visible from town
