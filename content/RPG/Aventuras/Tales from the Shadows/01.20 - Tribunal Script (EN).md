# Tribunal Script — The Case of Kael Thornnett

Use this English-language scene kit to run the Act I climax where the Agent of Maal argues before One-Eyed Cy to stop the execution of innocent dockworker Kael. Aligns with mechanics in [[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)#Climax: The Public Trial|Act I Climax]].

## Cast & Roles
- **Presiding Authority**: [[npc/apend.01 - Integrated NPCs Complete Guide#One-Eyed Cy|One-Eyed Cy]] — Governor acting as magistrate.
- **Spiritual Counsel (Prosecution)**: [[npc/apend.01 - Integrated NPCs Complete Guide#Prelate Faylene|Prelate Faylene]], presenting planted evidence.
- **Defense Advocate**: Player character (Agent of Maal), supported by Tor Underleaf (DMPC) for procedural cues.
- **Witnesses**:
  - Bonita Sandberg (alibi, journal transfer)
  - Callista Whitesail (hex victim; testimony risky)
  - Jorah Slate’s widow (identifies Abyssal Choir branding)

## Structure Overview

| Phase | Objectives | Suggested Lines |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Opening** | Set stakes, draw crowd reaction | Faylene: “We gather under Maal’s gaze to purge rot from our docks.”<br>Cy: “Order! Agent, the floor will be yours after counsel.” |
| **Prosecution Case** | Present forged evidence | Faylene waves bloodstained dagger (planted). Mentions Kael’s debt to Sefton. Calls for swift judgment. |
| **Defense Case** | Counter evidence, establish doubt | Agent cites Dagmara’s journal entry on Rotarist rituals ([[Dagmara's Journal - Investigative Summary (EN)|Entry Day -4]]). Tor prompts player with legal procedure if needed. |
| **Cross-Examination** | Expose inconsistencies | Bonita reveals arrest timeline; Jorah’s widow shows spiral brand not Onotanian icon; Callista (if brave) shows mirror hex. |
| **Rebuttal** | Faylene doubles down | Uses Onotanian scripture; attempts to redirect blame to Callista. |
| **Closing Arguments** | Appeal to justice, call for investigation | Agent: “Maal’s law demands truth, not expedient blood.” Faylene: “Mercy invites plague.” |
| **Judgment** | Cy rules based on success tally | Track per [[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)#Skill Challenge|Skill Challenge]] outcomes. |

## Skill Challenge Prompts
- **Persuasion (DC 12-18)**: “Governor, Dagmara’s final notes detail a cult using idols identical to those seized at the lighthouse.”
- **Investigation (DC 15)**: Display idol + journal sketch correlation (gains advantage if journal presented first).
- **Intimidation (DC 15)**: Threaten legal repercussion for evidence tampering; success rattles Faylene (disadvantage on her next check).
- **Insight (DC 13)**: Read Cy’s hesitation; tailor message (lawful duty versus keeping peace).

Failure or success outcomes follow table in Act I guide; remind yourself of faction-clock adjustments before session end.

## Crowd Interaction Table
Roll or choose when tension dips; each reaction can grant advantage/disadvantage on the next check.

| d6 | Reaction | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Dockworkers jeer, throwing rotten fish | Defense check at disadvantage unless calmed (DC 13 Performance). |
| 2 | Bonita shouts “Show them the brand!” | Grants advantage on Investigation when brand evidence used. |
| 3 | Rotarist plants whispers “Maal demands sacrifice” | Insight DC 12 to counter rumor; failure raises DC by +1 next check. |
| 4 | Tor Underleaf delivers impassioned plea | Advantage on next Persuasion roll; counts as Tor’s action. |
| 5 | Naval veterans chant “Justice, not fear” | Improves Cy’s attitude to Helpful for remainder of scene. |
| 6 | Faylene’s adherents begin prayer | If unchecked, adds +2 to Faylene’s next check; counter with Religion DC 14. |

## Evidence Quick Reference
- **Dagmara’s Journal** — links Faylene to lighthouse rituals.
- **Mirror Shard** — physical proof of Callista’s hex (from [[01.00 - Act I - Horror on the Docks (Lv1-4)#Scene 3: Mirror Realm Dive|Mirror Realm Dive]]).
- **Abyssal Choir Brand** — connects murder to cult activity.
- **Kael’s Work Ledger** — shows he was on night shift during murder (available via Bonita).

## Resolution Prompts
- **Full Success**: Cy: “Law stands. Kael walks free. Agent, by Maal’s authority, you are commissioned to uncover the rot.” Crowd cheers; Faylene’s mask cracks.
- **Partial Success**: Cy releases Kael but warns Agent to tread carefully; note Faylene’s parting curse (“You won’t always have the crowd.”).
- **Failure**: Cy reluctantly orders execution; describe solemn tolling bells. Record faction-clock shifts immediately.
