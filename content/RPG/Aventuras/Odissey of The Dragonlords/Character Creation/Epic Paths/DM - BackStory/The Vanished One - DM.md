# Integrating the Vanished One

The Oracle, Versi, has lived for millennia and has never loved anyone—until she laid eyes on the Vanished One. She will do anything to ensure that the Vanished One lives, but her jealousy knows no bounds.

## Meeting the Oracle
Versi asks the Vanished One to swear an oath that they will return to her once the prophecy has been fulfilled and the world has been saved from disaster. Once the oath is sworn, Versi will reveal that the Vanished One’s famed armor, along with the crown of the Dragonlords, are located in the Tomb of Xander at the Necropolis of Telamok. She refuses to offer this information unless the oath is sworn.

**Versi’s words:**
> "My heart, the Fates are cruel indeed to rip us apart so quickly after you have come to my rescue. Before I speak to you of your great destiny, I would ask you to swear an Oath to me, here and now, that once you have accomplished your fate and prevent the Doom of Thylea... that you will return to me. And let our hearts forever sing as one. (let Vanished One answer)  
> I always had such a 'soft spot' for mortals... but I had no idea what it meant to love, not truly, before I laid eyes upon you (Vanished One’s name). I shall speak now of your fate: you are destined to see your order rise once more - I see you, once more wearing your famed armour and crown. You must retake them. Seek them out in the Necropolis at Telamok. Old friends wait for you there."

## Versi’s Jealousy
Versi uses her divination magic to watch over the Vanished One. She becomes very jealous if anyone else shows romantic interest. If the Vanished One ever consummates another romance, Versi confronts the Vanished One one day later. She will try her best to kill whoever was intimate with the Vanished One.

## Gaius in Estoria
Gaius is a descendent of the Vanished One. Change his appearance as needed to fit the appearance of your Vanished One to some degree, but largely the passing of several centuries means he may not look exactly alike. Gaius knows the Vanished One is his ancestor but will not reveal this and remains interested in seeing the heroes slain in the sacrifice and will more readily suggest the Vanished One as the replacement for Anora, but will gladly accept all the heroes, to be better rid of them all.

## Your Tomb
At the Necropolis of Telamok, one of the early tombs to be discovered belongs to the Vanished One. It is resplendent in decor (design it to match the personality of your Vanished One), though the tomb itself seems to have been emptied of the relics that the Vanished One is seeking. 

## Speaking to Xander
The undead Xander at the Necropolis of Telamok recognizes the Vanished One and gives them a crown of the Dragonlords. However, their famed armor has been stolen by a man who “shared their blood.” 

This was Gaius, Commander of the Order of Sydon. He keeps the Vanished One's fabled relic in his room on the Island of Yonder. It rejects his attempts to attune to it but he continues to try.

## Speaking to Estor
Estor claims the Vanished One showed true valour in the days of the war, though he believes they’ve “grown fat on the affections of that watery tart.” He offers ‘redemption’ and to aid the Vanished One in recovering their memories by way of accepting his deal of possession.

## Versi’s Vision
When the heroes first set out in the Ultros, Versi appears and tells the Vanished One where to find one of the dragon eggs that are scattered across Thylea. 

- **(If Brass/Fire)**: She has had a vision of a dragon egg under the constellation of The Prisoner. This corresponds to the brass dragon egg on Fire Island (area V15).
- **(If Silver)**: An iceberg floats in the Forgotten Sea.
- **(Other/Alternative)**: She relays that ‘mysteries of the Dragonlords’ are hidden beneath the Fog in the Cerulean Gulf (in the Castle), or that it waits beneath the stars of the Turtle, “kept by an old friend” (Archdruid Sunarah keeps it in stasis at the Egg Hatchery).

## Memorials
Memorials to the Vanished One and the other Dragonlords exist on the Island of the Sleepy Dragon and at the Snout of Chelone. At both locations, the Vanished One in particular is treated with great reverence, and both the Elders of Sleeping Dragons and Archdruid Sunarah readily give an egg from their trust to the Vanished One. Sunarah in particular remembers the Vanished One from their time fighting the forces of the Titans, and says that they in particular inspired ‘her younger self’ to rebel against the gygans at Typhoon Palace.

## Confronting Gaius
Gaius reveals he is a descendent of the Vanished One, and claims that only by slaying his ancestor can he atone the sins the Dragonlords committed against the Twin Titans. As blood of their blood, Gaius was able to retrieve the relics from their tomb at Telamok.

## The Forgotten Dragon
If the heroes encounter Hezzebal, the dragon guarding the Mirror Prison in the Nether Sea, then the dragon blames the Vanished One for his long imprisonment.

## Divine Boon

If the Vanished One is ever killed, Versi will appear within 1 hour and kiss them, which restores them to life. This can only happen once.


# Memories

## Your Tomb at Telamok
Design the new Tomb at Telamok to reflect a bit of the personality of your player’s Vanished One (a more war-like character could have mosaics fighting a gygan or blemys, while a healer might be tending the wounded, etc.). 

Relay some sort of memory of these scenes, or ask your player to describe a scene showcasing what their hero was like during the war.

Add in details that show them to have been glorious and proud, but also that they left behind a loved one and child - or some other sign that may show they have progeny in some way, either because they were a philanderer of some sort or that they had a family; if your player would not have been comfortable with their character having children, have the tomb instead show a beloved younger sibling who carried the bloodline instead.

## The Mithral Mines
Here the Vanished One recalls Volkan and the dwarves forging weapons for them, perhaps even including the Vanished One’s relic.

## Mytros
The Vanished One recalls the city, so much smaller and crowded with desperate survivors and refugees during the war. Now, it is resplendent and bustling. A sense of pride fills them, as well as a sense that they missed seeing the city grow beyond what it once was. They may recall a memory of Telamok, who helped found the city.

## The Ghost Ship Ultros
The Vanished One recalls this place, and the grim nature of Estor. They do not remember him entirely however, and he takes advantage of this, trying to impress upon them that they were once friends. The Vanished One may have an inkling of something terrible afoot and should have advantage on checks to ascertain Estor’s intent - possessing their body.

## The Island of Yonder
The Vanished One recalls the slaughter of the gygans here at Yonder, that churned the seas red and dyed the fields with blood. Ask the player what their Vanished One’s reaction was to the massacre, and what role they played - either fighting on the island but stopping where Estor wouldn’t, or if they learned of it too late while fighting elsewhere.
